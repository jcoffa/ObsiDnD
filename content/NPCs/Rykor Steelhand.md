#  [[Rykor Steelhand]]

## Description

A large and hulking Hobgoblin covered in scars, with his iconic steel hand on his right arm. Not the kind of guy you want to mess with unless you have a death wish.

## Relationships

Directly manages [[The Iron Fang]]; the military group of mostly Hobgoblins operating out of [[Draskhrr]]. Rykor is the right-hand-man of [[Kaz the Cold]]; the Count of [[Draskhrr]] and its acting governer.

## Quests and Rumours

Rykor wishes to unite the Hobgoblins of [[The Frozen March]] under a unified military rule, either by loyalty, fear, desperation, or all of the above. His current goal is to use this militia to break through the only gate that leads into (and out of) [[The Frozen March]], presumably so that the Hobgoblins may take back their land from [[Utopia]] and enact revenge for their loss in the [[Clerical War]].
