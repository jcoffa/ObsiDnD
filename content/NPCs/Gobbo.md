#  [[Gobbo]]

>[!info]- Dramatic Irony - What the players know that the characters don't
>
> [[Gobbo]] is actually a character that the *players* know a **lot** about, even though to the *characters* he is steeped in mystery. [[Gobbo]] is the character played by Brodie (of [[The Collector]] fame) during "Campaign 2"; the campaign that followed "Triple G" which ended before this "Campaign 3" following The Reconstellators. Same goes for the [[Philip Fisher|Philip]] that he mentions; referencing the character played by Joseph (of [[Zrandovorski]] fame) in that same campaign.

## Description

A small goblin wearing a ~~toupee~~ hair system to cover up their bald head. They're adorned with only a tattered cloak and are otherwise naked.

They appear to possess **very** powerful psionic powers, being able to go completely invisible at will, can conjure psychic daggers out of thin air that never miss their target, and may be able to Astral Project (this may have been achieved with some assistance, however). Despite their easygoing, aloof, immature, friendly, and somewhat "dumb" demeanor, this is a goblin you do NOT want to underestimate or test your mettle against in combat.

He refers to himself as "The God of Good" and does not elaborate much on what that means (besides the obvious re-stating of himself). He appears to genuinely hold himself as if he were a literal God in the pantheon, alongside deities such as Helm and Mystryl, and reigns over the domain of... general "Goodness". As far as anyone can tell, this is simply not true despite the goblin's stubbornness to the contrary.

## Relationships

- Self-stated friendship with someone named "[[Philip Fisher|Philip]]" who lives on a different continent (i.e. not [[Teradin]]). From how [[Gobbo]] speaks about him, he sounds like a powerful magic user with a somewhat flippant personality.
