#  [[Toran the Red]]

## Description

An orcish(?) woman infamous in [[The Frozen March]] who seems to be a very violent arsonist trying to send a message. She is taller than a normal orc, and has blue skin and black hair with a streak of red through it. She has horns, and large teeth that perfectly fit into each other (like a Jack O' Lantern). While commonly thought to be an orc through the various wanted posters and descriptions of her throughout [[The Frozen March]], in-person she resembles an oni more than an orc.

She seems to have some sort of magic flowing through her (similar to the [[Session 5#The Most Horrific Encounter in D&D History|the Winter Wolf]] the party had encountered previously) that allows her to create portals throughout [[The Frozen March]] that lead to [[The Hearth of Hope]], but they move daily and she has no control over their location.

[[Toran the Red|Toran]] resides in [[The Hearth of Hope]]; a civilization she founded to bring together people in [[The Frozen March]] who don't necessarily want to leave their frigid new home.

## Relationships

- [[The Iron Fang]] has many active wanted posters to bring her to justice. They see her as a major threat to their own agenda and the safety of life in [[The Frozen March]] altogether.
- Views the leaders of [[The Iron Fang]]; [[Kaz the Cold]] and [[Rykor Steelhand]], as a threat to [[The Frozen March]].
- [[Session 14#An Uneventful Watch|Entered into an ad-hoc alliance with The Reconstellators]] after they were escorted to [[The Hearth of Hope]]. They share a common goal of hating [[The Iron Fang]]'s leadership.
