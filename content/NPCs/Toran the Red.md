#  [[Toran the Red]]

## Description

An orcish woman who seems to be a very violent arsonist trying to send a message. Who the message or is to, or what she's trying to say, are both entirely unknown.

She is taller than a normal orc, and has blue skin and black hair with a streak of red through it. She has horns, and large teeth that perfectly fit into each other (like a Jack O' Lantern).

She seems to have some sort of magic flowing through her (similar to the [[Session 5#The Most Horrific Encounter in D&D History|the Winter Wolf]] the party had encountered previously) that allows her to create portals throughout [[The Frozen March]] that lead to [[The Hearth of Hope]], but they move daily and she has no control of their location.
