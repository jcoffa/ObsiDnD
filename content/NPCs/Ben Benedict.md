#  [[Ben Benedict]]

## Description

An energetic, muscled, "silver fox of a man" with a full head of glorious black locks that gracefully fall to his shoulders, and a well-kept waxed curly moustache. While originally planned and trained from a young age to inherit his late father's bar, *Benedict's* in [[Fair Helm City]], about 10 years ago, a last-minute change to the will left him with only a sizeable fortune instead. With considerable riches and a sudden lack of responsibilities all at the same time, he bought a ship, *The SS Benedict* which he named after his late father, and hired a crew to explore the world as pirates.

## Relationships

- Son to Benedict Benedict (Benedict Sr.)
- The older twin brother to [[Robert Benedict]] by 6 seconds. They have a somewhat strained relationship due to [[Robert Benedict|his brother]]'s bitterness about his sudden responsibilities to maintain their late father's bar (a life that was long planned for himself).
- Likely enemies to The Reconstellators since [[Session 26#Lights, Bombs, Action|they stole his ship]], *The SS Benedict*, while he wasn't looking.

## Quests and Rumours

- Asked the party to free [[Robert Benedict|his brother]] from the shackles of duty to his late father by [[Burn Down Benedict's Bar|burning down his bar]].
- He and his pirate crew double as smugglers for [[Utopia]], delivering contraband and rare resources from [[The Frozen March]] to the benefit of the empire.
