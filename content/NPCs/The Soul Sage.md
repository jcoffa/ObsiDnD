#  [[The Soul Sage]]

- **Species**: Kalashtar
- **Age**: ~26
- **Discovered**: Learned name in [[Session 22#The Fault in Our Stars|Session 22]], met in-person [[Session 23#Backstage Passes, Part 2|Session 23]]
- **Aliases**: Vicci
- **Status**: Alive

## Description
A human-appearing male who wears a hooded navy blue robe with gold lace around the trim, the whites of his eyes are grey but the pupils are white, long black fingernails that appear painted, and glimmers of jewelry hidden beneath his hood. [[Session 25#Goddammit, We Committed a 9/11 Again|After the ritual failed]], his eye colour changed to normal blue eyes.

## Relationships

The leader of [[The Cult of the Stars]] that resided in [[Caelia]]. He used to be an adventurer as well.

## Quests and Rumours

### [[Session 23]]
- Has instructed [[Tilly]] to remove any dissenters from [[Caelia]] to prevent any interference with the ritual to summon [[The Abberant]]
- Has killed the only other Kalashtar he knew because of a difference of opinion regarding his plans in [[Caelia]]
- Has threatened The Reconstellators with interference with their plans if they don't leave [[Caelia]] without interfering with the ritual
- It is unclear if he knows the ritual will summon [[The Abberant]], and intends to go through with it without proof

### [[Session 24]]
- He told [[Tilly]] that the only person who would be hurt by the ritual would be him, and does truly believe that the ritual is only meant to let [[The Abberant]] speak through him, not summon him or take over his body. He could not be convinced to ignore the mission given to him by (what he believes is) his other half
- The Reconstellators successfully manage to place a badge on him, which causes all of the attuned badges to glow intensely. His hair begins to fall out of his head, body contorting unnaturally, fingers snapping and eyes turning pitch black
- After having *Remove Curse* cast on him by [[The Collector]], the effects stopped and he dropped to the ground, dying. [[Tilly]] casted *Spare the Dying* on him and [[Zrandovorski]] stored him in the [[Bag of Holding]] to be further questioned later

### [[Session 25]]
- Lost his 'chosen' status after the ritual failed
- Revealed that his adventuring party wanted him to not perform the ritual after discovering his life would be lost
- Gets a tongue-lashing from [[NG]] for putting his people at risk and tells him he should've died
- [[Tilly]] tells him that she doesn't regret saving his life, and now he needs to really lead his people and ensure their safety while staying alive
- Leads the survivors of [[Caelia]] (305) to [[The Hearth of Hope]] to save them from [[The Frozen March]] and starts going by [[The Soul Sage|Vicci]]
