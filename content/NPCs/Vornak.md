#  [[Vornak]]

## Description

A Fire Giant scholar who presides over the hybrid church-and-library devoted to [[Annam All-Father]] on the outskirts of [[Landon's Lamp]]. He's 15 feet tall with red long braids and blue skin, armed with a massive sword as tall as [[Tilly]].

Despite his stature and oversized weaponry, he earns the title of "gentle giant" as long as you don't threaten him or the library. [[Vornak]] is charitable to peaceful travellers, and willingly offered The Reconstellators access to the library after only speaking with them for a few minutes.

## Relationships

- Worships [[Annam All-Father]], as most of Giantkind does.
- "Is aware of a Frost Giant named [[Skane]]" who supposedly visits him sometimes. He did not elaborate on the exact details of their relationship.
