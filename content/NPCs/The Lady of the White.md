#  [[The Lady of the White]]

## Description

The lady of a white is a ~~witch~~ hag residing in a small cabin in [[The Frozen March]]. It seems like her cabin moves around the area using some sort of magic. 

## Quests and Rumours

- [[Find Dead Bodies for The Lady of the White]]
	- She did not accept the [[Session 11#Killing a Homeless Drug Addict|viscera left by the Lorizadune follower]] or [[Session 12#The Oblex|the Oblex corpse]], but did give 1 platinum with a marking on it for the corpse of [[Session 15#"Good Enough"|the Catoblepas]], and a 19 platinum and 1 soul coin for [[Session 15#Nothing Good Happens After 2am|the corpse of a human]].
	- She accepted but was troubled upon receiving [[Session 22#Our Reward is Getting The Fuck Out Of There|the corpse of a Cult of The Stars follower]], claiming that "saying this is a 'human' is a pretty strong choice of words...", and only offered 25 silver as she couldn't use the corpse for her *normal purposes*.
