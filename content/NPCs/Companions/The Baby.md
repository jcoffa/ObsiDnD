# [[The Baby]]

- **Aliases:** The Baby, Null, *start of spitting a lugie noise* (snow in Goblin), Null *start of spitting a lugie noise*
- **Parents:** Unknown
- **Species:** Half-Elf
- **Type:** Mundane
- **Source:** N/A
- **Currently Carried By:** The Kenku at [[The Everburning Chalice]]
- **Requires Attunement?** Unknown

---

A Half-Elven infant discovered in [[Session 17]] held by a cultist familiar with [[The Collector]]. The Baby is (was?) seemingly part of some kind of ritual that was claimed to be completed. It was used as a lure to get the Reconstellator's attention, one that worked.

It appears to have some sort of connection with [[Larizadune]], allowing [[Session 20#New Main Character Discovered|The Collector to speak directly with him]], and The Reconstellators decide to keep the baby in case it has more crazy powers.

Deciding that adventuring around a Frozen wasteland with a literal baby is not the best idea, they left The Baby in [[Draskhrr]] under the care of Teresa's right hand man, a kenku who had recently lost his own child (in [[Session 22]]).
