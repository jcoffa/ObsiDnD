#  [[Kaz the Cold]]

## Description

Somehow through all the talks around this guy I don't think we ever got a description.

## Relationships

The current leader and Count of [[Draskhrr]], he works closely with his right hand man, [[Rykor Steelhand]], who runs [[The Iron Fang]] military force out of the city. While he seems to be rolling with [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]]'s plan to liberate the goblins from [[The Frozen March]] by attacking the main gate, his personal motivations and reasons for such a violent plan remain unclear.
