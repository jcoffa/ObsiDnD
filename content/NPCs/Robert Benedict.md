#  [[Robert Benedict]]

## Description

A tired, lanky, bald, middle-aged man with a frayed curly moustache. Inherited his late father's bar, *Benedict's*, in [[Fair Helm City]] about 10 years ago after a last-minute change to the will. Before he was suddenly tied down with business responsibilities, he dreamed of becoming an adventurer and exploring the world.

## Relationships

- Son to Benedict Benedict (Benedict Sr.)
- The younger twin brother to [[Ben Benedict]] by 6 seconds. They have a somewhat strained relationship due to how his brother "stole his dream" of becoming an adventurer, while he himself is duty-bound to maintain his late father's bar (a life that was long planned for [[Ben Benedict|his brother]] instead).

## Quests and Rumours

- Asked the party to get revenge on [[Ben Benedict|his brother]] by [[Sink Benedict's Ship|sinking his ship; The SS Benedict]].
