#  [[Charisma]]

## Description

[[Charisma]] is a niche artist who lives near [[The Scar of the Plane]] and is one of the many tieflings who resides there. She is one of the closest things to tiefling nobility that exists, but would be considered more of a diplomat than some kind of Queen. The art she produces is more of a side-hustle that she does.

## Relationships

A diplomat for [[The Scar of the Plane]].
