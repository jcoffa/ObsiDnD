#  [[Volo]]

## Description

A cheerful and go-with-the-flow man dressed in flamboyant bard clothing. He often finds himself wrapped up in strange magic and unexplainable events, but always manages to come out of his trials completely unscathed.

## Relationships

Friends and academic partners with [[Luca]], the Sphinx of Wonder, who is notably *not* his familiar.
