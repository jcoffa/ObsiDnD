#  [[Kolvar]]

- **Played By:** Sage
- **Class:** Warlock
- **Species:** Elf
- **Sessions Active:** [[Session 15]] - present

Kolvar is an Elven man with shoulder-length white hair, a trench coat and a vague, mist-like energy surrounding him. The party met him in the mountains near [[The Hearth of Hope]] while walking through a blizzard, though Kolvar was seemingly unaffected by the cold.
