#  [[Kolvar]]

- **Played By:** Sage
- **Class:** Warlock
- **Species:** Changeling (introduced as an Elf)
- **Sessions Active:** [[Session 15]] - present

[[Kolvar]] was previously thought to be an Elven man, but was revealed to be a Changeling who happened to look like an Elf when The Reconstellators met them originally. He has shoulder-length white hair, a trench coat and a vague, mist-like energy surrounding him. The party met him in the mountains near [[The Hearth of Hope]] while walking through a blizzard, though [[Kolvar]] was seemingly unaffected by the cold.
