#  [[Kolvar]]

- **Played By:** Sage
- **Class:** Warlock
- **Species:** Changeling (introduced as an Elf)
- **Sessions Active:** [[Session 15]] - present
- **Age:** Unknown

#### Appearance
[[Kolvar]] is a Changeling who spends most of his time around the party as an elven man. He has shoulder-length white hair, a trench coat and a vague, mist-like energy surrounding him. That energy comes together to form his Pact Blade during battle.

#### Backstory
During an encounter with a creature in [[Session 16#Now Arriving The Tower That Makes You Loredump|The Tower That Makes You Loredump]] (it's actually the creature in the tower that makes you loredump), [[Kolvar]] was shown [[Session 16#Kolvar 's Trance|a vision of his past]]. Through this, the party discovered that he was a Changeling - not only that, but that part of his warlock pact was to turn him into one. He can't remember what he used to be or what his previous life was. He gave everything up to be everyone, and no one. 

The entity he made a pact with was [[Unseelie Queen|some kind of fae]]. In that vision of the past, he wasn't able to look directly at it, but it was described as beautiful, in a weird, gross sort of way. [[Kolvar]] seemed pretty obsessed with it. What a weirdo...

#### Trivia
- The party met him in the mountains near [[The Hearth of Hope]] while walking through a blizzard, though [[Kolvar]] was seemingly unaffected by the cold.
- [[Kolvar]] is just the name that comes with this form. He doesn't have a name - anymore.
- Since he was in his elf form when the party met him, he normally sticks to it to give them consistency. People tend to trust you more when they remember your face :)
- He is definitely not a spy, please stop asking ^\_^
- [[Kolvar]] loves studying people and learning more about them. There is no wrong way to be. It's all interesting to him.
	- And so it follows that, naturally, he also loves becoming those people afterwards.
- When [[Kolvar]] shapeshifts, it's a really gross mess of bones and flesh contorting into a new form. Personally, he finds this process quite elegant. What a weirdo... (x2)
