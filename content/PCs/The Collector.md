### The Museum Plans 

- Tilly's description must have the quote she provided me on our first interaction *"I breathe the Earth, and the Earth breaths with me"* - An amazing quote from an already great and powerful druid.
- Garrison is a fantastic marvel! I am sure his statue will hold great value for all attendees, as well as his ideals. Garrison mentioned his *need* to protect, his *urge* to, that's going to draw in some good numbers. He even values the monsters, sparing the life of that Toppleganger, for a being that may not have a heart like yours or mine, Garrison's heart holds room for all he comes across that walk the path of good.

I am excited to learn more about where he comes from, perhaps we have a piece of shared history.
