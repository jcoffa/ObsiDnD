#  [[Shield of Pursuit]]

- **Type:** [Shield](https://5e.tools/items.html#shield_xphb)
- **Source:** N/A
- **Currently Carried By:** ??? ([[Garrison]]?)
- **Requires Attunement?** No

**Grants a +1 bonus to AC** (instead of the normal +2 of a shield). When an ally within 5 feet of you is hit by a ranged attack and you can see the attacker, you can use your **Reaction** to teleport up to 60 feet and appear 5 feet next to the attacker. If the attacker is beyond 60 feet, then you cannot use this ability.
