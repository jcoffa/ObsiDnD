#  [[Rogueish Blade of Bleed]]

- **Type:** [Dagger](https://5e.tools/items.html#dagger_xphb)
- **Source:** N/A
- **Currently Carried By:** ??? (formerly [[Blake]], but they're dead now)
- **Requires Attunement?** Yes?

---

This magic dagger allows you to use your health as a resource and deal 3d6 extra damage on a [melee sneak attack](https://5e.tools/classes.html#rogue_xphb,state:feature=s0-1) to both you and the target enemy. After using this attack, you cannot heal by any means except via a [Wish](https://5e.tools/spells.html#wish_xphb) spell for the next minute.
