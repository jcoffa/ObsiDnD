#  [[Talisman of the Sphere]]

- **Type:** Legendary Artifact
- **Source:** N/A
- **Currently Carried By:** [[Zrandovorski]]
- **Requires Attunement?** Yes

---

A small chain necklace with a massive smooth black orb on the end. The orb is somewhat... *unwieldy*.

While holding or wearing this on your person, you have advantage on any Intelligence (Arcana) check you make to control a *[Sphere of Annihilation](https://5e.tools/items.html#sphere%20of%20annihilation_xdmg)*. In addition, when you start your turn in control of the *Sphere of Annihilation* you can take a Magic Action to move it 10 feet plus a number of feet equal to 5 times your Intelligence modifier. This movement doesn’t need to be in a straight line When this artifact touches a creature not of this plane, it takes 2d10 force damage. Notably, this talisman does **NOT** come with a *Sphere of Annihilation*.

Additionally, the talisman has the following properties:

- It can be used as a Holy Symbol.
- It's part of an unknown ritual.
- You may cast *[Fly](https://5e.tools/spells.html#fly_xphb)* once per 7 days.
