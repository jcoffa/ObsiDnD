#  [[Circlet of Control]]

- **Type:** Item
- **Source:** Found in the office in [[The Factory]]
- **Currently Carried By:** [[Bag of Holding]]
- **Requires Attunement?** Yes

---

 A metal circular band with a green gem sitting at its center, and silver flourishes along the sides, large enough to be worn like a crown.

While attuned to this circlet, the wearer can use a magic action to attempt to dominate a robot, automaton or warforged. They must make a DC 15 intelligence save or be dominated by the wearer. They may make a new save once each dawn. There is an additional magic item that could amplify the effectiveness of the circlet.
