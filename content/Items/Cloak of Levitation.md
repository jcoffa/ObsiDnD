#  [[Cloak of Levitation]]

- **Type:** Cloak
- **Source:** [DMG 239](https://5e.tools/items.html#boots%20of%20levitation_xdmg)
- **Currently Carried By:** [[NG]]
- **Requires Attunement?** No

---

While a creature wears this cloak, it can cast Levitate on itself. This can only be performed once every Long Rest regardless of if the cloak changes possession.
