#  [[Cloak of the Crowd]]

- **Type:** Cloak
- **Source:** N/A
- **Currently Carried By:** [[Bag of Holding]]
- **Requires Attunement?** No

---

While wearing this cloak you seem to blend into any crowd and become one of many. While there are at least 5 visible non hostile humanoids within 10 feet of you, you may use a **bonus action** to become invisible for 1 turn (once per long rest).

You also receive advantage to stealth and slight of hand rolls while in crowded areas.
