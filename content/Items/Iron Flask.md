#  [[Iron Flask]]

- **Type:** Wondrous Item
- **Source:** [DMG 274](https://5e.tools/items.html#iron%20flask_xdmg)
- **Currently Carried By:** [[Bag of Holding]]
- **Requires Attunement?** No

---

A brass-stoppered iron flask with some glowing eyes seen behind the metal. It contains a creature, but it's not known which one it is.

This specific flask is different than the normal one described in [the 2024 Dungeon Master's Guide](https://5e.tools/items.html#iron%20flask_xdmg). This flask does *NOT* have the feature where the creature will obey the party's commands for 1 hour. Instead, it acts as if the spell [*Crown of Madness*](https://5e.tools/spells.html#crown%20of%20madness_xphb) was affecting it, essentially acting in a blood rage.
