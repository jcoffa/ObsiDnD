#  [[Polaris]]

- **Type:** Airship
- **Source:** N/A
- **Currently Carried By:** N/A
- **Requires Attunement?** No
- **Travel Speed:** 3 hexes per day
- **Fuel Remaining:** 36 hexes (12 days of travel)

---

The airship created by [[NG]] and [[Zrandovorski]] after stealing [[Sink Benedict's Ship|The SS Benedict]] from [[Ben Benedict|Captain Benedict]] before [[Session 26#Lights, Bombs, Action|The Reconstellators made their great escape from Fair Helm City]]. It features fully OSHA-compliant railings and ramps, a covered helm, the ability to travel by both sea and air, and 2 unused Bastion Rooms of size *Cramped*.

Its fuel source is unknown, but it's likely some kind of magical reagent that must be synthesized as both coal and  cobalt did not work.
