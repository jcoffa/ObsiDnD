- **Fuel Remaining:** 49 hexes

The airship created by [[NG]] and [[Zrandovorski]] after stealing [[Sink Benedict's Ship|The SS Benedict]] from [[Ben Benedict|Captain Benedict]] before [[Session 26#Lights, Bombs, Action|The Reconstellators made their great escape from Fair Helm City]]. It features fully OSHA-compliant railings and ramps, a covered helm, the ability to travel by both sea and air, and 2 unused Bastion Rooms of size *Cramped*.

Its fuel source is unknown, but it's likely some kind of magical reagent that must be synthesized as both coal and  cobalt did not work.

## Bastion

The [[Polaris]] acts as a mobile Bastion for The Reconstellators. It started with 2 *Cramped* sized rooms and available space for a third, which The Reconstellators paid a shipwright in [[Kota-Ken]] to [[Session 36#Bastioning|expand the ship and turn it into a proper Bastion]], ending up with 3 *Roomy* sized rooms and a few facilities, mentioned below.

### Library

[DMG 342](https://5e.scrunko.monster/bastions.html#library_xdmg)

The Library allows The Reconstellators to issue a **Research** order to the hireling and commission them to research a topic. The topic can be any of the following, at the party's choosing when the Research order is given:

- Legend
- Known event or location
- Person of significance
- Type of creature
- Famous object

After 7 days, the hireling can provide *up to* 3 accurate pieces of information that the party doesn't already know about the topic.

### Storehouse

[DMG 347](https://5e.scrunko.monster/bastions.html#storehouse_xdmg)

The Storehouse allows The Reconstellators to issue a **Trade** order to the hireling and task them with either acquiring or selling [Trade Goods](https://5e.scrunko.monster/book.html#xdmg,6,trade%20goods). What task the hireling performs depends on the current state of the Storehouse when the order is issued.

- If the Storehouse is empty when the Trade order is issued, the hireling spends 7 days procuring non-magical items worth 500 GP (or less) and stores them in the Storehouse to fill it. The party bears the cost of any purchases made.
- If the Storehouse is full when the Trade order is issued, the hireling spends 7 days to sell the goods from the Storehouse instead and leaves it empty again. The buyer pays an additional 10% than the standard price (e.g., if 500 GP is sold then the party receives 550 GP).

The Storehouse improves as the party reaches higher levels, as outlined in the table below.

| Level | Maximum Storage | Extra Buyer Fee | Total Value On Full Sell |   Profit |
| ----: | --------------: | --------------: | -----------------------: | -------: |
|     5 |          500 GP |             10% |                   550 GP |    50 GP |
|     9 |        2,000 GP |             20% |                 2,400 GP |   400 GP |
|    13 |        5,000 GP |             50% |                 7,500 GP | 2,500 GP |
|    17 |        5,000 GP |            100% |                10,000 GP | 5,000 GP |

#### Tracking

- **Current value of Storehouse (0 GP if empty):** 500 GP
- **Current task (emptying or filling):** Emptying
- **Date started on current task (takes 7 days):** Excelcium 134 - Growth's Dawn 14
