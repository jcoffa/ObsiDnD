#  [[Orb of Golden Heat]]

- **Type:** Orb
- **Source:** N/A
- **Currently Carried By:** [[Zrandovorski]]
- **Requires Attunement?** Yes

---

A perfectly spherical, smooth, golden orb. It's comfortably warm to the touch, and calms one's nerves after holding it for a minute or two.

- When concentrating on the orb, you can feel its warm healing energy. Once per **Long Rest**, you can add +1 to all hit dice rolls for one creature when taking a **Short Rest**.
- The orb has 3 charges, regaining 1d3 charges on a **Long Rest**. While concentrating on the orb, you can cast either of the following spells as a [Ritual](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#ritual_xphb):
	- [Healing Word](https://5e.tools/spells.html#healing%20word_xphb) (costs 1 charge)
	- [Lesser Restoration](https://5e.tools/spells.html#lesser%20restoration_xphb) (costs 2 charges)
