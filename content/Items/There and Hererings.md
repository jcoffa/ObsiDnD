#  [[There and Hererings]]

- **Type:** Earrings
- **Source:** N/A
- **Currently Carried By:** [[Garrison]] and [[Kolvar]]
- **Requires Attunement?** Yes, by two different creatures (one earring each)

---

When two creatures each attune to one of the earrings in this pair, they can choose to swap places as long as they are no further than 120 feet from each other. The character who initiates it uses their **Bonus Action** and speaks the command phrase, and the other creature hears the command in their ear (as if it was playing from their earring). As a **Reaction**, the other creature can say the response phrase to swap places with the initiator.

- **Command to initiate:** *"You are there!"*
- **Command to respond:** *"Now I'm here!"*
