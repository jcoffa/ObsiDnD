#  [[Helm]]

- **Type:** Wondrous Item
- **Source:** ??
- **Currently Carried By:** Nobody (the party used it to convert *The SS Benedict* into an airship in [[Session 26#Lights, Bombs, Action|Session 26]])
- **Requires Attunement?** No

---

While wearing an airship mask and using the [[Helm]] as a spellcasting focus, you gain access to the following spells:

1. **Create Airship**, a level 3 spell. Material components (all consumed on cast): an electrum sculpture worth 750 EP, a [[Helm]]
2. **Create SpellJammer**, a level 5 spell. Material components (all consumed on cast): an electrum sculpture worth 10,000 EP, a [[Helm]]
