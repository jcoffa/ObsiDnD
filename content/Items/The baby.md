# The baby

- **Type:** *Mundane*
- **Source:** Unknown
- **Currently Carried By:** [[The Collector|Linus Snabberknackle]]
- **Requires Attunement?** Unknown
---

A Half-Elven infant discovered in [[Session 17]] held by a cultist familiar with The Collector. The Baby, is an infant seemingly a part of some kind of ritual that was claimed to be completed. It was used as a lure to get the Reconstellator's attention, one that worked.
