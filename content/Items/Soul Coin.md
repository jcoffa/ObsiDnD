#  [[Soul Coin]]

- **Type:** Wondrous Item
- **Source:** N/A
- **Currently Carried By:** [[Tilly]]
- **Requires Attunement?** No
- **Quantity:** 2

---

A black coin slightly larger than legal tender that glows red slightly. It has the markings of a star on both sides.

A currency used by the infernal and the damned, valued at an exchange rate of 1 soul in the blood trade. Known to grant innate magical properties when used in the forging of weapons and armour.
