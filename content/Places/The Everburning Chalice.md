# [[The Everburning Chalice]]

The de-facto tavern in [[Draskhrr]], run by the bartender Theresa and the head chef Arvind. The tavern gets its name from a large brazier in the center of the establishment which has never been extinguished (it may even be lit via [Continual Flame](https://5e.tools/spells.html#continual%20flame_xphb)).

The establishment is always at least a little busy thanks to the tendency for Goblinoids to prefer drinking to working even if they have real work to be doing. This makes the tavern a popular gathering spot for the entire city every night regardless of whether there's any special reason or event that would make it particularly busy.

### Offerings

- **A night's stay** in one of its three single-bedded rooms, one double-bedded premium room, or 8-cot hostel area. Thanks to the typical lack of visitors to [[Draskhrr]] or [[The Frozen March]] in general, the prices for its rooms are quite affordable and subsidized by the establishment's impressive alcohol and food sale figures.
- **Snow Rat Shit**; a popular alcoholic beverage among the Goblinoids thanks to its pungent odor and bitter taste. It's a type of mulled amber ale served steaming hot with small chunks of frozen fermented fruit that tend to settle to the bottom of the tankard and slowly thaw thanks to the ale's warmth.
	- It does not actually contain any snow rats (or their droppings), and is merely named in this way due to the snow rat's diet of fruit that it scavenges deep within [[The Frozen March]]'s permafrost, which they're unable to fully digest. This leaves large chunks of half-digested fruit in its droppings which then freeze over instantly due to the cold of their environment. Goblinoids find this naming convention and its reference to be highly endearing and not at all weird or gross. Other entrants to [[The Frozen March]] tend to hold a different opinion.
- **Buried Booze**; a popular alcoholic beverage among the Goblinoids thanks to its strong alcohol content and viscous texture. Its crisp and clear appearance allows the drinker to see through the liquid as if it were clean water.
	- The drink gets its name from the thick white foam that settles to the bottom of the tankard, which supposedly looks like the back of a snow rat buried under [[The Frozen March]]'s crystal-clear permafrost layer.
- **Globon Chill**; a highly unpopular alcoholic beverage among the Goblinoids thanks to its sweet flavour and relatively light alcohol content of only 5%; considered barely enough to avoid being classified as a soft-drink by Goblinoid standards. It's a mixed cocktail based on malt-liquor that's flavoured with citrus and a lot of sugar (both of which must be imported, rendering this drink an expensive luxury by the economic standards of [[The Frozen March]]).
	- Despite being on the complete opposite end of a typical Goblinoid's palate, the drink is still on offer due to its overwhelming popularity with the other entrants of [[The Frozen March]] who typically find the Goblinoid-centric beverages to be nigh unsafe for consumption.
