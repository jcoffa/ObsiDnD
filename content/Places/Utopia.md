# [[Utopia]]

The capital city of the [[Utopian Empire]] which controls much of [[Teradin]]. Reputed as one of the most prosperous places to live in the realm, rumours circulate that the reality inside its walls is more grim than its ruling class would have the outside world believe.

[[Utopia]] is founded on the ancestral homeland of an old Hobgoblin society who were cast out to [[The Frozen March]] after they lost The Clerical War.

Citizens of [[Utopia]] are called [[Utopia|Utopian]]s.
