# [[Fair Helm City]]

A [[Utopia]]-controlled city, in partnership with the [[Fair Helm Trading Company]], on the Northern coast of [[The Frozen March]]. While it's by no means defensible enough to act as a standalone military fort, it *is* equipped with a standing military to defend itself from bandits, and anti-air weaponry to defend itself from [[Find The White Dragon's Nest|White Dragons]].

It primarily acts as a trading hub to import goods from [[Utopia]] and export goods from [[The Frozen March]] back to [[Utopia]] via ship. It's one of the few locations where anything *leaves* of [[The Frozen March]].
