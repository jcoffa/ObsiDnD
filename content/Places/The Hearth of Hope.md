# [[The Hearth of Hope]]

One of the two major civilizations originating in [[The Frozen March]]; the other being [[Draskhrr]]. Created by [[Toran the Red]] as a place to unify the people of [[The Frozen March]] who mostly *aren't* terribly concerned with trying to leave their frigid prison.

Its buildings are surprisingly high quality in their construction; the most notable are the town hall (which is simply the biggest log cabin in town) and an inn called "[[The Mana Drop]]". It also has a special magical portal that [[Toran the Red|Toran]] can use to bring her people to and from places across [[The Frozen March]] with some limitations: the portal at [[The Hearth of Hope]] is always there, but the other side moves around [[The Frozen March|The March]] randomly, and remains in place for a day before moving.

After The Reconstellators freed [[Draskhrr]] from the control of [[The Iron Fang]], [[Toran the Red|Toran]] left [[The Hearth of Hope|The Hearth]] to rule there until a method of escape from [[The Frozen March]] was discovered, leaving one of her trusted lieutenants in charge of the city.
