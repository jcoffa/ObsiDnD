# [[The Hearth of Hope]]

One of the two major civilizations originating in [[The Frozen March]]; the other being [[Draskhrr]]. Created by [[Toran the Red]] as a place to unify the people of [[The Frozen March]] who mostly *aren't* terribly concerned with trying to leave their frigid prison.

Its buildings are surprisingly high quality in their construction; the most notable are the town hall (which is simply the biggest log cabin in town) and an inn called "[[The Mana Drop]]".
