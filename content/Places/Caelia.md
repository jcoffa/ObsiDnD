# [[Caelia]]

An underground city located to the west in [[The Frozen March]]. It is the home of [[The Cult of the Stars]] and appears to be run by [[The Soul Sage]]. It is a large underground shopping district housing at least 400 people of varying species, the most variety seen in [[The Frozen March]] so far. It is likely the oldest settlement in [[The Frozen March]], existing before [[The Frozen March|The March]] was even frozen.

It appears the city itself has had a long-term goal of completing a ritual to contact gods from [[The Far Realm]], with the plan far outlasting any current living residents. The Reconstellators learned from [[Session 22#Rule 34 Is Being Written Before Our Very Eyes| a vision Tilly had]] that they are trying to reach [[The Abberant]]. They also learned from [[Session 23#The God of Good? This Guy?| a goblin named Gobbo]] that the ritual will not be to commune with [[The Abberant]] but bring him back to life.
