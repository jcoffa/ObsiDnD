# [[The Scar of the Plane]]

An enormous chasm in the southeastern desert of [[Teradin]]. Not much is known about this place, but the myths say that a forgotten God tore the plane asunder with his blade; this is the spot where his weapon made impact. Now strange and dangerous wild magics course from within, constantly spewing it out into the surrounding area.

The Reconstellators currently know of a few people living near or coming from [[The Scar of the Plane]]. The artist, [[Charisma]], and [[Session 16#Tilly 's Trance, Again|another possible Kalashtar]].
