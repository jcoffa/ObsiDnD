# [[The Underdark]]

A complex network of pitch-black caves that spans most of the subterranean layer in The Material Plane. The Reconstellators have travelled to [[The Underdark]] beneath [[The Frozen March]] a few times and have heard of some locations:
- [[Trefawn]], a not friendly Drow city;
- [[Waddlewood Cove]], a halfling city that fell from the surface;
- [[Azhvan]], a recently ravaged city housing [[The Scholars of the Deep]] that has a portal leading to [[The Abyss]];
- [[Devawn's Blade]], a massive tavern for adventurers where a bi-yearly 'Get Hunted by Mind Flayers' event takes place;
- [[Investigate Underdraskhrr|Underdraskhrr]] (fake name), a small town in the upper levels of [[The Underdark]] that recently had an artifact stolen from them by [[The Cult of the Stars]] (maybe);

They also know of a few entrances into [[The Underdark]]:
- [[Session 7#Aftermath|A ladder]] between [[Draskhrr]] and the [[Adamantine Mine]] shown to them by the flumph, Disco;
- [[Session 12#Returning to the Surface| An unblocked hole with a makeshift ladder]] in the [[Adamantine Mine]], [[Session 13#Party 'Til You're Pregnant|which they reblocked before leaving]] to prevent [[The Iron Fang]] from entering [[The Underdark]];
