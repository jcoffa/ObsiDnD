# [[Adamantine Mine]]

Adamantine is a rare metal famed for its hardness and often used to make weapons and armour. There are seldom few known locations with the presence of adamantine ore, and their mining rights (or even the knowledge of the locations themselves) are guarded jealously by whoever controls them.

Adamantine is a deep blue/black metal that twinkles like the night sky when light is shone on its polished  surface.
