# [[Adamantine Mine]]

Adamantine is a rare metal famed for its hardness and often used to make weapons and armour. There are seldom few known locations with the presence of adamantine ore, and their mining rights (or even the knowledge of the locations themselves) are guarded jealously by whoever controls them.

Adamantine is a deep blue/black metal that twinkles like the night sky when light is shone on its polished surface.

The [[Adamantine Mine]] is under the control of [[The Iron Fang]], with a mining operation there run by a goblin named Tumor and a single Warforged. The operations had been interrupted by an Oblex deep in the mines, which was [[Session 12#The Oblex|killed by The Reconstellators]].

[[The Toppelgangers]] seem to have some interest in the [[Adamantine Mine]], but only asked questions to the goblins capturerd by the Oblex and left without taking anything of note.

The operating status and mining rights to the mines are unclear since [[The Iron Fang]] leadership was overthrown (we never notified them about the change in leadership).
