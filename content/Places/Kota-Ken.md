# [[Kota-Ken]]

Located on the southern tip of the Southern Island, [[Kota-Ken]] is a large up-and-coming city built on Gnomish industry. The city is relatively flat, made of mainly 1-story buildings with the exception of a few large metal spires that are so tall they scrape the clouds. They are the home of [[The Golden Gauntlet]] and have the most well-known entrance into [[The Underdark]]. 

It is the fastest growing city in [[Teradin]]. Though [[Weavewind]] has been around longer and is technically larger, [[Kota-Ken]] has popped up in the last 40 years from seemingly nothing. It was a tribal village before, and has now become a massive Gnomish hub. Many people come in and out of the city, with both normal and flying boats seen at its port.

[[The Collector]] and his father have visited the city once before ([[Session 31#Back To The Future Part 2 (The One Where They Go To The Future)|Session 31]]), but it was merely a tribal village at the time.
