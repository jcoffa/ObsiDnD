# [[The Observatory]]

A somewhat run-down observatory in the southern mountains along the border between [[The Frozen March]] and the [[Utopia|Utopian Empire]]. It's an enormous structure with multiple floors, its own dedicated sewage system, a library, multiple storage rooms, and likely much more that the party has yet to know about. Currently inhabited by a lich of unknown name and origin, who has seemingly gotten [[The Observatory]] operating again; a somewhat recent development as the structure was abandoned for decades.

[[Zrandovorski]] has visited this place multiple times. He was one of a few explorers who has picked clean the lower (and less heavily guarded) levels of the basements for all of its noteworthy resources (such as low-level spell scrolls and components).

[[Session 4#Dungeon Delved|Zrandovorski learned from Volo and Luca]] that [[The Cult of the Stars]] currently inhabits [[The Observatory]].

[[Session 13#The Obsidian Dragon|The Reconstellators learned from an Obsidian Dragon]] that [[The Observatory]] contains one of the most stable ways to leave [[The Frozen March]]. If they can get the key from the lich inhabiting it, they could come and go freely.

[[Session 14#Do Wizards Dream of Magic Sheep?|Zrandovorski had a dream]] where he returned to [[The Observatory]] and he witnessed a person there; 5'10" - 5'11" man, clean-shaven and well kempt, with glassy grey eyes who seemed to recognize [[Zrandovorski]] was returning to [[The Observatory]], even in a dream.

[[Session 15#++ Kolvar|The Reconstellators were told by Toran the Red]] that there are rumors of clerics residing in [[The Observatory]] with unknown loyalties, but strong enough magic to bring people back from the dead.

[[Session 21#Talking Heads|The Reconstellators attempted asking the dead Marquis of Draskhrr]] what he knew about [[The Observatory]], but all he could add was that the wizard who resides there was old beyond normal age (aka a lich) and knows more booyagh than anyone in the old Hobgolbin Empire.

[[Session 25#Wait, Where's The Main Quest Marker Again?|According to The Soul Sage]], [[The Cult of the Stars]] has no direct connection with [[The Observatory]], or any lich residing within. He suggests that if he was someone looking to control the location, a rumour of him being a lich would be very advantageous to keep people away.
