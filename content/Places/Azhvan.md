# [[Azhvan]]

A city in [[The Underdark]] acting as the capital for [[The Scholars of the Deep]]; a group of investigators and scientists who are committed to learning things about the world regardless of any personal danger, morality, or usefulness. This city was originally a research outpost for [[The Scholars of the Deep]], but over time it evolved into a fully-fledged city.

It was partially ruined by an enormous stone dragon-scorpion-like creature (which the locals called [[Azhvan's Collapse]]) who now slumbers in the middle of its destruction. After the creature left, a golden portal was discovered underneath the city where it escaped from. The portal appears to lead to [[The Abyss]]. 10 of its eggs were left behind in its wake.
