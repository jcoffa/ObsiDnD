# [[The Mana Drop]]

The only tavern in [[The Hearth of Hope]]. Built like a log cabin both inside and out, the cozy and warm interior is a welcoming experience to escape the -40 degree average temperature of [[The Frozen March]]. It contains exactly 4 tables; one adventurer-approved corner booth in each corner of its square floorplan.

They are known for their exceptional breakfast menu. The pink eggs are an iconic morning staple.
