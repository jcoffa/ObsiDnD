# [[The Factory]]

An old factory located in the center of the Southern Island that [[NG]] used to work. It is currently overrun by mycelium that have left it and the surrounding area uninhabitable, and includes [[Copper Rot]] to prevent any mechanical inventions from entering.

[[Garrison]]'s creator claims that [[The Factory]]'s true purpose was to create weapons to resist a creature coming to start [[The Great Reset]]. There were once many factories dedicated to this task, but this factory was the last one left.
