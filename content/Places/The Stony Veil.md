# [[The Stony Veil]]

A specific mountain within the southwestern range of [[The Frozen March]]. It's reputed for its immense store of precious gemstones, and its monsters (the Xorn) that love to eat gems and the adventurers who have come to claim the mountain's riches for themselves.
