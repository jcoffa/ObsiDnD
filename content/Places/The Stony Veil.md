# [[The Stony Veil]]

A specific mountain within the southwestern range of [[The Frozen March]]. It's reputed for its immense store of precious gemstones, and its monsters (the Xorn) that love to eat gems and the adventurers who have come to claim the mountain's riches for themselves.

[[Session 21#The Cave of Head-Crushed-By-A-Boulder|The Reconstellators briefly visited the Veil]] to acquire some precious gemstones, but were quickly chased out after encountering the Xorn. They managed to [[Session 22#Our Reward is Getting The Fuck Out Of There|steal some gems from a Cult of the Stars worshipper]] who was already in the Veil when they arrived.
