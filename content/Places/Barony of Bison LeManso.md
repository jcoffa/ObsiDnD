# [[Barony of Bison LeManso]]

A city in the [[LeManso Desert]], east of [[The Scar of the Plane]], ruled by an unknown baron. They are known to "harvest" or "farm" the chaotic magic fumes that leak out of [[The Scar of the Plane|The Scar]], but what exactly that means is not something The Reconstellators know.

Through various observations from outside the city, and some recon via [[Son]]'s imperfect descriptions, The Reconstellators have a patchwork understanding of the kinds of things that go on in the city:

- There are massive floating stones around [[The Scar of the Plane|The Scar]] that appear to lose their ability to float if they get too far away. The city appears to be fully encapsulated within the "safe" range that keeps the stones floating.
	- These stones have been seen to spontaneously crack and violently split apart for seemingly no reason.
- During one night, the [[The Scar of the Plane|The Scar]] was glowing a bright red, with fog coming out of it. The red smoke and fog twists upwards like a vortex and then twists downwards into the city.
- There was a large central tower with a red gem at the top of it. The red fog from [[The Scar of the Plane|The Scar]] may have twisted downwards into this gem, but [[Son]] wasn't sure.
- There is a large population of indentured servants who appear to mostly act as farmers.
- The guards beat [[Mike Spon]]'s ass for "spilling the beans". When they got to the barony, they threw him onto a farm for him to work.
- There were many farmers working the land. They *ALL* looked very thin and malnourished. The plants that they were farming looked like really big dandelions with actual lion faces on them.
- There were 4 towers that were wiry, like not fully solid. There was a huge spike in the middle of each tower that would rise and then plummet into the ground (like a pump?)
- Many of the citizens are up very late at night and nobody is ever seen resting.

The Reconstellators highly suspect, [[Session 30#Some Sick Sleuthing|based on some seemingly solid evidence]], that the Baron is a vampire and everyone living there are his vampire spawn/thralls. But they don't have any direct confirmation that this is true.
