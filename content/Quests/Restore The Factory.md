#  [[Restore The Factory]]


- **Status:** #pending
- **Given by:** [[Garrison]]'s Creator
- **Location:** [[The Factory]]
- **Reward(s):** Resolving [[NG]]'s trauma and restoring [[The Factory]]'s weapon production

## Details

[[NG]] would like to return to his factory and restore its functionality. After learning he was directly responsible for its destruction, he would really like to fix it. [[Garrison]] would also like to assist his friend on this task, and investigate exactly what may have possessed [[NG]] to destroy it in the first place.

[[Garrison]]'s Creator also told [[Garrison]] that the factory must be restored to resume weapon production so they can prevent a creature from starting [[The Great Reset]]. This creature will arrive in 1 to 2 years.
