#  [[Find Dead Bodies for The Lady of the White]]

- **Status:** #ongoing 
- **Given by:** [[The Lady of the White]]
- **Location:** [[The Frozen March]]
- **Reward(s):** Unknown

## Details

[[The Lady of the White]] gave the party a business card. She promises a reward for every dead body that the party places her business card on top of (which can then be picked back up immediately).

She did not accept the [[Session 11#Killing a Homeless Drug Addict|viscera left by the Lorizadune follower]] or [[Session 12#The Oblex|the Oblex corpse]], but did give 1 platinum with a marking on it for the corpse of [[Session 15#"Good Enough"|the Catoblepas]], and a TBD amount of platinum for [[Session 15#Nothing Good Happens After 2am|the corpse of a human]].
