## Details

An tomb outside control of the [[Draskhrr]] military is said to hold incredible treasure. Too bad everyone who goes in either never returns, or returns disfigured with terrible mutations!

Theresa tells us of [[The Tomb of Booyahg]]; a hot spot for treasure hunters and grave robbers. She says it's outside of [[Draskhrr]] so the military wouldn't care if we visit. It's a half day travel West of [[Draskhrr]]. The first couple floors are mostly picked clean at this point. The tomb has picked up its name after Goblins have gone in and walked out with various mutations; extra arms, permanently being blind or deaf, etc. But there's probably a bunch of great treasure on the lower levels!
