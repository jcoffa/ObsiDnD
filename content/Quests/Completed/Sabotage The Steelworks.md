#  [[Sabotage The Steelworks]]

- **Status:** #completed
	- [[Session 2#The Steeldoesnotworks]]
- **Given by:** Grit Coalbelly, at The Deep Forge in [[Draskhrr]]
- **Location:** The Steelworks, a forge in [[Draskhrr]]
- **Reward(s):** Access to The Deep Forge's equipment, and part ownership of its profits.

## Details

Local Blacksmith, Grit Coalbelly, requests help from "Independant Contractor". Terms of payment to be discussed.

Grit is the self-proclaimed best blacksmith in town for years, but now there's a new smithy named "The Steelworks" who are undercutting his prices and hoarding all of the iron ore accessible to [[Draskhrr]]. [[The Iron Fang]] have entered a contract with the Steelworks. He needs the Steelworks out of the equation, stealthily. Ideally this does not trace back to him, or us. For some reason, Steelworks aren't selling to the public. They are basically making weapons and stockpiling them for no discernible reason, presumably at the request of [[The Iron Fang]].
