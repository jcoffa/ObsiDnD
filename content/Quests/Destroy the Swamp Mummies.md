#  [[Destroy the Swamp Mummies]]

- **Status:** #ongoing 
- **Given by:** [[Rexamillion One-Billion]], through the quest board at [[The Golden Goose]]
- **Location:** The Mummy's Hole located to the west of [[Kota-Ken]]
- **Reward(s):** 1000 GP

## Details

A wealthy merchant, [[Rexamillion One-Billion]], is looking for someone to deal with the swamp mummies that have recently appeared on his trade routes between [[Kota-Ken]] and [[Weavewind]].

His merchants found a large hole that appear to be wear the mummies are spawning from.
