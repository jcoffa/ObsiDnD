#  [[Remove the Undead Dragon from The Deep]]

- **Status:** #pending
- **Given by:** [[Rexamillian One-Billion]] in [[Kota-Ken]]
- **Location:** *The Deep*; the entrance to [[The Underdark]] in [[Kota-Ken]]
- **Reward(s):** 8,500 GP plus a reimbursement for any entrance fees paid to *The Deep*

## Details

There's an undead presence in [[The Underdark]] who are killing adventurers, which is a problem since that's kind of part of the economy of [[Kota-Ken]] due to the 20 GP entry fee to get into *The Deep*. A zombified dragon is down there, and he needs someone competent enough to *remove* it.

He's a zombified black dragon, on the larger side for adult dragons. His breath weapon is acidic, and is reported as being "strange" in some way, but apparently none of the adventurers who made it back alive can describe it adequately. [[Rexamillian One-Billion]] has been told that adventurers hit by the breath weapon feel themselves zomfiying. He can fly in short bursts but lacks the ability to truly free-fly due to the decayed membrane on his wings. There have been rumours of a dragon down there for a few years now, but a problem was only noticeable as of 6 months ago.
