#  [[Find Magic Items for a General Store Owner]]

- **Status:** #pending
- **Given by:** The general store owner in [[Landon's Lamp]]
- **Location:** Anywhere
- **Reward(s):** "A handsome sum"

## Details

After talking with the general store owner as part of [[Inquire About Ancient Lore and Texts]], the owner mentions that some magic items were confiscated by [[Utopia|Utopian]] guards. He would be willing to pay handsomely for "similar" items to replace the ones he lost. His details on that are vague:

- A magic goblet
- A magic flute
- A magic flag

[[Session 29#Garrison Has His Tilly Moment|Later, these magic items were discovered in the guard's barracks]]. Looks like the [[Utopia|Utopian]] guards haven't had time to do anything with them since they were confiscated, maybe because of their heavily reduced numbers in the city.
