#  [[Inquire About Ancient Lore and Texts]]

- **Status:** #pending
- **Given by:** The general store owner in [[Landon's Lamp]] via a job posting on [[The Drooling Dog]]'s quest board
- **Location:** [[Landon's Lamp]]'s general store
- **Reward(s):** "A handsome payment"

## Details

> Looking for ancient lore and texts? I have a lead, I will pay you handsomely - From the general store

Upon investigation, this is actually about the Church of [[Annam All-Father|Annam]] that [[Session 29#Ancient Tomes & Prophetic Doom|the party had encountered previously]]. The old Elven owner of the [[Landon's Lamp]] general store will pay 500 GP for each tome the party gives him from the church.
