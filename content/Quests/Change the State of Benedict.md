#  [[Change the State of Benedict]]

- **Status:** #ongoing 
- **Given by:** Unseelie Shopkeeper outside of [[Fair Helm City]], on behalf of The Unseelie Queen
- **Location:** [[Fair Helm City]]
- **Reward(s):** Entrance into [[Fair Helm City]] (received)

## Details

The Reconstellators made a deal with the Unseelie Shopkeeper to "change the state" of a person named Benedict residing in [[Fair Helm City]].

"Change the state" is left up to interpretation. It could mean making him sad when he is happy, or making him liquid when he is solid. 

As far as we know we're doing this because the Unseelie Queen thinks it would be funny.
