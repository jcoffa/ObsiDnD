### On the Winds and Sea

- The Reconstellators make a plan to travel to the place where they found [[The Baby|Baby Null]] to be. This is on the way to [[The Factory]], so it's not much of a detour.
	- This consumes 16 hexes of fuel from [[Polaris]].
- After a few days of sailing, the weather gets very foggy. [[Tilly]] spies a beam of light around a kilometer away, probably a lighthouse. The [[Polaris]] is in deep waters, so if this is an island it wouldn't be big enough to show up on the map.
- The party sends [[Son]] to investigate. Apparently it's not a lighthouse, but actually a floating wooden door with a large revolving light on top of it. The party put it to a vote, and decide
- The door has a metal frame and doorknob, with the door itself being made of wood. The door is actually hanging from the large revolving light above it through a series of chains.
- [[Son]] opens the door, and it looks to be a bar inside. The floorplan is a circle. There are lots of adventuring species inside and a couple exotic people (including a mindlfayer!) There are 7 other doors that look similar to this one inside the bar.

#### Wood Woads

- [[Tilly]] spies a gaggle of small plant-like creatures carrying wooden weaponry and armour. Her druidic nature urges her to approach the plant-people. They reach for their tiny wooden swords and point them up at her as she gets close.
- She talks to them in Common, and they don't seem to understand her. She explains that she means no harm and she comes from a small druidic grove far away.
- They immediately drop to their knees before her, and speak in Sylvan to her. [[Tilly]] can't actually understand Sylvan so she mid links with them instead.
- Their voices in her mind are surprisingly deep. They revere [[Tilly]] as if she is royalty.
	- [[Zrandovorski]] knows that Wood Woads are rarely found in he material plane. They serve the Seelie Fae and protect the boundaries between the Fey Wild and the Material Plane. Most of them don't really know much about life on the Material Plane and only know what they've been told; that people from the Material Plane who enter the Fey Wild are intruders and need to be escorted back promptly. They are also told of a rare few legends called "Druids" who they should serve with vitriol if they ever find one.
- [[Tilly]] asks them about The Unseelie Queen at the behest of [[NG]]. They say that The Unseelie Queen is the darkness in the night of the Fey Wild. A faceless, shapeless evil upon the lands; a blight. 
- [[Tilly]] asks them about the Copper Rot that infected [[NG]]'s [[The Factory|Factory]]. They've not heard of that name before, but note that there isn't much metal in the Fey Wild so they're not sure it would be there.
	- This gives the players a chance to come up with what it actually looks like. It's a purple mushroom with a wide cap that turns a red/orange copper colour on the gills on its cap underside. It grows on copper and rust, which gets stored in and between its gills which give it the unique colour.
	- Apparently, there are some thieves who harvest these kinds of mushrooms from the Fey Wild and bring them back to the Material Plane. Neither of these Wood Woads have seen them, it's just stories and rumours that started around 100 years ago or so.
	- [[Tilly]] asks if there's a way to get rid of them. The Wood Woads say that the Seelie Court holds that all life has its place. If it lives, then it is. However, the fact that it's arrived in the Material Plane is... unfortunate. There's a lot more metal here than its native home in the Fey Wild. Getting rid of an invasive plant is difficult.
- The Seelie Queen protects the veil between the Material Plane and the Fey Wild. It's her main "job", according to the Wood Woads.
- [[NG]] has whittled them a wooden duck and gifts it to the pair as a gift to carry on their travels. They are very thankful, and one of them taps the head of the duck and it begins waddling up their arm and on its shoulder.
- [[Tilly]] gifts the pair one of the purple mushrooms she found in [[The Underdark]]. They are thankful, but state that they can't take it with them. They shouldn't bring plants from the Material Plane with them back home. She says that, if the Seelie Queen needs anything, The Reconstellators are happy to help or at least lend an ear. They say that this will be the first thing they report the next time they see her, and [[Tilly]] ushers them back to the Fey Wild at [[Zrandovorski]] suggestion.

### Welcome to Mushroom Town

- The party flies the [[Polaris]] inland so nobody can steal it. Seems like this area is an infested mushroom forest. 
- [[Kolvar]] and [[Garrison]] got 13 or below and starts hearing a booming voice.
	- *"I AM THE SOVEREIGN OF THE MYCONID. REMOVE YOUR FACE COVERINGS AND YOU WILL BE PROTECTED FROM ALL OTHER SPORES."*
	- [[Garrison]] relays this to the party and [[Tilly]] immediately takes hers off. The sovereign explains that they need help with something, and that the party should go deeper into the mushroom forest to find him where they can all speak more in person.
	- There is some difficulty convincing [[NG]], [[The Collector]], and [[Zrandovorski]] to remove their bandanas. The sovereign relents and says it's fine as long as [[Garrison]] can keep the line of communication open.
- The party walks for around an hour east and arrives at a dugout. Some tall mushroom creatures are standing alert, and nod at the party before guiding them to the sovereign. These are Myconids!
- There are maybe 12 total myconids, with one taller myconid clearly waiting for the party to arrive. This must be the myconid sovereign.
- It begins by asking [[Garrison]] what information the party seeks. [[Garrison]] explains that they're looking for [[The Baby]] and they know that it's somewhere in these lands after being stolen by a drow and a manticore. The sovereign explains that there an explosion in its domain somewhere recently, about half a day north. It's caused significant damage to its domain. It asks the party to solve whatever is causing this as the colony would like this to stop. In exchange, the sovereign reveals a helmet with a bunch of gemstones that they will reward the party with. Apparently this was worn by a powerful spellcaster who intruded on their colony, which the colony promptly merc'd.
	- The sovereign explains that, if the party accepts, the colony will make sure there's no difficult terrain or troublesome animals that will bother them on their travel throughout the colony.
- The party heads out in the direction indicated.

### Taking Back [[The Baby]]

- As the party approaches where the sovereign indicated, [[Tilly]] casts *Speak with Plants* on some nearby mushrooms to ask them... what the weather was yesterday. The mushroom indicates it was overcast.
	- [[The Collector]] and [[Garrison]] crash out at her for asking such a dumb question. [[Tilly]], exasperatedly, asks the mushrooms if any person has come out of the nearby lake recently.
	- [[Tilly]] keeps this from the party for now, only *Mind Link*ing with [[The Collector]]. Apparently, someone who looks like [[Kolvar]] was holding the baby. Something exploded in the lake, a cat person walked out while holding the baby *in a back-palm*, like its fingers were bending backwards, and then the cat transforms into [[Kolvar]]'s usual appearance. It uses a sword to cut open a rift in space (a portal?) and walks through, disappearing.
- [[Tilly]] guides the party over to a large crater in the ground. A few charred corpses are still in the crater, one with a sword-wound as if it was stabbed through its back. A section of the nearby lake is completely dried out with another crater nearby, still smoking. The person with [[The Baby]] opened some kind of rift in space and walked through it.
	- [[Zrandovorski]], from the description, knows that this is a *Rakshasa*.; a powerful and (in)famous type of shape-shifting cat fiend. It's bad news if this creature has stolen [[The Baby|Baby Null]].
- The party explores around more and finds a bunker near the lake, but it's been completely obliterated by an explosion. Some charred bodies are found in the rubble, as well as a smoldering note on the demolished war table.
	- Written in fancy cursive: *The next time you're in [[Utopia]], feel free to come see me. Your friend, R*
- [[Son]] inspects the rest of the war room. There's an **X** on top of [[Kota-Ken]] with a scribble saying *"Last known location"*. Seems like whoever's here has been tracking [[The Baby|Baby Null]] for a while, and has known the party has it.
- [[The Collector]] casts *Mending* on the destroyed map to try and repair more of it. This is able to reveal another portion; the place the party is currently standing. It's marked as *"Regroup first"*, and then an arrow leads to [[Weavewind]] that says *"Meetup"*. The party wonders if the remnants of this plan are still in effect, or if the Rakshasa has killed everyone who was part of it.
- [[The Collector]] investigates the corpses to see if any of them are the drow and manticore who originally stole [[The Baby]]. Sure enough, he finds the upper half of a charred manticore corpse and what could potentially be the drow corpse near it (notably its amulet is missing).
- [[Tilly]] asks [[Kolvar]] how they got their face. [[Kolvar]] doesn't really remember; the reason they have it is because of its insignificance. It was probably just someone they saw on the street at one point. [[Tilly]] explains that whoever stole [[The Baby]] had [[Kolvar]]'s usual face.
- [[Tilly]] communes with some nearby plants again. She sees a couple of the people from around this area who are bringing a Warforged to the edge of the lake. It looks docile, but not dormant. The Warforged hands are... twisted backwards like the Rakshasa. Perhaps this is the only fault of their highly versatile shapechanging abilities.
	- She walks to the spot she saw where the Rakshasa stepped through the rift, and feels a *wound* in the area. [[The Collector]] cuts open his palm and tries to resonate with the wound using his weird gross blood. DM says that while nothing happens on this instance, he likes this line of thinking and encourages trying this kind of thing in other situations.
