## Notes

### Level 5, Baby!

- The players discuss the details of [[Session 23#A God and His Apostles|their various plans]] and their level 5 choices for half an hour.
	- [[The Collector]] kills [[Son]] and re-summons him to benefit from the *Investiture of the Chain Master*. This grants him a fly speed (without needing to become a bat first) and allows [[The Collector]] to make him attack in combat.
	- [[Garrison]] laments how he has never ridden a horse, so he summons a giant ferret with *Find Steed*.
	- [[Zrandovorski]] can blow things up with his mind (Fireball).
	- [[Kolvar]] got extra attack.
	- [[Tilly]] can fake anyone else's death now (Feign Death). She can also get more spells if she doesn't wild shape, and vice versa.

### Hey Soul Sagester, Hey There Master Mister

- The Reconstellators manage to get 3 sheets of lead that they can divvy up. [[Garrison]], [[Tilly]], and [[Kolvar]] all get one of the sheets. The plan is to use this to cover up the attuned badges.
- With ferret in tow, the party ventures out to meet [[The Soul Sage]], as requested, and are stopped at the door by guards.
- [[Tilly]], wearing the robe of authority, kicks down the door to demand an audience. She rolls a -1 and the guards only let *her* in; the party must stay outside.
- Inside with [[The Soul Sage]], [[Tilly]] confronts him about her being charmed by the badge even though she was willing to be a peaceful participant in conversation. [[The Soul Sage]] performs some classic Male Manipulator Cult Leader mumbo jumbo bullshit against an impressionable 18 year old in his bedroom. He tells her that everyone in [[Caelia]] knew what they were signing up for so there's no problem; people are just scared of the power the ritual holds.
- [[Tilly]] mind links with [[The Soul Sage]] to ask him more intensely about the risks of the ritual. In her mind, he doubles down and says *"There is only one person who can be hurt by this ritual if it succeeds, and you're looking at him."*
- [[Tilly]] asks him if he's sure, as she doesn't feel the same level for certainty that [[The Soul Sage]] does. He basically just says "yeah but I have faith that my Kalashtar other half isn't lying". Notably, he has never claimed that his Kalashtar second soul *is* the aberrant or directly tied to it in some way.
- Unconvinced that [[The Soul Sage]]'s plan is ultimately good, [[Tilly]] says that she "Can't let him throw his life away like this... Not when I just found you..." and she casts *Hold Person* and [[The Soul Sage]] fails with a natural 1 as [[Tilly]] asks [[Son]] to perform [[Session 23#A God and His Apostles|Plan C]].
- The guards outside hear something fishy going on, so it's combat!

### Plan C is NOT [[Gunpowder Bomb|the Bomb]]

#### Round 1

- The guards attempt to close and bar the door, so [[Garrison]] has a contested Strength check to see who closes or keeps the door open. Both roll 22 at first, then reroll and both roll a 19, but unfortunately the 3rd time is NOT the charm as the guards beat his 15 with their 17.
- The other guard moves towards [[The Soul Sage]]'s room and fires a crossbow bolt at [[Tilly]], landing a hit and dealing 6 piercing damage. [[Tilly]] barely manages to maintain her concentration on *Hold Person* with an 11.
- [[The Collector]] has [[Son]] ask [[Tilly]] if she thinks [[The Soul Sage]] is cursed (planning to potentially use [[Son]] to cast *Remove Curse* through him as it is a touch spell). She has no idea. So [[The Collector]] uses *Eldritch Blast* to blast down the door, now that he's level 5 he gets two blasts, and misses with a 9 (roll 2) and barely hits with a 10 (roll 3). He makes a quasit-sized hole in the middle of the door. He uses *Misty Step* through the hole to get into the room.
	- He is attacked by one of the guards who hits with a 15 to deal 8 slashing damage.
- [[Tilly]] casts *Ice Knife* and throws it down the stairs to hit one of the guards. She hits with a 19 and the guard fails his Dexterity save with an 11. He takes 6 piercing damage and 7 cold damage. She then activates her *Starry Form: Dragon*.
- [[Zrandovorski]] debates using *Fireball* for the first time to hit both guards, but this would mean he also hits [[The Collector]] who went into the room. He instead casts *Chromatic Orb* against the guard already damaged by [[Tilly]]'s *Ice Knife*, but misses with a natural 1.
- [[The Soul Sage]] looks to be in rough shape. He is screeching, unnaturally contorting his body, his eyes turn black, and his hair begins to fall out. The badge that [[Son]] placed on him starts glowing, and the party who is still outside sees that the badges of all the citizens begin glowing as well.
- [[Garrison]] rolls a 12 to hit the door and deals 16 (max damage) to absolutely obliterate it. He uses his new Extra Attack to hit the guard (who *was* holding the door shut) with a 24, and combined with his free *Divine Smite* deals 10 slashing and 9 radiant damage. The guard just crumples under the attack and falls dead. He moves 25 feet into the building.
	- The party notices that these guards were not wearing badges. They recall that only a few guards have worn badges. Are most people close to [[The Soul Sage]] not wearing badges for a reason?
- [[Kolvar]], now that the door is no longer blocking their way in, moves in to position against the remaining guard. He misses with a 12, but (thanks to his new *Extra Attack*) hits with an 18 and deals 9 necrotic damage.
- It's [[Garrison]]'s ferret's turn. It barrels down the street and scampers into the building next to [[Garrison]], ready to be ridden.

#### Round 2

- It's a ~~Lair Action~~ *Abode Action* turn. A bunch of beams of bright lights start shooting up out of the ground inside the room, and all The Recontellators inside must save against this effect. It's 13 radiant damage, or 6 on a success. [[Garrison]] uses *Shield Master* to take 0 since he passed the save. [[The Collector]] uses *Investiture of the Chain* to grant [[Son]] resistance to radiant damage, so he only takes 3.
- [[Son]] raises his arm in sync with [[The Collector]] performing the same motion, and is the conduit to cast *Remove Curse* on [[The Soul Sage]] who begins lifting into the air. [[The Soul Sage]]'s eyes goes back to normal, closes, and he becomes limp and falls to the ground. At the very back of the room, a crack appears in the wall and grows in size. As if a powerful force is silently barreling into the room. A little bit of light peers in through the crack.
- [[Tilly]] rolls 13 for Arcana to see if she feels any different given what just happened. She feels somewhat... negatively... but cannot articulate how or in what way. She rolls 13 for a Medicine check to investigate the condition of [[The Soul Sage]]'s body, and determines he is alive but dying (making death saving throws).
	- She sobs and says "No, no, I can't lose you again... I just found you... First I lost my parents, then [[Blake]], I can't lose you too...". She casts *Spare the Dying* to stabilize him. His shallow, gargled breathing becomes normal.
	- She implores [[Zrandovorski]] to put the body of [[The Soul Sage]] into the [[Bag of Holding]].
	- She runs on stage and addresses all the citizens. She says that she is the sister of [[The Soul Sage]] and the city is no longer safe.
- [[Zrandovorski]] puts [[The Soul Sage]] in the [[Bag of Holding]] and investigates the crack in the wall. With a 16 Arcana check, he is able to identify this as the rift of a *Fiendwurm*; a kind of infernal worm that burrows through reality to travel between the planes. It has a portal in its belly which fiends use to leverage the wurm's burrowing ability to bring themselves to its destination. This crack is a mark of its destination as it breaks out from its inter-plane tunnel. Presumably, this is not specifically a ***Fiend**wurm*, but perhaps something more akin to an ***Aberration**wurm*.
	- The party forms a crackpot plan to throw the [[Delicious Gemstone of Self-Banishing]] down the mouth of the wurm and then [[The Collector]] could use *Suggestion* to tell it to cast *Banishment* on itself using the gemstone.
- The ferret moves beside the soon-to-be portal.

#### Round 3

- The crack tears open, and the party watches a tiny dot in the distance become larger and larger as it moves closer to the new portal. A gargantuan worm-like create bursts through the portal at **immense** speed! The ferret is instantly eviscerated.
	- [[Zrandovorski]]'s held Action triggers, and rolls 19 to throw the [[Delicious Gemstone of Self-Banishing]] at [[Garrison]] who bats it with [[Ol' Reliable]] and it flies right into the mouth of the wurm. It looks like it's going to miss, but the wurm's terrifyingly fast approach causes it to barrel directly into the tiny orb at mach 2 and swallows it without even noticing it was there. It continues barreling through the building, tearing through it like a hot knife through butter.
	- The creature SMASHES right through the ceiling of [[Caelia]]'s cave system in an instant. The underground city becomes incredibly unstable and starts caving in!
- It's now [[Caelia]]'s turn as all of the citizens are *instantly* convinced of the imminent threat and everyone starts rushing to leave the underground city. Unfortunately their only way out is a single-file ladder.
- [[Son]] is able to fly out the hole that the wurm created in the ceiling and lands beside the ass of the wurm.
- [[The Collector]] attempts to climb a pile or rubble next to the hole that the wurm created in the ceiling in order to make it to the surface. He fails the DC 10 Athletics and only makes it to the top of the pile.
- [[Tilly]], on the other hand, is able to make it out of the hole by just barely poking her head out. She casts *Entangle* on the mid-section of the wurm. It fails the save with a 12 and becomes entangled in the plants.
- [[Zrandovorski]] rolls a 13 on the Acrobatics check to leave through the ceiling and is able to make it within 10 feet of the surface (within the exit of the hole). He casts *Sleep* thanks to its 60 foot range and the wurm fails the saving throw with a 15. It is now Incapacitated and trapped in the Spike growth.
- [[Kolvar]] casts *Plant Growth* on the worm and creates a mass growth of plants to stop the creature's movement. Every 1 foot of distance takes 4 feet of movement.
- It's the wurm's turn. Thanks to being Incapacitated and in difficult terrain, it can't do anything but re-attempt the save against *Sleep*, which it passes at a 17.

#### Round 4

- [[Caelia]] makes a save on how well the citizens can escape. A natural 20 is rolled, so actually they're doing pretty well. The ceiling is collapsing though, and [[The Collector]] is still inside.
- [[Son]] flies towards the mouth of the wurm and begins pulling out the [[Gunpowder Bomb]].
- [[The Collector]] makes his way to the surface and is able to cast *Suggestion* on the wurm, with [[Zrandovorski]] using his last remaining [[Zrandovorski#Foretelling Rolls|foretelling roll]] to force the creature to take 14 as its saving throw which is a guaranteed failure!
	- However, the DM reminds [[The Collector]] that the language used matters. [[The Collector]] knows Common, Gnomish, and Elvish. DM confirms that *at least one* of those languages is actually known by the wurm. The party puts their heads together to figure out which of those three it may be.
	- [[Tilly]] looks at the bats as they fly overhead. They are actually giant eyeballs with bat wings. Definitely [[The Far Realm]].
	- [[Zrandovorski]] recalls what he [[#Round 2|found about the creature earlier]]. "The fiendwyrm is an infernal **engine**". The players lock in and decide it *must* be Gnomish. The ferret Akira-slides across the plant growth as [[The Collector]] casts the *Suggestion* in Gnomish. The wurm acknowledges the spell and follows its effect. Using the [[Delicious Gemstone of Self-Banishing]] to *Banish* itself with a satisfying *pop!* HOLY SHIT I CAN'T BELIEVE THAT WORKED!!!
- The party is not out of the woods yet, as [[Caelia]] continues to collapse. The entire party must make Dexterity saving throws as the ground falls out from underneath them. [[Garrison]], [[Tilly]], and [[Zrandovorski]] all failed the save and begin falling with the collapsing ceiling back into the cave of [[Caelia]]. However, [[Zrandovorski]] is able to use *Feather Fall* to prevent any of them from falling to their deaths, completely avoiding the need for follow-up saving throws or to survive a quite steep plummet.
- The session ends shortly after and skips forward a few minutes. The start of next session begins with the party recuperating and helping the citizens of [[Caelia]].

The party gains 1,000 XP for a total of 7,900 XP!
