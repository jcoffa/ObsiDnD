## Notes

- The reconstellators find themselves in the vast expanse of [[The Frozen March]], at the bottom of a pit, with the leader of the cult of Larizadune. [[Zrandovorski]] recalls that Larizadune is an old God, not one in the current pantheon. In fact, he only knows of Larizadune from his time of going into deep research. He knows that Larizadune has a constellation, called The Void, which depicts a swirling emptiness. Larizadune was, for a time, the God of darkness, decay, and entropy.
- [[Son]] is desperate to eat this man. The party prevents him from doing this.
- The cult leader says he gave up his name long ago, and reminds the party that he really wants them to free him. The party is really not into that, so [[NG]] plans to build a brick wall around him.
- The leader mentions that Larizadune is imprisoned in the Astral Sea. [[NG]] mentions he can take the party there at any time by constructing 2 bags of holding and making them kiss.
- [[The Collector]] notices an insignia on the underside of the man's arm. He walks into the binding circle to move the man's cloak out of the way, and he feels his hand touch his underarm. He recoils in pain as he feels the sharp point of a bone fracture stab into his hand, and the man whips his head around to look at him. He drops the torch and begins attempting to wrench himself out of his chains while swiping at [[The Collector]]. It's initiative!

### Killing a Homeless Drug Addict

- [[NG]] goes first, and constructs a pair of manacles to attempt to bind the cultist's hands. He succeeds in doing so, and the man's hands are bound in addition to being attached to the wall.
- [[Zrandovorski]] tries to use *Mage Hand* to force the torch back into the cultist's hands, and fails to do so.
- The cultist fails to break out of the manacles on his turn like the loser he is.
- [[Blake]] sneak attacks the cultist with a 17 to hit with 19 piercing damage.
- The cultist's bones and muscles shift viscerally, and his head swivels around to [[Blake]] saying "I'll see you soon" and turns into a pile of red sludge on the ground.

Uuuh, combat's over?

### What the Fuck Was That

- [[NG]] picks up some of the sludge in a vial for [[Tilly]] to use in a spell, or something.
- [[The Collector]] investigates the wound on his hand when he touched the cultist. It reminds him of a time where he was taking care of Father after he got hit by a poison arrow. His blood was coagulating strangely, and Father assured [[The Collector]] that it was just the poison. But [[The Collector]] can't recall a time where Father ever bled... "correctly".
- As they climb out of the pit trap, they see a [[Cline Daffenborn|wandering traveller]] without a winter coat. The party hails him, and he introduces himself as [[Cline Daffenborn]]. After some banter with [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] looking into [[The Collector]]'s wound and determining he will surely die, [[Tilly]] does a good job restraining herself and allowing the party to introduce themselves instead of her excitedly doing all of it on her own. The Reconstellators agree to let [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] tag along, because he will probably die on his own out here.
- While making small talk, the party asks [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] which God he follows (since he's a Cleric). [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] is super dodgy about the question and dances around it like they're doing the muthafuckin Can Can. They eventually stumble into saying that "they're obviously a Cleric of *Pell-ur* (Pelor)". The party is unconvinced.
- They march on towards the Adamantine mines, and [[Zrandovorski]] uses *Augury* to discover that their journey will be *Weal* (good).
- The party gets kind of lost because of their small talk and bickering. [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] switches up what God they follow from Pelor to Lilith and pretends that they were always saying that. They end up a bit off of their mark, and are a 1/3 days travel from the mines.
- [[NG]] attempts to bind [[The Collector]]'s hands before they sleep, in fear of him becoming possessed by Larizadune. He rolls a 1 (for a total of -1) and binds his own hands instead. He ends up holding the manacles as he goes to bed.
- During the first watch, [[Zrandovorski]], [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]], and [[Blake]] watch as a meteor appears to be careening into their camp before it jarringly veers off and lands 1 mile away before it hits. The impact is enough to wake up the sleeping party. [[Zrandovorski]] and [[Tilly]] gaze upwards once more to see, the brightest it has ever been in the night sky, is the constellation of Larizadune. They hurriedly make their way over to the landing zone.

### If We Sleep It'll Disappear In the Morning!

- The party sees a large, white object with several white spikes coming out of it. It's shaped like a ship's mas t and is about the size of a small sedan. It's caused a wall of flames to surround itself on all sides of the crater.
- [[Tilly]] is able to see that there's an open door on one side of it. It appears that it may have contained something inside of it, but it's currently just an empty space.
- [[Blake]] is able to notice that *something* has been stalking them as they investigate the crash site, while another *something* is wounded and crawling away from the crash site. The thing stalking them is a large beastial creature with 2 legs and 4 arms. It's like a buff bipedal displacer beast with 4 eyes, and tentacles coming out of its neck and flowing behind it like a scarf that's somehow terrifying.
- Meanwhile, [[NG]] investigates the inside of the structure. It contains many crates, and after opening one of them he finds it to be full of bronze-coloured coins. They're not copper or gold, and he feels a small hum of static electricity as he runs his fingers over it.
	- In one of the other crates is a small, white crystalline structure that looks similar to the structure itself.
	- In one other crate is a plain, white mask with slits for eyes and mouth, with a black lipstick-like mark around its mouth.
	- In every corner of this place, there are small maggot-like creatures constantly writhing and wriggling over top of each other.
- [[NG]] picks up the white crystal, and his vision adjusts to allow him to see a blue forcefield that prevents the fire from getting too close to the structure.
- [[Tilly]] attempts to identify whatever is stalking them, thinking back to the Gibbering Mouther, the weird fuzzy creature, the Doppelgangers, even the Darkmantle from the cave. And in all these scenarios she feels one consistent emotion; spiritual connection and familiarity. This entire area makes her feel this way as well.
- [[NG]] attempts to tinker with the white crystal to take advantage of its power. He feels almost a magnetic connection between it and the mask, sensing that if he were to wear the mask while holding the crystal he would be able to control the structure in some way.
- [[Tilly]] uses her weird gibberish speech to talk to the stalker, and [[Zrandovorski]] quickly uses *Comprehend Languages* to listen in. The stalker blinks at [[Tilly]] as she attempts to pacify it, and it responds *"Aberration, you must die!"*. Roll initiative!

### Aberration, You Must die!

- Despite some incredible initiative rolls involving 3 sets of ties (23, 23, 21, 21, 21, 17, 17), the stalker goes first. It slides down the walls of the crater to meet the party standing near the structure. It attacks [[Tilly]] with a 16 to deal 9 bludgeoning damage and 2 psychic damage, and is subjected to a Constitution saving throw while passing with a 14. It then attacks [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] with a 15 and misses. It then circles them to be in the middle of the party and uses a special attack to hit all of them.
	- It hits [[The Collector]] with a 17, misses [[Zrandovorski]] with a 15 due to him using *Shield*, hits [[Blake]] with a 23, misses [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] with a 10, and misses [[Tilly]] with an 11.
	- [[The Collector]] takes 5 bludgeoning, 2 psychic, and passes the Con save with a 13.
	- [[Blake]] takes 8 slashing, 4 psychic, and fails the Con save with a 6 and becomes Stunned.
- [[Son]] shovels Electrum into a sack he borrowed from [[NG]] and collects 150 EP.
- [[Zrandovorski]] casts *Magic Missile* at 2nd level to deal 14 force damage.
- [[The Collector]] leaves he range of the stalker to run towards the injured creature on the other side of the structure. Its opportunity attack deals 10 bludgeoning and 2 psychic, allowing [[The Collector]] to use *Hellish Rebuke* to deal 6 damage, and he fails his Con save with a 9 which stops him in his tracks.
- [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] casts *Blindness/Deafness* on the stalker, and [[Zrandovorski]] uses a *Foretelling Roll* to force it to take an 11 instead of making a roll. The creature is now blinded, allowing [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] to run away.
- [[Tilly]] enters her *Starry Form* as the Chalice form and casts *Pass Without Trace* as the party thinks it may be time to leave or at least spread out.
- [[NG]] puts on the mask while holding the white crystal. Many things around the structure (which he now knows is a ship) activate and light up in a different language (which he knows to be Deep Speech). Everything in the ship is at least partially function except for propulsion which was heavily damaged in the crash.
	- He attempts to use the weapons system, and learns he can cast *Lightning Bolt* or *Magic Missile* at 4th level. He decides to use the *Magic Missile* on the wounded creature that's crawling away.
	- Everyone on the outside watches as slits open up on the outside of the ship, and small white pellets fly out in a bunch of different directions and converge on the wounded creature crawling away. It absolutely obliterates it, and [[NG]] hears in the mask *Kill confirmed*.
	- He teleports into a hidden door that leads to the cockpit of the ship. It looks like the pilot attempted to eject and avoid the crashlanding but something went wrong. Instead, there is a mass of maggot guts, scraps of robes, and blood.
- [[The Collector]] is stunned, but the stalker is blind so it's normal rolling. He hits with a 14 and deals 7 damage. [[The Collector]] goes down. It attacks [[Zrandovorski]] with disadvantage and misses with a 16 (also because he uses *Shield* again).
- [[Son]] collects another 150 EP.
- [[Zrandovorski]] casts *Sleep* and the creature fails with an 11. It's incapacitated until the end of its next turn. He then runs 30 feet outside the crater to create space between him and the rest of the party.
- [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] is able to make it to [[The Collector]] and, because he is dying and [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] is a Grave Domain Cleric, max heals [[The Collector]] with 3d8+3 (27 hp restored).
- [[Blake]] runs 60 feet and, thanks to *Pass Without Trace*, is able to sneak out of the combat.
- [[Tilly]] follows [[Blake]]'s example and runs 60 feet and, also thanks to *Pass Without Trace*, is able to sneak out of the combat as well.
- [[NG]] honks the horn on the ship a couple times as he figures out all the controls. He's able to close the doors on  the ship and then use the ship's weapon to *Lightning Bolt* the creature, dealing 34 lightning damage. The stalker is now bloodied, and hears through the mask *"The creature is now bloodied."*
- The stalker, now no longer blinded or incapacitated, lumbers over to [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] and crits him to knock him to 0 hp. It then attacks [[The Collector]] to deal 9 bludgeoning and 3 psychic.
- [[Son]] collects another 150 EP.
- [[Zrandovorski]] uses one of his *Foretelling Rolls* to hit the stalker with a 15 on *Witch Bolt*, which is his AC, and deals 12 lightning damage. The stalker looks like it's nearing death.
- [[The Collector]] is able to use his *Healing Light* ability to heal [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] for 7 hp, then *Eldritch Blast*s the stalker and misses with a 7 on the attack roll.
- [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] casts *Guiding Bolt* on the stalker as it approaches him, hitting the creature with a 16 and dealing 13 radiant damage. THIS KILLS THE STALKER! The bolt of light strikes the stalker, and it looks around briefly as cracks appear along its body as beams of light pour out of the cracks. It explodes in a fantastic display of blood and viscera, ending the combat.

### Aftermath

- [[Tilly]] inspects the exploded remains of this creature and finds an intact eyeball to recover.
- [[The Collector]] joins the ship and inspects the cockpit. He finds the robes of the pilot and another mask
- [[NG]] is able to investigate the technology, and identifies that the white crystal is called a *[[Helm]]*. You can affix it to a large enclosed space and it becomes a flying bastion.
- [[Tilly]] attempts to use the [[Find Dead Bodies for The Lady of the White|business card for the Hag to deliver her dead bodies]], and the card turns into a *Magic Mouth* and complains to the party that she's only looking for humanoid bodies.

The party gains 620 XP, for a total of 2,780 XP and levels up to level 4! They'll be debating between taking an *Ability Score Improvement* or a Feat.

## Recap

The Reconstellators find themselves still deciding what to do with this cult leader of Larizadune that they've found chained to a strange room buried under mountains of snow. [[The Collector]] is able to investigate the cultists clothes and finds a strange sigil underneath one of his arms, but pricks his thumb on a bone spur while pulling the cultist's robes back. The blood reacts with the cultist's bindings, and he begins to free himself from his shackles. After a couple tense turns of combat, the cultist turns to [[Blake]] and delivers the harrowing message "I'll be seeing you soon" before turning into a pile of red sludge on the ground. Victory... achieved?

[[The Collector]] notices that the wound on his thumb is behaving strangely; it has coagulated almost immediately and despite being an open gash it's not really bleeding at all. After the party climbs out of the hole, they see a wandering traveler approaching who does not appear dressed for the weather. This is [[Cline Daffenborn]], a wandering Cleric who was dumped in [[The Frozen March]] for reasons they're very cagey about. He looks into [[The Collector]]'s wound and confidently determines that he will surely die within the week. Upon hearing that good news, The Reconstellators agree to have [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] accompany them for the time being on their way to the Adamantine Mines.

While travelling, the party makes small-talk with [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] and discovers they're cagey about more than just their reasons for being arrested. They switch up which God they worship every time they're asked, and can't pronounce any of their names correctly. The group misses their mark during navigation and ends up about 1/3 day's travel away from the mines, so they take a rest and decide to trek the remaining distance tomorrow. During the first watch of the night, a huge ball of fire comes hurtling directly toward their camp before veering a mile away before it got close enough to make impact. This wakes up everyone right away, and they [[#If We Sleep It'll Disappear In the Morning!|immediately investigate before the object mysteriously disappears by morning because... reasons]]. It's a gruesome sight when The Reconstellators arrive to the crash zone. What appears to be a spaceship has hurtled directly into the ground and formed an enormous crater where the wreckage sits. A dying creature appears to be crawling away from the wreckage on one side, but the party notices a terrifying tentacle creature stalking them under the cover of night off to the other side, and it looks ready to fuck them up. [[Tilly]] attempts to communicate with it, but it only replies "*Abberation, you must die!*".

[[NG]] manages to get inside the wrecked ship and discovers a set of controls an a mask that he thinks may allow him to influence the ship in some way. Meanwhile the rest of the party is getting their shit rocked by this horrifying stalker creature outside. [[NG]] is able to activate the weapons system and uses it to absolutely obliterate the injured creature crawling away from the crash site before using a lightning beam to zap the stalker and wound it significantly. This allows [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] to finally take it down for good with a *Guiding Bolt* that hits its mark square in the chest, causing the stalker to explode in holy radiant light from the blast. With the area safe and the party gravely injured from the fight, everyone is happy to investigate the inside of the ship. [[NG]] is able to find an advanced piece of technology called a *[[Helm]]*, which is a core component of creating a flying Bastion. The rest of the ship contains only dead maggot-like creatures, torn clothing, and what appears to be both pilots of the ship dead in the cockpit.

What the hell happened here?
