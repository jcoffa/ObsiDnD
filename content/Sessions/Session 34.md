## Notes

### Do [[Kolvar]]s Dream of Faceless Sheep?

- The Reconstellators settle back into the scrying room in the Mummy's Hole, debating if they should just walk back to [[Kota-Ken]] now or long rest in the citadel. They decide to stay for the warlocks to not be dying, and [[Kolvar]] and [[NG]] take first watch.
- [[NG]] and [[Kolvar]] debate the ethics of [[Kolvar]]'s face switching, and [[NG]] reveals that he used to have another name, but everyone who knew that name is now dead.
- [[Tilly]] and [[The Collector]] briefly discuss [[Squail Bigglebottom]] and [[Kwalish]], and the aging rates of elves, and [[Tilly]] insults [[NG]] during his trance by saying he's "like 1,000".
- While they're talking, [[Kolvar]] is having a dream. He's in a beautiful flower field with a bright sun. But now there are clouds overhead, blocking out the sunshine and there's a body next to him, a knife in a woman's back.
- A group of Marauders runs towards him, and he looks down at the body, feeling remorse as he runs away. An arrow strikes the tree next to him, and he feels something is... off. This isn't how it went.
- An arrow strikes his leg and he collapses to the ground, with marauders surrounding him all with the same face. HIS face. But he can't see any of the features, it's hard to make out, but he KNOWS it's him.
- The marauder kneel down and land the killing blow, and [[Kolvar]] wakes up, not losing his exhaustion from the night before.
- Immediately upon waking, [[NG]] asks [[Tilly]] what she meant by saying she's 1000, and they argue about how rude it is to be THAT off when [[NG]] has been hyping up his 777th birthday coming up the whole time they've known each other.
- Somehow, this all devolves into [[Tilly]] being unsure how long she'll be alive, since her mom said she'd become really dope one day ([[Tilly]]'s words) and live longer than normal.
- The party suggests asking her other self and she begrudgingly tries, resting herself on the broken citadel alter and using her *Mind Link* on herself, reaching out to her other half. [[NG]] watches in case anything crazy happens.
- [[Tilly]] speaks in her mind, hearing her own voice echo back to her but with specific intention, as if her echo is being controlled by someone else.
	- First she asks what their name is, but her own question echoes back to her unchanged.
	- She asks how old she will be, and while the voice doesn't echo back, [[Tilly]] feels a gust of wind through her hair and the sense of being outside, despite still being in the citadel.
	- She asks if they know where the other Kalashtar are, and she opens her eyes to see the stars from her necklace shifting, as constellations of the dragon and the chalice move, showing 12 stars light up outside of the constellations.
	- Before they finish, [[Tilly]] thanks her other half and says they should talk more, and the words "they should talk more" echoes back in her head.
- Before they leave, [[NG]] goes back up the ladder to see if he can take the portable hole, but finds it has been bolted down in some way, not acting like a regular portable hole. He could remove it with a special magic item.
- They start heading back to [[Kota-Ken]] at a fast pace to make it back just in time for [[The Golden Gauntlet]]'s Mixer. As they jog along, a giant hand blocks their path, revealing a hill giant, who asks them where they're going.
- [[The Collector]] says they're traveling to [[Kota-Ken]], and the hill giant immediately questions them for going to the invaders' city. [[The Collector]] and [[Kolvar]] tell them that they're only going because they have their child and are holding him for ransom, which convinces the hill giant that they're probably alright.
- He says they can come out now, and 2 more hill giants emerge from the trees, one older giant and another with several teeth knocked out.
- [[NG]] asks if they've heard of [[Skane]], the Ice Giant mentioned by [[Vornak]], and they're offended they think that all giants know each other. [[The Collector]] asks who the most famous Hill Giant is, and they say it must be Dagg Jawbog, the leader of their clan (all of these giants are from the Jawbog clan).
- They say their leader would love to "have them for dinner" and they decline for now since they have to go "save" [[The Baby|Baby Null]], but say they could come back at another time if he could tell them where their clan is located, and he says it's 2 clicks East of here. With no time to make a map at this fast pace, [[Kolvar]] ensures them that they will definitely, 100% be back and they continue their fast pace.

- The Reconstellators arrive at [[Kota-Ken]], receiving a level of exhaustion (and [[Kolvar]]'s now stacking to 2 levels) as they head over to [[The Golden Gauntlet]]'s tower, which appears to have a lot of lights on. [[NG]] has [[The Collector]] use prestidigitation on their clothing so they're no longer covered in swamp for the adventurer's party.
- They approach the door and inform the two guards on duty that they're here for the mixer. They seem a bit surprised they're not more dressed up, but agree to let them in. Before they head up, [[The Collector]] confirms that someone who can fulfill their [[Remove The Kaiju Nest|quest reward]] will be available.
- They enter the tower and ascend a spiral staircase spanning multiple stories (except for [[NG]] who teleports his way up). [[Kolvar]] decides to head into the party disguised as a girl to hopefully distract whoever's in charge should the need arise, so he transforms into Theresa (Cup Size: D), the bartender from [[The Everburning Chalice]].

Everyone is dressed very hoity-toity rich and sexy, with skintight black dresses and tuxedos with ascots instead of ties, over the top jewelry and thick glasses and monocles. The Reconstellators are VERY out of place in their adventuring gear.
- [[NG]] goes looking for a server, who he can spot by their more business formal attire compared to the gala-like wardrobe of everyone else. [[The Collector]] flags one down and asks where they can redeem their reward, and they say he can go to [[Quartermaster Jade]], who is currently in the balcony area upstairs.

- The Chaos Crew splits up: [[NG]] will use his waiter disguise to hear of any gossip among the adventurers, [[The Collector]] and [[Son]] will go up to the [[Quartermaster Jade|quartermaster]] to collect their reward, and [[Tilly]] and [[Kolvar|Kolresa]] will... go to the bar and get free drinks. [[Kolvar|Kolresa]] is very confident she can teach [[Tilly]] her (his?) ways.
- We start with [[The Collector]] as he heads up onto the balcony, which has several game tables. There is one table where a dice game for high rollers is happening. One is a half-elven woman, with some crows feet and emerald green hair tied in a bun at the top of her head held with a chopstick. [[The Collector]] moves to talk with her, and finds himself introduced to [[Quartermaster Jade]]. 
- He mentions that they've completed their quest for the [[Remove The Kaiju Nest|kaiju nest removal]] and are looking to collect their reward. [[Quartermaster Jade|Jade]] is surprised; normally people come to brag to her about taking on that mission before they go.
- [[The Collector]] says he has evidence of their victory if she'd like to see, and she quickly  leaves her game and leads him off to a quieter balcony to discuss the details.
- She asks him if his party is here with them, and [[The Collector]] says they are, and they look out over the party and see a huge group of men at the bar, surrounding [[Tilly]] and [[Kolvar|Kolresa]] who seem to be getting an endless amount of drinks from male adventurers. [[Tilly]] even tells [[NG]]'s 'Fashioned' joke to ruckus laughter.
- While [[Kolvar|Kolresa]] is entirely focused on her plethora of drinks, [[Tilly]] attempts to look around in her drunken stupor for anything suspicious happening in the tower. She notices a couple of waiters whispering off to the side about something, but with no one around to send off to investigate and a new drink already in front of her, she puts a pause on it for now.
- [[NG]] is running around the party, serving adventurers champagne and hors d'oeuvres. They're mostly bragging to each other about their recent adventures, and [[NG]] soon finds his serving tray empty, so heads to the back to get more.
- He overhears some servers talking about a new bounty being posted tomorrow by [[The Golden Gauntlet]]. Apparently, people in [[Kota-Ken]] are having nightmares and waking up tired, and they believe there's a Dream Eater in town.
- [[NG]] makes a move to grab some champagne to bring this info back to [[The Collector]], but the suspicious servers from earlier ask who he is, and who hired him. He says someone named 'Jane' hired him, and they don't believe him, so he genuinely asks if they want him to stop working.
- When they ask if he expects to be paid and he says no, they actually admit he's doing a pretty good job, and one of the servers tip him with 1 SP and they let him continue on. They give him more champagne and a cursed tray of pigs in a blanket everyone refuses to eat, and encourages him to try and get rid of them.
- Back with [[The Collector]] and [[Quartermaster Jade]], she asks for his proof, and he provides the egg shells. She says she'll have to verify the shells with their forensic team but it looks correct, so she gives him the first 5,000 GP now and they can collect the rest tomorrow at her chambers.
- [[The Collector]] asks exactly what [[The Golden Gauntlet]] does, and [[Quartermaster Jade|Jade]] is confused by the question. She reiterates that their group protects the realm. They protect the little guys; merchants murdered in streets, innocents caught in crossfires. She appears to earnestly believe that their intentions are pure and they have no ulterior motives.
- He asks the purpose of this mixer/gala, and she admits that part of it is networking for the adventurers, but also to schmooze investors and make them feel more important. They occasionally hire other adventurers to assist them on big jobs, and The Reconstellators could be one of those groups if what he says is true.
- Since she mentioned investors, [[The Collector]] asks if [[Rexamillian One-Billion]] is here, and she chuckles a bit and says no; he's an investor, but he never shows up to these events.
- Back at the bar, [[Kolvar|Kolresa]] and [[Tilly]] are SWAMPED with adventuring bros trying to buy them drinks, a new one popping up in front of them every time they finish one. [[Tilly]] manages to pass the CON save, but [[Kolvar|Kolresa]] fails, hitting a whole new level of inebriation.  She notices [[NG]] walking down the stairs holding a tray, as he announces "CHALLENGE HOTDOGS, GET YOUR CHALLENGE HOTDOGS HERE".
- [[Kolvar|Kolresa]] and [[Tilly]] immediately start fawning over the adventurers, saying only their hero could eat 4 hotdogs, and all the guys immediately jump the tray, attempting to eat the danger wieners.
- The first guy immediately projectile vomits all over [[NG]] after eating just 1, but this does not discourage the next guy, who puts 4 in his mouth and manages to hold back the barf. They declare him the hero as he prestidigitates himself, and throws a handkerchief at [[NG]] for his troubles.
- [[Tilly]] tries to psychically tell [[NG]] about the waiters she saw earlier (the ones suspecting [[NG]]), and that he needs to go investigate, but he just goes to the back to look for a washroom.
- During all the commotion, [[Kolvar|Kolresa]] hears the voice of the marauder from her dream, saying "your face will vanish from history" off in another room. She briefly freezes up, but heads into the room anyway, seeing a woman sitting with a tall purple collar, and she asks what [[Kolvar|Kolresa]]'s doing back there.
- Before she answers, [[Kolvar|Kolresa]] asks who she is, but the strange woman says that she's just a concerned citizen, and mentions how tired [[Kolvar|Kolresa]] looks. It's going around town: maybe like 100 or so other people have looked the same way recently.
- [[Kolvar|Kolresa]], in their drunken stupor, asks why her, out of all the people in town, why would she be getting affected, and the stranger says they don't know. [[Kolvar|Kolresa]] keeps talking, saying she had a dream and now she heard those voices in this room, and it's just this woman inside, and that is disturbing. All the woman says is that it's fascinating that he doesn't remember, and that's why it tasted so good.
- [[Kolvar|Kolresa]] tries to leave, but the stranger asks why they're leaving, if they wanted to know why her. This woman, this creature, says that dreams like HIS are so delicious, but it needs more. And it can help [[Kolvar]] finally get a good night's sleep, if she's willing to help her.
- [[Kolvar|Kolresa]] watches as the woman pulls 5 of her hairs out of her head, and tells [[Kolvar]] to put them under 5 pillows tonight. If he does, he'll sleep well, she guarantees it. She wishes [[Kolvar]] (said his name despite never introducing himself) good luck.
- The woman blinks and bright purple flashes in her eyes, saying don't fail me before vanishing from [[Kolvar]]'s sight. [[NG]] walks into the room right after, angered that this isn't the bathroom before leaving.

### [I Want My Baby Back](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y1XfGTO2vh8)

- [[NG]] and [[The Collector]] continue their pursuit, finding a hooded man holding a swaddled child. [[The Collector]] casts *Fly* on himself to keep a better eye on the man as they run through the alleys of [[Kota-Ken]], keeping up with him even as they exit the city into the wooded areas surrounding them.
- They find themselves in an open glade, the hooded, armoured man mounting onto a [Manticore](https://5e.scrunko.monster/bestiary.html#manticore_xmm) to make his escape. [[NG]] aims his gun at him, telling him to drop baby. The mercenary says he'll pass and continues mounting, so [[NG]] takes the shot, missing with an 11 and sending them into combat.

#### Round 1
- The man raises his hand and casts *Fireball* at the two of them, hitting with 26 fire damage ([[The Collector]] takes half) as the same explosion they saw in town goes off right in their faces. 
- Next is the manticore's turn, who raises his tail and shoots spines at [[The Collector]], hitting with the first attack and dealing 7 piercing damage. It begins flying away, hovering 5 feet above the ground. If it doesn't take any damage before its next turn, it will fly away with the mercenary and the child.
- [[The Collector]] is up, using his free *Misty Step* to teleport closer and moves close enough to cast *Suggestion* on the mercenary to "return the baby to me, and leave this place immediately." The mercenary fails his saving throw! On his turn he will carry out that suggestion to the best of his abilities.
- Next up is [[NG]], who holds his head in his hands and says there's nothing he can do as he doesn't want to attack the man to break [[The Collector]]'s suggestion.

#### Round 2
- Back to the mercenary's turn, who is compelled by [[The Collector]]'s suggestion to dismount the Manticore. He walks over and hands the baby off to [[The Collector]], and it is confirmed to be [[The Baby|Baby Null]]. He can now see the hooded figure's face: it is a Drow man, who says he'll regret this.
- The Manticore is up again, and, not being suggested, it fires its tail spikes at [[The Collector]], downing him and ending the suggestion. As the spell breaks the Drow looks down at him, then smiles.
- [[The Collector]] immediately makes a death saving throw, which he passes.
- [[NG]]'s up again, and he prepares to cast *Levitate*, but is unsure if he should use it on [[The Collector]] so he can avoid being attacked or [[The Baby|Baby Null]] so he can avoid being taken. He uses it on [[The Baby|Baby Null]], threatening with full sincerity that if the Drow doesn't leave he will drop him. The mercenary stares him down and says he doesn't realize how dangerous the creature is, and dropping it won't kill it. [[NG]] calls his bluff, saying if it won't work then he shouldn't mind if [[NG]] drops [[The Baby]].

#### Round 3
- The Drow looks up at the floating child and points the manticore towards the baby. He tells [[NG]] he's getting that baby, and casts *Eldritch Blast* at him, hitting with a 16 to deal 7 force damage. The second one misses with an 11.
- The manticore swoops down, grabbing [[The Baby|Baby Null]] in its claws, and flies down in front of the robed man, using 1 of its remaining attacks to hit [[The Collector]] for 2 death saving throws, and hitting [[NG]] with his barbs.
- [[The Collector]] rolls his final death saving throw (:sweat:)... and passes.
- [[NG]] uses *Healing Word* on [[The Collector]] getting him up. He also shoots at the manticore with his gun, hitting and angering the creature.

#### Round 4
- The Drow uses his turn to mount the manticore.
- The manticore uses its turn to dash, getting 100 feet away from [[The Collector]] and fleeing with [[The Baby|Baby Null]]. 
- [[The Collector]] says "no, NO" and fires Eldritch Blasts at the manticor, hitting the second time for 5 force damage.
- [[NG]] fires his gun and hits the manticore, angering it again, but neither deal enough damage to stop the fleeing drow or manticore, as they fly into the night with [[The Baby|Baby Null]].
