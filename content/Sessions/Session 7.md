## Notes

- Recalling that at least 8 archers fled into the wilderness just over an hour ago, the party decides to hold out in the tower of the stronghold to better keep watch on the archers potentially returning.
- Trying to determine why [[The Iron Fang]] is here, [[Zrandovorski]] knows that part of [[The Iron Fang]] "shtick" is to look powerful. One of the ways they attempt to do this is to claim territory and appear as to effectively control the area. So they are definitely here to claim it for themselves, but as for any deeper meaning it's impossible to tell.
- [[Zrandovorski]] attempts to cast Augury, but is rudely interrupted by [[NG]] and [[Tilly]] who are freaked out by his ritual. [[Zrandovorski]] goes to the treasure room in the basement to perform the spell instead. He inquires the fates about whether it's safe to stay in the stronghold, and after gaining a result of *Weal* feels safe staying here. He also identifies the magic items from [[Session 6#Bunker Down|yesterday]], and also reads the research notes.
	- The heart-in-a-jar is a key to opening a portal to The Far Realms. There are mentions of a cult "gone dark" who are interested in "Larizadune". [[The Iron Fang]] is interested in discovering *how* the heart can be used to open this portal, but they are also very interested in making sure that does *NOT* actually happen.
- [[Garrison]] asks what [[Blake]] and [[The Collector]] would do when they leave [[The Frozen March]]. [[The Collector]] wants to open a museum (of course), while [[Garrison]] admits he isn't quite sure what to do but is interested in owning a Bastion.
	- Their conversation is cut short as [[Tilly]] starts floating into the air as her eyes glow purple. [[Garrison]] is unconvinced that this is anything worth being concerned about, and remarks that he would be more worried if [[Tilly]] *didn't* do random bullshit in the middle of the night sometimes.
	- [[Tilly]] dreams of a mirror, standing in a black, inky void. Inside the mirror, is a version of herself with pale, white eyes.
- When the party wakes up, [[Tilly]] discovers she is now 6 inches taller. While discussing with the party about what happened during the night, she discovers she can use *Mind Link* to telepathically communicate with people.
- Before setting out, [[NG]] gives everyone (except [[The Collector]]) a magical map he made overnight. It allows the holders to know where the other holders are, and be able to cast spells on them even if they cannot directly see them.

### Painting Bronze

- As the party sets out, they all blink at the same time and a castle appears at the top of a nearby hill. It is made of black stone, and is adorned with flags depicting 6-sided dice. A moat of lava surrounds it, and an enormous drawbridge automatically falls down as if to invite the party inside.
- [[NG]] and [[The Collector]] excitedly run inside, as [[Zrandovorski]] and [[Blake]] lag behind. Inside the castle is [[The Game Master]].; it's literally the DM. After some back-and-forth, he claims that he "doesn't really like Dungeons and Dragons, so you should all Draw Steel instead".
- It's literally Draw Steel rules. Incredible. Attacks never miss, and initiative alternates with players choosing who gets to take a turn and when.
- One of the Gargoyles moves first, attacking [[Blake]] for 2x5 damage and then flying away. It has Flyby, so they don't provoke opportunity attacks.
- After seeing that the Gargoyles are too far apart to both be hit by [[Zrandovorski]]'s Shatter spell, [[The Collector]] goes next. He hits one of them with Guiding Bolt for big radiant damage and then backs up.
- Gargoyle 2 attacks [[Zrandovorski]] for 5 + 9 damage. He asks if he can use his Shield spell to halve the damage (which is how they work in Draw Steel). DM approves, reducing the damage to 5 + 4 instead.
- [[Garrison]] smacks the same Gargoyle that was damaged earlier for 9 slashing and 12 radiant damage, winding it.
- The Spectator attacks the group with several beam attacks.
	- [[Zrandovorski]] takes 8 psychic damage, cannot use reactions, cannot move, and is *Confused*.
	- [[Garrison]] is paralyzed, takes 5 psychic damage, and is frightened.
	- [[The Collector]] takes ~~12~~ 6 necrotic damage.
- [[Blake]] uses this opportunity to sneak attack the damaged Gargoyle before [[Garrison]] runs away due to his fear. They deal 10  piercing damage and 9 sneak attack damage. This kills the Gargoyle, and is the first enemy killed in the fight.
- [[The Game Master]] uses a "Malice Action" to force all the heroes to make Dexterity saves. Anyone who fails takes 5 fire damage.
- [[Tilly]] attempts to Thunderwave the remaining Gargoyle to push it off its perch. Gargoyles are vulnerable to Thunder damage, so it takes a whopping 21 damage and then an additional 6 as it smacks into the ground. She heals [[Zrandovorski]] for 8 HP.
- [[NG]] sinks into a puddle of black ink and uses his new *Portal Jump* feature to teleport next to a map-holder (in this case [[Garrison]]). He then Guiding Bolts the remaining Gargoyle and deals 11 radiant damage to it.
- [[Zrandovorski]], functionally affected by the *Confusion* spell, he rolls a d8 to determine who to randomly attack. He rolls a 5, and casts Toll the Dead on [[Tilly]]. Thankfully, he rolls like trash and only deals 1 necrotic damage.
- The Spectator fires 4 more beams, hitting [[Son]] twice, [[The Collector]] once, and [[Tilly]] once.
	- [[Tilly]] takes ~~7~~ 3 psychic damage on account of her natural resistance, and is *Confused*.
	- [[The Collector]] dodges his beam.
	- [[Son]] takes ~~2~~ 1 psychic damage from the first beam, and then takes 26 damage from the second one. He is completely eviscerated.
- [[The Collector]] hits the Gargoyles with Eldritch Blast, dealing 6 force damage and 1 necrotic damage from Hex.
- The Gargoyle flies over to [[NG]] and deals 6 slashing damage.
- [[Garrison]] manages to un-paralyze and un-fear himself.
- Now that they have an available ally allows them to perform sneak attack, they demolish the Gargoyle with a 22 damage strike. Only the Spectator is left.
- [[The Game Master]] takes another "Malice Action" and the Spectator sprouts 2 additional eye stalks.
- [[NG]] walks forward and throws a net at the Spectator. [[Zrandovorski]] uses portent to guarantee that it fails its saving throw, as it falls to the ground.
- [[Tilly]], still *Confused*, hits [[Zrandovorski]] with Starry Wisp for 1 damage. They are now even.
- [[Zrandovorski]], still *Confused*, hits [[Garrison]] with Toll the Dead for 3 necrotic damage.
- The Spectator makes more beam attacks.
	- [[Tilly]] takes ~~12~~ 6 poison damage and evades being poisoned.
	- [[The Collector]] takes ~~16~~ 8 necrotic damage from the first beam, then takes ~~4~~ 2 psychic damage and is at 1 HP from the second beam.
	- [[Zrandovorski]] evades being paralyzed.
- [[Garrison]] deals 19 damage to the Spectator now that it's stuck on the ground under the net.
- [[Blake]] deals 15 damage with a sneak attack while two-weapon fighting.
- [[Tilly]] conjures a Moonbeam, but the Spectator is able to reflect the spell back at her, dealing 8 force damage.
- [[The Collector]] uses Eldritch Blast to deal 12 force damage, and successfully kills the Spectator!

### Aftermath

- [[The Game Master]] summons a bag of 75 GP, and offers to wager "double or nothing" and by double he means 10x if the party beats another combat. They refuse and take their money. The castle instantly disappears after they cross the threshold.
- After a short rest, the party trudges on. They come across a crater that's 8 feet deep in the snow, with a small disc-shaped creature inside. It's slumped to the side as if it sleeping or injured. [[NG]] knows that this is a Flumph and it's friendly. [[NG]] and [[Tilly]] slide into the crater while [[Zrandovorski]] casts Augury.
- The flumph has obviously been stabbed and is bleeding black ichor. It says it was in the [[Underdark]] and ended up here.
- [[Tilly]] attempts to heal the creature, and it looks successful at first as the wound closes. Unfortunately, it re-opens shortly after. The flumph says that a bird-like adventurer was fighting a big monster with a scorpion tail, and it was grazed by the tail and is now suffering the effects.
- The party expresses interest in long resting as the flumph asks them what they want. It reveals a ladder hidden by the snow that supposedly leads to the [[Underdark]]. There is a cave halfway down that they could stay in, which isn't deep enough to actually be in the [[Underdark]]. The flumph specifically mentions that the cave is "temperate".
- Disco (the flumph, which the party named affectionately) offers to lead them to the cave down the ladder. After a bit of a scare with the ladder wobbling, which [[NG]] and [[Garrison]] fix on-the-fly, the party makes it to the cave. It's surprisingly hot down here.
- [[Zrandovorski]] investigates the moss growing in the cave, and notices that it removes any feeling of ambient static electricity from his body (like when you remove woolen clothing and your hair kinda sticks up).
- The cave has a pool of strangely warm water. [[Tilly]] jumps in and it heals her wounds, so Disco follows and his wounds heal for good this time.
- [[Zrandovorski]] uses Detect Magic and discovers the water is not magical, but something at the bottom of the lake is. [[Tilly]] dives down and discovers that it's surprisingly difficult to swim deeper in this water; it's as if the water is unnaturally buoyant. She finds a golden orb at the bottom, and pulls it out of the rock it was stuck to. Turns out this was plugging a hole, and she starts to get swept into a current. [[NG]] throws down a rope to try and pull her out, but the rope is slick with water and she is swept into the hole.

The party gains 275 XP, for their total of 1330!

## Recap

The Reconstellators recall that a large group of archers fled the stronghold they holed up in, and are worried that they may be returning with some backup if they stick around for too long. While they determine watches for the night, [[Zrandovorski]] attempts to use Augury to see if it's safe for the party to remain in the stronghold, but is interrupted by [[NG]] and [[Tilly]] who are spooked by his ritual. They throw bells at him, in fear that he is actually a Doppelganger who has infiltrated their group and must be eliminated. After retiring to the treasury room in the stronghold's basement, he completed the spell and the fates inform him that the stronghold is likely safe for the duration of their rest. He reads over the research notes that the party found earlier, written in Goblin, and discovers that the heart-in-a-jar that they found earlier may be a key to the far realms which can be used to awaken an ancient deity named Larizadune. [[The Iron Fang]] is bent on discovering the secrets behind this process, so that they can ensure *nobody* performs it, including themselves. [[Zrandovorski]] neglects to inform the party of this revelation purely because they kinda pissed him off earlier and then he forgot about it.

Meanwhile, in blatant display of hypocrisy, [[Tilly]] awakens from her slumber by floating in the air while her eyes glow purple and [[Blake]] and [[Garrison]] do not even toss a single bell in her general direction. She grows 6 inches taller and discovers that she can telepathically communicate with creatures close to her. The party begins to think that there is a small chance that she may not be human, potentially.

After trudging through the snow for a bit to further their journey to the Adamantine mines, the party blinks in unison and finds a foreboding castle over the crest of a hill that was certainly not there before. After some deliberation, the party enters the castle and finds [[The Game Master]]; a tongue-in-cheek character who is essentially the DM personified as an NPC in the game. He muses that, if the party can manage to triumph over a random combat, he will pay them an unknown amount of gold coins. The party takes that bet, and prepares to fight. However. [[The Game Master]] decides that he "doesn't like D&D very much" and declares that they will be "playing Draw Steel" instead. The party is faced with combat against a few Gargoyles and a Spectator, and must use some of the rules of the [TTRPG Draw Steel!](https://shop.mcdmproductions.com/collections/draw-steel) to achieve victory. They choose their initiative order and alternate between monsters and heroes, nobody's attacks can miss, and they make a ton of jokes about forgetting to use *Hesitation is Weakness*.

After a very deadly combat, the party emerges victorious and is awarded with 75 gold. [[The Game Master]] suggests they could try another combat and receive 75 platinum instead; a 10x payout of the original spoils. Deciding that gold is worth nothing to the dead, the party chooses to leave with their gold and live to spend it another day.

While continuing their journey through [[The Frozen March]], they happen upon a flumph bleeding out cinematically in a snowy crater. After discovering that healing magic cannot fix its wounds, the party gives up trying to help it almost immediately and pivots to asking it for help. The flumph mentions that it witnessed a bird-like creature fighting a scorpion-like creature in a cavern underground and that's where it got this wound from. Disco (which at this point the party has decided to name the flumph) offers to offers to show them to a temperate cave to hide out from the encroaching blizzard, and they accept. After climbing down a rickety ladder, the party finds the temperate cave and [[Tilly]] steals a golden ball from the bottom of the hot tub and is sucked down the drain, never to be seen again.
