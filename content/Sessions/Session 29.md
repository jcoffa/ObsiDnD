## Notes


### Master Gave The Party an Airship, The Party is Free!! As Long as We Can Secure a Constant Source of Fuel in a Pre-Industrial Era!!

- As the [[Polaris]] crosses the mountain at sunrise, The Reconstellators and their 30 civilians see the [[Utopian Empire]], warm air brushing their skin for the first time in forever.
- They land the ship in what would normally be a swampy clearing to the East of [[Landon's Lamp]], but has since solidified a bit in the cold weather (a balmy 5°C instead of the -25°C of [[The Frozen March]]).
- The party debates whether they should go to [[Landon's Lamp]] for anything; [[Tilly]] mentions the idea of finding fuel, and [[The Collector]] is curious about getting an update about what has been happening on the continent since their arrests. [[NG]] and [[Garrison]] can't recall their being anything of note in the town, but they could have found some fuel in the mines there since the last visit.
- They decide to head into town to scope out the getting-immediately-arrested situation for the [[Draskhrr]]ites, leaving them to guard the boat and leaving [[The Baby]] with the scariest motherfucker they could find on board.

### Ancient Tomes & Prophetic Doom

- They walk through the frozen swampscape and make it!.. about 6 hours before [[Garrison]]  gets ~~goosebumps~~ rustbumps as he feels a somewhat diving presence somewhere in the tree line, immediately redirecting The Reconstellators off their path.
- As they approach, the divine energy continues to rise and they see a church made of some sort of black stone. [[The Collector]] notices some writing on the walls, so he casts *Comprehend Languages* while [[Garrison]] identifies the language to be Giant. It says "The Library of [[Annam All-Father]], The King of Learning and Magic".
	- [[NG]] has heard of [[Annam All-Father|Annam]], and he's like the Bahamut for Giants. He has many aspects other than learning and magic, but this temple is dedicated to those aspects specifically. He is considered the major deity to all of Giantkind.
- [[Tilly]] peeps through one of the windows, using [[Son]] as a stepstool. Inside looks to be what used to be a church now converted into a sort of library. A large man, 15 ft, with red long braids and blue skin is organizing oversized books and armed with a massive sword as tall as she is.
- [[NG]] knocks on the door, and the man lets them in, asking how he can be of service. [[NG]] introduces himself and the giant holds out a pinky in greeting, which [[NG]] shakes. He asks if they require any knowledge; the library contains many old tomes relating to gods and old history. His name is [[Vornak]], and he's been tasked with taking care of this church.
- Most of his knowledge is outdated, but he offers his library to The Reconstellators to investigate for anything they're curious about. Most of his knowledge is ancient though, [[Bring Books to Vornak|so he would happily except any tomes or knowledge The Reconstellators could bring him in the future]].

#### Loredumping on Larizadune

- [[The Collector]] asks if he has anything on [[Larizadune]] as he pulls out a giant sized book for this poor gnome to begin to read and struggle through.
- [[NG]] asks about *The Great Reset* to [[Vornak]]. He is wary to answer but does so, he pulls another large book, Gargantuan Creatures and You. Cameron lists a bunch of other scary names associated to this "Great Reset"
	- The creature is depicted like a cryptid (sort of looks like a Tarrasaque) absolutely destroying towns and locations in a wake of apocalyptic destruction.
	- [[Vornak]] tells of why the [[Utopia|Utopians]] hate this being as it stands against their order, a being that could cause their destruction.

He was seen as an abberation, an entity of cosmic evil that sought things beyond its standard domain, and the domain of gods in general. Most historians agree that he sought the end of all things, an endless void.
- [[Tilly]] asks if there's any relation to [[The Abberant]], but [[Vornak]] says that he hasn't heard of them, which surprises The Reconstellators since he seemed like THE Abberant guy when they heard about him from [[Gobbo]].

- [[The Collector]] asks for any knowledge of [[Hadar]], and [[Vornak]] explains he is 'a great old one', an elder evil, the father of the Star Spawn, and The Great Hunger. Based on how he discusses them, it seems like [[Hadar]] and [[Larizadune]] are two separate entities.
- [[The Collector]] tries to ask him about the current state of [[Utopia]], but he hasn't heard any current events since the [[Clerical War]] has outlawed most religious practices, so no one comes to the church anymore
	- **Some [[Utopia|Utopian]] Religious Context**: [[Utopia]] believes the gods exist, but has no sway over mortal events anymore, and [[The Grand Excelcium]] (the leader of [[Utopia]]) is a grand oracle who can see future and leads the people, so the populous follows because he is down here guiding them, as opposed to the gods
	- [[Vornak]] has heard that once or twice a year a huge group of people gather in [[Utopia]] to hear some religious preaching from [[The Grand Excelcium]], but is unaware of what happens there.

- [[NG]] asks if he's heard anything about the Kalashtar since [[Tilly]]'s too busy ~~taking notes~~ thinking. He's heard of one who refer to themselves as two-spirited, a Goliath of Stone named Geldra, who he'd last heard of a few decades ago in the Elemental Plane of Earth. She was a powerful warrior, capable of combatting Stone Giants herself, but he hasn't heard of her recent whereabouts.
- [[Tilly]] asks about anything from [[The Far Realm]], but [[Vornak]] says it's not the same as other planes; things don't really happen there, and time doesn't flow the same way. He does know of one person who may know: he pulls out a transcribed tome written by someone named [[Volo]] who may know more about [[The Far Realm]] (of course he does).
- [[The Collector]] asks what he knows about what is happening "up north": he questions if the frozen nature of [[The Frozen March]] is necessarily unnatural, and [[Tilly]] debates its sudden nature, and [[NG]] mentions the flash-frozen state of things they've seen.
- He's aware of a Frost Giant named [[Skane]], who lives in a mountain range on a different body of land than this. If someone were to know about elemental energies, it would be him. But he doesn't know where he currently is since [[Skane]] always visited him.
- Finally, [[The Collector]] asks if he knows what a Herald of [[Hadar]] is. [[Vornak]] doesn't know for sure, but says that while some gods have "chosens", others have heralds, who announce the arrival of a god. [[Vornak]] says that if there is a legitimate Herald of [[Hadar]] out there, then the mortal realm is in grave danger.
- [[Tilly]] asks if someone could be a herald and not know it, or grandfathered in, but [[Vornak]] highly doubts it; it's something you sign up for, not some backhanded deal that they wouldn't know about. It's unlikely it could just be passed on either.
- [[Vornak]] is suspicious on whether this is all a hypothetical, and [[The Collector]]'s very convincing "nuh-uh" leads him to cast *Zone of Truth* on the party to confirm the details. [[Tilly]] reveals the whole story of [[Session 17|finding the baby]] and [[Session 20#New Main Character Discovered|The Collector's engagement with Larizadune/Hadar]] and [[The Collector]] confirms that it is all true. He also tells him of [[Session 16#The Collector 's Trance|his vision of his father where he heard the term Herald of Hadar]].
- [[Vornak]] is unsure if the heraldry can be passed down from his father, but considering his father was at least a known acolyte of [[Hadar]], it is crucial that he not make any agreements or deals with him (oopsies). [[The Collector]] confirms that he understands the stakes before the *Zone of Truth* is dropped and they decide to head out.

His creator couldn't disagree more and demands that he is killed.
