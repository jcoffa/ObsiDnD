## Notes

### Goddammit, We Committed a 9/11 Again

- The sun rises over the horizon on a new day as The Reconstellators spend their first few hours helping the people of [[Caelia]], managing to save another 30 people from the rubble.
- While [[NG]] takes in all of the innocent lives lost, [[Tilly]] releases [[The Soul Sage]] from his bag. His eyes have changed colour; they're now normal white, with blue pupils. [[The Collector]] asks him what he remembers, which is just being in his room and having a badge put on him to start the ritual. He remembers the searing pain, and someone crying over his body.
- [[The Soul Sage|The Sage]] doesn't feel great about the ritual his people worked on for a century not happening, and it being his fault because it happened too early. They show him his new eyes, and he claims that he is no longer 'chosen'. He says he should be furious and angry, but he's just sad.
- [[NG]] asks how long he's been on this plan, and it's as long as he can remember. His old adventuring party tried to stop him because he revealed the ritual would kill him upon completion. Now that it's failed, he doesn't understand what he is supposed to do.
- [[NG]] LAYS INTO THIS MAN, telling him that he should've died and he was pathetic for allowing such a risk to befall his people if the ritual failed. He demands to know how [[The Soul Sage]] thought that getting himself killed to act as a vessel for an Outer God would possibly be better. [[The Soul Sage|The Sage]] claims that with the knowledge they would have gained he could have fixed [[The Frozen March]] and brought his people back to prosperity, but [[NG]] does not back down. He says his people had prosperity and he squandered it by risking their lives, and leaves the conversation.
- [[Tilly]] tells him that she has no regrets and is happy that he's alive. She says he tried to take the easy way out; by sacrificing himself for the betterment of his people and [[The Frozen March]] he would have left [[Caelia]] without a leader. She tells him that now all he can do is take on the responsibility he thought he had, to truly lead the people of [[Caelia]] that have nowhere to go. He admits that she's right and agrees to lead his people to safety, and both [[NG]] and [[Tilly]] are granted Heroic Inspiration for their empassioned speeches.
- After building a jumbo tent to fit Furret inside, The Reconstellators discuss what to do with the people of [[Caelia]], and [[The Soul Sage]] with his new lease on life. [[The Collector]] is a bit embarrassed that they actually did cause trouble just like he said he would, and they express concern about them just trying the ritual again once they're separated.

### (1) Unread Message(s)
- The Reconstellators decide to rest to relieve them from exhaustion, but only a few hours pass before [[The Collector]] feels a tap on his shoulder as [[Gobbo]] reappears to provide the information they asked the Kuo-Toa for. Sadly, they've already accomplished what he wanted, and he's glad that they stopped the ritual, while The Reconstellators don't feel great about how stopping it all went. [[Gobbo]] reveals he is currently at home in Bastrilixia, but he's not there very often. Only [[The Collector]] has heard of this city on the other side of the world that hosts a special event called "The Abberant Games".
- [[Kolvar]] asks [[Gobbo]] when he killed [[The Abberant]] and it was only 10 years ago, despite this ritual having been worked towards for 100 years. [[The Collector]] asks for a boon for accomplishing their task, and [[Gobbo]] offers gold if they come to him physically On another continent. This also leads into an extensive discussion about his wishes for infinite bacon and an infinite drumsticks.
- They try to grill [[Gobbo]] for additional details, but he reveals he doesn't know much outside of this ritual. He hasn't heard about a lich in [[The Observatory]], or any other activity from the many cults in [[The Frozen March]] despite being a God of Good. The Reconstellators quickly grow tired of his shenanigans and he leaves, allowing them to finish their long rest.

### Wait, Where's The Main Quest Marker Again?
- After their long rest, The Reconstellators awake the next day discussing the idiocy of [[Gobbo|The God of Good]], and [[NG]] reveals he COULD summon a lil guy, but won't for now (this is part of his Level 5 abilities).
- [[The Soul Sage]] is talking with some of the citizens, and notices the party waking up, telling them he plans to bring his people to the next closest civilization, [[The Hearth of Hope]]. [[The Collector]] informs him that they have good relations with [[The Hearth of Hope|The Hearth]], and to inform them they have their approval if they give him any trouble, which [[The Soul Sage|Vicci]] is thankful for.
- [[The Soul Sage]] offers any help they could give them and [[The Collector]], naturally, asks if he has any magic items to offer. There aren't any that he's aware of in [[Caelia]], other than the box containing the badges. Turns out it was a lead box that could not be tracked through scrying, which [[The Collector]] sends [[Son]] to look for.
- [[Tilly]] asks about the rumours they've heard about the [[The Cult of the Stars]] on their travels;
	- Regarding the ritual item from [[Investigate Underdraskhrr|Underdraskhrr]], he conflates that to rumours being mixed up; some people say they are [[The Cult of the Stars]] follows Larizadune, or is ruled by a lich. They don't even refer to themselves as [[The Cult of the Stars]], so rumours about their activities could not even be connected to them. The only thing they could've taken was the lead box, but [[The Soul Sage|Vicci]] is under the impression that was traded.
	- [[The Soul Sage|Vicci]] reveals that a man named [[Grain Verigor|Grain]] did pass through here, but he was a bit too crazy for their taste. [[Grain Verigor|Grain]] believed he needed to see the stars up close, and that he would fly a grand vessel and witness their beauty. [[The Soul Sage|Vicci]] never met him personally, but the guards say he was raving about seeing the stars as close as he possibly could.
	- They ask about the lich in [[The Observatory]], and even [[The Soul Sage|Vicci]] doesn't know where this rumour is coming from, and confirms with them that they actually SAW a lich. He thinks if he was there and not a lich, it would be very advantageous for people to think he is a lich to scare them away. The Reconstellators debate some theories about this lich: he could be Utopian, and could have caused [[The Frozen March]] to gain many bodies for sacrifice, or was attracted here because of the many deaths caused by the frozen wasteland.
- After their questioning and fully rested, they start their journey to [[Fair Helm City]], deciding to travel 7 days with the people of [[Caelia]] before splitting off. The people will travel another 4 days to reach [[The Hearth of Hope|The Hearth]], while they travel another day and a half to reach [[Fair Helm City]]
- The first day is uneventful, Tilly made a map.
- The second day, as they're walking along, they notice 2 bumps moving in the snow, larger than a human. They send [[Son]] over to scope it out, who notices that one lump is pointy while the other is smooth. He decides to investigate the lumps by sitting on the smooth one, and immediately loses his fly speed. What looks to be tentacles shoot up from the snow closing in on [[Son]] and crushing him, and everyone rolles initiative!

#### Round 1
- [[The Collector]] watches as [[Son]] gets hjs shit rocked by this creature, which emerges from the snow. It's a Flail Snail, a large snail with 6 eye stalks with morning stars where its eyes should be and a reflective shell. He first casts *Hex*, then fires off his *Eldritch Blasts*. His first misses with a 12 but the second hits with a 26, dealing 8 force damage and 5 necrotic. One of its eye stalks breaks off and falls to the ground, leaving 4 stalks remaining as the blast reflects on [[The Collector]]. As the spell hits the shell it glows and a concussive blast shoots back out at [[The Collector]], dealing 8 force damage as it reflected the effects of his *Eldritch Blast*. Turns out the Flail Snail's shell has a chance to magically reflect any magical effects cast on it.
- [[Tilly]] casts *Call Lightning*, striking the snail and [[Son]] at the same time. Both fail their DEX saves, taking 20 lightning damage and leaving 3 stalks. After hitting the creature, [[Tilly]] makes a CON save, passing and dodging the reflected concussive blast.
- [[NG]] is up next, and he moves 20 ft. forward and casts *Faerie Fire* on the snail, also hitting [[Son]]. [[NG]]'s *Fairie Fire* is now upgraded; his concentration on the spell cannot be broken by taking damage, and he can add his intelligence modifier to any damage a spell or attack makes on creatures affected by it. He moves next to [[Garrison]] and casts *Magic Weapon* on [[Garrison]]'s lance. This time the spell does not reflect on [[NG]].
- The Flail Snail is next, swinging and killing [[Son]] (I missed the numbers, sorry Joseph). It swings on [[Tilly]] with a natural 20, dealing 7 bludgeoning damage before retreating into its shell, increasing its AC.
- [[Kolvar]] attacks next with his pact blade, missing with the first hit at 14 but hitting on the next with a natural 20. He deals 16 psychic damage as another eye stalk falls out of the shell and then backs up 10 feet.
- [[Garrison]] rides up on Furret, hitting the second snail on his first attack with a 24 and dealing 16 piercing damage and 6 radiant damage from his *Divine Smite*. He attacks again, hitting with a 27 and dealing 16 piercing damage. 2 more eye stalks drop from the shell, leaving 1 remaining.
- It's now the second flail snail's turn, who uses it's BA to emit bright coloured light within 30 feet, causing anyone within range to make a WIS save. Everyone passes (even Furret!) except [[Garrison]], who becomes *Stunned*. The flail snail then attacks [[Garrison]], hitting twice with a 21 each, dealing 9 bludgeoning damage and 6 bludgeoning damage. One of its eye stalks regenerate after hitting [[Garrison]], bringing it back up to 4 stalks.
- Next is [[The Soul Sage|Vicci]]'s turn, telling the people to back up as he attempts to *Eldritch Blast* the front snail, but misses and ends his turn.

#### Round 2
- We are back at [[The Collector]]'s turn. the first Flail Snail appears bloody, and looks like he could attack less now that he's down to 1 eye stalk. He fires another *Eldtritch Blast*, hitting with a 25 and dealing 8 force damage and 5 necrotic. The *Eldritch Blast* reflects back, dealing 8 force damage back to [[The Collector]], and dropping its last eye stalk. The snail starts screeching inside its shell, loud enough to be heard 600 ft. away. [[The Collector]] misses his second Eldritch Blast with a 15, but uses his Healing Light ability to heal [[Garrison]] for 14 HP.
- [[Tilly]] uses her magic action to re-summon her lightning call from earlier, this time striking the second snail for 16 lightning damage. She fails her CON save and the damage is reflected back on her, dealing 16 lightning damage as the wind is knocked out of her and her concentration is broken. Gasping for breathe, she uses *Healing Word* on herself for 12 HP.
- [[NG]] backs up so he can fire his gun at the front snail and gets a 19 but it STILL MISSES while the snail is hidden in its shell. He reloads his gun and ends his turn.
- The first snail continues to scream and ends its turn.
- [[Kolvar]] hits him and deals 7 bludgeoning damage, killing the front flail snail. He *Misty Step*'s to [[Tilly]] and grants her 2 Temp HP.
- [[Garrison]], stunned, is unable to do anything except move. He uses half his movement to dismout from Furrett, giving it a turn in initiative. 
- The remaining Flail Snail uses his advantage to swing on [[Garrison]], but miracurously misses all 4 attacks and decides to move closer to the other party members elsewhere. It takes an attack of opportunity from Furret, hitting with a 20 and dealing 4 psychic damage.
- Next up is Furrett! He moves towards the snail and hits with a 16, their unshelled armour class, and he deals 5 psychic damage.
- [[The Soul Sage|Vicci]] is up next, attempting to hit him with another *Eldritch Blast* and missing again, ending his turn.

#### Round 3
- Back to [[The Collector]], he uses his BA to move Hex to the other snail, and fires both Eldritch Blast's at it, both hitting with a natural 20 and 17. The first deals 10 force damage and 12 necrotic damage, with the force damage reflected on [[The Collector]]. The second deals 11 force damage. [[The Collector]] hits his classic anime glasses push, blinding the creature with the light reflected off the lenses and channeling his *Eldritch Blasts* through the light, hitting both remaining stalks and killing the snail!

#### Aftermath
- [[Tilly]] goes searching its gooey trail and finds crystalline structures left behind, with [[Tilly]] collecting 7 Flail Snail crystals.
- [[NG]] investigates where the snails came from, and it looks like more lumps are approaching them from the horizon 600 ft. away, coming to the screams of its breathren.
- They go to redirect their walking party from the straight path, but [[The Collector]] casts Fly on himself and moves 200 ft. away, casting Thurmaturgy to call the snail's attention. This works thanks to a 9 from [[The Collector]] and a -2 from the approaching snails.

### Can You Lend Me A Hand?
- The rest of their journey goes off without a hitch until it's time to split with [[The Soul Sage]] and the people of [[Caelia]]. [[NG]] takes one final moment with [[The Soul Sage|Vicci]], telling him that this is his second chance and not to waste it. [[Tilly]] tells him to stay alive, and he sends them off with hopes for their safety as well.
- Nothing eventful happens their first day of solo travel, and their second half-day of travel brings them into view of [[Fair Helm City]]. Giant stone walls appear before them, with 2 massive ballistas visible at the top. As they make their way up, they see a little man wearing a toque and fluffy white coat, operating a small shop. It is... unclear what species he is, but definitely not of this realm. He introduces himself as an unseelie shop owner. 
- The party tries to ask him his name but he's unwilling to offer without receiving a name first. [[Tilly]] tries to volunteer hers but decides not to after it's described as a 'deal' (and the party yells at her A LOT not to do it). They ask about his wares, which includes concepts, feelings, and fruits and vegetables. Let's be real, they're most interested in the produce.
- He reveals he is looking for people to help his queen and asks if they would volunteer their bodies. He reveals there is something inside [[Fair Helm City]] the queen requires, and he can help them get into [[Fair Helm City]] if they can do something for her.
- They need to "change the state" of someone inside [[Fair Helm City]], someone named Benedict, and this is VERY loose on interpretation. It could be making him sad after being happy, or making him liquid after being solid. During these conversations they learn that the Unseelie Queen is the one who helped [[Kolvar]] become a changeling.
- The party spends the last 25 minutes of the session debating whether it's worth it to accept the deal from the unseelie shopkeeper. They try to grill the unseelie for details on Benedict, but all they get is that the queen thinks it would be really funny.
- [[The Collector]] is outvoted as the rest of the party agrees to [[Change the State of Benedict|the unseelie deal]], snapping his fingers. As they teleport they briefly see petals, before they appear at their new destination, behind the bars of a prison cell.

The party gains 600 XP, for a total of 8,500 XP!
