### [[Toran the Red]] Arrives

- [[Toran the Red]] arrives at the longhouse with a small posse of hers who accompanied her on her travels. [[Zrandovorski]] stays in the basement to cast *Augury* and see if he can divine how the hand off would go. He receives a result of *Weal* (positive results). This isn't necessarily definitive of how this plan will work long-term as *Augury* is only a 2nd-level spell and can only divine the results of a course of action within the next 30 minutes. This means, literally, *the handoff* will be a positive event and not necessarily that *this course of action will ultimately lead to positive results long-term*. But it's better than nothing.
- [[Toran the Red|Toran]] asks what preparations the party has made to get [[Draskhrr]] ready for the handoff (they have done nothing and forgot) so [[NG]] lies with a natural 20 for Deception to reassure her that they totally planned this out.
- The party asks her if she knows the dangers of [[The Stony Veil]], and she says that there isn't much risk of "rocks falling"; it's largely the elementals in [[The Stony Veil]] that make you die. But she has successfully sent people there and they have returned with great riches, so it's by no means a guaranteed death trap.
- After some out-of-character debate, the party decides that they don't really need to worry about the handoff much as they can spin this that [[Toran the Red|Toran]] helped them remove [[Rykor Steelhand]] and [[Kaz the Cold]] so there really isn't a handoff at all.
- [[The Collector]] whips up a rousing speech and successfully deceives the crowd, who begin chanting "All hail temporary leader [[Toran the Red]]!". Could've been worse, I guess!
- The party heads out towards [[The Stony Veil]] and [[Fair Helm City]].

	- [[The Collector]] sends in [[Son]] to go scout it out. After MUCH difficulty because [[Son]]'s IQ is literally 0 and he lacks the Common Sense language proficiency, the party decides to put [[Tilly]] in the [[bag of holding]] and send her through the portal with [[Son]] (since [[The Collector]] can continually recall him, and anything he carries, back as the portal is only one-way).
	- [[Tilly]] is met with a lot of snow and not much else. There's a mountain range that comes to a point off in the distance with a small treeline near its base.
	- After a total of approximately 25 real-life minutes of debate and investigating the portal and giving [[Son]] instructions and [[Tilly]] looking around, [[NG]] remembers that he can know the exact location of any creature holding one of his special Artificer maps to instantly know exactly where the portal leads to. It leads exactly halfway between both [[The Stony Veil]] and [[Fair Helm City]], which is really convenient! This saves them almost 3 weeks of travel from [[Draskhrr]]!
- **Day 2**: Nothing of note.
- **Day 3**: The party encounters a "warbling" effect (like that thing when you drive on a hot, straight road and the horizon line is kinda wavy and looks like a puddle of water) about 100 feet away off to a slight detour. [[Zrandovorski]] casts *Augury* to see what it would be like if they got in close and "investigated thoroughly", and receives a result of *Woe* (bad). That's not a good sign.
	- [[Tilly]] detects an emanation of psychic energy coming off of it. She attempts to *Mind Link* to it, and rolls a 13 on an Intelligence save. The DM pulls Dani away for a secret conversation, and she comes back with [[Tilly]] taking 6 psychic damage.
	- She explains there's, like, tentacles and shit and she connected to hundreds of Mindlfayer colonies across the planes. She urges the party that they should leave.
	- [[Zrandovorski]] rolls 20 on Arcana, [[NG]] rolls 17 on Arcana, and [[The Collector]] rolls 11 on Arcana to see if they know anything about it. Together, they put together that it's an Aberration connecting to the hivemind of Mindflayers.
		- [[NG]] suspects that the description reminds him of a Beholder.
		- [[Zrandovorski]] remembers a creature called a Mindwitness; essentially a Mindflayer-infected Beholder  that is used as an amplifying connection hub to connect a large amount of Elder Brains across great distances.
		- [[The Collector]] feels like he's heard of "Mindwitness" before. He recalls that his Father says that Beholders are at their most powerful when they are in full control of themselves. Any time they are altered, bio-manced, taken over, possessed, etc. they are actually not as powerful as normal Beholders.
	- [[Zrandovorski]] uses *Augury* again to see how well a fight would end up in our favour. He receives a result of *Weal and Woe* (could be good or bad depending on what happens). They decide to continue on and ignore the scary Beholder mirage.
	- The party ends the day near the base of [[The Stony Veil|the mountain]] by a cave entrance.

They tend to have differing tastes and preferences for what they consider a delicacy. Some may prefer gems that can act as spell components, some only care about specifically Amethyst worth 500 GP or more, some prefer gemstones with sentimental or historical value. He is able to stand up and reaches for his sword on the ground beside him. He picks up only a hilt, noticing the actual blade of the sword is completely missing. The man mumbles that "it was magical..." and laments that the Xorn probably ate it while he was passed out. Seems like they don't just have an appetite for gemstones, but magic items as well.

## Recap

The session began with a brief catchup for how [[Kolvar]] spent their 2 weeks of downtime in [[Draskhrr]] while waiting for [[Toran the Red]] to show up since Sage couldn't make it that session.

Fast forwarding back to the present day, The Reconstellators reconvene in the basement of [[Draskhrr]]'s longhouse to ask the Marquis' corpse more questions using *Speak With Dead*. From this, they learn that the Marquis suspects [[Zrandovorski]]'s youngest sister, Gedak, may still be alive if she also possesses his gift of agelessness, but even then he isn't sure. He also doesn't appear to know very much about this "gift of agelessness" and has only ever heard of a select few people to have it ([[Zrandovorski]] being one of them). The party attempts to carefully move him into the [[Bag of Holding]] by having [[The Collector]] kick his body to the ground, and it turns to dust immediately on contact. Well, shit! [[NG]], please get the dustpan.

They sheepishly head back upstairs to see that [[Toran the Red|Toran]] and her escort party are approaching the city. The handoff is near. [[Zrandovorski]] stays back to use *Augury* to determine how well the handoff will go, and receives a result of *Weal* (positive result). This isn't necessarily definitive of how this plan to leave [[Draskhrr]] to [[Toran the Red|Toran]] will work out long-term as *Augury* only grants information on the 30 minutes after a potential action would be performed. Despite this, getting a positive result in the immediate is still better than nothing. The handoff does go fairly well thanks to a quick speech from [[The Collector]] to make sure the people of [[Draskhrr]] understand [[Toran the Red|Toran]] is on their side, and the party sets off to [[The Stony Veil]] (partially at the suggestion of [[Toran the Red|Toran]]). This ancient mountain is reputed to have a rich store of gemstones within its bowels, and [[Tilly]] is in dire need of 50 GP of ruby dust to cast *Continual Flame* [[Session 20#Downtime Time|as discussed during her previous downtime]]. [[The Stony Veil]] is also much closer to [[Fair Helm City]] which they're hoping to be able to steal a ship from, convert it into a flying Bastion using their [[Helm]], and then hightail it back to [[Draskhrr]] and leave [[The Frozen March]] for good.

[[The Stony Veil]] is on the opposite side of [[The Frozen March]] than [[Draskhrr]], so The Reconstellators have a long journey ahead of them. Or at least they would if they didn't discover a portal that leads to an unknown location within [[The Frozen March]] on their first full day of travel. After dicking around for about 30 minutes and punting [[Son]] through the portal and summoning him back to the other side 11 times as a scouting mission, the party determines it's worth the risk and appears safe enough to go through. The odds that the portal takes them further away from [[The Stony Veil]] is highly unlikely as [[Draskhrr]] is already pretty darn close to being as far away from it as possible already. [[NG]] hilariously didn't realize that his magical map would tell him exactly where any Reconstellator would end up until after they already decided to go through it. Using this, they discover the portal spits them out almost exactly halfway between [[The Stony Veil]] and [[Fair Helm City]], saving them approximately 19 days of relatively straightforward overland travel. Good DM good DM good DM!

On the remaining 2 days of travel, the party encounters a strange warbling mirage. After some investigation by [[Zrandovorski]], [[The Collector]], and [[NG]], plus some mind-linking mumbo-jumbo by [[Tilly]], the group determines that this is some sort of camouflage containing a *Mindwitness*; a mindflayer-ified Beholder that acts as a signal booster for their psionic hivemind to connect multiple mindlfayer colonies together. [[Zrandovorski]] uses *Augury* to determine how well a fight would go, and gets an inconclusive result of *Weal and Woe* (both positive and negative results). Not wanting to press their luck while so close to their destination, the party anxiously continues onwards.

[[The Stony Veil]] has a clear opening into its cave system from the outside, with an ominous skeleton crushed by an enormous boulder right by the entrance. It clutches a gemstone, which the party places bets on to determine what specific kind of gemstone it is. [[Garrison]] correctly guesses that it's a Sapphire and claims the 50 GP stone for himself. Entering the cave silently thanks to a *Pass Without Trace* from [[Tilly]], the party enters the bowels of [[The Stony Veil]] and hears a strange chirping and clicking sound from deeper within. [[Zrandovorski]] feels he has heard about this strange sound before, and recalls a species of creature named "Xorn". They're rock-like monsters that eat gemstones and magic items, boasting many powerful limbs to help them burrow and move around through solid rock. They should be careful, and ensure they be mindful of the magic items they have on their person.

Deeper into the cave they find a seemingly fresh corpse holding the empty hilt of a sword with no blade and wearing a backpack that appears full to the brim and agonizingly heavy. Turns out this corpse isn't dead, just an adventurer who is very badly injured. [[Garrison]] is able to heal him with *Lay on Hands* just in time for a Xorn to come barreling into the room as combat breaks out! [[Zrandovorski]] uses a [[Zrandovorski#Foretelling Rolls|foretelling roll]] to set its initiative to 4, allowing (almost) the entire party to act before the monster. They deal a lot of damage but are unable to kill it before it acts, and it almost knocks [[Garrison]] unconscious in a single turn as it uses many of its bulky limbs and gaping maw to set him down to exactly 1 HP. [[Kolvar]] is able to land the killing blow thanks to clever use of the [[There and Hererings]] to get into position, and a good tentacle'd *thwack!* from their *Arms of Hadar* spell to take out the monster for good. The group has little time to celebrate their victory as, deeper within the cave, they hear the sound of *several more Xorn* fast approaching.
