### Talking Heads

- [[Tilly]] wonders how old the Marquis is (or would be while he was alive; given how his body is so well-preserved), rolls a 14 for Medicine, and determines he looks to be a little bit older than how [[Zrandovorski]] looks. A little "touch of grey". Perhaps late 30s to early 40s.
- [[The Collector]] and [[Tilly]] interrogate [[Zrandovorski]] to try and map how old he looks to how old the Marquis may have been when he died (he looks about mid-30s).
- They ask the Marquis 5 more questions from *Speak With Dead*:

1. Who of [[Zrandovorski]]'s family is most likely to still be alive?
	- This is stolen from [[Session 19#Speak With Dead Marquis|the previous time the party did this]].
	- *It truly depends on whether they also hold his gift. If they hold his gift of agelessness, any number of them could be alive. If you're looking for "most likely", based on very little information, it would be Gedak. And I have heard whispers from my informant that the [[Utopia|Utopian Empire]] has some sort of access to an oracle. Whether they are related to [[Zrandovorski]] or not, I do not know.*
2. What do you know about [[Toran the Red]]?
	- *[[Toran the Red]] is a terrorist. She attacked our city many times, and has caused many homes to go up in flames. Lots of damage and problems in our city. A very evil person.*
3. What is your unfinished business?
	- *I don't have any. My only regret is that I didn't catch on to [[Kaz the Cold]]'s betrayal sooner.*
4. What do you know about [[The Observatory]]?
	- *What I have been told by my scouts is that a very powerful wizard of extreme booyagh lives there. He is old beyond normal age and he knows more about booyagh than anybody in our old empire.*
5. What do you know about [[Zrandovorski]] gift of agelessness?
	- *I have only known a select few people who possess this gift. You are one of them.*

- They debate what to do with the tax dollars. [[NG]] suggests that they should either take all of it or none of it; very much leaning on "none of it". The Reconstellators have done so much good in liberating [[Draskhrr]] that they should leave them their well-earned funds.
- Deciding it would be a good idea to take the body of the Marquis with them in the [[Bag of Holding]] so that they can keep asking more questions. [[The Collector]] kicks the body over and it instantly turns into dust upon hitting the ground. Shit. Well, whatever!

### [[Toran the Red]] Arrives

- [[Toran the Red]] arrives at the longhouse with a small posse of hers who accompanied her on her travels. [[Zrandovorski]] stays in the basement to cast *Augury* and see if he can divine how the hand off would go. He receives a result of *Weal* (positive results). This isn't necessarily definitive of how this plan will work long-term as *Augury* is only a 2nd-level spell and can only divine the results of a course of action within the next 30 minutes. This means, literally, *the handoff* will be a positive event and not necessarily that *this course of action will ultimately lead to positive results long-term*. But it's better than nothing.
- [[Toran the Red|Toran]] asks what preparations the party has made to get [[Draskhrr]] ready for the handoff (they have done nothing and forgot) so [[NG]] lies with a natural 20 for Deception to reassure her that they totally planned this out.
- The party asks her if she knows the dangers of [[The Stony Veil]], and she says that there isn't much risk of "rocks falling"; it's largely the elementals in [[The Stony Veil]] that make you die. But she has successfully sent people there and they have returned with great riches, so it's by no means a guaranteed death trap.
- After some out-of-character debate, the party decides that they don't really need to worry about the handoff much as they can spin this that [[Toran the Red|Toran]] helped them remove [[Rykor Steelhand]] and [[Kaz the Cold]] so there really isn't a handoff at all.
- [[The Collector]] whips up a rousing speech and successfully deceives the crowd, who begin chanting "All hail temporary leader [[Toran the Red]]!". Could've been worse, I guess!
- The party heads out towards [[The Stony Veil]] and [[Fair Helm City]].

### On the Road Again

- **Day 1**: The party comes across a floating sphere of snow and blue energy. [[Zrandovorski]] rolls a 13 for Investigation, seeing that the snowy ice orb is larger than the average person. Its school is *Conjuration*, so [[Zrandovorski]] suggests it may be a portal.
	- [[The Collector]] sends in [[Son]] to go scout it out. After MUCH difficulty because [[Son]]'s IQ is literally 0 and he lacks the Common Sense language proficiency, the party decides to put [[Tilly]] in the bag of holding and send her through the portal with [[Son]] (since [[The Collector]] can continually recall him, and anything he carries, back as the portal is only one-way).
	- [[Tilly]] is met with a lot of snow and not much else. There's a mountain range that comes to a point off in the distance with a small treeline near its base.
	- After a total of approximately 25 real-life minutes of debate and investigating the portal and giving [[Son]] instructions and [[Tilly]] looking around, [[NG]] remembers that he can know the exact location of any creature holding one of his special Artificer maps to instantly know exactly where the portal leads to. It leads exactly halfway between both [[The Stony Veil]] and [[Fair Helm City]], which is really convenient! This saves them almost 3 weeks of travel from [[Draskhrr]]!
- **Day 2**: Nothing of note.
- **Day 3**: The party encounters a "warbling" effect (like that thing when you drive on a hot, straight road and the horizon line is kinda wavy and looks like a puddle of water) about 100 feet away off to a slight detour. [[Zrandovorski]] casts *Augury* to see what it would be like if they got in close and "investigated thoroughly", and receives a result of *Woe* (bad). That's not a good sign.
	- [[Tilly]] detects an emanation of psychic energy coming off of it. She attempts to *Mind Link* to it, and rolls a 13 on an Intelligence save. The DM pulls Dani away for a secret conversation, and she comes back with [[Tilly]] taking 6 psychic damage.
	- She explains there's, like, tentacles and shit and she connected to hundreds of Mindlfayer colonies across the planes. She urges the party that they should leave.
	- [[Zrandovorski]] rolls 20 on Arcana, [[NG]] rolls 17 on Arcana, and [[The Collector]] rolls 11 on Arcana to see if they know anything about it. Together, they put together that it's an Aberration connecting to the hivemind of Mindflayers.
		- [[NG]] suspects that the description reminds him of a Beholder.
		- [[Zrandovorski]] remembers a creature called a Mindwitness; essentially a Mindflayer-infected Beholder  that is used as an amplifying connection hub to connect a large amount of Elder Brains across great distances.
		- [[The Collector]] feels like he's heard of "Mindwitness" before. He recalls that his Father says that Beholders are at their most powerful when they are in full control of themselves. Any time they are altered, bio-manced, taken over, possessed, etc. they are actually not as powerful as normal Beholders.
	- [[Zrandovorski]] uses *Augury* again to see how well a fight would end up in our favour. He receives a result of *Weal and Woe* (could be good or bad depending on what happens). They decide to continue on and ignore the scary Beholder mirage.
	- The party ends the day near the base of [[The Stony Veil|the mountain]] with a cave entrance. By the front, there is a skeleton under a huge boulder that has cracked in half. In the skeleton's hand, it appears to be holding an unknown gemstone. [[Zrandovorski]] calls for the party to each guess what kind of gemstone it is before they pick it up. For fun!
		- [[NG]] thinks Garnet.
		- [[Garrison]] thinks Sapphire.
		- [[The Collector]] thinks Emerald.
		- [[Son]] thinks it's a rock.
		- [[Kolvar]] thinks Amethyst.
		- [[Zrandovorski]] think Quartz.
		- [[Tilly]] thinks Amber.
	- [[Son]] collects it, holds it in his hand, and opens it in front of the party. Turns out it's a Sapphire worth 50 GP! [[Garrison]] guessed correctly, so he gets to keep it.
	- Getting closer, they can see there is a minecart and some tracks slightly into the mouth of the cave. [[Tilly]] and [[The Collector]] hear the sounds of stone-against-stone crunching and cracking from deep within the cave, as well as a chirping sound.
	- [[The Collector]] gets closer to the minecart and finds that there is another skeleton
	- [[Zrandovorski]] rolls a 27 on Arcana and recalls that he has heard the word "Xorn" before. Xorn are specifically earth elementals that look for precious gemstones to eat them. They tend to have differing tastes and preferences for what they consider a delicacy. Some may prefer gems that can act as spell components, some only care about specifically Amethyst worth 500 GP or more, some prefer gemstones with sentimental or historical value. They are a bit taller than the average humanoid but *much* wider. They also have a large maw on top of their bodies, and have many arms and legs.
	- The party decides to go deeper into the cave. [[Tilly]] casts *Pass Without Trace* to give everyone +10. The only person to get below a 20 is [[NG]] with a 17.

### The Cave of Head-Crushed-By-A-Boulder

- The DM asks the party what items they have on them that would be worth money, which is somewhat concerning.
- They quickly make their way into the cave, and [[NG]] notices some small diamonds embedded into the floor. Using his Jeweler's Tools he is able to carefully and skillfully pop them out (natural 20). +100 GP worth of diamonds! There was a small tremor that was felt as he popped them out, but there doesn't seem to be anything around them that would have caused that.
- The next room is an eerily fresh corpse of an adventurer holding a smoldering torch with a seemingly full backpack. He has a large chest wound covered in blood. The bare light from the torch twinkles some gemstones overheard.
- [[NG]] taps the body on the corpse, and its eyes open. Turns out they're not dead, just very badly injured. [[Garrison]] is able to use *Lay on Hands* to close the man's chest wound. He is able to stand up and reaches for his sword on the ground beside him. He picks up only a hilt, noticing the actual blade of the sword has been completely. The man mumbles that "it was magical..." and laments that the Xorn probably ate it while he was passed out.
- Speak of the devil, a large rocky monster bursts out of the ground! This is a Xorn, fitting the exact description that [[Zrandovorski]] recalled earlier!

### Rock Monster!

- [[Zrandovorski]] uses one of his [[Zrandovorski#Foretelling Rolls|Foretelling Rolls]] to make the creature's initiative a 4, allowing the party to all but guarantee that they can move before it.
- **Round 1!**
- [[The Collector]] acts quickly by casting *Hex* on the Xorn, following that up with a good ol' fashion *Eldritch Blast* to hit with a 22 and deal 13 force damage + 3 necrotic damage. He backs up 30 feet to create some space between him and the monster.
- [[Zrandovorski]] casts *Shatter* 8 feet underground so that it only hits the Xorn. It rolls an 8 and takes 11 thunder damage (as does the floor underneath it). Any more significant damage to the floor in that area will cause it to cave in.
- [[Son]] uses his Help action to give the next attack advantage.
- [[Garrison]] attacks the Xorn with his [[Ol' Reliable]]. He hits with a 23 and deals 13 slashing damage and + 7 radiant damage. A chunk of stone is hewn off the creature, and it recoils in pain with a slight clinking and chirping sound. He holds his ground next to the Xorn.
- [[NG]] plans to start moving toward the exit and uses his Neutral Special to use Gun. He *misses* with an 18. Wtf.
	- The DM does specify that the creature was bloodied after [[Garrison]]'s hit, so it doesn't have too much HP even though its AC is quite scary.
- [[Tilly]] uses *Thunderclap* and amplifies the sound of [[NG]]'s bullet pinging off the Xorn. The Xorn, [[Garrison]], and [[Son]] all pass and take ~~8~~ 4 thunder damage. A thunderous boom is heard for 300 feet, echoing down the halls of the cave.
- The man throws down the broken hilt of his sword, casts *Firebolt*, hits with a natural 20, and deals 18 fire damage. The Xorn is not dead, but is looking really bad!
- The Xorn looks back and forth between [[Garrison]] and [[NG]] before deciding on [[Garrison]] who is closer. It makes 3 claw attacks, missing with a natural 1 and a 20(!!!), but hitting with a 24 to deal 11 slashing damage. The natural 1 causes it to wildly swing and hits itself for 6 slashing damage. It then follows that up with a bite attack, hitting with a 22 and dealing 24 piercing damage. Holy SHIT that thing really hurts! [[Garrison]] is alive at 1 HP!
- [[Kolvar]] uses the [[There and Hererings]] to swap themself and [[Garrison]] to bring him to safety. They then use *Arms of Hadar* against the Xorn, which passes the exact DC at a 14, and takes ~~13~~ 6 necrotic damage.
	- The tentacles sprout of multiple cracks in the walls, and bash up the Xorn into smaller pieces. The creature is dead!

While the combat is over, the danger is not. The rumbling sounds of other Xorn approaching to investigate all of the *very* loud  thunder-damage noises that the party has caused. They need to get out of here ASAP!

The party gains 400 XP for a total of 6,175 XP!
