## Recap

After a brief conversation with Berkley Longnose now that they are safely back in [[Draskhrr]], the party returns to The Everburning Chalice to get shitfaced after almost dying in [[The Tomb of Booyahg]] earlier that day. [[Volo]], as thanks for being saved by the party, graciously pays for their drinks that night. [[NG]] decides that it's technically safe for [[Garrison]] to drink alcohol, and the group fears that he may have developed something of an addiction to it.

While deep in their cups, the party discusses the most important decision of their adventure yet; their party name. Among a laundry list of generally kind of junk ideas (*because we were fooling around a lot and making lots of dumb jokes*), [[NG]] suggests "The Reconstellators". This came from some inspiration on how multiple characters (namely [[Tilly]] and [[Zrandovorski]]) have been talking about how the stars have been behaving strangely as of late, and also the symbology of how "each member of the party is an individual star and together we are reconstellating". It has a nice ring to it, so "the party" becomes "The Reconstellators".

With their new name chosen, The Reconstellators figure out what to do next. They decide to go check out the Toppelgangers and swing by the Adamantine Mine along the way. There is one problem though; Doppelgangers can look like anyone they've seen before, and so the party decides to make some identifying items that they can carry in their pockets so that the Doppelgangers won't know to copy them until it's too late. After [[NG]] destroys *100 rocks* while abysmally failing to chisel cool designs into them, [[Tilly]] uses spiderweb to wrap bugs around them instead. Theoretically, The Reconstellators will be able to hide these in their pockets and pull them out when suspicious of some body-snatching chicanery. Satisfied, they set out into the snow barrens and head out for the Adamantine Mine.

They get sidetracked by a dope looking cave with a big glowing wolf and some blue fire inside it. [[NG]] crafts a hunting trap, which [[Garrison]] and [[Tilly]] set near the mouth of the cave, and then attempt to draw out the big scary wolf alone. The wolves, very famously being pack hunters, all leave the cave together and the big glowing one is intelligent enough to immediately know what the situation is and alert its packmates. The party are thrown into a very deadly fight and barely take down the Winter Wolf thanks to some lucky crits from [[Garrison]] and [[Blake]] plus a tactic from [[NG]] to cleverly douse it in oil to boost the fire damage it takes. Turns out a creature called a "Winter Wolf" doesn't appreciate being set on fire, and it is courteous enough to die before any of The Reconstellators are killed outright. The remaining wolves flee, and the party is rewarded with a super cool ice-themed shortsword for their efforts before turning in for the night in the cave they have now claimed.
