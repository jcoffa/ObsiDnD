### Spinning Our Wheels

- The Reconstellators debate [[Session 18#The Plan|their plan]] for like 30 minutes.
- [[NG]] summarizes his understanding of the plan:
	- Leave [[The Deep Forge]].
	- Send [[The Collector]] to [[The Everburning Chalice]] to get [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]].
	- Simultaneously, we send [[Kolvar]] to [[Kaz the Cold|Kaz]]'s Longhouse to get him.
	- [[Garrison]] and [[Zrandovorski]] fake fight outside the longhouse.
	- When [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] and [[Kaz the Cold]] show up, they reveal the fight is fake.
	- [[The Collector]] makes an impassioned speech to the people of [[Draskhrr]] to show them that life is worth living and they don't really need to leave [[The Frozen March]].
	- After this inevitably fails, they fight to the death with basically no recourse for escape.
- [[Tilly]] tries to think of ways that the party could reduce the number of active military combatants around them. 
	- [[Kolvar]] suggests that they could stage a bomb threat.
- [[NG]] and [[Tilly]] kind of realize that they don't really have anything to do. So [[NG]] comes up with a second speech to rouse the crowd.

### Countrymen! To Arms Against The Tyrants!

- [[Garrison]] draws his sword and marches up to [[Kaz the Cold]], saying "No; this ends here!". He hits [[Kaz the Cold]] with a 22 to deal 8 slashing and 16 radiant damage.
	- He min-rolled the greatsword (2d6+6 = 2+6 = 8) and max-rolled the Divine Smite (2d8 = 16)
- [[Tilly]] says "So much for protecting your people!" while grabbing a sunbeam and attacking [[Rykor Steelhand]] with *Heat Metal*. This is bad news for [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] as his hand is made of steel which is, uh, metal. And he cant really remove his arm. [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] takes 12 fire damage and, since he can't drop his metal arm, will have disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks until the start of [[Tilly]]'s next turn.
- [[The Collector|Linus Snabberknackle]] runs up to the group and goes back-to-back with [[Zrandovorski]], whispering in his ear "My plan worked out pretty well!" as he glances at the enormous crowd revolting against their tyrannical leadership. He casts *Guiding Bolt* at [[Rykor Steelhand]] and unfortunately misses with a 12.
- [[NG]] gets around 20 feet behind [[Kaz the Cold]], feeling threatened by his sudden reveal to have magic. He tries his hand at a *Guiding Bolt* [[Kaz the Cold|Kaz]] and also misses with an 11.
- [[Rykor Steelhand]] says "You insolent child!" and walks over to [[Tilly]] to grapple her with his burning hand. He barely hits with a 15 despite the disadvantage, dealing 13 damage with his first attack and misses with his second at an 8 to hit.
- [[Kolvar]], still disguised as an [[The Iron Fang|Iron Fang]] hobgoblin, summons their *Pact of the Blade* while chuckling and says "What an unfortunate situation for you, [[Kaz the Cold|Kaz]]". They swing with their pact blade and hit with a 17 and deal 14 necrotic damage.
	- [[Kaz the Cold]] looks a bit shocked at the "betrayal", but says *"Ah, I see..."*.
- [[Zrandovorski]], having just heard [[The Collector|Linus Snabberknackle]] rejoice at the success of his plan, smiles as he watches The Reconstellators fighting the tyrannical leadership of [[Draskhrr]], and says "Yes, I'm glad to see my plan working as well...". He casts *Witch Bolt* against [[Kaz the Cold]] and misses. He'll have to wait until next turn to deal any damage with it.
- [[Kaz the Cold]] laments the "cowardice" of the people of [[Draskhrr]] for allowing outsiders to sway their opinion, casts *Ice Storm*. That's a 4th-level cold spell which deals 8 bludgeoning damage and 14 cold damage (half to [[Kolvar]] who saved) to everyone except [[Tilly]] (as she is fighting [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]]) and the area is now difficult terrain.
- [[Son]] debates whether to blow himself up with the [[Gunpowder Bomb]] or help [[Tilly]]. He reluctantly decides on the latter, giving [[Tilly]] advantage on her next attack against [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]].
- **Round 2!** [[Garrison]] hits [[Kaz the Cold]] with a 16, and uses another *Divine Smite* to deal 23 radiant damage.
- [[Tilly]], upset that [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] is grappling her but deciding to use it to her advantage, smugly tells him "But you didn't even take me home first... ;)" and casts *Thunderwave* to push him back and deal 8 thunder damage. She moves 30 feet away from [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] towards [[Kaz the Cold]], stopping before entering the difficult terrain. Because her concentration wasn't broken, she can user her bonus action to deal more damage to [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] by continuing *Heat Metal* to deal 15 fire damage.
- [[The Collector|Linus Snabberknackle]] turns his gaze towards [[Kaz the Cold]] and shoots off an *Eldritch Blast* to miss with a 10. Not letting this ruin his moment, he raises his hand to the sunset and heals himself for 13 with *Healing Light*.
- [[NG]] moves 5 feet away from [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] and casts *Faerie Fire* on him. He fails with a 12 on the save, being affected by the spell to make The Reconstellators have advantage on attack rolls against him. He uses his bonus action to cast *Healing Word* and heal [[Zrandovorski]] for 9 HP. He uses his map to teleport out of range of [[Kaz the Cold]], preparing to avoid his next *Ice Storm*.
- [[Rykor Steelhand]] walks back up to [[Tilly]] and misses with a natural 1 thanks to the disadvantage. He takes 4 damage from his burning arm as a penalty. Frustrated with how his 1-on-1 duel with [[Tilly]] is going, he eyes [[Zrandovorski]] the squishy wizard and runs past [[Tilly]]. [[Tilly]] unfortunately misses her opportunity attack so [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] can get to [[Zrandovorski]] unscathed. He swings with a 17 to hit and misses thanks to him casting *Shield*.
- [[Kolvar]] rolls a natural 1 on their attack roll against [[Kaz the Cold]], and accidentally hits [[Garrison]] instead to deal 10 slashing damage. They use *Refreshing Step* to teleport next to [[Zrandovorski]] and grant him 2 temp HP.
- [[Zrandovorski]] tells [[Kaz the Cold]] that "My spells never miss" and uses his bonus action to deal 10 lightning damage with the residual *Witch Bolt*. He then casts *Chromatic Orb* and hits with a 24 to deal 23 fire damage against [[Kaz the Cold]]. However, since 2 of the die were identical (4) it can hit a second target! This hits [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] with a 22 to deal another 21 fire damage.

He misses the first with an 11, and crits the next with a natural 20. [[Zrandovorski]] takes 22 damage, dropping him down and dying.

	- "I hope you enjoyed hell. Good luck at whatever's next!"

### Smash Cut to the Bar?

- [[NG]] drunkenly explains that "They used to just be called *Fashioneds* and now it's *Old Fashioneds* and that makes me feel like I'm gonna die soon."
	- He follow this up with asking [[Tilly]] what she thinks about delivering bodies to [[The Lady of the White]]. She says she doesn't really care and feels she has no reason to doubt the hag's intentions.
- The party wakes up the next morning pretty hungover. As their first order of business, they decide to investigate the basement of the longhouse.
- [[Zrandovorski]] uses *Detect Magic* and finds that there's necromantic magic in the sketchy basement that [[Garrison]] found the last time he was here. They send [[Son]] in to scope it out, and he finds a corpse in *very* good condition wearing a sketchy necklace. [[Zrandovorski]] recognizes this is the Marquis who founded [[Draskhrr]] and went missing 5-8 years ago.
- The corpse's eyes glow red along with the hobgoblin face carved into the necklace, and it tells the party that they have 5 questions. It seems like the necklace preserves the corpse and allows *Speak With Dead* to be cast on it automatically.
- The party decides on which questions to ask the Marquis.

#### Speak With Dead Marquis

1. What were the last 5 question you were asked?
	1. How do I find [[Zrandovorski]]?
	2. What is the best way to draw him out based on what you know?
	3. Why is [[Zrandovorski]] still alive?
	4. Who of [[Zrandovorski]]'s family is most likely to still be alive?
	5. Where is [[Zrandovorski]]'s family crest now to the best of your knowledge?
2. How did you die?
	- [[Kaz the Cold]] found out that the Marquis was a secret informant for [[Utopia]] and he used the necklace to keep him as an informant for [[The Frozen March]].
3. Who are the other [[Utopia]] informants that you're aware of?
	- As far as he's aware, he's the only one. But [[Utopia]] was very tight-lipped about who they trusted and who they didn't. So while he was never directly told that there were or weren't others, he's pretty sure he wasn't the only one. He does believe he's the highest profile informant, though.
4. How did you inform [[Utopia]] about the goings on in [[The Frozen March]]?
	- He had a specific contact who would come out on cart and meet him at The Marquis Tree. The informant on the cart would then leave back to the wall and head into [[Utopia]].
5. Where is [[Zrandovorski]]'s family crest now to the best of your knowledge?
	- It, and many other crests of notable Hobgoblin families, are on display in the rebuilt castle in [[Zrandovorski]]'s hometown.
