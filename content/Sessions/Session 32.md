## Notes

### [[NG]] Wants a Training Arc

- The [[Polaris]] moves into the docks of [[Kota-Ken]], a bustling port town, with tons of ships already docked. There's a large shipwright's building with people moving in and out. There are a couple of gryphons flying out of the city with people riding them, and people sitting on the docks drinking from bottles and generally milling about.
- There are ships from all over; there's a couple Utopian vessels, and many, many more flags they don't recognize. As they dock, some Orcish workers approach and ask if they need any repairs, which they decline. They tell them it's 10G/day that they're docked to be paid to the portmaster when they leave, and they tie down the ship with an arcane lock.
- They ask the Orcish workers a variety of questions about [[Kota-Ken]] (sorry team, this part here is my weakest notes section):
	- [[The Collector]] asks about anything happening around town, and they haven't heard of much down at the docks, but lots of adventurers come in and out and there's a bar, [[The Golden Goose]] where they could find quests.
	- [[The Collector]] also asks about [[The Golden Gauntlet]]. They're said to be 'unkillable and indestructable'. They don't have any particular political leaning, they're mainly known for being heroic. They operate out of one of the large towers in the city.
	- [[Tilly]] asks if there's anywhere to buy airship fuel, and they say there aren't any sellers they know in the city, but there are certainly plenty of boats with airship fuel. The ships travel all over the place, to places like [[Sygil]], The City of Doors, and there may be fuel there.
	- There's also the entrance to [[The Underdark]], which you need special access for, like a ticket, to get down for the day.
- First, they head to the shipwright's building to pay for their docking. They enter and there's a man, tall in a sailor's suit with a bowtie on one arm and a nice curly mustache. This is the Head shipwright, [[Zip]]. [[NG]] asks what exactly a shipwright does, and he says his role is primarily interior redesign of adventurer's ships, since most come 'acquired'. 
- [[NG]] requests to be taught his shipwright-ing ways, and [[Zip]] says he could teach him but it would take a month to master the skills. Their current time crunch on [[Restore The Factory|restoring the factory]] prevents him from accepting the offer now, but [[NG]] assures him that he will be back to learn his ways.
- [[Tilly]] asks again about airship fuel, and he says he knows no sellers but there are definitely a lot of people WITH airship fuel. There's a possibility some of them may have extra, or some could be acquired through less savory means, but [[Tilly]] turns down that option for now since it would make [[Garrison]] sad.
- [[The Collector]] asks where they get people from, mentioning all the unkown flags he spotted when they arrived. [[Zip]] says they come from all over the place, with a few even being from other continents like Aen and Glazobaen.

### Gonna Be, Gonna Be Golden (Gauntlet Initiates)

- They make their way into town, deciding to head over to [[The Golden Gauntlet]]'s tower to investigate the guild and here what they're deal is in person. As they walk, [[Kolvar]] plays peek-a-boo with [[The Baby|Baby Null]] by changing his face every time he reappears.
- They arrive at [[The Golden Gauntlet]]'s tower, where two soldiers are standing guard at the door. They're wearing leather armor, with golden palisades on their shoulders and golden helmets that look like a crown at the top with their hair sticking up as well.
- As they approach [[The Collector]] introduces The Reconstellators and ask the guards ask if they've come to join up, and they specify that they'll need some more details before enlisting in anythings
- The guards explain [[The Golden Gauntlet]] to them; they say a lot of the same that everyone else has, that they're a heroic guild with no political leanings. They weren't always the gryphon-riding heroes of today, and the group itself has existed before under other names where there actions were...less renown-worthy.
- [[NG]] confirms that they are hiring, and the guards say that they always are, since it's a dangerous line of work. They also learn that being based in the tower is advantageous to have a high point to take off with the gryphons since they don't take off particularly well. They actually don't wear plate so they're light enough to ride the gryphons.
- They're having a mixer in a few days (the 22nd of Cold's Dusk), and invites them to mingle and give them a chance to see if they'd like to join (and to get a read on The Reconstellators to see if they're a good fit). When they have outsiders looking for a spot, there tends to be a ~~hazing~~ trial that they'd participate in before joining, some minor mission to prove their skills.
- Before they leave [[Kolvar]] makes an honest attempt to find more airship fuel and a daycare for [[The Baby]], but the former is met with "well, we ride gryphons", and the latter making [[Kolvar]] sound like a predator, so they decide to head out.
- The guards giving them directions to [[The Golden Goose]] just down the road since they don't have a map (we should probably get one of those). As they leave, they see the tower up close: it appears to be solid black stone with flhouecks of gold at places where it's taken damage.

### What's a Soda?

- The doors swing open to [[The Golden Goose]], the local bar. There's a large nest in the center of the room with big goldens eggs in it. The whole tavern is oddly shaped, larger at the entrance and narrows at the bar (if you had a birdseye view it would be shaped like a bird). There's a fireplace with crossed swords above it on the far side and lots of adventurers and pirates and merchants talking all around.
- In the back there is a Hill Giant, Greg, that comes out dressed in chef's garb, calling for someone named Larry, and a gnome pops out bringing more ingredients to the chef. He pulls a tiny staircase and climbs up to the bar, welcoming The Reconstellators.
- Asking what they need, [[Kolvar]] asks for a corner booth, but they're all currently occupied by adventurers, so they order some drinks at the bar while they wait. He asks if they want alcohol or soda, and after some discussion on what exactly a soda is, they all decide to order one. 
- The gnome pushes a button on the wall and a door slides open, revealing a goddamn soda dispenser with glowing neon and a sign saying 'FIzzy's' that looks kinda like the [Lick's logo](https://licksburgers.ca/). There are 5 flavours: Orange Creamsicle, Butterscotch, Blueberry, Pineapple or Goodberry Punch.
	- [[The Collector]] gets a pineapple and says it's the best pineapple he's ever had, like summer in a bottle.
	- [[Kolvar]] gets a blueberry and is appropriately whelmed.
	- [[NG]] gets the most and least popular flavor: Goodberry Punch and ...Soda Water. It is unclear if this is even a flavour they regularly sell, but it's sold to him anyway. He thinks the soda is great and asks Larry if they've ever sold hard versions, but he informs them they've actually been explicitely told they can't do that. [[NG]] orders a shot of vodka to mix in his sodas as well.
	- Inspired by [[NG]]'s quick thinking, [[Tilly]] gets an orange creamsicle soda and a shot of vodka.
- As soon as everyone arrives to the now available corner booth, [[NG]] immediately heads off to the quest board to find ~~something to kill the time until the mixer~~ an epic quest for them to take on while in [[Kota-Ken]]. 
	- [[Destroy the Swamp Mummies|Swamp mummies killing adventurers and merchants on the Southern Isle]] - 3rd Party quest from a wealthy merchant in town offering 1000 GP.
	- A couple of quests from the Gnomish Industrial Complex that say the same thing - [[Dispose of Natives for the Gnomish Industrial Complex|There are native intruders on the edge of town that are preventing expansion. Deal with them and get paid.]] 500GP per native tribe, there are 3 tribes.
	- An all hands on deck request from [[The Golden Gauntlet]]: [[Remove The Kaiju Nest|10,000GP for the removal of a kaiju nest.]]
- Before they completely settle in their corner booth [[The Collector]] asks Larry if he knows of a daycare nearby. His kids are actually at one nearby named [[Tyke Tycoon]], so they should be good to leave [[The Baby]] there while they're in the city.
- The Reconstellators debate what they should do. Everyone's in agreement that they want to go to [[The Golden Gauntlet]] mixer, so they have to decide on which quest to take for the next 3 days.
- They decide to pursue the [[Destroy the Swamp Mummies|swamp mummy mission]], and head over to Tyke Tycoons to drop [[The Baby]] off. [[The Collector]] confirms its safety (seems nice, has a little playplace) and they leave [[The Baby|Baby Null]] behind (5GP/day).

### Maximillian's Cooler Cousin

- They head over to the merchant's estate, and The Reconstellators are guided int, heading towards the study. They see a human man with a bald spot sitting in a velvet chair with a Hugh Heffner robe on. With a glass of whiskey in one hand and the overconfidence of wealth in the other, he introduces himself as [[Rexamillian One-Billion]], but they can call him Rex (they willl not).
- [[The Collector]] introduces them as The Reconstellators, and [[Rexamillian One-Billion]] explains the situation: they're losing a lot of resources in their trade with [[Weavewind]] because of this recent mummy problem, and it's already hard enough to trade with the mycellium infection on the isle. At least the mushrooms are easy to dodge as long as you don't to their nests, the mummies not so much. [[NG]] asks where the mummies are, and they're about 4 hexes away (one above tree chopping icon).
- They confirm if the route is safe, and [[Rexamillian One-Billion]] confirms that most of the way will be clear as the issue starts closer to the tree line, once you enter the swamp region to the west.
- Some of his merchants found a hole along their travels and and when they dropped a torch down there they heard a visceral mummy scream at the bottom, so they suspect the mummies are coming from there. [[Rexamillian One-Billion]] believes that if they can kill whatever is in that hole, the mummy problem will likely stop.
- [[NG]] and [[Rexamillian One-Billion]] bond over their love of whiskey as The Reconstellators head out for the Mummy Hole, briefly stopping by [[The Golden Gauntlet]] to see if the gryphons offer Uber services, but are denied.


### It's Amazing There Wasn't A Shrek Joke Here

- They head towards the Mummy's Hole at a fast pace to make it there with enough time to return for the mixer. Most of the crew walks in front including [[Garrison]], [[Kolvar]], [[NG]] and [[Son]] while [[Tilly]] and [[The Collector]] walk behind.
- In their hurried state they've already hit the swampy area, but are not paying close enough attention as they slosh through the rising water, gettting to  ~~Collector drowining level~~ knee-high level. The water around them begins to ripple, and arms tear out of the murky depths, grabbing at the group in front and causing the party to roll initiative!

#### Round 1

- As soon as they start, [[NG]], [[Kolvar]] and [[Garrison]], and [[Son]] make dexterity throws and fail as undead creatures crawl out of the water, the swamp mummies, clawing at them and grappling all of them except for [[Son]]. They're clothes are loose scraps with a few beads hanging off their skeletal forms. They have some sort of purple energy pulsating in the center of their chests.
- We start with [[Son]], who uses the *Help* action to hold up one of the mummy's arms, indicating to everyone while invisible exactly where they should attack. The next attack against that mummy has advantage. 
- His father immediately takes advantage of that as [[The Collector]] is next. He backs up to be 15 feet away from the group and fires 2 *Eldritch Blasts* at the mummy, hitting both times, but not killing the creature.
- [[Tilly]]'s up next, attempting to cast *Guiding Bolt* on the skeleton holding [[Garrison]], but misses. She uses her BA to activate *Starry Form - Archer* and hits, dealing 8 radiant damage and moving backing up 15 feet as well, but still 10 feet from [[The Collector]].
- [[Kolvar]] uses a *Misty Step* to escape the skeleton's grasp, then attacks the skeleton with his blade, hitting both times but not killing the undead.
- [[Garrison]] is up next, swinging at the skeleton grappling him, hitting him for 14 and 16 slashing damage, which was just enough to drop the skeleton. As he slices it in half the purple energy spills out of the bones before they drop, floating harmlessly in the water.
- It is now the one of the mummies' turns, with the only one not grappling someone heading towards [[Tilly]], having no trouble moving the water. It misses the first attack but hits with a 16, dealing 9 bludgeoning damage and 11 necrotic damage, and cursing [[Tilly]]. It's torso turns 180 degrees without moving its legs and stares down [[The Collector]], causing him to make a wisdom saving throw. He passes with an 18, but briefly saw a vision of the mummy that shook him to his core.
- [[NG]] is next, casting *Levitate* on the mummy grappling him, and it fails. [[NG]] *Inky Steps* away as the mummy harmlessly floats in the air above them with nowhere to go. He walks out of the swamp with the remainder of his movement to end his turn.
- Now it is more mummy's turns, with the floating mummy attempting to use *Dreadful Glare* on [[NG]], but he passes, avoiding being frightened.
- The one previously grappling [[Kolvar]] swings back, hitting with a 15 and dealing 12 bludgeoning damage and 9 points of necrotic damage, also cursing [[Kolvar]]. The mummy decides to maximize its effectiveness by moving away to hit [[Garrison]], and [[Kolvar]] unfortunately misses his opportunity attack. It misses its attack on [[Garrison]] and makes a *Dreadful Glare* attempt at [[Kolvar]], but he also passes.

#### Round 2

- Back to [[Son]], he says "my queen!" and flies over to [[Tilly]], using the *Help* action to grab the mummy, giving the next attack advantage.
- [[The Collector]] comes to the lady's aid, firing off 2 Eldritch Blasts at the mummy attacking [[Tilly]], dealing 9 force damage on the first and 12 on the second. dropping another undead. [[The Collector]] attempts to use his *Healing Light* on [[Tilly]], but it does nothing.
- [[Tilly]] is up next and she does not feel good; it feels like her arm is getting heavier at the spot where she was hit and it's getting more difficult to breathe and move. She casts *Moonbeam* and manages to have the orbital laser line up with the floating mummy and [[Kolvar]]'s mummy, killing the landlocked one.
- This would end combat, but [[NG]] makes a wonderful suggestion to attempt putting [[The Lady of the White]] card on the undead creature to see what happens, since it is a body.
- [[Tilly]] places the card on the floating mummy and it contorts into its usual mouth communication form, but they can't here much of anything. Some garbled speech comes in but it's cut off, like it's out of range, and the card returns to normal without doing anything.
- They kill the last mummy and take a quick short rest just outside the swamp. They decide to power forward and venture through the night, so they'll be arriving at the Mummy Hole during the night.

### Entering The Mummy's Hole

- They approach the hole, and the area surrounding it looks dead, like all the life has been sucked away. In the nighttime it's hard for most of the party to see, but [[NG]] and [[The Collector]] see the bottom of the hole and it's not dirt. It appears to be some sort of tile or granite with pillars on the sides. There is most definitely some sort of structure down there.
- Naturally, they send [[Son]] down to be "killed by whatever's down there". As [[Son]] enters the structure at the bottom of the hole, he enters a room and torches burst to life around him. It appears to be an ancient tomb built of ceramic and tile with a short highway leading to what looks like a spiral ramp going down deeper.
- [[Son]] can't see anyone in this room, but his skin is crawling: he can tell he's in a place that seems to actively reject what he is. There doesn't appear to be a way to get The Reconstellators down in the room, so [[NG]] ties off a rope and they lower themselves into the tomb.
- While [[Son]] feels bad in here, [[Garrison]] feels right at home. It seems to be some sort of holding ground, but different from a crypt where you'd find undead. The presence of mummies does indicate a certain level of holiness about the place.
- [[Garrison]] has previously heard of a handful of forgotten relic-storage sites from an ancient Celestial race. Most people don't know where they went, but those that claim to find these holy sites can never seem to find it again upon returning. [[Garrison]] thinks this could be one of those sites, and at the mention of relics [[The Collector]] is immediately intrigued.
- [[Garrison]] can sense the holy relic below, so The Reconstellators head down the ramp, delving deeper into the tomb. The session ends with them finding themselves in another room, a floatin skeletal form hovering in front of them.

The party earns 600 XP, for a total of 10,920 XP!
