## Notes

### A Hop, a Timeskip, and a Jump!

Due to real-life scheduling concerns (you may notice only 3 full-time party members are here), this session is a bit of a timeskip! This is when the party would leave [[Landon's Lamp]].

- All of the civilians that the party carried out of [[The Frozen March]] on [[Polaris]] get off at [[Landon's Lamp]], leaving the party for good.
- One civilian stays behind, [[Big Bill]]. He's a 2-foot tall halfling carrying a 12-foot tall bill hook, and he's thirsting for adventure!
- The party debates where to go next, deciding that this is an opportune moment to go investigate [[The Scar of the Plane]]. They plan a 2.333-day route off the eastern mountain range into the ocean, which is then a 15-day sail to a place near some kind of settlement (with 4 towers?) in the desert near [[The Scar of the Plane|The Scar]].
- On the first day of their journey, [[Son]] sees a man crying for help in the mountains. [[NG]] stops the ship and uses a rope with a bolt and tackle to hoist him up.
- The man is thankful that he has been rescued. [[The Collector]] tries to ascertain *what* he is, but he is so malnourished that it's actually not clear whether he's human or not.
	- He attempts to use *Healing Light* on the man, but oddly enough it doesn't appear to work.
	- [[Big Bill]] offers him a bowl of porridge with some fruit in it, and he eats a spoonful and thanks the party.
- He says he escaped from *"the [[Barony of Bison LeManso]]"*. [[The Collector]] has heard of this before, and asks the man what he was doing there. He replies that he was an indentured servant.
	- Supposedly, the [[Barony of Bison LeManso]] are a group in [[The Scar of the Plane]] who farm chaos magic from the fumes emanating from.
	- [[The Collector]] asks his name, and the man admits it's been a while since he last heard it. But his name is [[Mike Spon]].
- [[NG]] asks what we can do for this man, and [[Mike Spon]] immediately clams up and stops talking. After a moment, he quielty says he wants to go back to the [[Barony of Bison LeManso]].
	- [[Zrandovorski]] is suspicious, and casts *Detect Magic*. This reveals that he is affected by Abjuration magic.
	- [[The Collector]] casts *Remove Curse* on him, and the spell fails. Whatever's going on with him isn't a curse.
- [[NG]] moves him to the barracks, leaving him alone while the rest of the party remains on the top deck. [[NG]] points to [[Mike Spon]] at the bottom of the stairs and casts *Message*, saying "It's safe to talk here, only I can hear you".
	- He hears [[Mike Spon]] again say *"Please take me back to the [[Barony of Bison LeManso]]."* No dice.
- [[NG]] has one last idea, and puts an arm around his shoulder to say "Congratulations, you passed the test. I'm actually from the barony."
	- [[Mike Spon]] says... "You are?"
	- He seems confused, and asks "Is your enchantment going to disappear as well?"
	- [[NG]] says "Yes of course. And then we will go to the, uh, place!"
	- [[Mike Spon]] is confused about the "place", but urges that if he does not return to the [[Barony of Bison LeManso]] then he will burst into flames and die.

### Downtime

- [[Zrandovorski]] spends the journey transcribing spell scrolls.
	- He borrows [[NG]]'s *Invisibility* (lvl 2) and takes the *Water Breathing* (lvl 3) scroll from the [[Bag of Holding]]. This is a total of `(2 + 3) * 2 = 10` hours of transcribing and costs `(2 + 3) * 50 = 250` GP of materials.
- [[The Collector]] pulls out one of the heart-in-a-jars (specifically the one from [[Session 6#Heart to Heart|Session 6]]) from the stockhold and removes the heart to cast *Identify*.
	- The heart enlarges as it beats harder and harder as [[The Collector]] loses feeling in his arm. Then his chest. Then all of his body.
	- He drops to the ground, and begins making death saving throws. The DM asks the party to roll initiative. What the fuck is happening here?

#### Downedtime

##### Round 1

- [[NG]] hears some nefarious shit going on from the stockhold and watches the engorged heart grow in size on top of [[The Collector]] lifeless body as a large demon vulture-like creature tears out of the heart and unleashes a terrible screech. It appears uncomfortable to be below decks; anxious that it can't see the sky above it. Is it... trying to go somewhere?
	- [[NG]] attempts to throw a net at the creature. It fails the DC 13 Dex save (narrwoly missing with a 12!) and is now restrained by the net.
	- [[NG]] follows that up by closing the door and casts *Healing Word* on [[The Collector]], healing him for 9 HP. The word is "Get up!"
- [[Zrandovorski]] hits the creature with a 26 using *Chromatic Orb* (thanks to the advantage from the creature being restrained by the net) and deals 25 thunder damage thanks to casting it at 3rd level. He then backs up 20 feet.
- [[The Collector]] is alive now thanks to [[NG]] healing. He also uses *Healing Light* to restore 12 more HP. He casts *Dissonant Whispers* and the creature barely passes with a 15, the exact DC.
	- The creature screeches again, and [[The Collector]] hears *"YOU HAVE FREED ME FROM MY CAGE. NOW I WILL RETURN HOME TO MY MASTER."*
	- [[The Collector]] asks who its master is, and the creature does not respond. He attempts to flee from the creature since he knows it's restrained, and takes an opportunity attack.
	- It uses its *Shred* attack, despite lacking a guitar, and hits [[The Collector]] with a 14, despite the disadvantage, to deal 11 piercing damage and 11 poison damage.
	- [[The Collector]] casts *Hellish Rebuke* and the creature fails with an 8 on the Dex save thanks to the disadvantage offered by the net, and takes ~~25~~ 12 fire damage. [[The Collector]] took enough damage to go back down, unfortunately.
- Now it's the creature's real turn. It uses its *Stunning Screech* ability and everyone within a 20 foot radius ([[NG]], [[The Collector]], and [[Son]]) must make a Con save or be stunned. [[NG]] and [[Son]] fail the save, while [[The Collector]] (thankfully) passes.
- [[Big Bill]] waddles into the room and uses telekinesis(!) to drag [[The Collector]] out of the room to relative safety. He then closes the door behind him, leaving only himself and [[Son]] in the room with the creature.
- [[Son]] can't do anything as he's both stunned and a familiar.
- [[Mike Spon]] takes one look at what's happening and retreats upstairs.

##### Round 2

- [[NG]] stammers *"I... I... Idea..."* and stumbles twards the stairs that [[Mike Spon]] went up.
- [[Zrandovorski]], not really having much else to do and needing to kill the creature, casts *Chromatic Orb* at 3rd level again to deal 19 more thunder damage. He leaves the door open and backs up 10 feet.
- [[The Collector]] fails a death save. 0S/1F
- The creature breaks out of the neck by attacking it with a spiked tail. It hits [[Big Bill]] for 10 piercing damage and 11 poison damage. It runs past [[Big Bill]] and [[Zrandovorski]], taking opportunity attacks from both of them. It takes 8 damage from [[Big Bill]] and [[Zrandovorski]] misses. The creature moves 20 feet past the door to head back up to the top deck.
- Speaking of which, [[Big Bill]]'s turn! He hits the cretaure with his glaive and deals a whopping 30 slashing damage and 4 force damage. The creature is still alive. [[Big Bill]] finishes off his turn by using *Second Wind* to heal himself.
- [[Son]] attempts to stabilize [[The Collector]] by succeeding on the normal DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine) check. Unfortunately he rolls an 8, ending his turn.
- [[Mike Spon]] does... something on the top deck. None of the party can see him!

##### Round 3

- [[NG]] teleports 15 feet straight up to the top deck. He sees [[Mike Spon]] throwing a rope overboard to escape the ship and swim. [[NG]] makes it to the steering wheel and brings [[Polaris]] 100 feet up, out of the water. He is then able to cast *Faerie Fire* on the creature since it's the first time it's not been within 5 feet of an ally. The creature narrowly fails the DC 14 Dex save with a 13.
- [[The Collector]] opts to *not* take [[Zrandovorski]]'s *Foretelling Roll* of a 15 and rolls his death save, despite a 5% chance of instantly dying upon rolling a 1. He rolls a 9, which is a failure but not an insatnt death. 0S/2F
- The creature shakes a bunch of spores off of its body, subjecting [[Big Bill]] and [[Zrandovorski]] to poisonous spores. [[Big Bill]] passes with a 23, but [[Zrandovorski]] fails with a 7 and becomes poisoned. He will take 1d10 poison damage at the start of each of his turns. The creature runs towards the top deck more, running by [[Big Bill]] again, and takes an opportunity attack for 15 damage. **This kills the creature!**
	- The creature explodes into *sludge* and seeps through the floorboards.

- [[The Collector]] attempts to take some of the sludge into a vial, but it burns through the bottom.
- [[Big Bill]] puts a wooden coin (that he won from [[Son]] in a game of craps) into the [[Box With a Coin Slot]]. Nothing in particular happens.

### Arrival at The [[LeManso Desert]]

- The Reconstellators arrive at the desert. They see floating stones above the actual [[The Scar of the Plane|scar itself]], which is still far away. [[NG]] is able to identify that the desert here is unnaturally formed. It's as if the landmass was pulled up from the ocean floor.
- [[Mike Spon]] is eager to return to the [[Barony of Bison LeManso]], and he points in the direction of the barony (which is 2.5 days of travel away from their landing point).
- The plan is to land-lock [[Polaris]], walk with [[Mike Spon]] for a day, and then send him to the [[Barony of Bison LeManso|barony]] with an invisible [[Son]] for the remaining day of travel so they can scope it out.
- The party makes Stealth rolls during their walk. Rolls are generally good except for [[NG]] who rolled a 6. There are guard patrolling the desert who spot [[NG]] and [[Big Bill]], so the party's compromised.
	- They aren't openly hostile, but do recognize [[Mike Spon]] without trouble. They take him back, and say "The Baron would want you to come be a guest in his city."
	- The guards introduce themselves as Umbra and Axel.
	- [[The Collector]] doesn't take kindly to a somewhat forceful suggestion, and is resistant to the invitation. The guards make it clear that they think the Baron would want to reward them.
	- The guards ask why the party is here, and [[NG]] successfully lies that they're here to return [[The Baby]] to its mother, who isn't too far away.
	- The guards once again invite The Reconstellators to join them, and suggest that if they want access to [[The Scar of the Plane|The Scar]] the main entrance is through the [[Barony of Bison LeManso|Barony]]. People come here all the time looking for access, but there's lots of resources in the [[Barony of Bison LeManso|Barony]] as well. They list Airship Fuel as one of the things, and The Reconstellators ears perk up in real time.
- The guards escort [[Mike Spon]] and once again invite the party to the city, and they leave.
- The party notes at how frequently the guards suggested they visit the [[Barony of Bison LeManso]]. They feel like sticking to their previous plan and stay a day back from [[Mike Spon]] while allowing [[Son]] to trail them while invisible.

### Night at the Desert

- [[NG]] and [[Big Bill]] take first watch together. [[NG]] is curious if [[Big Bill]]'s parents named him that or if his name is only Bill. He explains that the mercenaries in [[The Frozen March]] called him that and he stuck with it.
- [[Big Bill]] swears a LOT in his explanation of how many larger creatures he's killed in his many travelers. The pair end their watch with a game of craps, with [[NG]] losing.
- [[Zrandovorski]] and [[The Collector]] talk about how hot it is in the desert compared to [[The Frozen March]]. They notice that [[The Scar of the Plane|The Scar]] is glowing a bright red, with fog coming out of it. The floating stones in the air above it begin to crack and float off to other directions. One small chunk comes off into the direction of their party (still miles away, just close enough to notice) and once it gets far enough away from [[The Scar of the Plane|The Scar]] it plummets to the ground.
- The red smoke and fog twists upwards like a vortex and then twists downwards into the [[Barony of Bison LeManso|Barony]]. Very odd.
- [[Zrandovorski]] looks to the stars, and he notices the stars... aren't visible here. Even though the prime moon is a new moon and the secondary moon is a small sliver, they are also not visible. This seems odd.
- The watch ends with [[The Collector]] asking [[Zrandovorski]] if he ever quickly forgets things that happened recently. [[Zrandovorski]] remarks that he may be experiencing dimentia, and he should do some puzzles to keep his mind sharp. He hands him a 12-sided puzzle cube for him "to start with".

### Towards the [[Barony of Bison LeManso|Barony]]

- In the morning, [[The Collector]] summons [[Son]] back to relay what he found.
	- The guards beat [[Mike Spon]]'s ass for "spilling the beans". When they got to the barony, they threw him onto a farm for him to work.
	- There were many farmers working the land, like [[Mike Spon]]. They *ALL* looked very malnourished. The plants that they were farming looked like really big dandelions with actual lion faces on them.
	- There were 4 towers that were wiry, like not fully solid. There was a huge spike in the middle of each tower that would rise and then plummet into the ground (like a pump?)
	- The guards and [[Mike Spon]] didn't really rest at all during their 1.5-day journey besides a quick 10 minute rest.
- [[The Collector]] wonders if [[Mike Spon]] was a vampire spawn. In addition to the whole "not resting" thing, he also recalls him not eating very much and the *Healing Light* didn't work on him.
- The party slowly puts the puzzle pieces together using the following realizations:
	- A malnourished man getting his ass beat and then being immediately sent to work farming, which he *actually performs successfully and does not seem terribly injured*.
	- "Spilling the beans"
	- [[Mike Spon]]'s Abjuration magic around him, and his worry that he'll explode when it wears off, might be a protection against the sun.
	- He also barely ate any real food, and we never saw him sleeping on the [[Polaris]].
	- The guards were so insistent on inviting the party to meet the Baron, even stating he *specifically* likes meeting new travelers. Assuming they are vampires, the Baron could be the main vampire and everyone else is a vampire spawn/thrall created by them.
- The party feels very proud for absolutely 100% correctly guessing what's going on in the [[Barony of Bison LeManso]] without fail, and vows to not visit there. Maybe.

The party earns 500 XP, for a total of 9,930 XP!
