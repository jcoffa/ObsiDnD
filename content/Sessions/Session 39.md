## Notes

### Big Talk

- The Reconstellators, still eating dinner, discuss the recent revelations regarding [[NG]] and his, you know, complicit-ness in mass casualties. [[Kolvar]] compliments him on his' good backstory', and [[Tilly]] immediately walks out, with [[Son]] following her outside.
- She's back inside very quickly, asking [[NG]] how he could be willing to let so many people die due to his actions. The party comes to his defense a bit, with [[Garrison]] mentioning that he's never forgotten any of those people for the past 150 years, and [[NG]] once again admits that he's had a lot more time to get over this tragedy. They also weren't intentional on his part, just a side effect of his actions.
- She judges him a bit for the destruction his actions have wrought on the environment of the Southern Isles, and asks if this was truly the only way. [[NG]], despite this factory being his home and the place he belonged, didn't necessarily fit in with the others working there, so there was no one to call on to assist his plans.
- They ask [[Tilly]] why it bothers her so much, and she admits that back when everything happened in [[Caelia]], she didn't feel all that guilty about the people who died in the crossfire until [[NG]] started mourning their deaths. But if he can get over the deaths of these people, then where is the line? How many deaths are acceptable for the 'greater good'?
- The party attempts to convince the young, sheltered 18-year old that the world is a lot bigger than their individual actions, and you can't go about life living with regrets about the consequences of your actions. She's still unsure, but [[NG]] assures her that, if it will make her feel better, he can tell her about all the people who died so their memory will live on, and she says that would be nice.
- They get ready for bed, with [[NG]] taking the first watch alone, placing an *Alarm* on the door. While he lingers in this familiar place, he hears a voice outside, something like a whisper on the wind. He doesn't understand the words, but he gets the sense they're calling for him specifically. [[NG]] decides to head out, casting *Pass Without Trace* on himself and slipping out without triggering the alarm.
- The whispers seem to be coming from the edge of the hallowed clearing, and as he walks forward, he sees what looks like a metal child sitting on a stump, and she says something that doesn't align with her mouth movements, causing [[NG]] to make a wisdom saving throw. He passes and blinks, and the girl is gone.
- [[NG]] heads back to the cabin, spending the rest of the evening feeling on edge, as if something is off. He wakes up the second watch and informs them he saw a little girl, but she was gone. [[Garrison]] scolds [[NG]] for going out into the woods alone, but he assures him that everything is fine before heading to bed, leaving everyone else on watch.
- There's still a sense of unease in the air as [[The Collector]] asks [[Garrison]] how he feels about [[NG]]'s revelations, and he's still processing (heh). It's one thing for the rest of The Reconstellators to find out right now, but he and [[NG]] have been travelling together for decades.
- [[The Collector]] asks [[Kolvar]] and [[Tilly]] what they would have done in [[NG]]'s position, because he feels strongly he would not have done any different. [[Kolvar]] agrees that he would probably do the same; the part he doesn't like is [[NG]] saying that now, even knowing the unintended consequences, he would do it again. 
- He assures [[Tilly]] that what happened in [[Caelia]] is different than what happened here because those deaths were unintentional, but [[Tilly]] points out the deaths here were unintentional too.
- [[Tilly]] wonders if [[NG]] really did explore all of his options, since there had to be other inventors at [[The Factory]] who didn't like the sudden military shift and may have assisted him. But others point out that this is easier said than done; with [[Utopia]] being a growing military force, it might have been hard to take a stand even if they felt that it was wrong.
- The conversation suddenly shifts as [[Garrison]] asks [[The Collector]] what his greatest desire is. [[The Collector]] is a bit confused and questions why he's asking that, but everyone else is confused as to why he's being so sketchy about not replying. He eventually answers that it's to build his museum, and the rest of the watch passes with a lot of confusion.

### "You Are Not The Doppelganger"
- The sun rises on their long rest, and The Reconstellators awaken, feeling no less uneasy than the night before. They debate heading over to the office, since there aren't any spores there, but everyone is a little hesitant to leave. Something feels... off. 
- [[Garrison]] insists that they should do their "usual morning ritual" of showing their silk bug rocks to each other, and everyone has theirs except [[Son]] who ate his frame one. [[NG]] blurts out that he's getting a really sketchy vibe coming from everyone, and everyone agrees, they just don't know what to do about it.
- [[Tilly]] suspiciously pulls [[The Collector]] off to the side and casts *Dispel Magic* on him, then asks how he feels, but he says he's still uneasy, which seems to confuse her. 
- She admits to the party that she did feel a bit uneasy about everyone the night before, but now she suspects that something is making them feel this way. Everyone has their rocks, so it's not as though a doppelganger replaces them.
- Since there's not much they can do about it right now, the party decide to head off to the office. [[Garrison]] waits outside to avoid any chance of getting hit with [[Copper Rot]], so he sends in [[Furret]] instead, watching on through its eyes.
- They head into the office with [[NG]] leading the way. There's a messy desk with papers and knick-knacks and a small cot where the foreman likely rested.
- [[The Collector]] takes a look around with 1 charge of help from [[Son]], investigating the desk. He feels a metal piece shift in the drawer. He looks down and sees a small lock appear in the floor.
- [[NG]] uses his Manifold tools to pick the lock, and it takes a bit, but he hits the sweet spot and now a safe in the floor is fully visible, built into the building. [[NG]] opens it to find a wax-sealed letter, and what looks to be a metal circular band with a green gem sitting at its center, and silver flourishes along the sides. The symbol on the letter looks similar to [[NG]]'s maker mark, but with many more flourishes and embellishments. 
- [[NG]] identifies the metal circlet, discovering... something, but instead redirects their attention to the letter for the time being. It's a Bill of Sale, or an Invoice of some kind. It is sealed by the signet of a high ranking member of the factory to [[The Grand Excelcium]], for 150 warforged units, and 200 automatons, and something named the [[Circlet of Control]], signed by one of the members of their council. It is dated about 170 years ago, and before [[The Grand Excelcium]] came to power, but their title is on here.
- Based on the document, it appears to be the business dealing confirming [[The Factory]]'s connection to [[Utopia]], but it's weird that the [[Circlet of Control]] is still here, and the [[The Grand Excelcium]] never got the robots they ordered. The order was for the sum of 25,000 PP.
- At this point, [[NG]] confirms that the metal band is the [[Circlet of Control]]: While attuned to this circlet, the wearer can use a magic action to attempt to dominate a robot, automaton or warforged. They must make a DC 15 intelligence save or be dominated by the wearer. They may make a new save once each dawn. There is an additional magic item that could amplify the effectiveness of the circlet.
- [[The Collector]] investigates the names of those who signed the document and finds one name of note, [[Natasha The Dark]]. No one recognizes the person's name.
- They continue scouring the office for PPE for [[Garrison]] but can't find any (yeah, I guess if they were prepared for this it wouldn't have worked). [[Tilly]] looks through the interior windows and sees a light fog or mist along the ground of the abandoned shop floor, and some entities lurching forward. The faint sounds of metal against stone is heard as they move unpredictably and erratically through [[The Factory]], seemingly reacting to any small noise or movement.
- As they're talking, [[Furret]] starts scratching at the door and heads outside, with everyone following and [[NG]] immediately teleporting to [[Garrison]] and asking what's up. He points in the direction of their house but there's nothing there.
- He immediately heads over to the house and brings back some green powder, saying he saw a creature spreading this over the roof of the house and staring at him hungrily. As soon as he brings the powder over everyone makes a CON save, and [[Kolvar]] and [[The Collector]] are both struck with the OVERWHELMING feeling that one of the party members are going to stab them imminently.
- [[Tilly]] looks around and notices a path of the green powder leading into the woods. [[Garrison]] insists that whatever this creatue is doing is causing this paranoia and they need to follow, but the pair of warlocks don't trust anyone, backing away from the group.
- [[NG]] pulls out his dagger and insists that HE is the one that is going to stab him and nobody else, and his intimidating stature leads them to buy it, so the pair agree to follow the party into the woods as long as they can walk behind [[NG]].

### Nilbogism

- The Reconstellators walk for about an hour, following the green powder until it suddenly stops in a little clearing. There are some muddy footprints leading to the middle of the clearing, that then disappear, and [[Kolvar]] notices a faint warble of light in the middle of the space.
- [[Tilly]] waves her hand in front of her and feels something close on her finger as a large figure of tentacles with a giant eye appears in front of her, with the green creature from [[Garrison]]'s description sitting on its head. Everyone roll intiative!

#### Round 1
- The Reconstellators recognize the tentacle creature from [[The Frozen March]] as a [[Mind Witness]], the Mind Flayer Beholder thing they dodged previously. Oops!
- [[Tilly]]'s up first, casting *Thunderwave* at third level to try to get the creatures out of range, but they both pass. She deals ~~22~~ 11 thunder damage, then backs away 30 feet, triggering an opportunity attack from the Witness. She uses her new *Cosmic Omen* ability to reduce it's attack roll by 4, causing it to miss with a 3.
- [[NG]] is up next, casting *Faerie Fire* on the Mind Witness and green creature, and both fail, leaving them outlined in an inky glow. He heads over to [[Garrison]] and ends his turn.
- [[Son]] flies over to the [[Mind Witness]], performing the *Help* action by grabbing its eyelid and shaking it.
- The green creature, later confirmed to be a [Nilbog](https://5e.scrunko.monster/bestiary.html#nilbog_mpmm), asks which of them looks the biggest and points at [[Garrison]], who fails with a 6 on a WIS save against *Tasha's Hideous Laugther* and suddenly everything is fucking HILARIOUS, and he collapses to ground prone and incapacitated. He makes another saving throw at the end of his turn and fails, heading into another fit of laughter at [[Kolvar]]'s shapeshifting shenanigans.
- It's the [[Mind Witness]]' turn now, causing a variety of saving throws through the party. [[Tilly]] briefly feels frightened and [[Kolvar]] feels lethargic but the feeling passes. [[The Collector]], however, is lifted into the air by a beam, flying up 30 ft. and being restrained until the start of its next turn.
- [[The Collector]] casts both *Eldritch Blasts* at the [[Mind Witness|witness]], rolling flat thanks to [[Son]] and Faerie Fire, but both miss, so he remains restrained above the combat.
- [[Kolvar]]'s up next, charging in with his 2 attacks and critting! He deals 18 slashing damage and 44 force for 66 total damage thanks to a Warlock's Smite, declaring that he's gonna stab the [[Mind Witness]] before it can stab him. The blade enters its eye as the force damage surrounds his arm, tunneling a hole into the creature's head as it drops dead. [[Kolvar]] goes to activate some secondary Warlock effect to attack the Nilbog, but falls under the effect of *Nilbogism* before he can strike and he is charmed to say nothing but compliments for the next 6 seconds (aka until the end of his next turn. 
#### Round 2
- Back to [[Tilly]]'s turn, she attempts to attack the Nilbog but also falls under the effect of *Nilbogism*, backhand complimenting the creature before ending her turn.
- [[NG]] is up again, attempting to throw a net at the Nilbog, and despite the wills of fate from [[Tilly]], he rolls a nat 20 and passes the DEX save, avoiding the net.
- [[Son]] is up next and, unsure of what to do next, yells *Ooga Booga*, succeeding in frightening the creature as it remarks that he is genuinely terrifying.
- The Nilbog, seeing the writing on the wall of a 5v1 and shaking in his boots thanks to [[Son]] decides to flee, dashing 90 feet away.
- [[Garrison]] is up next. After some debate about whether being knocked prone caused him to fall off his mount (TLDR he has to make a DEX save to see if he falls, he chooses to fail), [[Furret]] gets a turn in initiative and [[Garrison]] passes his save to stop laughing.
- [[The Collector]] is up next, successfully resisting the pull of *Nilbogism* to upcast *Guiding Bolt* at the Nilbog, which WOULD have dealt 30 radiant damage, if not for it spinning its staff above his head and declaring "heal me instead!", redirecting the holy energy to heal 2HP.
- [[Kolvar]] MOVES to catch up with the creature, moving 30 ft. and *Misty Step*ping next to the creature, giving himself 10 temp HP. He continues complimenting the creature until the end of his turn, which drops the effect of *Nilbogism*
- [[Furret]] is up, moving next to [[NG]] to prepare for him to mount on his next turn.

#### Round 3
- Back to [[Tilly]], she rushes forward 30 feet to attempt to catch up to the creature, ending her turn and losing the effects of *Nilbogism*.
- [[NG]] mounts onto [[Furret]] and cuts off the Nilbog's path, throwing a net at it AGAIN but missing. He decides to use the infinite movement glitch that is [[Furret]] to move back over to [[Garrison]] and dismounts, ending his turn.
- [[Son]] dashes forward and moves to cut off the Nilbog's path, who goes "oh my" as he is forced to abandon his current path of escape.
- It's the Nilbog's turn next, who attempts to flee perpendicularly from the party, but [[Kolvar]] attempts to swing at him, dodging *Nilbogism* and hitting for 10 slashing damage. Or it WOULD, but as the sword hits him he chuckles and heals for 6HP. He continues fleeing, this time 60 feet to the right.
- [[Garrison]] is up next, using instant [[Furret]] transportation to charge at him with his lance, successfully dodging *Nibolgism* and striking the creature, dealing 20 damage. [Charging through the trees like vampires playing baseball](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qhDPJ7ozL6s), his lance strikes him and diving energy pulses through him, turning him into dust and ending the combat there.

The party receives 350 XP, for a total of 14,530 XP! Onwards to level 7 at 23,000 XP!
