## Notes

### I Want to Catch a Drowsee

- The Reconstellators reconvene on [[Polaris]] to figure out what to do with the dream eater hairs.
- [[Zrandovorski]] recalls some info about dream eaters. They're a catch-all term for any creature that feeds off the psychic energies from sleep and dreaming. Could be a Mindlfayer, a certain type of Incubus or Succubus, a fae like a Night Hag, even an Intellect Devourer could potentially be classified as a dream easter.
- The party devises a plan to watch over Kolvar and see what happens. Kolvar is going to sleep, and the party is staying up in watches to see what happens to them. The idea is that either *something* happens, or the party finishes a long rest. [[Tilly]] will attempt to *Mind Link* with [[Kolvar]] as they sleep to see if they hear anything in their mind.
- Anyone sleeping must roll a d6. Both [[Zrandovorski]] and [[The Collector]] roll a 1, so they must reroll. [[Zrandovorski]] rolls a 3 and [[The Collector]] rolls another 1.
- [[Garrison]] and [[NG]] ask [[Zrandovorski]] about what it's like to be an oracle. While this is happening, [[The Collector]]'s leitmotif begins playing. He experiences the beginning of a happy memory he has with Father. They're in an ancient tomb and opened up a coffin to ancient artifacts and treasure. Father picks up a golden chalice and shines it, saying "It's the answer to our prayers" and gives it to [[The Collector]] to hold. He asks, "Son, how much do you really remember?" as his eyes flash purple. 
	- "*About what, Father?*", [[The Collector]] asks.
	- He rolls a 15 on a Wisdom save, and he can tell that something is wrong. This isn't how it happened.
	- "*That's an odd question, Father. do you have memories you forget?*", [[The Collector]] asks.
	- "Not that I remember. But you on the other hand... you have things that you don't care to remember. Where did you go to get my ashes? After I died?"
	- "*The volcanoe. It was in your last note to me.*"
	- "Was it?", and Father explodes into dust in front of him.
		- Son I don't feel so good - Sage
	- [[The Collector]] feels himself covered in the ashes that he knows are in his backpack. He hears a chuckle; the sounds of multiple marauders laughing in the distance. And he awakes in the morning.
- [[The Collector]] and [[Kolvar]] wake up and remain exhausted. They also don't restore any of their hit die.
- [[Tilly]] didn't feel anything happening to [[Kolvar]], but she did still sense some kind of presence nearby and it affected [[The Collector]]. It seems that, whatever is causing this, doesn't actually need to be physically present in order to maintain connections and create new ones nearby.
- The party is running out of options and needs to do some research. They plan to head over to [[The Golden Gauntlet]] and maybe the church or library to learn more about this creature.

- She confirms with another employee in the room that forensics confirmed that The Reconstellators did, in fact, actually kill [[Remove The Kaiju Nest|the Kaiju nest]], earning them another 5000 GP.
- [[The Collector]] asks about the new Dream Eater job posting, and [[Quartermaster Jade|Jade]] is surprised the party knows about it a day ahead of it being posted.
	- [[Find and Kill the Dream Eater Haunting Kota-Ken]]
- The party follows up by asking about the attack on [[Tyke Tycoon]] from [[Session 34#The Tyke Tycoon Roof Is On FIRE!!!!|last night]]. [[Quartermaster Jade|Jade]] doesn't know much about it, but finds it suspicious that it just so happened to be on the night of their mixer, when policing would be at its most lax due to most of them watching over the party itself.
- [[NG]] decides to be blunt about the dream eater thing and interviews some soldiers who mentioned they've been feeling particularly tired as of late, around a week at this point. They do feel like the "laughing marauders" is familiar. They've mostly been part of the city watch, posted near the entrance to *The Deep*; the entrance to [[The Underdark]] in/near town. [[NG]] scours the room to try and find more dream eater hairs and anything else suspicious.
	- The fountain has been frozen over and appears to be mid-splash, as if something fell in as the water froze all at once. The railing on the upper balcony is broken and has scuff marks as if something heavy and unstable fell through the railing.

During the mixer, he recalls someone he was talking to and she had kind of weirdly purple eyes [[Session 34#^coin-slot|talking about a "coin slot"]]. [[NG]] lies to him about 1/2-price breakfast across the street to mark that his responses were not particularly restful.

	- [[Garrison]]'s guard slept terribly last night and was otherwise fine in previous nights. Last night, he dreamt of mercenaries or something. He was positioned in [[The Golden Goose]] for the past week. [[Garrison]] tells there's free breakfast across the street.

- [[The Collector]] approaches the golem, who shouts *"WELCOME TO THE LIBRARY! WHAT CAN I HELP YOU WITH?"*, the other patrons flinch. [[The Collector]] asks about books on monsters of [[The Underdark]], and the golem shouts "*RE-CALIBRATING. DOWN THIS HALLWAY!*" as he swings an enormous sword down a hallway.
	- [[Garrison]] asks for books on dreams, and the golem violently swivels around to point down a different hallway.
	- [[The Collector]] asks for materials on Old Gods, and is pointed to the back of the library.
- The readers make Intelligence checks to see how much info they can gather from their books.
	- [[The Collector]] rolls a 1 for his book on Old Gods (butchered due to his exhaustion). He's so tired that he picks a book up on Old *Dogs* instead.
	- [[Garrison]] rolls a 10 for his book on *The Monsters of Sleep*. [[Zrandovorski]] rolls a 15 for his book on *Monsters of [[The Underdark]], Vol 1.*, and they realize they can cross-reference their texts with eachother as they share half the authors. Looks like there's a research group that covers this topic in their published works. There are three possible options this monster could be: Mindflayer, Incubus/Succubus, or "Ancient Demons". All three can technically be found in [[The Underdark]] and are all sleep-related.

### The All-Faith Shrine

- The church looks a bit run-down. The ground floor seems relatively fine, but the upper floors are noticeably less well-kept. There are quite a few people present, around 50% capacity, all doing separate prayers. There's room available to put down faith-specific equipment if needed.
- At the front of the church is a dragonborn woman who appears to be in charge. She's reading out of a book and writing down notes in the margins, putting it away when [[NG]] calls out to her.
- [[NG]] asks if anyone here knows *Greater Restoration*, but it's too high a level for anyone to have it prepared. Maybe someone has a scroll for it, she muses, but otherwise it's too advanced for the people in [[Kota-Ken]].
- [[Tilly]] explains the situation with their dreams while [[NG]] stares *daggers* into the dragonborn. He rolls well on an Insight check and gets a secret message from the DM. [[Tilly]] notices and mind links, [[NG]] urges her to not move her head. He strongly suspects an elf in the corner is the dream eater due to their glowing eyes and he senses the psychic energy.
	- [[Tilly]] links to [[Son]] and relays the info, telling him to go notify [[The Collector]]. He is invisible but wearing a turtleneck so he very obviously runs out the church in a hurry. [[NG]] notices that the elf walks out of the church, and then slumps over shortly after. The party suspects that the dream eater can possess people temporarily and leave their mind at will.
	- *The players giggle that this is likely inspired by Jean Grey from Spider-Man: Brand New Day.*
- [[NG]] goes to talk to the now slumped-over elf, but he is unresponsive. He does some first aid and finds no pulse. He immediately causes a panic as the dragonborn rushes over to cast *Revivify*, but the spell fails. She checks for a pulse and finds one, chastising [[NG]] for not doing it properly. She does confirm that he's alive but in a coma.

### That One Fish Guy from The Boys

- The Reconstellators head over to *The Deep* and talk to the guards about the dream eater situation, and end up confirming that melatonin does, in fact, exist in this world.
- The party do some shopping before heading down, picking up healing potions and a couple scrolls of *Protection from Evil and Good* ([[Zrandovorski]] stashes one in the [[Bag of Holding]] so he can copy it into his spellbook later).
- The party each pays their 20 GP fee to enter *The Deep*, which is basically a giant pit 80-feet in diameter with a staircase that wraps around the inside. There is a small drop at the bottom, which would require a Medium-sized creature to reach upwards and do a pull-up to bring themselves back to the staircase. [[The Collector]] will need some assistance due to his pathetic Gnome stature. The main area is like the top of a stone hill that opens in a cavern 250 feet in any direction before turning into tunnels. The Reconstellators are the only ones around here.
- [[Tilly]] hears two things of note down here. The first is a shrill, guttural screech (as if it's gargling liquid) to the southwest. The second is hooting and hollering in celebration, as if someone won a recent battle, to the north. Suspecting that the gurgling may be the [[Remove the Undead Dragon from The Deep|undead dragon]], the party heads north and hopes that the dream eater would seek out the noise of the cheering for its next meal.
- [[Tilly]] investigates the flora and fauna down here, spying a mushroom similar to the [[Session 9#It Had to Be Spiders...|ones they saw a couple months ago]].
- [[Son]] scouts ahead and gets a nat 20 on his Perception check. He spies a straight-up party happening down here with a crowd of around 30 people (a mix of humans, gnomes, halflings, a couple dwarves) and two other strange-looking creatures. It appeared to be a male and female humanoid with blue skin, floating hair, purple eyes, and blue bat-like wings. They appear to be making a speech in some language that isn't Common.
- [[Tilly]] casts *Pass Without Trace* and the party makes some great Stealth checks thanks to the +10 bonus from the spell (except [[Garrison]] and [[Furret]] who both got a 12). They inch up to hopefully hear what the strange humanoids are saying.
- [[Kolvar]] rolls a 5 for Perception and hears glimpses of what they're saying.
	- They're talking about some kind of party... no wrong translation, more like an *orgy*?
	- Something about how "they've almost got enough people in an insomniac state to do an orgy". [[Kolvar]] is struggling to translate this "orgy" word correctly. It's like... `orgy (nefarious)` but there's not a great translation to Common.
	- [[Kolvar]] and [[The Collector]], the two people who were subjected to the dream eater [[#I Want to Catch a Drowsee|earlier]], make a Wisdom save. They both fail miserably, and become tranced. They start getting excited about what the blue humanoids are saying, and start walking over.
	- [[Garrison]] tries to stop [[The Collector]] from going, and [[NG]] and [[Zrandovorski]] try to hold down [[Kolvar]]. Thankfully, since the two are exhausted, they also pathetically fail their forced checks to escape the grapples.
- [[Tilly]] wonders if the range of the dream eaters are more limited than they seem, and they're specifically camped out in this room because it allows them to target whatever's *above them* on the surface. She takes out a map of [[Kota-Ken]] and successfully makes a new one of this section of [[The Underdark]] and overlays on top of each other. To the party's surprise, their current location is underneath the section of the city that has [[The Golden Goose]], [[The Golden Gauntlet]]'s tower, and [[Tyke Tycoon]].
- After dragging them another 30 feet, they snap out of it. Looks like the party needs to come up with a gameplan. The crowd is around 20 feet into the hallway, and the blue humanoids are on a stone pillar 60 feet into the room.
	- [[Tilly]] suggests she can use *Plant Growth* on the crowd of people to greatly limit their movement around the room, hopefully allowing the party to more safely move around the room.
	- The party is uneasy about this because of how many charmed bystanders there are, and we know nothing about the power-level of the dream eaters. This could be a *very* dangerous fight.

The party receives 210 XP, for a total of 12,730 XP! They're only 1,270 XP away from level 6!
