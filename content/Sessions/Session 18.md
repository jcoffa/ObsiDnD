## Notes

### So What Do We Do With This Baby?

- [[NG]] starts off the day by crafting [[The Baby]] a warm fur coat so that it may survive the cold.
- [[Tilly]] hands [[The Collector]] a Goodberry to crush up and feed to [[The Baby]]. [[Zrandovorski]] is able to cast Detect Magic and confirm [[the baby]] is nonmagical.
- After letting [[Garrison]] and [[Kolvar]] attune to the [[There and Hererings]], the party sets off on day 3/7 to [[Draskhrr]].

### On the Road Again

- Justin rolls a 93 on the d100 random encounter table, and the party must make a Dex save. [[Garrison]] and [[NG]], who have disadvantage since they're in the front, fail.
- The party walks up on what looks to be an abandoned ruined town. [[Garrison]] and [[NG]] fall into a hidden pit trap buried under the snow.
- The klutzy group falls into a pit and rolls 20 feet down a slope to land at the start of an underground portion of the town, all built of stone. It's covered in ice along all of the walls in erratic patterns, as if someone had taken a hose and sprayed it all over the place as the water freezes instantly. In the place they landed there are oval-shaped stone orbs bigger than a human head that are an icy blue.
- [[NG]] is able to teleport back to the group that didn't fall in and relay what they see. [[Zrandovorski]], recalling that there is at least one [[Find The White Dragon's Nest|White Dragon]] that resides in [[The Frozen March]], suspects that they are dragon eggs. They shouldn't dwell in this area for too long.
- [[Kolvar]] uses the [[There and Hererings]] to get [[Garrison]] out of the pit, and then investigates the rest of the space with [[NG]] (who teleports back down). They find a few scattered weapons and armour, but otherwise nothing of note.
- Before they leave the area for good, [[NG]] holds his ear up to one of the "eggs" and taps on it. He hears stirring and movement inside the orb. [[Tilly]] is able to investigate the surround area and determines that there is a large creature routinely moves in and out of this area regularly.
- The party attempts to leave the area with [[Tilly]] casting Pass Without Trace. Unfortunately the party has some truly awful Stealth rolls and only gets 100 feet away from the pit before they hear the wings of a huge creature overhead, and the ground shakes as it lands.
- [[NG]] and [[Kolvar]] aim to potentially distract the dragon with Elementalism and Minor Illusion as the rest of the party runs for cover to hide.
	- [[Kolvar]]'s Minor Illusion is to create the sound of the two of them chanting to cast a spell for 1 minute, then becomes Invisible.
	- [[NG]]'s Elementalism is used to create the scent of the party on the same spot that the noise is coming from.
	- They then leave the area immediately to catch up with the rest of the party.
- The dragon slams into the ground a mere 30 feet from where they were a moment ago, and blasts a devastating breath weapon... at the spot where they cast their spells.
- Through the snowstorm, the party can see the icy blue glowing eyes of this dragon as it lunges over to the pair with its talons outstretched. It manages to catch [[Kolvar]] and turn to [[NG]]. It threatens them with the obvious death, eating them, cutting them in half, and the like.
	- [[NG]] looks the dragon dead in the eyes and explains that they fell into the pit by mistake, and they didn't take anything. The dragon is unconvinced, but [[NG]] warns the dragon that it may want to reconsider its threats, he pulls out the [[Session 13#The Obsidian Dragon|obsidian dragon scale]] and claims "Well, you wouldn't be our first."
	- The dragon chuckles, and throws [[Kolvar]] to the ground before threatening them not to return. It flies away.
- [[NG]] narrowly prevents soiling himself, but he is visibly shaken. And yet, also alive.

### End of Day 3/7

- [[NG]], [[Kolvar]], and [[The Collector]] take first watch. They discuss the plan to [[Overthrow The Iron Fang]] and their uncertainty of its success. [[NG]] in particular is worried that they're "just replacing one tyrant with another" and not really changing anything.
	- In a lighter conversation, they decide what to name [[The Baby]] after much debate and land on "Null" to relate it to the constellation of the void.
- On their watch, [[Garrison]] immediately says that they should come up with a name for [[the baby]]. [[Zrandovorski]] confidently claims that they should name it ***HHGGUUUUKKK*** (like coughing up phlegm) as that is how you say "Snow" in Goblin. The party reluctantly agrees because that's hilarious.

### Baby Null HHGGUUUUKKK In [[Draskhrr]]

- The next morning, both of the watch groups proudly confront the other group with their names, and decide to combine them. And thus [[The Baby|Null HHGGUUUUKKK]] is born. Well, he was born 3-9 months ago, but you get the point.
- The next 4 days of travel are uneventful, save for some discussion about whether the party should switch sides if [[Draskhrr]] has created a city-sized cannon since they've last been in the city.
- While there is no cannon, the military presence has increased *dramatically*. They meet [[Grit Coalbelly]] in [[The Deep Forge]] and get their 25% share of his profits (43 GP each!)
- The Reconstellators ask [[Grit Coalbelly|Grit]] what's up with the increased military presence. He explains that [[The Iron Fang]] has heavily subsidized weaponry at his forge, so there's essentially a 70% discount on weapons and armour. The citizens of [[Draskhrr]] are buying them since they're cheap and [[The Frozen March]] is dangerous. There isn't a definitive and direct order from [[The Iron Fang]] that people must arm themselves; it's just the subsidy.
- [[Tilly]], having since been convinced that [[Kolvar]] is actually a Changeling and not a Doppelganger, grants [[Kolvar]] their own Anti-Doppelganger Bug Rock; a leaf-bug due to its ability to blend in.

### Sleuthing and Stalking

- The Reconstellators all split up to discover more about [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] and [[Kaz the Cold]]'s schedules to help them plan the assassination.
- [[NG]] opts to stay behind and use the Adamantine they got from [[Session 13#Party 'Til You're Pregnant|the Adamantine mines]] to start crafting [[Garrison]] a set of Adamantine Plate Armour, and also a normal Lance (or Halberd?)
- [[Zrandovorski]] relays information about them, since he has lived in [[Draskhrr]] for most of his life.
	- They know that [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] patrols the streets to basically behave like a police captain or warden. He has an aura of authority and intimidation around him that naturally quells criminal activity and people subconsciously act on their best behaviour around him.
	- [[Kaz the Cold]] is much more reclusive. He mostly hangs out in his longhouse and engages in the planning and administrative side of [[The Iron Fang]]. He is the true leader, after all.
- [[Zrandovorski]] rolls a 6 for Stealth and uses his [[Zrandovorski#Foretelling Rolls]] to make sure [[Kaz the Cold]] rolls a 2 on Perception. He also rolls a 1 + 7 = 8 for Investigation and doesn't find much, unfortunately. The only thing that really catches his eye is a map of [[The Frozen March]] which has a series of tally marks on the margins, many of which are crossed out. Only 3 tallies remain unstruck.
- [[Garrison]], aiming to pose as an interested recruit, find a group of [[The Iron Fang|Iron Fang]] members having a picnic. It's a few Bugbears and a Hobgoblin. He straight shoots them and says "If you guys are planning to leave [[The Frozen March]] I want to join that effort." One of the Bugbears confirm that they are planning to leave soon, so he has impeccable timing. [[Garrison]] asks for clarification on when they plan to leave, rolling a 19 Persuasion, and the Bugbear says that [[Kaz the Cold]] and [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] don't have an exact date to give them but they think it's likely this week.
	- The Hobgoblin shoots him a look, and the Bugbear sheepishly dials it back a bit and corrects himself saying that it's "only a likelihood, I don't know exactly. I don't have any more information than that." [[Garrison]] reminds them that if they want him to fight, he needs a precise timeline.
- [[Tilly]] and [[The Collector]] go back to [[The Everburning Chalice]] to try and get a high-ranking official drunk and drill them for information. [[Tilly]] makes a big scene and tells the whole bar that she will pay for a helmet-full of booze for whoever has the largest helmet.  (Un?)fortunately, [[Rykor Steelhand]] is drinking here, and he *definitely* has the largest helmet; he has the large and overbearing physique of a Bugbear but is clearly a Hobgoblin. [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] invites them to sit with him, and after some small talk they engage in something of a drinking contest.
	- After a series of 3 Constitution saving throws (followed by a tiebreaker round), they are unable to out-drink [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]]. None of them are belligerent, but [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] is definitely not any more drunk than the two of them.
	- It's at this time that [[Kolvar]] and Son are able to find [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] at the bar. Shortly after, [[Garrison]] also finds his mark ([[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]]) at the bar. This is turning into a bit of a mess.
	- [[Garrison]], again with the straight shooting, asks [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] directly "One of your commanders told me you plan on leaving soon. I want to leave [[The Frozen March]] just like you. I would be an ally to your cause." [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] raises an eyebrow and seems a bit peeved that his commanders were blabbing about a plan to leave.
	- [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] pulls a feather from his cloak, and hands it to [[Garrison]]. He commands "Booyagh!" and one of his three soldiers touches the feather so it bursts into flame. He says that this is his token to get in and see [[Kaz the Cold]]. [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] says that he knows that a band of do-gooders Druid, a metal man, a gnome, an elf, a rogue, and [[Zrandovorski]] were causing problems when they showed up a month ago.
	- [[The Collector]] successfully lies by pretending to have no fucking clue who or what a "[[Zrandovorski]]" is. [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] claims that he would have at least spoken with "the hobgoblin". [[The Collector]] doubles down and tells [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] that he never even left [[Draskhrr]] with that guy. [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] stands up and says that the two of them are free to leave. He raises his "steelhand" and points at the door. After an unsuccessful attempt to goad [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] into spending the night with her, she speaks into his head to say "what you're doing to the innocent people of [[Draskhrr]] is really fucked up."
		- [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] does not take kindly to that. He tells her that his ancestors were slaughtered and their children sent [[The Frozen March|to hell]]; it's not fucked up that they are fighting for their lives and their future. He sternly tells her to not lecture him on what is good for his people, and essentially kicks them out of [[The Everburning Chalice]].
	- [[The Collector]] notices that [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] had motioned for his guards to block the door to [[The Everburning Chalice]] at the beginning of this entire conversation, but has since motioned for them to leave the door unblocked.
- Right as [[Zrandovorski]] is about to leave the longhouse, he prepares for the Invisibility to drop by first using Disguise Self to make him look like an old golbin woman. He is asked to roll a "comedic d100", and a result of 54 makes the Invisibility drop *right* as he is about to leave which is also when [[Garrison]] is escorted in to meet [[Kaz the Cold]].
- [[Garrison]] says the same thing he said to [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]]; and asks [[Kaz the Cold]] about his plan to leave [[The Frozen March]]. He says they don't accept just anybody, so [[Garrison]] would need to pass some form of initiation ritual. [[Kaz the Cold]] says that [[Garrison]] must find someone who has been reported in the city, a hobgoblin who has been a thorn in their side for a while; [[Zrandovorski]]. He has 24 hours to find him and bring him here. Before he ushers [[Garrison]] out, [[Kaz the Cold]] reaches into a bag and hands him a standard [[The Iron Fang|Iron Fang]] badge. It's not like a symbol of impressive rank, it's just to show that they are part of an alliance with [[The Iron Fang]].

### The Plan

[[Garrison]] fakes handing over [[Zrandovorski]] to [[Kaz the Cold]], making a commotion outside of the longhouse. Potentially involving a staged fight between [[Garrison]] and [[Zrandovorski]] to cause a maximum commotion and draw out [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] and [[Kaz the Cold]]. When they both show up, the party will turn the fight back on them, showing that it was all fake, and *saying something that will make the current leadership's plan not seem a good idea.*

While [[The Iron Fang]] is pinning their public motivation on hope that they can fight their way out and reclaim their ancestral homeland, the party can use [[Zrandovorski]] to help persuade [[Draskhrr]] that [[The Frozen March]] is not hell and life is what you make of it. The idea is to sway the court of public opinion and disarm their goal that they need to fight their way out.

The party gains 650 XP, for a total of 5,300!
