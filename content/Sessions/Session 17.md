### The Party is Going to Die

- The party sees a man standing in the snow, facing away from them. Slouched over, wearing a brown matted cloak, looks to be no taller than 5'5" (manlet af lmao), and seems to be holding something in his hands. He stands directly in the path to [[Draskhrr]], and due to the barren nature of [[The Frozen March]] it would take a full day of walking to go around him without him being able to see.
	- Cameron starts playing some [VERY unsettling music](https://youtu.be/fLERkyTRFi8) at this point.
- [[The Collector]] calls out to the man, and receives no response.
- [[Tilly]] tries to see if there's anything unnatural in the area. She notices that the snow is moving under the creature's feet. Not in like a windy way, like as if there's something moving underneath him. She also notices the same weird scabbing that reminds her of [[Session 11|the cultist from earlier in their journey]].
- [[The Collector]] has Son turn in to a bat and investigate. He keeps a berth around the man while invisible. Son rolls a 7 for Stealth. He sees his entire body is covered in these scabbed-over scratches. His eyebrows have been torn off. His eyes are grey and blank, his teeth rotting. In his arms, he holds an infant. It locks eyes with Son; his invisibility seemingly doing nothing.
- In everyone's heads they hear *"I hunger..."*

### The Party Dies

Music: [False Hydra Combat Music](https://youtu.be/fLERkyTRFi8) (after the initial spooky intro). Cameron assures the party this is not a False Hydra, and he's just using it because the music is awesome.

- [[The Collector]] casts *Eldritch Blast* on the man, saying *"Oi! Stranger!"*, and hits with a 16 to deal 6 force damage. He then casts *Hex* on the man (order lol) and penalizes his future Strength checks.
- [[Zrandovorski]] walks forward 30 ft and casts *Sleep*, using one of his [[Zrandovorski#Foretelling Rolls|foretelling rolls]] to force the result to be a 12 to automatically fail the first save. The man is incapacitated until the end of its next turn.
- Speak of the devil, it's the man's turn. While incapacitated he can't really do much, but a black tendril comes out from under him and lifts him 10 feet into the air. Unfortunately for the party, the man rolls a 15 Wisdom save against the 2nd part of *Sleep*, which ends the effect instead of going unconscious.
- [[Tilly]] casts *Guiding Bolt* at the man, hitting with a 24 for 16 radiant damage. The man cackles when the radiant damage hits him. He appears resistant to the damage, so likely only 8 damage was dealt. She goes into her *Starry Form: Chalice* and ends her turn.
- [[Garrison]] walks up next to [[Zrandovorski]], holding his action to cast *Channel Divinity: Divine Sense* if the man comes within 60 feet.
	- As a quick question outside of her turn, [[Tilly]] wonders if the tentacle is corporeal or not. Upon further investigation, it *seems* corporeal but it didn't really look like it actually burst out of the ground.

- [[The Collector]] moves 30 feet towards the man, and casts *Dissonant Whispers*. The man rolls a 13 and fails the save. He casts *Hunger of Hadar* on the both of them while speaking in tongues (like the Gibbering Mouther) as tentacles wrap around his arms. The man says *"...Reconstellators..."* and ends his turn.

## Recap

The Reconstellators mull over their collective loredumps from [[Find Gordon|The Nothic That Makes You Loredump]], ultimately deciding that they have more pressing issues to worry about than dealing with all their tragic backstories at the moment. After a brief follow-up conversation with [[Find Gordon|Gordon]], the party ventures out on their 7-day journey back to [[Draskhrr]]. On only the second day of travel, they encounter a cloaked figure clutching an unidentified object in the middle of the snow. [[The Collector]] sends in Son to investigate, and discovers that the figure is holding a baby swaddled in a blanket. It immediately sees Son, even though he is invisible, and a combat breaks out.

It is a tense and dangerous combat as this man has supernatural powers gifted to him by Hadar; a Great Old One. The Reconstellators desperately try to wrench [[the baby]] from the man's clutches at the cost of their own safety, as heroes are want to do. The man does not make this task easy, as he controls tentacles to move them around the battlefield, keep himself out of harm's way, and create areas of magical darkness that no light can pierce. After a great deal of effort, [[the baby]] is pulled from the man's grasp and a final deadly blow is struck to him by [[The Collector]] who used a spell scroll of *Arms of Hadar* to seal his fate. Before the party can have a moment to react to their victory, the man's body is violently grabbed by a tentacle reaching out of a portal which wrenches all of his internal organs out from under his skin and the whole mass is sent through the portal, which then immediately closes. The Reconstellators are left in stunned silence near the man's mutilated corpse and the swaddled baby.

On the body, they find a [[Bag of Holding]] which contains a number of spell scrolls and a peculiar magic item called an [[Iron Flask]]; a powerful prison that holds an unknown creature that will be sent into a blood-rage madness if it is ever released.

What the hell are they going to do with this baby?
