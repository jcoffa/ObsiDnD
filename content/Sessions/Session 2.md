It's a half day travel West; the same direction we'd be going to for the Observatory. The first couple floors are mostly picked clean at this point. The tomb has picked up its name after Goblins have gone in and walked out with various mutations; extra arms, permanently being blind or deaf, etc.
	- [[Investigate the Tomb of Booyahg]]
- Theresa finds [[Tilly]]'s desire to find someone who can undo *Modify Memory* very interesting. She knows of a group who has ancient knowledge of forbidden secrets. One in particular may have knowledge that could point us in the right direction. She doesn't know what this creature is, but calls it "Gordon". It's not friendly, and its helpfulness is dependant on the situation. It seems like it would "judge" us in some way. It's located in the tower West of [[Draskhrr]].
	- [[Find Gordon]]

### Forging Into the Forge

- Son sneaks in to The Steelworks while invisible, and triggers an *Alarm* spell. There is a sleeping Goblin that wakes up and frantically runs to the front of the shop, readying a crossbow.
- A stone wall opens up to reveal a metallic bull spewing green gas (which we assume is a Gorgon). It delivers a devastating hit to Son, but [[Tilly]] suggests that Son try to bait the bull into charging into the crucible to damage it. This ends up working with a successful taunt from Son, and we watch the bull charge directly into it. It yells and roars and we hear the sound of metal grinding on metal, a loud *CRASH!*, and an explosion.
- We hear the sound of rapidly approaching footsteps and screaming for "the thieves to stop". We manage to escape the scene and head back to the Everburning Chalice.

### The Creature

- We enter to see Theresa beating the shit out of a shapeshifting Hobgoblin; its face changes into something that looks more like a Drow every time Theresa smacks him with the bat.
- [[Zrandovorski]] recalls if he's seen this before, and thinks he can identify it as a Doppelganger. However, these monsters are typically able to maintain their form without issue even when suffering from "bat in the face".
- [[The Collector]] persuades it into cooperating and we drag it outside to interrogate the creature. For some reason it seems to recognize [[Tilly]]. The Collector tries to cast *Friends* but turns out this creature is immune to Charm.
- [[Zrandovorski]] is able to search its pockets and finds the following:
	- 17 Platinum
	- An unidentified scroll, which he immediately goes inside the tavern to *Identify* (Disguise Self)
	- A contract written in Undercommon, which none of us can understand
- The creature reads [[Tilly]]'s mind and asks her why she thinks she will ever find her family. It says "They died like dogs in the wild. And you will too". Tilly smacks him with a *Shillelagh* for the comment.
- The Doppelganger is able to get up and leave after easily escaping its bonds. It warns the party not to follow or interrupt it, which they immediately do anyways of course.
- [[Tilly]] uses *Starry Wisp* to get [[Zrandovorski]]'s attention from within the tavern, as the Doppelganger is kind of rocking their shit.
- [[Garrison]] ends up non-lethally striking the monster so we can interrogate it; landing the final blow for the combat. He interrogates it to get the location of where his clan comes from, on the condition that he will be able to leave with his life. 
- The creature says that he is from a gang of "Topplegangers", coming from the castle East of [[Draskhrr]]. It's a small group of Doppelgangers who have taken over the abandoned fortress. It said they have a larger goal and purpose that forces them to be stationed there. It looks for some kind of relic that's important to their tribe. There's a vein of Adamantine ore in a Mine, also East of [[Draskhrr]]. The Doppelgangers really need that Adamantine for some reason he wouldn't say. They also mentioned they're not native to this plane of existence.
- Curious that [[Garrison]] let the creature go, [[The Collector]] asks what keeps him to his word. Garrison responds that there's some urge he has to protect and show compassion. He doesn't like that everyone keeps calling the creature a "monster". He's a Paladin, through and through!

### The Night

- [[Tilly]] dreams she is sitting in a grove; an area of warm, bright light. Grass and trees are all around, smelling like pine. It feels like home. For a moment, her parents flash in front of her as if there for a moment. They flicker in front of her for a bit. Whenever they disappear, she sees the man who knocked her out; the man who brought her to [[Utopia]]. He smiles; and his eyes turn white. And then she awakens; leaving the tavern in a flurry of tears.
- As [[Garrison]] finishes his "sentry mode" sleeping, he hears the sound of crunching snow. A sniffing noise, maybe? He grabs his sword to investigate outside. At first he doesn't see anything, and then a creature waddles over. Like a tiny little ball of fluff with spider legs and rows of fangs. It barks and growls. *Well, it's puntable*, he thinks. He attempts to kick and it deftly dodges to the side. Tentacles come out of its back as [[Tilly]] throws open the door. The two roll initiative.
	- [[Tilly]] feels a weird sense of connection with the creature for a moment, but shakes it off. She attempts to pacify it by coaxing it into the tavern where it's warm. She speaks in some crazy monster gibberish and the creature instantly becomes docile, and skitters into the night.
	- [[Garrison]] is horrified, but [[Tilly]] has no idea what he's talking about. Garrison chalks it up to a hardware malfunction. He reflects back to the Doppelganger conversation, and asks if she's alright or if there's anything she needs help with (as she was very shaken up that the Doppelganger knew her). [[Garrison]] offers to help search for her family.

### The Steeldoesnotworks

## Recap

Still figuring out how to [[Sabotage The Steelworks]] to complete the quest from Grit Coalbelly, the party must devise a new plan with their Artificer gone, as their previous idea of using a steam bomb is no longer possible without him. After picking up more Quest leads than ale from Theresa, they decide to sneak into The Steelworks under the cover of night. [[The Collector]] sends in Son who easily sneaks into the forge while invisible, and effortlessly triggers an Alarm spell that he didn't notice at all. A sleeping Goblin in the forge frantically wakes up and runs right past the invisible Son to ready a crossbow aimed at the building's entrance. Unfortunately for Son, the forge's defense system kicks up and unleashes a metallic bull spewing green gas from its nostrils. This is a Gorgon; known for its petrifying gas, ability to see invisible creatures, and its favourite food of sneaky Quasits. Thankfully they are not known for their intelligence, and Son is able to expertly bait the Gorgon into ramming headfirst into the forge's crucible, sacrificing himself in the process.

With the quest now done and the forge collapsed, the party returns to The Everburning Chalice to find Theresa beating the ever-loving shit out of a tied-up Hobgoblin using a baseball bat. After some questioning and investigation, they discover that this Hobgoblin is actually a shape-changing creature called a Doppelganger, and Theresa caught it stealing barrels of ale from her cellar. [[Zrandovorski]] mercilessly rifles through its pockets and returns to the tavern to identify the scroll he found while leaving the interrogation to the others. The creature ends up breaking free of its bindings and goes to escape, and a fight breaks out as the party (minus [[Zrandovorski]]) tries to stop it.

After a somewhat narrow victory over the surprisingly powerful creature, the party learns it's from a small group of Doppelgangers in the East who have taken over an abandoned castle. They call themselves the Toppelgangers, and they're looking to steal booze and adamantine ore from [[Draskhrr]] and its surrounding area. They also have a cryptic "larger purpose" that the creature refuses to elaborate on, and instead states that they're not from this plane. They seem to be searching for some kind of artifact as well. At the request of [[Garrison]], who sees no reason for the party to kill this creature as it has not directly harmed anyone in its actions, the group lets the creature go on the promise that it will never return to [[Draskhrr]] or steal from its citizens again.

That night, [[Tilly]] has a dream of home, and of her parents, and of the man who arrested her and brought her to [[Utopia]]. She wakes up crying in the middle of the night, unable to shake the white, pupil-less eyes of that horrible man. When she leaves the tavern to get some fresh air and encounters [[Garrison]] standing watch outside, the pair encounter a white fuzz ball with giant spider legs and tentacles. [[Tilly]] is able to communicate with it using some form of guttural speech, and the creature leaves them alone. She has no recollection of this detail, and swears that she spoke to it perfectly normally. Relaying this encounter to the rest of the group in the morning, nobody recognizes the description of the creature or the language [[Tilly]] spoke to it in (which she continues to deny ever happened at all, leading [[Garrison]] to wonder if his auditory processing system is malfunctioning).

Lastly, the party turns in their quest to Grit Coalbelly and enters into a business agreement to receive 25% of its profits. They also convince him to start a bakery as a side-hustle, on account of his incredibly delicious pastries and muffins.
