## Notes

### Get The Fuck Out Of There

- The Reconstellators attempt to book it out of [[The Stony Veil]] now that they can hear several Xorn approaching them. There are some absolutely *awful* athletics checks from the party to book it out of there. The only people to roll above 10 are [[Zrandovorski]] (12) and [[NG]] (15). The unnamed NPC that the party saved [[Session 21#The Cave of Head-Crushed-By-A-Boulder|previously]] rolled a 1. Big yikes.
- The group gets about 70 feet from the exit of the cave before a Xorn pops up from the ground 30 feet in front of them. Looks like it's not going down without a fight!

### Fight The Fuck Out Of There

- **Round 1!**
- [[The Collector]] uses his bonus action to re-apply his old *Hex* to a new target (since he's been concentrating on it, and it's been less than 4 hours). He rolls a 21 to hit with *Eldritch Blast* to deal 10 force damage + 1 necrotic damage.
- [[Kolvar]] uses *Phantasmal Force* to mentally trick the Xorn into seeing a bunch of gems fall down from the ceiling and into the hole that it burrowed out of. The Xorn fails with a 9 on the Intelligence saving throw, and successfully tricks the Xorn into thinking a bunch of gems have fallen behind it.
- [[Son]] turns into a bat and flies past the Xorn, attempting to bait it into performing an opportunity attack. The Xorn easily hits [[Son]] with a bite attack and eats him in a single bite for 16 damage.
- [[Tilly]] is eager to get the fuck out of here and uses her action to dash 60 feet towards the exit. 10 feet to go!
- The NPC dashes *twice* and makes it 90 feet forward, making it out of the cave by 20 feet.
- [[NG]] uses *Expeditious Retreat* and moves makes it out of the cave with exactly 70 feet of movement.
- The Xorn climbs into the hole full of fake gemstones, and will be preoccupied for the foreseeable future.
- This is enough of a distraction to allow the party to get out the cave.

### Our Reward is Getting The Fuck Out Of There

- The NPC does not stop running once he makes it out of there...
- [[NG]] is faster than the man thanks to *Expeditious Retreat* and catches up easily. He cuts the straps of the backpack and picks it up when it drops. The man does not stop running.
- [[Tilly]] uses *Entangle* to try and restrain him, but ends up only restraining [[NG]].
- [[The Collector]] uses *Eldritch Blast* to just kill him instead. He rolls a 22 to hit and a 10 for damage to kill him instantly.
- [[Zrandovorski]] rolls an 18 for Investigation (with help from [[Tilly]]) and finds a badge on his person that looks like an eye with some squiggly lines emanating from it. Black sludge begins trickling out of his agape mouth. His backpack contains:
	- 35 GP of actual money
	- 50 GP worth of sapphire
	- 350 GP worth of ruby
	- 100 GP worth of diamond
	- 200 GP worth of obsidian
- [[Tilly]] hurriedly puts the [[Lady of the White]] calling card on the body. The card turns into a door and the Lady walks through. She looks somewhat troubled, claiming that the body doesn't belong to a human ("Saying this is a 'human' is a pretty strong choice of words..."). She tastes the sludge and makes a sour face. "Rancid...", she says. Claiming that it's unusable for her *normal purposes*, she pays only 25 SP before leaving.
- Because [[Son]] is dead, [[Tilly]] summons her cat familiar for the first time by constructing it out of the stars in her necklace. The players (not characters) debate its name for 15 real-life minutes before Dani dismisses it while [[The Collector]] brings back [[Son]].
- After a short rest, the party sets out for [[Fair Helm City]].

### The Fault in Our Stars

- [[The Collector]] sees a small group of people in the distance. On guard, [[The Collector]] eavesdrops on their conversation after getting a bit closer. They're talking about *an upcoming ritual*.
- The party decides to get a little closer and have [[Kolvar]] shapechange into the guy the party killed and stole his backpack. [[NG]] goes with [[Kolvar]], intending to be posing as a new recruit to whatever cult these guys are from.
	- It honestly goes pretty well as [[Kolvar]] is able to suss out the secret dap-up handshake and they fully believe that [[Kolvar]] is "Kev" as they recognize him as the guy the party killed.
- [[Kolvar]] and [[NG]] agree to follow the other cultists and begin following them West. The rest of the party picks up on the plan and start tailing them at a distance.
- [[NG]] asks the cultists what their cult is about while they make camp. The leader explains that they aim to perform a ritual to commune with otherworldly beings who know far more than their mortal knowledge. Pretty standard fare. They're largely interested in having the access to this deity, and the more people they can get for the ritual the better.
- [[The Collector]] turns [[Tilly]] invisible while she uses *Pass Without Trace* to get within 40 feet of their camp. She mentally connects with [[NG]] and he relays what happened. He thinks he's in a little over his head with this. The party discusses whether to follow the cultists and see what's up, and ultimately decides this would be fun and probably solve their levelling issue (need to be level 5 to use the [[Helm]], which is how they intend to escape [[Fair Helm City]]).
- [[The Collector]] and [[Garrison]] aim to run ahead of the cultist group to fake meeting up with them by chance. [[Tilly]] and [[Zrandovorski]] lag behind to make sure that some of the party is available *outside* of the cult just in case they need to break in and do some rescuing ~~also they have the worst Charisma out of the entire party and are an active liability to be within the cult~~.
- [[NG]] asks he cultists what leader of their cult is called. The leader of this group clarifies that, although they are often called [[The Cult of the Stars]], they're not really a cult. Anyways, their organization-wide leader goes by [[The Soul Sage]].
- After a couple more days of travel, the groups are less than a day from the cult's destination. [[The Collector]] and [[Garrison]] execute the plan to pretend to join the group. He rolls an 18 for Deception and is graciously allowed into the travelling group.
- They finally make it to their destination; a ruined town made of stone. Their leader/guide smacks a brick on the side of a ruined well, and a trapdoor opens nearby. [[NG]] tries to shmooze his way into asking the guide how that works, but fails with a nat 1 on Persuasion. They all climb down a ladder under the trapdoor.

### Stone Road Mall

- At the bottom of the ladder is an enormous... underground shopping district in a huge expansive cave. The guide welcomes the party to "[[Caelia]]". [[The Collector]] marvels that he didn't know there was another city in [[The Frozen March]], and the guide corrects him that this place has existed before the [[The Frozen March]] was frozen. WTF!
- [[NG]] climbs back up the ladder and fakes needing a smoke break in order to get some privacy. A guard near the trapdoor says that its fine and he can just smoke here; he doesn't need to leave the ruined towns. This is not ideal, so he uses *Elementalism* to create a ton of smoke to hotbox the guard into letting him leave. This works and the guard lets him out.
- He sends up a neon orange smoke signal and writes a note to wedge in the trapdoor and prevent it from closing. The note says that the people down there "know [[The Collector]] and [[Garrison]], and they're suspicious of [[Kolvar]]". [[Tilly]] brings [[Zrandovorski]] down the trapdoor by putting him in the [[Bag of Holding]] and turning into a spider to climb down unseen.
- Meanwhile, [[Kolvar]] (still disguised as Kev) is being interrogated by some of the other cultists here. They're asking what happened at [[The Stony Veil]], and "why did his badge send out a ping?". [[Kolvar]] says that they thought they were going to die and sent out a distress. The interrogator raises an eyebrow and reminds "Kev" ([[Kolvar]]) that badges only ping *when* they die. Another cultist slowly moves to block the door.
- [[Tilly]] and [[Zrandovorski]] disguise themselves and make their way over to [[Kolvar]]. [[Zrandovorski]] (disguised as a cultist) urgently knocks on the door and claims that [[Toran the Red]] is fast approaching and has discovered their hideout. He uses a 19 from his [[Zrandovorski#Foretelling Rolls|foretelling rolls]] to make the lie very convincing, and the guards leave the room in a hurry.
- The party is able to reconvene right as the alarms start blaring to alert that [[Toran the Red]] is approaching. Perhaps this is not [[Zrandovorski]]'s proudest moment; even when he forces a 19 on Deception he still isn't very good at making good lies.
- They decide to lay low in tavern room #3 to wait until the alarms are gone. [[NG]] has the idea to take all of the gems from Kev's old pack into the [[Bag of Holding]], then give the badge to [[Son]] and kills him to create a "ping" when its owner dies. That would make them think Kev has died *again* and sow more confusion.
- They swap from tavern room #3 into tavern room #4 and listen in on the wall joining their room. [[Tilly]] wild shapes into a spider and goes over to check it out after they hear a muffled conversation come from the scene of "the crime".
- Two cultists are talking to an elf with glowing blue eyes investigating the dropped backpack and the badge that "Kev" died from again. After some discussion about the details, of which there are not very many (which they find suspicious), they leave.

## Recap

After their [[Session 21#Rock Monster!|battle with the Xorn]], The Reconstellators decide that fleeing is the best option. Before they can escape, another Xorn breaks through the floor, blocking their path. With some quick thinking from [[Kolvar]] and some not quick moving from [[Son]] (RIP Session 9 - Session 22 Son), they manage to tempt the Xorn away with fake gems made by [[Kolvar]]'s [Phantasmal Force](https://5e.scrunko.monster/spells.html#phantasmal%20force_xphb) and manage to all escape the cave.

However, the newly saved NPC seems uninterested in stopping his fleeing attempt, and begins running down the mountain with the Reconstellators in pursuit. [[NG]] catches up easily and manages to cut the straps on his backpack leaving them with all of his jewels, while the man continues to flee. After failed attempts to stop him, [[The Collector]] stops him the only way possible; a gun (an [Eldritch Blast](https://5e.scrunko.monster/spells.html#eldritch%20blast_xphb)). Not wanting to deal with the man's body, the party does not stop [[Tilly]] from placing [[The Lady of The White]]'s card on the corpse and the hag takes the corpse but seems troubled. "Saying this is a 'human' is a pretty strong choice of words…". She gives the party 25 SP since it can't be used for her normal purposes, and leaves.

As they descend the mountaintop and [[The Collector]] debates bringing [[Son]] back right away, [[Tilly]] reveals her familiar, a cat made from the constellation of stars on her necklace. 15 real life minutes pass debating the many dumb possible names for this cat before Dani decides she's not taking suggestions and the cat remains unnamed for now while [[Son]] is summoned back.

After a short rest, the party begins their 9-day journey back to [[Fair Helm City]], but quickly encounter a pair of travelers. [[The Collector]] eavesdrops on them and hears them discussing "an upcoming ritual", so [[Kolvar]] shapeshifts into the man they just murdered to approach them, with [[NG]] joining him. [[Kolvar|Disguised Kolvar]] manages to pass the dap check and they discover the NPC's name was Kev, and the travelers fully believe [[Kolvar]] is Kev, and convinces them to bring [[NG]] along with them, while the party follows distantly.

While they travel, [[NG]] manages to learn that they are [[The Cult of the Stars]] but are totally not a cult. Their leader is [[The Soul Sage]], and their ritual is meant to commune with otherwordly beings to gain knowledge beyond mortal means; the more people the better. They decide to have [[The Collector]] and [[Garrison]] join them on their travels, while [[Tilly]] and [[Zrandovorski]] stay back outside of their base in case they need additional support ~~or an escape route~~.

The cultist group arrives at a ruined town, but a secret passage leads them underground into an enormous underground shopping district in an expansive cave; this is [[Caelia]], a place so old that it has been here since before the March was frozen! It is bustling with people of many different species, the most diverse they've seen in [[The Frozen March]].

[[NG]] manages to send a smoke signal to [[Tilly]] and [[Zrandovorski]] and leaves a note for them while leaving the door ajar, stating [[Caelia]] knows [[The Collector]] and [[Garrison]], and they're suspicious of [[Kolvar]]. They decide this is a very good reason to ignore the plan and head inside, with [[Zrandovorski]] jumping into the bag of holding and [[Tilly]] turning into a spider to sneak them in.

The cultists figure out pretty quickly that [[Kolvar]] is not Kev, but
 [[Zrandovorski]] comes up with a brilliant plan; he uses [Disguise Self](https://5e.scrunko.monster/spells.html#disguise%20self_xphb) to look like a cultist, urgently knocks on the door, and tells the cultists that [[Toran the Red]], the known terrorist, has found the city and is coming to attack, putting the entire city on high alert but manages to get the cultists out of the room before they discover [[Kolvar]].

[[NG]] decides that at this point they should reconvene. [[Zrandovorski]] and [[Tilly]] reveals they are the reason for the alarm, [[NG]] reveals that [[The Collector]] and [[Garrison]] are apparently enemies of this state, and [[Kolvar]] reveals that they could definitely tell he wasn't Kev and they'll definitely come looking for them once they realize [[Toran the Red|Toran]] is not here.

They decide the best course is to fake Kev's disappearance and hide right under their noses, one room over. They put the jewels in the bag of holding and leave the empty bag and fake Kev's death by having [[Son]] hold his badge while [[NG]] kills him (RIP [[Son]] Session 22 - Session 22). They debate whether to stay for this ritual,  which they do, but they need some additional details, which they can gain tomorrow.

While they rest for the night, [[Tilly]] has a vision where she is [[The Soul Sage]] who is apparently also a Kalashtar, who is holding a ceremony where they are handing out badges to everyone in [[Caelia]], and their ritual is to reach [[The Abberant]]. After waking up and sharing this info, The Reconstellators decide, once again, the best plan is to split up; [[Garrison]] and [[The Collector]] will stay in the room, [[NG]] and [[Tilly]] will go to the ritual to get badges of their own, and [[Zrandovorski]] and [[Kolvar]] will go blend with the populace.

With just [[Garrison]] and [[The Collector]] left in the room, [[The Collector]] feels a knife press to his throat as [[Gobbo|Gobbo, the God of Good]], says that he needs their help.
