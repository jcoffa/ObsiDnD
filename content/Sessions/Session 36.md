## Notes

### [Inc/Succ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nip/Tuck)

- The Reconstellators sit 60 feet away from the pre-orgy festivities, knowing that they can't delay any further: if they long rest, it just gets worse. They debate some of their options, but ultimately decide to have [[The Collector]] cast *Remove Curse* on himself to try and remove the charmed effect before heading in. 
- There isn't any noticeable effects, and [[Tilly]] can't hear anything change, so they send [[Son]] to peak around the corner. Some excellent roles let him see the situation changing: the floating forms now look like they have nebulas shooting out of their hands, slowly creating a miasma of stars in the room.
- They get ready to rush into combat, hoping to surprise the pair. They cast *Protection from Evil and Good* on [[The Collector]] and [[Tilly]] (with [[NG]] and [[Garrison]] concentrating) as they head into the room, starting combat!

#### Round 1

- The male succubus is up first, their neck doing a 180 to see the party as their form shifts from starry blue to demonically red, straight out of Hot Topic. He flies down to 5 feet above the party and attacks [[NG]] with a *Fiendish Touch*, hitting with a 19. It deals 16 damage, and then points at [[Garrison]], casting *Dominate Person*, telling him to kill the others. He passes with an 18, narrowly avoiding friendly fire (thank god).
- [[Son]] is up next, using the *Help* action to assist the next attack by giving the succubus a wet willy.
- [[Garrison]] is up next, hitting with a 23. He uses his Daily Free Divine Smite™, dealing 19 damage and toppling the succubus, knocking him prone. He hits with a 28, dealing 15 more damage.
- The incubus (female) is up next, remaining in her blue form. She sighs and sweeps her hand, removing the starry effect from the room and causing all the civilians to drop to the ground limp. She looks over at the group and smiles at [[Tilly]], flying over to her and using her *Fiendish Touch*. She hits with a 19, dealing ~~14~~ 7 psychic damage. 
- [[NG]] approaches the downed succubus, striking with his [[Shortsword of Frost]] and hitting, dealing 4 cold damage. He uses his *Inky Step* to teleport 15 ft. out of the room.
- [[Tilly]] uses her orbital laser (Moonbeam) at third level on the downed succubus, dealing 19 radiant damage from the sparkles in her eyes in the presence of the incubus. She uses her bonus action to cast her Daily Free Healing Word™ on [[NG]], healing 4 HP.
- [[The Collector]] chooses to NOT move forward with the 'kill one first' plan since his *Eldrith Blasts* would have disadvantage, *Misty Step*ping away and aiming at the incubus. He crits 1 roll but hits both, dealing 5 + 8(crit) force damage.

#### Round 2
- The succubus, recently jumped, tries to *Dominate* [[Garrison]] again, but he passes again. The succubus rises (non-sexual) and uses his *Fiendish Touch* on [[Garrison]], hitting and dealing 19 psychic damage, before trying to retreat. [[Tilly]] and [[Garrison]] both make opportunity attack with [[Garrison]] hitting.
- [[Son]] redirects his wet willy to the incubus, giving advantage to...
- [[Garrison]], who crits with a natural 20! Through an upcasted divine smite and extra divine energy piercing the fiend, he deals a whopping 68 damage as the divine energy pulses from his chest down his lance, light shining through the incubus' body as she dies. His attention shifts to the fleeing succubus, throwing the corpse towards the succubus. He's distracted for a moment by the body as he looks up and says 'oh', seeing a javelin hurdling toward him as it hits, killing the succubus (glad he's on our side) and ending the combat.

#### Aftermath

- [[NG]] and [[The Collector]] go to investigate the bodies, but don't find anything of worth on either of the fiends' corpses.
- [[Garrison]] assists [[Tilly]] with checking on the civilians. They're all alive, and they seem to have more signs of life then the comatose man from the Church. [[Tilly]] slaps him awake and he's up fine, but doesn't remember how he got here.
- [[NG]] uses *Thurmaturgy* to wake the rest of the civilians, who are all confused how they got there but are happy to be lead out of [[The Underdark]]. One civilian does run off after [[NG]] reports that [[Tyke Tycoon]] burned down.

### [Hadar, Can You Hear Me?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QwCPAo5e_F8)

- Once they exit [[The Underdark|The Deep]] they head over to [[The Golden Gauntlet]] tower to collect their reward, with severed heads and civilian testimony to boot. They easily collect their reward with their overwhelming evidence, gaining 5,000 GP.
- They head off to the [[Polaris]] for a good night's sleep, with [[Tilly]] passing out immediately on [[Furret]], while [[The Collector]] heads off to his room alone. Guess [[Garrison]] and [[NG]] are on first watch, whether they like it or not.
- In [[The Collector]]'s room, he finds himself making a small prayer to [[Hadar]], saying he still doesn't know what he wants with him, but asks that he watch over [[The Baby|Baby Null]] as well. With a glorious 3, [[The Collector]] is pulled aside to commune with [[Hadar|God]].
- Over their watches, [[NG]] and [[Garrison]] debate whether it would be better to have 1 coconut-sized ball or 30 acorn-sized balls, leading to discussions about whether [[Garrison]] even has those parts, and [[NG]] tells him that's up to him to build and figure out for himself.
- Once they wake [[The Collector]] and [[Tilly]], [[The Collector|Linus]] is clearly in a state, and [[Tilly]] asks what's wrong. He admits that he prayed to [[Hadar]] again and he was offered a deal, but he's unsure if it's worth taking, and wants to talk with the whole party once everyone is awake. He asks [[Tilly]] about her parents and their Druidic circle, and what they could've been protecting, but he's not very convinced by her answer that they 'protected the travelers' in [[The Hexes]]. She admits that despite wanting to find her parents, she can't go home without them: it feels like her home is haunted (metaphorically).
- For the first time in days, The Reconstellators finally sleep through a night, relieving all of those effected of 1 level of exhaustion.
- [[The Collector]] talks with the party in the morning about his conversation with [[Hadar]], and admits he offered him a deal: if [[The Collector]] promises to do the next thing he commands, no questions asked, he'll protect [[The Baby|Baby Null]] for a month.
- Everybody is against the deal immediately, including [[The Collector]]. He seems pretty confident that if they wanted [[The Baby|Baby Null]] dead they could've killed him already, but [[NG]] points out the likely use of the baby for ritualistic sacrifice, which is not helpful for [[The Collector]]'s sanity.
- They debate what they should do next: they've got to start heading towards the factory, but [[The Collector]] wants to be sure to exhaust every lead on [[The Baby|Baby Null]] before they go, and all of their new funds mean they can FINALLY start turning the [[Polaris]] into a proper bastion.
- Much IRL conversing about bastion rules and mechanics happen:
	- The [[Polaris]] already has 1 cramped room
	- There is space to expand that cramped room to a roomy room, and construct 2 more roomy rooms below deck.
	- Since they're in the city with a shipwright, it will take 7 days to create/expand existing rooms, and 1 additional day to convert those rooms into bastion facilities.
	- Since they're hiring a shipwright to makes these expansions on a shorter timeframe, the normal 500GP/25 day upgrade is raised to 750GP/8 days per room.
	- Since they are level 5, they can have 2 bastion facilities. They decide on the [Library](https://5e.scrunko.monster/bastions.html#library_xdmg) and the [Storehouse](https://5e.scrunko.monster/bastions.html#storehouse_xdmg).

## Recap

We return to the Reconstellators, still sleepless in [[Kota-Ken]], getting ready to head into battle with the Dream Eaters, uncertain if [[The Collector]] is still whipped, I mean charmed. He casts Remove Curse on himself to no visible effects, and they charge into battle.

The pair are an Incubus and Succubus, which is incredible news to the local paladin, [[Garrison]]. He basically jumps into solo combat against the pair and takes them both down on the same turn, dealing a record 68 damage with a single attack. Killing the pair frees the civilians in the cave from their control, and The Reconstellators lead them out and successfully collect yet another reward from [[The Golden Gauntlet]]. Man, they are rolling in it!

Guess it's time to figure out what to do next, and [[The Collector]] is not keen to leave the city before getting some leads on [[The Baby|Baby Null]]. Their freshly lined pockets also open up the opportunity for bastion upgrades to the [[Polaris]], so The Reconstellators take 8 days of downtime while they wait for their upgrades.

Here's what they got:
- [[NG]] gets 8 days of progress on his 30 day shipwrighting apprenticeship
- [[Tilly]] makes a Snail Flail Mail Veil and does some map stuff
- [[The Collector]] posts a bounty for information on the mercenary and manticore who stole [[The Baby]]. A man comes forward who saw someone matching the description using a water portal outside of [[Kota-Ken]], but he doesn't know where he went or how he activated the portal.
- [[Garrison]] goes looking for information on the myconids and [[Copper Rot]] at [[The Factory]]. He learns that both originated from the Feywild, and while the myconids were present in the [[The Underdark|Underdark]] before coming up to the Southern Isles, the [[Copper Rot]] would have to have been brought here on purpose.

With bastion facilities built (Library and Storehouse) and more fuel overpriced fuel provided by another large ship, The Reconstellators get ready to set out to [[The Factory]], making a quick pit stop at the Water Portal to get any leads on [[The Baby]].
