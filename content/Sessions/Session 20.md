## Notes

### Exploring the Rest of the Longhouse

- The Reconstellators, still a bit unsettled with finding [[Session 19#Speak With Dead Marquis|the preserved corpse of the Marquis in the longhouse basement]], decide to continue searching their new residence in less... morbid areas.
- The map of [[The Frozen March]] is built into the war table, so it can't be carried with the party. A few differences are added to the map [[The Frozen March|that note]], including a star, a mountain linked to the Earthen Elemental plane, and a lumberyard near [[The Hearth of Hope]] is identified as not being controlled by either the goblins or [[The Hearth of Hope]].
- [[Kaz the Cold]] had a coinpurse with 100 GP in it, and a scroll of *Ice Storm*.
	- [[Zrandovorski]] also finds a carving on the side of [[Kaz the Cold]]'s side table. Scratched in with a knife or dagger, is the phrase *"Gobbo was here"*.
	- He isn't sure where he's heard this, maybe a story from someone who was dropped in [[The Frozen March]], but there was an adventurer from across the ocean who dreamed of becoming a God. Supposedly, he had a calling card of writing this exact phrase wherever he went. The question is, was this authentic? Or just a follower or copycat imitating their idol?

### The Prison

- A bugbear, Warden Stefan, is guarding the entrance. He says that everyone in the jail are spies for [[Utopia]].
- The Reconstellators figure out a Good Cop/Bad Cop routine that can somehow involve all six of them.
	- [[Tilly]]: Good Cop, a.k.a. Raven
	- [[NG]]: Bad Cop who is old, a.k.a. Crow
	- [[Zrandovorski]]: Dad Cop, a.k.a. Owl
	- [[Garrison]]: Cop who steps in if things get too spicy, a.k.a. Eagle
	- [[The Collector]]: Detective Cop?, a.k.a. Sparrow
	- [[Son]]: Exploding Cop, a.k.a. Toucan
- The jail is made of iron bars that have been welded together from destroyed prison wagons that drop people off in [[The Frozen March]].
- "Raven" talks to the person who has been in the jail the longest; a drow man with shrivelled white hair. Not just white because he's drow, but also because he's super old. The drow introduces himself as Viticus, and claims that he was wrongfully put in here by [[Kaz the Cold]] and [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]] for being a spy for [[Utopia]]. He admits he cannot prove that he isn't, but it was probably that he just stole bread at some point.
	- "Crow" asks if the drow would like to be let out. The drow says yes. Turns out the "Bad" in "Bad Cop" means "shit at their job".
- "Raven" asks how the drow ended up in [[The Frozen March]]. He explains that, once upon a time, the [[Utopia|Utopian]] people are not very forgiving of different ideologies. He was once a follower of Lolth, and he was banished to [[The Frozen March]] for it. Honestly they might've been onto something with that one.
- "Crow" explains that he doesn't care if he's a spy for [[Utopia]] or not, if he's truthful then there's a chance the party can let them out. The drow explains that he was not a spy, but he was sent here as a "disruptor" to stir up shit and make sure the goblins couldn't amass too much organizational power.
	- "Crow" gives Viticus his bedroll, and the party leaves.

### Downtime Time

- The party discusses what to do with their free time since [[Toran the Red|Toran]] won't be here for another 2 weeks.
	- [[Tilly]] wants to gild an acorn in [[Find Dead Bodies for The Lady of the White|Hag Platinum]] and then melt some of the ice around [[Draskhrr]] to allow for agriculture.
	- [[The Collector]] wants to install democracy.
	- [[Garrison]] wants to start a 2-week crossfit training session.
- The party does a little 20 minute detour to [[The Everburning Chalice]] to speak with Theresa about [[The Baby]]. Theresa is really not into taking care of it. But she calls over one of the cooks, a Kenku named Arvind, who has lost a child recently and seems receptive to taking care of him. [[The Collector]] is not sure he's ready to part with [[The Baby]] just yet, so he keeps him for now.
- [[Garrison]]'s training goes decently well! He rolls an Athletic and a Performance check for each week. The first week is a measly Athletics check but a pretty good Performance check. More of a "do as I say not as I do" sort of deal. For the second week he rolls an incredible Athletics check and a subpar Performance check, so he mostly just beats up goblins during sparring. Overall, lots of the goblins do feel more confident in battle. So we call that a success!
- [[Tilly]] enlists [[NG]] and [[Grit Coalbelly]] to help her gild an acorn (an expensive spell component for *Summon Beast*). She also melted some of the snow to reveal the roads underneath the snow. Turns out there's a complex network of inter-woven stonework covered with fossilized moss.
- [[The Collector]] and [[Zrandovorski]] have a joint venture where they hold.
	- Some goblin asks for "more bacon".
	- [[NG]] and [[Garrison]] ask for regular oil changes. [[Zrandovorski]] asks what needs them, and they immediately flee.
	- A goblin guard, named Dezi, has a proposal. He says that [[Utopia]] is very militarist and greedy, and some people still want to leave [[The Frozen March]]. He proposes that an excursion go to [[The Stony Veil]] to get gems and use them to barter with [[Utopia]] to let some people out. [[The Collector]] and [[Zrandovorski]] are uncertain that [[Utopia]] would hold up their end of the hypothetical bargain.
	- [[Tilly]] suggests casting *Continual Flame* on the walls around [[Draskhrr]] to provide light, make it easy to find from far away (for travelers), and scare the shit out of potential invaders.
	- A group of goblins ask for every goblin in [[Draskhrr]] to be given both a personal gun and a personal cannon.
	- Miscellaneous other requests, some good, many bad. It's assumed we have a decent list of positive improvements to the city.
	- [[NG]] makes a gun. He leverages [[The Deep Forge]] to create a mundane one, and is struck with a flash of inspiration to improve it with magic. And then enchants it to become a +1 gun using 100 GP of party gold and the 100 GP the party got off [[Rykor Steelhand|Rykor]]. 

### New Main Character Discovered

- [[The Collector]] suspects that [[The Baby]] may be given to him to watch him in some way. He lights some ceremonial candles, sets him to sit on a table, and implores baby Null to let him know what his purpose is.
	- [[The Baby]]'s eyes flash a bright silver for a moment. [[The Collector]] feels his hair float up in the air slightly. Null watches him, seeing how he would react.
	- "Null, is that you?" [[The Collector]] asks. [[The Baby]] babbles and grabs at the air. He then points at the door that [[The Collector]] entered the room with.
	- Unwilling to leave him alone in a room with open flame, [[The Collector]] picks up [[The Baby]] and notices he is much lighter than usual. As he leaves the room, the candle flames seem to angle themselves towards him.
	- The door opens into the main area of [[The Everburning Chalice]], but everyone in the bar is completely frozen. Theresa has spilled a platter of drinks and one of them is flipped upside-down mid-air, liquid suspended halfway out of the cup. [[The Collector]] and [[The Baby]] are the only ones who can move.
	- [[The Collector]], suspecting that they're under the effects of *Time Stop*, walks around the bar to see how [[The Baby]]'s hands react. He seems pointing outside of the bar. [[The Collector]], not wanting to be rude, scoops the spilled drinks back into their mugs before leaving.
	- Outside, [[The Baby]] points to the constellation of The Void. Except, there's an indescribable, gargantuan creature that isn't fully present in this plane. It says;
	- *[[The Collector|Linus Snabberknackle]]. Your father requested power of me. You would do the same. You have done the same.*
	- [[The Collector]]: "Might I assume you go by Hadar"?
	- *That's one of the names.*
	- [[The Collector]]: "I have not requested any of your power!"
	- *You may not remember doing it. Would you like to see?*
	- [[The Collector]]: "... I do".
		- The constellation that [[The Baby]] was pointing at grows, and now [[The Collector]] finds himself standing above his fallen Father, on his deathbed. He reflects; *I don't think I had these abilities before Father died. I knew cantrips and basics, but not magic like this.*
		- He recalls the moments where Father was able to bring him back from the dead. He begs and pleads in his mind, can anyone help, can anything save my Father like he saved me? "Please, let me carry on his legacy, don't let me lose him." And then he felt it, for the first time. The presence of a Celestial. But, he was not strong enough at this time to save him.
	- *You will carry on your Father's mission. You will do as he willed, and I will bring him back for you.*
	- [[The Collector]]: "But why?"
	- *Because you asked.*
	- [[The Collector]]: "... Thank you."
	- *My friends call me Hadar. You may know me by a different name. I am [[Larizadune]].*
- He snaps back to reality. Oh, there goes gravity! The mugs he fixed clatter back onto the platter that Theresa was carrying.

### We Might Have to Keep the Baby

- [[The Collector]] barges back into the bar and hurriedly gathers the party. He confirms with the party that, hypothetically, if someone were to learn a big thing about themselves that they previously didn't know, they would share it to the party. Right?
- The party agrees, and [[The Collector]] relays what he experienced, and heavily suspects that [[Kolvar]] was right and he is indeed a Warlock. He also relays that Hadar and [[Larizadune]] are the same thing, and he believes his magic actually comes from a Celestial. Finally, he reveals that he was instructed by Hadar/Larizadune to carry on his Father's mission, but he has no idea what it actually is.
- [[NG]] asks if [[The Collector]] would appreciate The Reconstellator's help for whatever the mission would happen to be. [[The Collector]] isn't sure, and agrees that the time between "finding out what the mission is" and "needing to complete the mission" would be very slim. He eventually determines that if the mission is evil he would want to be stopped. This includes if he is forced to chop his own dick off. He eventually agrees that The Reconstellators are bound by something stronger than blood, and they're in this together.
- Also they need to keep [[The Baby]].

### Where Do You See Yourself In 5 Years?

- The party discusses their long-term goals after they leave [[The Frozen March]].
	- [[Tilly]] would like to find the other Kalashtar at the scar of the plane she saw in [[Session 16#Tilly 's Trance, Again|her vision]], show the party her home in The Hexes, and find out what happened to her parents.
	- [[Zrandovorski]] would like to visit [[Session 16#Zrandovorski 's Trance|his old home in the Utopian Empire]], but isn't confident they could go there right away after escaping [[The Frozen March]].
	- [[The Collector]] wants to figure out his mission from [[Larizadune]], and open his museum ~~which may or may not involve overthrowing the [[Utopia|The Utopian Empire]].~~
	- [[NG]] wants to return to his factory and cleanse it of the mycelium infection.
	- [[Garrison]] wants to find out who did this to the factory and figure out the spore situation. [[NG]] proposes he could also find [[Session 16#Garrison 's Trance|his creator]], which isn't off the table but not a priority.
