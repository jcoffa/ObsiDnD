## Notes

- The Reconstellators have a day to spend in [[The Hearth of Hope]] while they wait for [[Polaris]] to be repaired.
- [[Zrandovorski]] helps [[Tilly]] look for a 300 GP diamond. He finds a brooch abandoned in the corner of an abandoned building buried in the snow. The brooch has the design of thin golden strands that make the shape of a fist. The diamond is in the center of that fist. [[Zrandovorski]] recognizes this symbol as [[The Golden Gauntlet]]. He vaguely recalls the history books mentioning Gryphons whenever this group is mentioned.
	- [[NG]] is able to identify it as being a 400 GP diamond.
- [[Kolvar]] attempts to find information on what's going on outside of [[The Frozen March]]. It's hard to trust the rumours they hear, but he does hear that the [[Utopia|Utopian]] guard are doubling their defense on the main gate to [[The Frozen March]].
- The party debates performing grave robbing so that [[NG]] can get 14 GP. [[Tilly]] gets fed up since the party won't let her send the bodies to [[The Lady of the White]] and just gives him the money. He uses it to get the materials for [[Zrandovorski]] to help him craft a scroll of *Comprehend Languages*.
- They christen the boat with a bottle of *Snow Rat Shit* ([[The Everburning Chalice#Offerings|the alcoholic beverage]]) and [[The Collector]] wishes the boat good luck for its life.

### On the Winds to [[Draskhrr]]

- The party takes off for [[Draskhrr]] having picked up the 200 [[Caelia|Caelians]].
- [[NG]] rigs up one of those Dairy Queen-style dishes that you can put coins in to have it slowly spin around before falling into the coin slot on the [[Box With a Coin Slot]]. [[The Collector]] puts in a GP and watches it spin into the box. *Kerplunk!*
- Arriving at [[Draskhrr]], the party runs into [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]]! Looks like they made it to [[Draskhrr]] since the last time the party saw them. They notice that he's wearing a silver medallion, resembling a crescent moon with two emeralds in it that look like eyes.
- [[The Collector]] could not care less about any of this and books it to [[Grit Coalbelly]]'s place to go collect their 25% profit share from [[The Deep Forge]]. Turns out it's been doing EXTREMELY well, so the party receives 330 GP (55 GP each, including [[Garrison]] who is not here).
- [[NG]], [[Tilly]], [[Zrandovorski]], and [[Kolvar]] go to see [[Toran the Red]] and discuss what to do with the refugees from [[Caelia]]. [[The Collector]], [[Son]], and [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] go check on [[The Baby]].

### [[The Baby]] is Back!

- Team Baby heads to [[The Everburning Chalice]] and see Theresa making drinks, and she points them to Arvind in the kitchen. He has a backpack on with [[The Baby]] swaddled in, looking very comfortable and quite happy.
- Arvind hands over the backpack swaddle with the baby in it. He explains that the baby has started eating solid food.
- [[The Collector]] asks if Arvind can come with them. Theresa explains that he is severely wanted by [[Utopia]] due to his previous... actions. Roughly 2/3 of the [[Utopia|Utopian]] spies that infiltrate [[Draskhrr]] are here for Arvind. He's gleefully cagey about his past and shows no shame whatsoever. Arvind seems content staying in [[The Everburning Chalice]]; he says he's happy here.

### ~~The Marquis~~ ~~[[Kaz the Cold]]~~ [[Toran the Red|Toran]]'s Longhouse

- Team [[Toran the Red|Toran]] heads over to the longhouse and ask if her people are ready to leave.
- [[NG]] asks if she knows a potentially safe place to drop them off once they're in the [[Utopian Empire]]'s territory. The party knows that the Southern regions of the continent aren't controlled by [[Utopia]], but that's far. [[Toran the Red|Toran]] muses that a place perceived as dangerous will not have much [[Utopia|Utopian]] presence even if it's *technically* within the [[Utopian Empire]]'s control. But it also needs to be survivable for the people who have to continue, you know, *surviving* wherever the party drops them off. She suggests that the actual people themselves might have places they want to go and would also have a better idea of what might be good places.
- [[Toran the Red|Toran]] reaches into her pouch and places it into [[Kolvar]]'s fist. *"Keep this safe. You will know to use it when you need to."*
- [[NG]] is itching to start a bar fight so the party leaves and heads back to [[The Everburning Chalice]].

### Bar Fight?

- The party earns free drinks for the night as they are heralded as returning heroes. Theresa takes [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]]'s drink order since this is their first night in the tavern, and she sticks to a classic G&T. [[Zrandovorski]] approves of another party member drinking a beverage without any sweetener.
- The bar is filled with a ton of colourful characters. A human walking a Cockatrice, a man with metal hands, a man in a brown cloak that they saw earlier, two Goliaths, and more.

#### A Cloaked Man

- [[NG]] asks the brown cloaked man about if he found who he was looking for. He hasn't. The cloaked man asks about the big crater where [[Caelia]] used to be, and [[NG]] says he was "there but not really present for it" (Thomas wasn't there for [[Session 24|that session]]).
	- The man leaves, and [[NG]] wishes him well on his journey. The man places a hand on [[NG]]'s shoulder and says "I'm certain we'll meet again some day. This time was a coincidence, but the next time won't be." and hands him a letter. The man allows [[NG]] to read it, and then takes the letter back before he leaves.
	- [[NG]] remarks to [[Son]], who's been watching this conversation while invisible, that he needs to write something down.

#### Michael Cera Twink With a Cockatrice

- A tall, thin man is herding a cockatrice (a half-lizard half-rooster). [[Tilly]], [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]], and [[Kolvar]] are super into this and go talk to the man. He is absolutely cooing and coddling the animal, enamoured with his... pet?
- Turns out this man is a ranger and the cockatrice is his spirit animal or whatever. [[Tilly]] excitedly reaches out to pet the cockatrice, and it goes to bite her hand. It crits on the attack and deals 13 piercing damage as it bites through her palm. She then fails a Con save afterwards, and feels the muscles in her arm begin to stiffen. It appears the poison in this creature's bite causes paralysis.
- [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] is able to *Bless* [[Tilly]] who uses her *Starry Form* to ensure she can't get lower than a 10 on a Con saving throw. She rolls a 19 and passes the save, feeling her body shrug off the paralysis.
- [[Tilly]] is PISSED but the Ranger is pretty sure it was her fault for being friendly with a clearly wild ~~animal~~ monstrosity. They bicker back and forth for a bit before [[Tilly]] storms out of the conversation.

#### The [[Draskhrr]]-ites Who Want to Leave

- [[The Collector]] and [[NG]] go to talk to the people in [[Draskhrr]] who want to leave [[The Frozen March]] to see where they'd want to be dropped off.
- One of them explains that a bunch of people actually do want to "give [[Utopia]] their comeuppance", but honestly everybody will probably follow the party's lead and won't fight with wherever they get dropped off (within reason, of course).
- [[The Collector]] suggests that it'd be important to amass allies against [[Utopia]], so there should be a code for the refugees to use that can identify them in secret. They decide on asking if they're "Friends of Tony Soprano" (a stand-in for [[Toran the Red|Toran]] that's less identifiable).
- [[NG]] suggests that they go to [[Landon's Lamp]] to drop off the people, then the party should continue South to [[The Scar of the Plane|The Scar]] to meet [[Tilly]]'s Kalashtar friend.
- The party debates this for a bit, and decides it's a sensible solution. This is also probably the only convenient time that they'll have to visit [[The Scar of the Plane|The Scar]].
	- On the subject of [[Tilly]] being a Kalashtar, [[Zrandovorski]] asks the name of her "other half". [[Tilly]] realizes she doesn't actually know.

### A Night, A Day, A Box

- The party decides to turn in for the night, but [[Tilly]] is too drunk to care.
- She heads up to the crow's nest of the ship and asks the stars what the name of her other half is. She receives no response.
- The next morning, [[NG]] opens the door to the Captain's Quarters (where he sleeps), whispers something, and slams a GP into the [[Box With a Coin Slot]]. [[The Collector]] and [[Kolvar]] are super sussed and try to listen to what he whispered. [[Kolvar]] seemed to be able to hear it but remains quiet. The party (except for [[Zrandovorski]]) is sussed.
- [[Tilly]] says "I wish my other half would talk to me" and tosses a GP into the box. Nothing happens in particular.
- [[Kolvar]] says "I wish to see more of my future" and puts a GP into the box. Nothing happens in particular.
- [[The Collector]] says "If wishes don't do anything then... Fireball!" and puts a GP into the box. Nothing happens in particular.
- [[Cline Daffenborn|Cline]] mumbles "... I wish to see her again..." and puts a GP into the box. Nothing happens in particular.
- [[Tilly]] gives [[Son]] a copper piece and he says "Milk duds!!!" and puts in the CP. Nothing happens in particular.
- [[The Collector]] and [[Kolvar]] debate using an Electrum piece instead. [[The Collector]] slides an EP into the box and says "A *Heroes Feast*!". Nothing happens in particular.
- [[Kolvar]] puts in 2 GP and says "I want to see more of my future". Nothing happens in particular.
- [[Kolvar]] shakes the box to see if they can hear the coins rattle. Curiously... they don't hear anything no matter how hard they shake it.
- The party (minus [[NG]] and [[Zrandovorski]]) are fed up with the box and leave it alone.

### The Everburning City

- The Reconstellators prepare the final ceremony before they leave [[Draskhrr]]; finally able to execute [[Session 20#Downtime Time|Tilly's plan to light the walls of the city]].
- [[Tilly]] says "You would be a beacon of hope to the people of [[The Frozen March]]! And now... you will light the way!" as she casts the ruby dust over the walls of the city. She casts *Continual Flame* to light the walls of [[Draskhrr]] with the harmless, infinitely-burning fires.
- "You are now... The Everburning [[Draskhrr]]!"
	- *[[Draskhrr]]* means *City* in Goblin.
- The party flies South to [[Landon's Lamp]] with their new guests onboard, and cross over the mountain range that has kept them trapped in [[The Frozen March]] for 28 sessions. As they crest over the peak, the sun rises on a new arc of their adventure.

**END OF ARC 1***

The party gains 170 XP, for a total of 9,070 XP!
