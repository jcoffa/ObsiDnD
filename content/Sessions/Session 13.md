## Notes

### Party 'Til You're Pregnant

- The party debates what next to do and whether to follow [[Session 12#The Plan|"The Plan" from the previous session]] to go into [[The Underdark]] entrance that they found previously. They put it to a vote and after a 4-2 result they decide to go to [[The Toppelgangers]] at their tower. Since they likely won't be down here again, they decide to cover up the entrance they found. [[Garrison]] moves a rock to cover the entrance and [[Zrandovorski]] writes a WHMIS (Wizard Harmful Magical Information System) symbol on it to quickly explain that there is both poisonous gas and dangerous Booyahg beyond this point.
- They return to the surface while escorting the rescued goblins. Tumor happily pays out the party their agreed-upon 75 GP and some Adamantine ore scraps. Easy money!
- [[NG]] attempts to put a band together in order to prepare for the party. He hands out some megaphones and makes some makeshift instruments. They end up doing a pretty good job!
- At this point in the night, it's far too late for a potential other group of Iron Fang to show up to the mine, so The Reconstellators have a rare night off! They begin playing a drinking game which involves making Constitution saving throws to chug wine as quickly as possible.
- This game is quickly abandoned after some drunk goblins approach [[Zrandovorski]] (as the only new goblinoid in this place) and asks "what his deal is". He mentions that he is a fortune teller and starts letting people pull tarot cards!

### The Tower of Toppelgangers

- The party leaves the mines first thing on the following morning. After 4 days of travel, they arrive at [[The Toppelgangers]] tower. It looks broken down and crumbling; it's either very old or something is compromising the structural integrity. There appears to be 3 patrolling units of hobgoblins and wargs around the walls that surround the tower.
- Debating how to get inside, their current plan is to pretend they're new doppelgangers from [[Utopia]] who have come to join their ranks. They're hoping to get past the patrols and into the castle and find the Obsidian Dragon that supposedly rules over the doppelgangers here.
- The party knows that Obsidian Dragons are Neutral Evil. They're mostly out for themselves, generally cruel, and do whatever is best for them. They only really take deals where they feel that they're getting more out of it than the other side, although they're not necessarily unfair about it. While they're interested in winning, they aren't looking for domination. They originate from the Elemental Planes.
- [[Zrandovorski]] knows that this is likely a similar structure to the one they found a bit ago. It's unclear who owned them back when the towers were new, but it's likely from a long-lost group of natives in [[The Frozen March]]. They all have this sort of green mold/mildew that forms in between the individual bricks of these structures.
- Following their plan, the party walks up to the guards at the entrance of the tower. [[NG]] takes point and says they're from [[Utopia]] seeking refuge. The guards ask for a password, which the party does not have. [[NG]] pleads more, saying they came here and are in need of a place to stay. The guards exchange a look, and read the surface thoughts of each party member. After some deliberation, they let the party inside.
- There are undisguised doppelgangers throughout the inside of the keep, which appears to be a relatively normal society. There are adults and children, military presence as a policing force, a tavern with several patrons, and so on. There are 7 large wolf-like creatures with red eyes held in horse stables.
- Debating whether to look for the dragon or hide out in the tavern, the party decides to try meeting the dragon. They ask an undisguised doppelganger if we can see the leader. They seem confused that the party is still disguised, and are pretty suspicious of their resistance to dropping their disguises and taking on their natural doppelganger forms. One of the guards leaves behind a large door, and [[The Collector]] sends in Son to follow.
- Son sees the guard talk to an imposing human dressed in ornate black and purple clothing. This appears to be the obsidian dragon in his humanoid form. The guard informs the dragon that imposters have infiltrated the keep and must be killed. The dragon is wholly unconcerned and says not to kill them, just send them into the throne room.
- As two more guards come in to flank the party outside, the guard who left returns from behind the door and asks the party to follow him.

### The Obsidian Dragon

- [[NG]] gets down on one knee and venerates the dragon immediately. It seems wholly unconcerned and unimpressed. It does not seem keen to deal with the party, citing that they're "more like fish in a barrel" and are even more constrained by leaving this keep alive before they can even bother with leaving [[The Frozen March]].
- [[NG]] fesses up information about the portal to [[The Abyss]] under [[Azhvan]], and the existence of [[The Game Master]] and how we have had multiple encounters with them.

The dragon presents the following two routes of leaving:

1. The observatory has one of the stable ways to [[The Frozen March]]. If you can steal the Lich's key, you can come and go freely.
2. One of the few areas in [[The Frozen March]] that is controlled by [[Utopia]] is a port town, heavily protected by the Utopian empire called [[Fair Helm City]]. If the party were to convince them that they're part of the Utopian empire *and* deserve the right to leave, it's possible to leave via that route. It would require immense cunning to sneak on to one of these ships.
	- [[Zrandovorski]] does not know this city, as the goblins wouldn't care to learn it. It's too far away from any [[The Iron Fang]] outpost to worry about assaulting it. It's roughly 3x the distance as from [[Draskhrr]] to the gate to leave [[The Frozen March]] compared to [[Draskhrr]] to [[Fair Helm City]]. The city is probably less defensible, but has more of a standing military.

- Before [[NG]] tells the dragon about [[The Game Master]], it breaks off a fleck of obsidian from a nearby rock and hands it to [[NG]]. He instructs the party to "slay [[The Game Master]]" next time they see him. The party is bewildered by this, as in their previous encounters they did not seem particularly susceptible to dying. The obsidian fleck is for *after* the party kills him. This is quite worrying.
- Before they leave, [[Tilly]] wants to try asking the dragon for information. She fesses up about the [[Session 11|crashed spaceship]].
	- The dragon responds that the party may be useful to support "his people" who have infiltrated [[The Cult of the Stars]]. They're somewhere in the West of [[The Frozen March]], but does not divulge anything more.
	- [[Tilly]] asks what she is, as she knows she isn't human. The dragon turns into his dragon form (likely for truesight) and claims she is Kalashtar. A being of two souls.
- The party is able to leave with their lives. Incredible.

### You Stumble Across A Small Hole

- The party leaves the keep, and heads back to the Adamantine mines. They encounter a small hole in the ground, and see a small animal shivering inside.
- [[Tilly]] casts *Speak With Animals* and the creature looks up at her. The creature is canine in nature; white-grey fur with glowing blue lines down its body. It has spots on its fur, but its ears appear to have feathers on it? It says *"Hungry..."* to her.
	- She succeeds on an Animal Handling check, and attempts to feed it a *Goodberry*. It's hesitant at first, but is motivated by the food and eats it from her hand. It teleports 2 feet forward and dives into her lap. She swaddles the creature in her cloak to warm it up.
	- There are no parents in sight. [[Tilly]] adopts the creature, which she identifies as a Blink Dog!
- Before their watch for the night, the party grills [[Tilly]] on her newfound species knowledge. What the hell is a Kalashtar? There is a spirit which is intertwined with their host, and these spirits are "of [[The Far Realm]]". This is not a possession or pupping type of thing, it's very much a "two become one" sort of deal.
- [[Tilly]] feels somewhat lost with this information. Her whole deal with [[The Toppelgangers]] was that they weren't supposed to be here, and now she discovers that she isn't even really from this plane either. The party tries to cheer her up by explaining that lots of things that "seem unnatural" are still part of the natural order.
	- [[NG]] says that doppelgangers aren't from this plane, but it doesn't mean they're not natural. Things arrive on the Material Plane all the time. The natural order isn't just one stream, one forest, one country, one biome. Nature involves the complex interactions between many systems interacting with each other. [[Tilly]] is as much a part of the natural order of things as anything else.
	- [[Zrandovorski]] reminds her that elves aren't native to the Material Plane either; they come from the Fey Wild.

The party earns 180 XP, for a total of 3,320 XP!
