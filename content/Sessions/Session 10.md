## Notes

### Approaching [[Azhvan]]

- After confirming that the stone-dragon-scorpion monster is roughly 2 miles to the East, the party feels confident enough to quietly approach [[Azhvan]].
- [[The Collector]] wants to swing by a tavern and asks Disco to lead them there. After being *Guided* by [[Tilly]], he does so as the party ~~sneaks~~ quietly mumbles the lyrics to [A Thousand Miles by Vanessa Carlton](https://youtu.be/Cwkej79U3ek).
- The tavern is a bit taller than most other buildings here, and the roof has partially caved in. There's a prominent stained-glass window on the front. It's one of the only buildings nearby that appears largely intact.
- 5 individuals in purple cloaks are at a table, writing fervently into some parchment as if taking a test. Another individual in front of them appears to be a lecturer of some sort, and his purple cloak is trimmed with golden fabric. A few others are off to the side in a corner, not even paying attention to the rest of them.
- The leader, noticing the party's entrance, approaches. He's a tall elf with pale skin and white hair, with a thin pencil moustache and silver spectacles. He offers the party a place to stay in this building, and explains that he is [[Dean Zee]], one of the Deans of [[The Scholars of the Deep]]. He asks the party if they're available for a bit of work.
	- He suspects that the creature is not here on its own, and that there's a specific reason it was drawn here. Potentially because of the magical presence on the city. It's either here to eat, in which case it's looking to eat magic items and other magic energy, OR it's here to nest and procreate. Like how cats prefer to lay down on warm things. They don't know what the creature is called and don't have a name for it, but kaiju-like creatures are not uncommon to hear about in [[The Underdark]].
	- [[The Collector]] asks if the magic items can be moved, and [[Dean Zee]] says "absolutely not". They're being researched and can't be moved.
	- The job is to seek some form of bio-matter from this creature. Preferably an egg, but *ahem* fecal matter will do if it's not here to nest. 1000 GP reward.
	- Stay away from its tail. That shit is dangerous. But at the very least, it's unlikely to want to fight again as it has already "conquered" this area.
- The party talks to one of the squatters in the corner (named [[Zale]]), who seems to have more info on the Kenku that Disco saw fighting the creature. They call it "The Tanku" because he was huge. It was fighting the creature as if to study it, and eventually seemed to get bored and left.
	- [[Zale]] says that he is the acting mayor of [[Azhvan]] and is looking to find whoever survived the attack. He calls the monster [[Azhvan's Collapse]].
- The party moves to a nearby 2-story building so that they can have a vantage point on [[Azhvan's Collapse]], and take a long rest.

### Rest

- The robots at [[NG]]'s old factory had a sensor to detect how large something is, useful when dealing with adults versus children. Sometimes, they would check the logs and see that they'd detect creatures with size `-1`; an error code that indicates a creature is so large that it overloaded their sensors.
- [[Tilly]] apologizes for being so flippant in the past couple days (particularly with her annoyance with [[Garrison]]'s [[Session 9#It Had to Be Spiders...|hesitation to attack the large fungal creature]]).
- During the second watch, while [[The Collector]] and [[Zrandovorski]] are arguing, [[Blake]] notices that [[Azhvan's Collapse]] is no longer in its nest and the rumbling has stopped. [[Zrandovorski]] uses *Augury* to see if it's a good idea to stick around or not, and gets a result of *Woe* (bad). The party decides to leave ASAP.

### Oh Fuck Oh Shit Oh Fuck

- Right before they leave the building, the party looks over to the creature's resting spot and see 10 egg-shaped objects in the dead center of the crater it left behind. Seems like it was both nesting *AND* planning on eating all the magic items.
- [[NG]] goes back to [[Zale]] to collect the squatters in hopes that the party can help them escape. After gathering 7 survivors, they leave. Disco leads the way out of [[The Underdark]].
- After some pretty darn good Stealth rolls, The Reconstellators feel a rumbling beneath their feet once more. In the distance they hear a villager cry for help, and then witness in horror as an enormous scorpion tail shoots up out of the ground and tears him asunder.
- As they run farther away from the city, following Disco's lead, they see in the distance a huge creature with a plump belly standing at 13 feet tall carrying a large club. It walks to nearby bushes and shouts *"FOUND YOU!"* while plucking animals from the bush and swallowing them whole. Disco confirms the creature is a hill giant, and that the ladder exit is about 300 feet behind it.
- [[The Collector]] and [[Blake]] plan to bring the giant to the side and distract it with a story about the 10 eggs near [[Azhvan]]. [[NG]] can use *Elementalism* to make bacon-scented smoke come from behind the party to hopefully help persuade the giant to head in that direction. The plan works, and with some convincing the giant enthusiastically runs over in that direction.
- [[NG]] and [[Garrison]] climb the ladder first, since the previous one was unstable and they can use some tinkering ability to stabilize the ladder. They make it to the top after securing the ladder, and [[NG]] pulls out a piece of scrap cloth from his Weaver's Tool kit and wraps a pebble with *Light* cast on it to drop down the hole. It hits the bottom as expected, so the rest of the party is able to climb up the (now well-secured) ladder without issue.
- The party is able to stop in the same cave halfway up the ladder where they originally entered [[The Underdark]] from. It's a convenient place to try another long rest.

### Rest (for real this time)

- [[NG]] asks [[Blake]] "what their deal is". [[Blake]] essentially says that they're a contract killer who got caught. [[NG]] remarks that being a contract killer is a nihilistic way to live, and he doesn't understand the line of work very well. [[Garrison]] is shocked that [[Blake]] kills people for money. [[NG]] imparts the wisdom that killing for money tends to come back around and bite you in the ass in unexpected ways down the line.
- During the next watch, Son returns by climbing up the ladder ([[The Collector]] didn't recall him like he usually does) and confirms the creature has left through a giant golden portal underground. Wait, what? Son isn't sure where it leads. After *MUCH* difficulty, [[The Collector]] is able to give Son more commands to check on [[Zale]] and see what's on the other side of the golden portal.
- After finishing the rest, Son returns. It was unable to find [[Zale]] and presumes he's not in [[Azhvan]] anymore. He confirmed that the golden portal leads to [[The Abyss]].

### The Hag

- It's a very nice day in [[The Frozen March]] today. The sun is shining and it's barely even a half-blizzard. It's a balmy -15 degrees!
- As the party walks, a small snow flurry blocks their vision briefly, and when they regain vision there is a quaint wooden shack nearby. On its door is a flip-sign that says *"The Witch is Inn ;)"* as like a coy little note.
- After knocking, a voice is heard inside welcome the party inside. The witch is quite friendly, and cheerfully offers to barter for items.
- Disco is super skeptical of this Witch, claiming it's obviously a Hag. The party quickly leaves, but not before the ~~witch~~ hag gives [[Tilly]] a business card for "[[Lady of the White]] - Hag Extraordinaire". She says that the party can place the card on top of dead bodies and she'll grant them a reward.

### The Pit Trap

- As the party continues, they successfully identify a pit trap before falling into it. After getting Son to trigger it, they look into the hole and see 10 stone statues of determined-looking humanoids and a bunch of gold haphazardly across the floor of the pit. After some investigation, they find some poop on the bottom and a painting on one of the far walls.
- The painting is of 4 tieflings, all of different shades. One violet, one gold, and the 2 children are red. The painting is titled "My Family" and signed *[[Charisma]]*. This is the second painting they've found by this artist.
	- [[Zrandovorski]] knows this is a niche artist who lives near [[The Scar of the Plane]] and is one of the many tieflings who resides there. She is one of the closest things to tiefling nobility that exists, but would be considered more of a diplomat than some kind of Queen. The art she produces is more of a side-hustle that she does.
	- One of the two children in this painting matches the description of *[[Charisma]]*. She is an adult now, so this would be a painting of her and her family when she was a little girl.
- [[The Collector]], looking through Son's eyes in the pit trap, finds a door hidden on one of the walls. Behind it is a long hallway, and a humanoid holding a lit torch. Though the torch is lit it's difficult to make out its features, as though the light does not extend past the humanoid's forearm. As Son walks closer, the darkness dissipates and in front of him sits a held-out torch by what looks to be a person whose arm is shackled to the ground, a pentagram drawn on the floor, and his jaw is slack open as if it broke a long time ago. The humanoid appears alive, but due to its jaw is unable to speak coherently beyond muffled gurgling.
- The human's eyes track Son, but otherwise does not seem to be particularly cognizant.
- The party tries to figure out what's going on here. [[Blake]] and [[Zrandovorski]] take another look at the pit trap, and determine that it's probably unlikely to have been *intended* as a pit trap. It's more likely that this was an underground room and the roof caved in from age and general disrepair.
- Son determines the pentagram is a *Circle of [Planar Binding](https://5e.tools/spells.html#planar%20binding_xphb)*. He walks closer to the human and attempts to grab the torch. It doesn't budge, but he tracks Son's movements and drops the torch. It takes a spinal disc out by reaching into a hole on its shoulder and attempts to pick the lock binding it to the wall. [[Tilly]] uses *Druidcraft* to re-light the torch, and the human looks down at the torch, stops picking the lock, and picks the torch back up again
- [[Tilly]] uses her mind link to attempt to communicate with the human. After connecting and allowing communication back, she is succumbed to a Wisdom saving throw. After succeeding, she hears in her head that the creature is the leader of the cult of Larizadune.

The party gains 290 XP, for a total of 2,160!
