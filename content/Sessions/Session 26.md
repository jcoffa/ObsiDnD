## Recap

The mission: [[Change the State of Benedict]]. The problem: we are in a [[Fair Helm City]] prison cell and have no idea who or what "Benedict" is.

As it turns out, escaping prison was the easy part as the guard near their cell is asleep and passed out drunk allowing [[NG]] to pick the lock on their cell and quietly open the door. In his drunken stupor, the guard easily believes [[NG]]'s lie that they're penetration testers sent here by [[Utopia]] to ensure their assets are well handled, essentially granting them free access to [[Fair Helm City]] without much issue.

Unfortunately, they do discover the *real* hard part: Who the hell is Benedict? And why is everyone acting like there's more than one of them? Perhaps you can't expect a quest given by a Fae to be straightforward. After some sleuthing with various residents and guards in [[Fair Helm City]], the party discovers there are 4 things that could potentially be considered "Benedict" for the sake of [[Change the State of Benedict|their puzzlingly vague quest]]:

1. *Benedict's*: a popular bar that is currently hosting a huge party for most of the city's night-life.
2. *[[Robert Benedict]]*: the depressed owner of *Benedict's* who wishes for a life of adventure that was taken from him after inheriting the bar of his late father; Benedict Benedict Sr.
3. *The SS Benedict*: the famed ship of [[Robert Benedict]]'s brother, [[Ben Benedict|Benedict Benedict Jr.]] (he mostly goes by [[Ben Benedict]]).
4. *[[Ben Benedict|Captain Ben Benedict]]*: a famed adventurer and twin brother of [[Robert Benedict]] who's goal is to see the world with his pirate crew.

Well. That's a bit of a wrinkle. The plot thickens as some of the party continues talking to [[Robert Benedict]] at *Benedict's*, and the other half later meet up with his brother, [[Ben Benedict]], while posing as reporters for *The Utopian Spectator* to get both sides of the story. They discover that the brothers have a bit of a feud going on after their father died 10 years ago. You see, Benedict Benedict Sr. raised his eldest son, [[Ben Benedict|Benedict Benedict Jr.]], to inherit his famed bar, *Benedict's*. But mere minutes before his death, he changed his will to give it to the other brother, [[Robert Benedict]], instead. It was always [[Robert Benedict]]'s dream to see the world as an adventurer, but now he feels duty-bound to upkeep his late father's bar out of respect to him and his legacy. Since his twin brother, [[Ben Benedict]], doesn't have anything tying him down, he is now free to live out [[Robert Benedict]]'s dream of seeing the world after getting himself a ship with his inheritance money and becoming [[Ben Benedict|Captain Benedict]] sailing on *The SS Benedict*, named after his late father, Benedict Benedict Sr. [[Robert Benedict]] feels cheated out of his dream and resents his brother, [[Ben Benedict|Captain Benedict]], who he feels has never really wanted to be an adventurer and is only doing it to spite him. Meanwhile, [[Ben Benedict|Captain Benedict]] feels his brother, [[Robert Benedict]], is a stick-in-the-mud and a sad sack of shit. Both brothers implore the party to carry out various deeds in order to shake-up the other brother's life somehow; [[Robert Benedict]] wants [[Sink Benedict's Ship|to see The SS Benedict sink]], and [[Ben Benedict|Captain Benedict]] wants to "free his brother from the shackles of duty" by [[Burn Down Benedict's Bar|burning down Benedict's bar]]; *Benedict's*. The party is overjoyed at how easy it is to keep track of all these details due to how unique and easily identifiable all the different puzzle pieces are.

Meanwhile, [[Kolvar]] talks to some of [[Ben Benedict|Captain Benedict]]'s crew and discovers that they're acting as smugglers for [[Utopia]] to ferry out contraband and rare resources from [[The Frozen March]] to the ~~Benedict~~ *benefit* of the empire. [[The Collector]] and [[Son]] are able to search the cargo hold of *The SS Benedict* while the others keep [[Ben Benedict|Captain Benedict]] busy in his quarters via their reporting scheme. [[Son]] is able to get past a trapped door rigged to explode by polymorphing into a centipede, and he discovers the decaying corpse of a hobgoblin, its still-beating heart in a jar (much like [[Session 6#Heart to Heart|the one they picked up a while ago]]), and its gruesome pulsating innards spilled out on the floor. [[Son]], of course, collects all of it into the [[Bag of Holding]]. He also collects several jars filled with rocks and cuts off the head of the hobgoblin corpse to add to the [[Bag of Holding]] as well. [[The Collector]] and [[Son]] are the only ones who know these two last items are in there.

The party is worried they're losing the plot (literally), so they reconvene and figure out a plan to free them of their [[Change the State of Benedict|ridiculously confusing quest]]. They come up with [[#The Plan|a plan]] that they hope will be a laughably ironic outcome and satisfy the [[Unseelie Queen]] who got them into this mess in the first place. Essentially, they aim to complete both of the brother's quests at the same time by luring them out into the middle of the city, then [[Burn Down Benedict's Bar]] *AND* [[Sink Benedict's Ship]] while they're both occupied with each other, finally converting *The SS Benedict* into their bastion using their [[Helm]] and sailing out of the city as fast as they can.

[[Garrison]], [[Furret]], and [[Tilly]] successfully get all the bar patrons out of [[Robert Benedict]]'s bar, *Benedict's*, so that [[Kolvar]] can disguise themselves as [[Ben Benedict|Captain Benedict]] to lure [[Robert Benedict]] out by urging him to sell the bar and set himself up for his own life of adventure (which is actually what the real [[Ben Benedict|Captain Benedict]] said anyways). This works, and [[Robert Benedict]] follows [[Kolvar|Captain Kolbenedict]] to *The SS Benedict*. Once they're out of range, [[Son]] finally detonates the [[Gunpowder Bomb]] while also managing to notice *and successfully escape with* [[Sink Benedict's Ship|the rewards from fulfilling Robert Benedict's quest to sink his brother's ship]] before it brings down the building. [[NG]], who was already at *The SS Benedict* since it's his job to get the real [[Ben Benedict|Captain Ben Benedict]] off his ship, tells him about his brother's bar going up in flames and urges him to go see his brother. This works, partially because the fire is spreading to the other nearby buildings as well, and the ship is now empty save for [[NG]] and [[Zrandovorski]]. Good timing too, as the rest of The Reconstellators make a mad dash to get on the ship so they can all escape together. [[Kolvar|Captain Kolbenedict]] pulls the oldest trick in the book ("hey look a cool bird!") to shake off [[Robert Benedict]] so that the *real* [[Ben Benedict|Captain Ben Benedict]] can meet up with him later. [[Zrandovorski]] and [[NG]] are finally able to use their [[Helm]], thanks to [[NG]] fashioning his maker's mark out of Electrum for the necessary spell component and [[Zrandovorski]]'s ability to cast **Create Airship** since the party is now 5th level. Suddenly, an army of thousands of magical snow rats with tiny toolbelts and hard hats begin expanding the boat to turn it into a fully functional OSHA-compliant airship. The Reconstellators are reunited once again, their airship-bastion fully formed (with wheelchair-accessible on-ramp and railings on both sides of all stairwells) carefully and peacefully sail out of [[Fair Helm City]]'s port and then *HOLY CRAP GET US THE HELL OUT OF HERE* by flying away at full speed once they're out of range of the city's anti-aircraft weaponry.

The session ends with the party taking inventory on all the magic items they put into the [[Bag of Holding]] throughout their day, including a lengthy investigation into a [[Box With a Coin Slot]] that was on [[Ben Benedict|Captain Benedict]]'s ship. They saw him put a gold piece into the slot absentmindedly at one point during their reporting scheme, and he didn't seem to think much of it beyond that moment. [[Zrandovorski]] identified the box, but refused to explain what he discovered, if anything. [[NG]] quickly got fed up with the situation, and also identified it. He also became tight-lipped about it after. Both of them strongly urged [[The Collector]] to *not* succumb to his curiosity and explained that "he would be far more disappointed if he identified it himself". Both refused to elaborate on what that meant, or provide any more details about the box beyond a simple "It's a box. With a coin slot."

Easiest quest ever. We can never return here. Wait, did anybody remember to [[Session 20#Downtime Time|buy ruby dust]] for [[Tilly]] before we set the city on fire?
