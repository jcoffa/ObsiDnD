## Notes

### Behind Bars Before Breaking Benedict

- The Reconstellators feel feathers and leaves fall around them onto the cold concrete floor of [[Fair Helm City]]'s temporary prison. They're behind bars in a cell with not much besides a couple cots. 40 feet outside of the bars is an old man with a long white beard in a rocking chair, half asleep. A brown glass bottle with an **XXX** on its label is by his chair.
- [[NG]] suggests the party pretend they're spies for [[Utopia]] but there was a mixup and we got charged as *real* prisoners and put here by mistake. He's able to create thieve's tools and unlock the door to their cell. He attempts to lift the keyring off of the old man, but notices it's attached to a string that leads off to... somewhere. Could be trapped!
- [[NG]], with the help of [[Tilly]], cuts his belt off to get around the trapped string. He acquires the keyring, and braces the party to start acting like they belong here. He goes to backhand the sleeping guard, setting the party up as if they're auditors and this guard just failed his audit.
- [[Kolvar]] thinks about what he could disguise himself as, and thinks about modern [[Utopia|Utopian]] society. The upper class are very snobby and full of themselves, basically pretending that "the lower class" does not exist. They disguise themselves as a snobby elf.
- [[NG]] proceeds with backhanding the guard and commands him to "stand at attention", claiming they're penetration testers and he has failed hilariously. The guard stumbles and is clearly flustered. [[NG]] asks to speak to the manager, and the man is visibly distraught. He says that he can get the party someone who will lead them to their superiors.
- A man walks down a hallway further down, named "Jerry". He seems well-kept, a human, and [[NG]] explains to him what happened. Jerry laments that "this is the 2nd time this month" and that they "did so well last time".
- [[NG]] takes this opportunity to slip in "oh, and get Benedict as well" to which Jerry replies "Oh... well, which one?".
- Fuck.

### Benedict's

- The jail in [[Fair Helm City]] is at the top of a large hill that gently slopes downwards. Man-made stone steps are every 10 feet. The docks are visible from this position; there appears to be two ships there but the docks can hold 10.
- There's a stone tower that scoops upward like a talon, converging in a peak at the top. These are the guard's quarters. Jerry asks [[NG]] if he wants to wake up the captain now. [[NG]] answers his question with a question; asking "what can you get done in an hour?". Jerry is confused, and [[NG]] reiterates that he's providing him an opportunity to "clean up" and make the results of the penetration test more favourably. Jerry runs back to the jail in a hurry. The party has successfully lost any incoming suspicion!
- [[Zrandovorski]] looks down the steps to the water, and notices that the top 2 layers of are pretty dark and quiet while the 3rd and 4th layers, closest to the water, still is very well lit and has a lot of buildings with chimneys billowing smoke.
- Guards patrol in threes. One holding a torch, one holding a sword, and one holding a crossbow.
- As the party walks down to the port, someone asks them if they're "coming down to the party", which apparently started hours ago. The party is taking place at... *Benedict's*. Interesting.
- Benedict's is apparently a bar, and it's PACKED in here. All manner of people are inside; humans, tabaxi, dragonborn, even a goblin.
- [[NG]] walks up to the nearest elf, who's drinking a whiskey-based drink, and asks if he's drinking a Fashioned. The elf is ecstatic that someone else knows about Fashioneds, and all the elves in the bar immediately turn to the person closest to them and ask "Hey, did you know that it used to be-" and everyone else is like "YES WE KNOW THEY USED TO BE CALLED FASHIONEDS, BUT THEY'RE *OLD FASHIONEDS* NOW". Apparently this has been going on all night.
- [[Tilly]] and [[The Collector]] walk up to the bartender, who looks kind of depressed. [[Tilly]] asks if he's Benedict, and he's like "Oh, well that's my family name. you can just call me Robert." Apparently, his twin brother was supposed to take over the tavern, but he was off travelling the world. But he's supposed to be in town right now.
	- Sounds like the  target of [[Change the State of Benedict]] could be either of the twins?
- Benedict explains that his twin brother gets to explore the world, and he's stuck here. His brother stole his dream and he can't sell the tavern out of respect for his father. The only thing holding him back is his brother; he's an egotistical prick who may have stolen his own dream out of spite. He doesn't even think his brother likes boats. He's been ferrying imports and exports back and forth to [[Utopia]].
- The conversation switches to Benedict's wants (after the party "accidentally" lets slip that they're spies for [[Utopia]]), he would be able to reward them handsomely if his brother's ship, *The SS Benedict*, were to sink.
	- Benedict then confirms that the only Benedict's here are him and his brother. And technically the ship.
	- [[Sink Benedict's Ship]]

### Interrogation

- [[Kolvar]] asks around the bar and finds a Tabaxi merchant. Turns out this person is a sailor on Benedict's ship under captain Benedict; Benedict's brother whom the party was just hired to sink. [[Kolvar]] says that they're a merchant as well, and the tabaxi asks what's the name of his ship in port. Sage loses their mind trying to guess what that ship might be called, settling on "The Silver Schooner" and the tabaxi goes "Oh yeah that's a cool name. Nice." Sage thought they knew the name and was testing them, but he was just making conversation. He ends up accidentally implying that they perform human trafficking between [[The Frozen March]] and [[Utopia]] on behalf of the [[Utopia|Utopian]] government, and the tabaxi becomes fearful.
- Ultimately [[Kolvar]] learns that Benedict's ship leaves [[Fair Helm City]] in a few days after they pick up "some important cargo", and Benedict, their captain, is just chilling on the ship for now.
- The party debates what to do next. Do they sink *The SS Benedict*, or go talk to Captain Benedict, or kill Bartender Benedict, or burn down *Benedict's*, or steal *The SS Benedict* and use the [[Helm]] to turn it into the airship and use it to escape?
- The party decides to go talk to Captain Benedict. [[Garrison]] walks outside to see a small group ogling his ferret steed. He majestically mounts it and shouts "Onward!" as he rides it off down to the port with the rest of The Reconstellators.
- *The SS Benedict* has its gangplank down so the ship is accessible, but Captain Benedict is in his quarters. The party formulates a plan:
	- [[Kolvar]] and [[NG]] pretend to be reporters for "The Utopian Spectator" and want to interview the captain.
	- [[Tilly]] will Mind Link with [[NG]] so that he can communicate if they need an out. She will pretend to be drunk and ask them all to come to the party.

### Hit Two Benedicts With One Crime (Arson)

- The party reconvenes and catches each other up to speed. They discuss the possibility of achieving both quests at the same time. [[NG]] suggests that the fae LOVE ironic outcomes, and the most ironic outcome would be to have the Benedict's swap each other's lives. Captain Ben Benedict would take over the bar and probably grow to hate his life, and Robert Benedict would fulfill his dream of becoming an adventurer.
	- Alternatively they could just follow both quests to [[Burn Down Benedict's Bar]] and [[Sink Benedict's Ship]]. This is more straightforward.
- They decide to combine the plans and have the brothers meet up in the middle and then [[Burn Down Benedict's Bar]] and [[Sink Benedict's Ship]] while they're occupied.
- After some honestly pretty good cooking, the party comes up with the following plan:

#### The Plan

1. [[The Collector]] and [[Tilly]] lie to the people in the bar that a neighbouring establishment is giving out free booze and a ferret petting zoo.
2. [[Son]] sets the bar on fire using [[Gunpowder Bomb|Plan C (blow himself up with the bomb)]] to lure Ben Benedict into getting off the boat to go check it out.
3. [[NG]] and [[Zrandovorski]] hold out on the boat to use the [[Helm]] and convert it into an airship.
4. While the Benedicts are dealing with the burning bar and meeting up with each other (probably wasting time having a heart-to-heart) everyone reconvenes back at the ship.
5. The party flies the airship while it's still in the water so they can appear to be sailing out of the dock normally. Once they're out of range of the city, they make it fly and get the hell out of it.

### Lights, Bombs, Action

- Ferret performs a Performance check to make itself really cute and help lure people out of the bar. It rolls an 8, so not like crazy cutesy at the moment.
- [[Kolvar]] disguises them-self as Captain Ben Benedict and walks over to the bar.
- [[The Collector]] and [[Tilly]] come into *Benedict's* and say that a neighbouring bar, *Donovan's*, is giving out a bunch of free booze and has a ferret petting zoo. With a 22 Persuasion, most people leave the bar. Robert Benedict looks betrayed and stares daggers into them.
- [[Garrison]] would like to help the rest of the patrons out of the bar. They're all super drunk so he can easily just pick them up and bring them out. Barkeeper Robert Benedict is very confused.
- [[Kolvar|Kolben]] walks in to the bar and Barkeeper Robert Benedict is shocked. He mostly repeats the talking points of the *real* Captain Ben Benedict; how he is duty-bound for no reason and it would be better to just sell the bar and set himself up for his own life of adventure. Barkeeper Robert Benedict follows [[Kolvar|Kolben]] to the boat after an invitation to come drinking in celebration.
- [[Son]] prepares to light the bomb after covering the ground and walls with booze from the bar. He notices [[Sink Benedict's Ship|the rewards from Barkeeper Robert Benedict's quest]]. Without hesitation he picks them up and starts running away from the bar. The bomb explodes in a blaze of glory as [[Son]] makes his escape.
- [[NG]] is using a spyglass to see that the first part of the plan is done. [[Zrandovorski]] is in the hold, using the [[Helm]] to create an airship. [[NG]], still as the reporter, urges Captain Ben Benedict to go see his brother. He leaves without any check needed.
- [[Kolvar|Kolben]] needs to ditch Barkeeper Robert Benedict. So he pulls out the oldest trick in the book; "Hey look a cool bird!" and rolls a 25 for Deception. Flawless. He goes invisible and easily ditches the brother.
- [[NG]] quickly fashions the Electrum into his maker's mark, and [[Zrandovorski]] casts **Create Airship** using the [[Helm]].
	- An army of thousands of magical OSHA-compliant snow rants with tiny toolbelts and hard hats begin expanding the boat to turn it into a fully functional airship.
- [[NG]] is able to steer the ship with a 21 Tinker check, and makes it out of the hex far enough away from [[Fair Helm City]] so that it can take off without being targeted by its anti-aircraft weaponry.
