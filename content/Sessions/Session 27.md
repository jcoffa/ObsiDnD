## Notes

### The Great Escape

- The Reconstellators have taken off on their stolen ship-turned-bastion, successfully making it out of [[Fair Helm City]]. As they head back to [[Draskhrr]], there are a few items to consider:
	1. What should the ship be named?
	2. What do we do with the active bombs that [[Son]] removed from the trapped door?
	3. Do we want to go anywhere before we get to [[Draskhrr]]?
	4. What do we do with the hobgoblin corpse?
	5. Is [[Garrison]] OK after all those morally grey actions in [[Fair Helm City]]?

#### What Should the Shop Be Named?

[[Polaris]], by unanimous decision. That was easy!

#### Some Days You Just Can't Get Rid of a Bomb!

- The bomb was set to go off when it was *impacted* by something (mainly, the door closing or opening). So it is live, but if handled carefully it should be OK.
- [[NG]] casts *Enlarge/Reduce* on the bomb to make it half as big. He, with [[The Collector]]'s help and *Guidance*, is able to roll a 4+4+4=12 total for Sleight of Hand. The bomb begins arming and [[NG]] only rolls a 5-2=3 for Athletics so he is unable to make it to the edge fast enough. Instead, he throws the bomb upwards to get it as far away from the ship as possible. With a 15 DC, he gets it away from the ship but the explosion still clips him and he takes 22 force damage.

#### Is [[Garrison]] OK?

- [[NG]] visits [[Garrison]], who has holed himself up in the barracks of the ship. He remarks that this would be a resounding success for most people, but he knows [[Garrison]] better than most.
- [[Garrison]] expresses remorse for how they messed up the lives of 2 people (the Benedicts). It reminds him of "how he and [[NG]] got to [[The Frozen March]] in the first place".
- [[NG]] says that, the core of why he took that [[Change the State of Benedict|Fae deal]], is because he's a coward. The party has assured people that [[The Frozen March]] isn't a Hell, but as far as he's seen that's not been the case. This place SUCKS. He sees that, from a moral calculus standpoint, being able to save a city for the price of 2 messed up lives is pretty good. But it sure doesn't feel that way.
- [[NG]] reminds [[Garrison]] that he knows his purpose now; he was created to hunt him down. That's going to come to a head eventually, despite how they've been loyal friends for hundreds of years.
- [[Garrison]] recalls that all Warforged are created for some purpose, but for a long time he thought he was created just to... live. But now, he knows that his purpose to hunt down his greatest friend. He agrees that they will have to resolve that at some point, but he genuinely hopes that there's a silver lining here that would absolve [[NG]] of any guilt for what he may have done to the factory. But regardless of the *reason*, if that outcome is resolved... then what does he do?
- [[NG]] assures him that he is *the most unique bot he's ever met*. There is nobody else like him. He has never met another person with a greater sense of justice and valour. "Don't let my cowardice screw up what you've got going on". He reconciles that, although a lot of good came out of them having access to an airship, it can't be called a good deed. As a friend, he reminds [[Garrison]] that, no matter what, they're friends to the end.
- [[Garrison]] smiles, pats [[NG]] on the head, and remarks that it's good to have a friend as good as [[NG]] by his side.

#### What to Do With the Corpse?

- [[The Collector]] shares that [[Son]] cut off its head and put it in the [[Bag of Holding]].
- [[Zrandovorski]] uses *Detect Magic* to see what's up. The body is non-magical, but the heart-in-a-jar is magical. It has 3 schools of magic; Divination, Enchantment, and Necromancy.
- [[The Collector]], recalling that both heart-in-a-jars that they have are beating in sync, suggests that they're beating for the benefit of some other creature.
- [[Tilly]] makes a Medicine check (17) to see if the heart belongs to the hobgoblin corpse. It does appear that the cavity in his chest does look like it would have been his heart. However, its off-colour suggests that something was done to it between the time it was removed from his chest and put in the jar. It's almost like... the blood pumping through the heart is of the wrong blood type compared to the blood type of the hobgoblin it came from.
- The party suggests they could put the *Speak With Dead* necklace they got from the Marquis on the corpse. Somewhat complicated since [[Son]] cut the head off and ate the neck.

#### Do We Go Somewhere Before [[Draskhrr]]?

- The party maps out some possible courses given the starting position of [[Fair Helm City]].
	- *Note for the map calculations: it's currently measured by days of travel (1 day = 2 tiles by walking). The airship gives 3 tiles per day by flight. So you just multiply the ruler's "days" output by 2/3 to get the "days by flight".*
	- [[Fair Helm City]] to [[Draskhrr]] is 9 days by flight. They can stop by [[The Hearth of Hope]] essentially for free on day 2.667 of that journey.
	- They aim to pick up the people from [[Caelia]] who set out on their pilgrimage to [[The Hearth of Hope]] and would arrive there by the time the party arrives. They can pick up the [[Caelia|Caelians]] and then stop by [[Draskhrr]] and pick up [[The Hearth of Hope]] people who arrived in [[Draskhrr]] with [[Toran the Red|Toran]] that already said they wanted to leave.
- [[The Collector]] throws a fistful of Father's ashes overboard while they fly, then falls asleep.

### On the Winds Again

The party still takes watches because it's possible that a dragon can still show up. That's basically the only thing that can challenge them while flying though.

#### Watch 1

- On the first watch, [[NG]] and [[Kolvar]] talk to pass the time. [[NG]] asks them if there's anything they want to do, since it kind of seems that they're just people-pleasing and hasn't really weighed in on any major party decisions. [[Kolvar]] doesn't seem bothered and is happy to go with the flow.
	- [[NG]] asks if they consider their shape-changing ability a "miracle". [[Kolvar]] seems pretty nonchalant about it all and is quite happy with their choice. They do have a flashback to a field of flowers, and see a bleeding wound in their chest before snapping back to reality. They double-down and nonchalantly confirm that nothing's really wrong and there's no reason to really think about their past at all.
- [[NG]] offers to go wake up the others for their watch, leaving [[Kolvar]] alone for a few minutes.
- [[Kolvar]], once the coast is clear, begins entreating with their patron. Sitting cross-legged speaking in tongues. 
	- For a moment, their body is now of a Changeling; unmodified. They sit in front of their patron. They are an unwavering beauty; the encapsulation of sunlight, glitter reflecting, angelic wings and a feathered cloak, the ripple of a calm lake, and so on.
	- "Your majesty, forgive me of my sudden intrusion.", [[Kolvar]] says.
	- [[Kolvar]] asks if the patron was the one who gave the mission to [[Change the State of Benedict]], and if they succeeded?
	- The patron asks why they would care of success or failure.
	- An enormous feathered arm from within the cloak reaches out and grabs [[Kolvar]]'s shoulder. *"I had fun."*, it says.
	- [[Kolvar]] mentions the flash of their past that they saw. The patron asks, *"How certain do you consider it to not be of your future?"*
	- [[Kolvar]] thanks their patron for the wisdom, and they receive a dandelion. When they awake from their trance, they are holding the dandelion; the same flower they saw in their previous vision.

- He sees a feminine figure with a blade at their side, unclear whether its the past, present, or future. This is the most vivid vision he's had in over 100 years; typically they're cryptic riddles. The last time this happened was when.. he was with his family.

- As the blade drips with the blood of the [[Utopia]] guard, and she screams in the air. The perspective changes and her face becomes visible; the face of [[Zrandovorski]]'s sister [[Ekvenur]]. Around her neck, a pendant with the symbol of [[Savras]].

#### Watch 2.1 (What's [[Garrison]] Up To?)

- [[Garrison]] has sent the night atoning for his actions, alone in the barracks. He doesn't appear to be doing anything physically; his atonement is all in his head as he thinks.
- He goes over the tenants of his oath, making promises to do better and uphold them as much as he can, vowing to do more good elsewhere to makeup for his mistakes.
- [[Garrison]] does not have a higher power; it's more his own internal sense of justice and the Oath he upholds for himself. He is not weak, but many other people are. Therefore, it is his duty to uphold the righteous path so that others do not have to.

### The Journey to [[The Hearth of Hope]]

- Day 1 passes by uneventfully.
- On day 2, [[NG]] notices 5 white flying creatures off in the distance. He attempts to steer the ship out of the way, but is unable to fully escape their sight. These are 5 white dragon wyrmlings. Combat is inevitable, but his Intelligence roll to steer allows for 2 preparatory cannon shots and advantage on Initiative rolls.
- Supposing that these wyrmlings are the hatchlings from [[Session 18#On the Road Again|the eggs that they found previously]], the party is hesitant to kill them outright. the wyrmlings take advantage of their hesitation, and 2 of them unleash an icy breath that strike the hull of the ship. 2 more are flying overhead, and the last one lands on the front of the deck. The party has missed their opportunity to use the cannons, but can still benefit from the advantage on Initiative.
- [[Tilly]] casts *Fog Cloud* on the deck of the ship to cover them in camouflage, hopefully allowing them to deter the dragons.

#### Round 1

- [[Garrison]] swings [[Ol' Reliable]] at the dragon on the deck, rolling a 13 to hit and missing. He rolled a 2 on the die; this is the lowest he could've possibly rolled without critical failing. His second attack is a 17, which hits! He deals 13 slashing damage and 13 radiant damage with a *Divine Smite*. The dragon is visibly shaken (and considered bloodied) after being hit this hard, and suspects that the dragon has realized it's bitten off more than it can chew. Its morale is broken and it will likely flee.
- [[Kolvar]] doesn't feel great about trying to kill the now injured one on the deck, and looks to the ones flying beside the ship which just attacked. They *Eldritch Blast* one of the ones off to the side, hitting with a 25 on the first and an 18 with the second. They deal 2 and 5 force damage respectively, and the dragon does not appear shaken at all. It's still eager for a supposedly easy meal.
- [[The Collector]] also uses *Eldritch Blast* on the same dragon that [[Kolvar]] attacked. It hits with 17 for 6 force damage, and the dragon still holds its courage. The second also hits with 17 and deals 12 force damage, which clips the joint on the dragon's wing and *does* manage to break its morale. The dragon begins to veer away from the combat which it has now realized is more dangerous than expected.
- [[NG]] assesses the situation and throws a *Faerie Fire* at the two dragons flying overhead. One dragon rolls a 15 which exactly passes the DC, but the other fails and is now visible through the clouds. He teleports below deck and begins to arm the cannons.
- [[Zrandovorski]], now seeing the outline of one of the dragons overhead, attempts to guess at where the second overhead dragon is positioned. He guesses that the one they can see is to the right of the one they can't see, so he places the *Shatter* to the left of the visible dragon. This ends up being correct and he manages to hit both dragons with the spell. The visible one succeeds on the DC with a 17 and remains stoic after taking 6 thunder damage, and the invisible one fails and has its morale broken after taking 13 thunder damage.
- [[Son]] flies up above the clouds and attempts to scare the visible dragon overhead. The dragon fails its save with a 13, and is now frightened.
- [[Furret]] attempts to attack the dragon on the deck, and misses with a 9. i crie ery tiem
- The dragon on the deck takes off and flies away, half limping, down towards the ground.
- The remaining stoic dragon on the side regenerates its breath weapon and fires it at [[NG]] who is still peeking out over the side. He rolls a nat 20 (for a total of 21 for a Con save) and takes ~~22~~ 11 cold damage. It moves onto the deck and lands in front of [[The Collector]] and [[Kolvar]].
- [[Tilly]] does a sweet slide under the dragons legs and slams the ground with a *Thunderwave* to attack the stoic dragon that just landed on the deck. It rolls a 13 and fails the Con save, taking 17 thunder damage and is pushed 10 feet away. It keeps its morale and does not flee. She uses her bonus action to use *Starry Form: Archer* and hits the dragon to deal 7 radiant damage. This does finally break its morale!
- The overhead dragon notices the 3 adventurers in a line, and uses its breath weapon. [[The Collector]] passes with a 14 to take ~~27~~ 13 cold damage, while [[Tilly]] and [[Kolvar]] fail and take 27 cold damage. The dragon flies down to land next to [[NG]] while he preps the cannons.

#### Round 2

- [[Garrison]] mounts [[Furret]] and has his mount dash 120 feet towards the stairs and down into the lower decks to support [[NG]] with the dragon. He hits with a 20 and deals 15 piercing damage with his lance and 6 more radiant damage. This is enough to kill the dragon as his lance goes in one side and out the other, which ends the combat as this is the last stoic dragon in combat.

### Picking Up the Dregs of [[Caelia]]

- The party arrives in [[The Hearth of Hope]] and [[NG]] immediately sets out repairing the ship.
- [[The Soul Sage|Vicci]] comes over to greet the party. [[The Collector]] explains that 30 spots on their ship are promised to [[Toran the Red|Toran]]'s band who wants to leave, meaning they can't afford to take every [[Caelia|Caelian]] with them out of [[The Frozen March]].
- [[The Collector]] addresses the [[Caelia|Caelians]] and tells them that they are able to be dropped off in [[Draskhrr]] if they wish. His speech is rousing enough that they allow the party to choose who comes with them.
	- The ship can hold 300-ish people excluding the party and there are 305 [[Caelia|Caelians]] (including [[The Soul Sage|Vicci]]).
	- 30 spots are promised to [[Toran the Red|Toran]]'s group.
	- [[The Hearth of Hope]] is a way smaller town than [[Draskhrr]], so any amount that they leave here would be fine due to the smaller number.
	- The party suspects that leaving 105 [[Caelia|Caelians]] here would work and then they could bring 200 back to [[Draskhrr]], assuming some of them would stay there and some would probably journey back to [[The Hearth of Hope]] with supplies and knowledge from [[Draskhrr]]. Perhaps helping to establish more of a relationship between the two.
- [[Tilly]] visits the grave of [[Blake]] to regale him of The Reconstellators escapades. This is the first time [[Kolvar]] "meets" [[Blake]]. [[The Collector]] places a gold piece on his grave as a token of respect, and [[Son]] attempts to stomp it into the ground unsuccessfully.

The party gains 400 XP, for a total of 8,900 XP!

## Recap

The Reconstellators, hot off their escape from [[Fair Helm City]] aboard their shiny new flying airship (which they name [[Polaris]]), take stock of their current situation and tackle a few pressing concerns.

1. [[NG]] attempts to remove the bomb that is trapping a door in the cargo hold. He fumbles a Sleight of Hand check and arms the bomb. With some quick thinking, he casts *Enlarge/Reduce* to shrink the bomb so that it only kind of blows up on him.
2. [[NG]] goes to check up on [[Garrison]], who, for some indescribable reason, is kind of upset that The Reconstellators fucked up the lives of two guys and then set a city on fire. They have a heart-to-heart, [[Garrison]] assures the party that he'll be fine and just needs to atone for his actions alone, and the two reaffirm their time-tested friendship. It's very cute!
3. [[The Collector]], [[Son]], [[Zrandovorski]], and [[Tilly]] look at the weird corpse in the cargo hold. They discover that the heart is emanating Necromancy, Enchantment, and Divination (which is kind of concerning). They also discover that it belongs to the corpse but it has a different blood type (which is pretty concerning).
4. The party discusses where to go next, and decides that they should go to [[The Hearth of Hope]] and pick up the stray [[Caelia|Caelians]], then head to [[Draskhrr]] to pick up the people who want to leave, and then leave [[The Frozen March]].

The party decides to keep up their watches for the night in case they get attacked by dragons. [[NG]] and [[Kolvar]] discuss what [[Kolvar]]'s goals are once they make it out of [[The Frozen March|the March]]. They have no idea. When they're alone, [[Kolvar]] entreats with the [[Unseelie Queen]] (their patron) to double-check that they're happy with the results of [[Change the State of Benedict]]. Queen's happy and says they had fun, gives them a minor existential crisis by suggesting their visions of the past are actually visions of the future, and gives them a dandelion. [[Zrandovorski]] has a nightmare about his older sister, who appears to be in [[Utopia]] and is looking for his *younger* sister.

On the next watch, [[The Collector]] confirms to [[Tilly]] that he would totally try to sell them out to [[Hadar]] if it could bring his Father back, but wants [[Tilly]] and The Reconstellators to try and stop him.

On the flight back in the morning, they are attacked by a pack of 5 white dragon wyrmlings. Hesitant to kill them as they suspect these are the newborn babies of the white dragon eggs they found previously. They're able to scare them off by stabbing them in the face a couple times, although they do overdo it for one of them and it falls to the snowy wastes below. [[Son]] reclaims a dragon scale for his queen, [[Tilly]].

The Reconstellators make it to [[The Hearth of Hope]] and [[The Collector]] explains the situation to [[The Soul Sage|Vicci]]. They end up taking 200 [[Caelia|Caelians]] onto the ship and will bring them back to [[Draskhrr]], leaving 105 of them here in [[The Hearth of Hope|The Hearth]] to help establish more than one viable city in [[The Frozen March]].

To end the session, [[Tilly]] and [[Kolvar]] visit the grave of [[Blake]] and [[Son]] stomps on his burial mound.
