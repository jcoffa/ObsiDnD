## Notes

- [[Zrandovorski]] uses *Detect Magic* to investigate the cave since the party is on edge after the [[Session 8#The Shoal That Makes You Old|Gibbering Mouther attack]]. He sees that the corpse of the Gibbering Mouther has something magical somewhere inside it. Son crawls inside to get it.
	- Son pulls out a fist-sized gemstone, which [[Zrandovorski]] is able to identify it as a [[Delicious Gemstone of Self-Banishing|gemstome intended to cast Banishment on the creature who consumes it]]. In other words, the Gibbering Mouther was preparing to *Banish* itself after completing its task here. It was probably sent to this cave by some master, with the goal of killing and consuming [[The Collector]]
- [[Zrandovorski]] harvests some of the purple grass to research it later. It appears to shrink inwards slightly after he cut it, as if reacting to the movement. [[Tilly]] is able to identify that it's actually "violet fungus".
- In the next room, through the opposite hallway, is a room with an enormous tree-like mushroom in the middle of it. It appears to grab onto the roof, as if it were sinking its "fingers" into the stone. Mushroom roots are scattered throughout the room, as are a few humanoid skulls which do not appear to have died here due to their missing bodies. A couple pieces of adventuring gear is haphazardly placed near the opposite hallway.
- While [[The Collector]] goes to investigate the adventuring gear, the party is oblivious to how the skulls start rising as they're being controlled by fungal tendrils as if they were spider legs. The floor appears to be sinking slightly as well, as the skull-mushroom-spiders close in. Roll initiative!

### It Had to Be Spiders...

- 9 of these skull spiders have the party surrounded, and they're surprised!
- [[Blake]], even while surprised, is fast enough to go first. He deals 16 piercing damage to one of the spiders.
- The 3 spiders to the right of the spiders attack next. The jaws on the skull open and lunge in to bite [[NG]] for 5 + 2 necrotic damage, [[Tilly]] dodges her first attack and then takes 5 necrotic damage, and [[Zrandovorski]] dodges both of his attacks.
- [[Garrison]] attacks one of the skulls in the left group, the same one [[Blake]] hit earlier, and deals 9 damage to kill it. He notices that the violet moss on the ground reacts to the monster dying.
- One group of skull spiders sink into the violet moss and "swim" through the moss at crazy speeds towards [[The Collector]]. The skull spiders deal 1 + miss + 2 + 6 necrotic damage, and [[The Collector]] *Hellish Rebuke*s one of them to deal 20 fire damage, killing it instantly. The cave itself reacts to the fire, and the party hears shrieking and groaning all around them.
- [[Tilly]] attacks the 3 skull spiders to the right with *Flaming Sphere*. They all fail their Dexterity saves (their Dex is very bad, and one gets a -1 save), which is bad news for the enemy. [[Tilly]] pulls a wad of earwax out of her ear and chucks it at them and it lights up in flame, dealing 10 fire damage as the shrieking continues. She's able to use her bonus action to move the sphere to the group of 2 on the left, and one of them passes the save (exactly meeting the DC of 14). They take 7 (or 3) fire damage. As the ball moves, it sets the violet moss on fire as well. The room may be going up in flames, shortly!
- [[The Collector]] attacks with *Eldritch Blast*, hitting with a 15 attack roll and dealing 11 force + 5 necrotic damage.
- [[Zrandovorski]] casts *Shatter*, dealing 13 damage to the group of 3. 2 of them die, and he is able to use his [[Booyahg Staff]] to allow [[Garrison]] to make an Opportunity Attack, dealing an extra 8 damage to one of the skull spiders.
- [[NG]] casts *Faerie Fire* on the group of 3, and the party discovers they have a 1 for their Dexterity score as one of them rolls a -4 for its Dexterity save.
- From the top of the tree-like mushroom, something tears its way out of the inside of the cap. It's a huge purple monster covered in violet fungus, with many skulls adorning its body. It's mostly a large mass of purple fungus and bones with many arms and legs; it doesn't resemble any particular animal at all. Light begins pouring out from the hole in the mushroom.
- [[Blake]] shoots one of the skull spiders near [[The Collector]] and kills it.
- The right side skull spider attacks [[Blake]], and misses with a 13 but hits with an 18 to deal 8 necrotic damage.
- [[Garrison]] is able to kill one of the spiders by dealing 13 damage. He is able to cast *Compelled Duel* on the large creature, but unfortunately it saves.
- One of the remaining skull spiders hits [[The Collector]] for 4 necrotic damage. He is looking rough, and has only 6 HP remaining.
- [[Tilly]] moves her *Flaming Sphere* to one of the two remaining skull spiders, and is able to kill it.  She casts *Ice Knife* against the large creature and deals 6 piercing + 2 cold damage.
- Son used the Help action on the remaining skull spider, giving the next attack roll against it Advantage.
- [[Zrandovorski]] casts *Witch Bolt* and misses the large creature. That is his turn.
- [[NG]] uses his *Illuminated Cartography* feature to cast *Faerie Fire* on [[Garrison]], which is also able to target the large creature since it's within range. Very clever usage of the feature! For better or worse, both the creature and [[Garrison]] are affected by *Faerie Fire*.
	- [[NG]]: "Hey [[Garrison]], do you want me to drop the spell?"
	- [[Garrison]]: "No, why?"
	- [[NG]]: "Because the creature has advantage on you now!"
	- [[Garrison]] (confidently): "But now I have advantage on it."
- [[Blake]] rolls a double natural 1 with Advantage against the large creature, being the first time in this group's history for this to happen. Huzzah! He chucks his sword across the room as his blade slips from his fingers.
- The large creature attempts to swipe [[Blake]] out of the way, but fails to hit. [[Blake]] is able to hit it with an opportunity attack to deal 7 piercing damage. The creature runs past both of them to attempt to put out the fire, and doesn't attack anyone.
- [[Garrison]] faces a moral dilemma about whether to attack the creature or help it put out the fire. He chooses peace, and begins using his shield to help the beast put out the flames.
- [[Tilly]], bemoaning that the party is over-complicating the circle of life and we should just kill these things, casts *Create or Destroy Water* to cause 75 liters of rain to begin putting out the fire.

- [[The Collector]] attempts to break out of the grapple and fails, after a 5 minute detour to check the rules on if he's able to use Acrobatics instead of a Strength check.
- [[Zrandovorski]] uses *Prestidigitation* to extinguish a "small campfire"-sized area of fire.
- [[NG]] wants to communicate with the large creature, and runs into the spore-filled room and takes a huge whiff of the spores. He gains 2 exhaustion, and he can understand "the song of the myconids". He attempts to sing to the beast, and pulls off a sick rhyme to communicate that the party understands they've overstepped and will leave the cave after putting out the rest of the fire. The previous feeling of anxiety he developed in his stomach after inhaling the spores dissipates, and the creature becomes somewhat docile. Combat ends!

### The City

- Disco says that the group is nearing the city, so if they have anything to prepare then they should do it. It's possible that the giant Scorpian Kaiju is still there. The party anxiously presses onward.
- The cave opens up and the city is well within view. It's almost completely torn apart with obvious paths of destruction from where it ran through the buildings like a hot knife through butter. A giant, four-legged lizard-like creature made of stone rests in the middle of a pile of rubble in one of the demolished sections of the city. It appears to be sleeping quite soundly, and wraps its enormous scorpion tail around itself like how a cat might sleep.
- [[Zrandovorski]]'s *Augury* gives a result of "Indifference" (neither good nor bad), and he grumbles that "Savas has nothing for us. We know what we already knew; that we have to be cautious." They roll Stealth and achieve middling results on average.
- [[Zrandovorski]] knows that this city is called [[Azhvan]]; which is the capital city for [[The Scholars of the Deep]]; a group of investigators and scientists who are committed to learning things about the world regardless of any personal danger, morality, or usefulness. They view the pursuit of knowledge as above all else. Most people see them in a positive light because much of their findings end up being net-positive, but [[Zrandovorski]] knows that they don't really care about doing good or helping people as a motivation. People just mostly only notice and care about the cool stuff they do, while the shady stuff is swept under the rug.
- As the party gets closer, they feel the ground rumble as if it's breathing. But of course, it's not the ground itself that breathes, it's the fact that the creature is breathing while it sleeps and it's so enormous that it rumbles the ground.

The party earns 310 XP, for a total of 1870 XP!

## Recap

Still reeling from [[Session 8#The Shoal That Makes You Old|the dangerous fight against a Gibbering Mouther from earlier]], The Reconstellators investigate its corpse and the lake that it emerged from suddenly. [[Zrandovorski]], using *Detect Magic*, finds that the monster has something magical in its belly. Son carves into it and pulls out a fist-sized gemstone that turns out to be a magic item that lets a creature cast *Banishment* on itself if it eats the gem. The party suspects the Gibbering Mouther was sent here by someone, and then given the gem so that it could return to its master after completing some task. Based on how it was repeatedly gurgling out the word "*[[The Collector|Linus]]...*", they think it was sent here to kill and consume [[The Collector]]. Someone out there really wants him dead.

Metaphorically and literally moving on from that terrifying notion, the party continues deeper into the cavern. The next room has an enormous tree-like mushroom in the center, with skulls and abandoned adventuring gear littering the mossy floor. [[The Collector]] investigates the gear and quickly discovers that it is a mass of semi-sentient fungus, as it has grappled him to the floor. To make matters worse, 9 of the skulls in the room become possessed by more fungus and grow spider legs, and combat begins!

The party quickly discovers that these creatures really hate fire, and slowly discover that they have a -5 to their Dexterity score. [[Tilly]] makes quick work of many spiders using her *Flaming Sphere* spell to burn up large groups of them at once, while also setting parts of the ground on fire as it rolls across the mossy flooring. Mid-way through the combat, once most of the skull spiders are dead, the cap of the giant mushroom rips open and a much larger, bulkier, more menacing fungal creature emerges, with more limbs and bones peppered over its body. While readying themselves to take down the new foe, the party is surprised to watch it barrel past them and attempt to put out the fire. [[Garrison]], still on his [[Session 2#The Creature|"not all creatures are monsters"]] arc, lowers his weapon and helps the creature put the fire out. After grumbling something about "the circle of life" and how "killing it would still be self-defence", [[Tilly]] relents and casts *Create or Destroy Water* to create rain to put out the majority of the fire.

[[NG]] runs into the previous room to huff some fungal spores, and feels a mental connection to the Myconid hivemind. Their communications present themselves in the form of music, so he speaks in rhyme to communicate his feelings towards the large fungal monster. After some sick slam poetry bars from [[NG]] (and a heartfelt rendition featuring "chicken parm" from [[Garrison]]), combat is ended (somewhat) peacefully and the fungal creature returns to the trunk of the giant mushroom.

Pressing onward, the party finds a tunnel-slide that leads deeper into [[The Underdark]] and finds that Disco is waiting for them at the bottom. He attempts to lead them to the nearby city about a half-days travel from their current position, but a group of orcs block one of the paths there. Not wanting to spend another full day of travel taking a detour, [[The Collector]] sends in Son to eavesdrop on their conversation (where they appear to be talking about [[Toran the Red]]) to gather information and see if the party can slip past them. Son hilariously fucks it up by gleefully asking "Hey, what are you guys talking about?" while invisible and is absolutely brutalized by a greatclub 10 times his size. [[NG]] comes up with an incredible plan of re-summoning Son past the Orcs, and using him as a distraction to portray the party as if they are Ironfang members chasing a voice through the tunnels.

At long last they reach the city of [[Azhvan]], but unfortunately it has been demolished by an enormous stone dragon-scorpion-like creature that sleeps on a pile of rubble that used to be a city block. They carefully approach the city as the creature's heavy breathing is enough to shake the very ground they walk on.
