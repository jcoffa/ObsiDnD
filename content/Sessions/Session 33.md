## Notes

### There's a Form

- The room has pillars near the corners and vaulted ceilings, 80 ft long and 40 ft wide. In the center is an altar with something on top. A robed individual floats above it, facing away from the party.
- On the sides, are 6 ft tall doors that are sunken into the walls with no discernable handle, 3 on each side. At the back is a larger 12 foot tall double-door.
- [[Kolvar]] looks up to the ceiling and sees the faded, cracked carvings of some hero discovering a necklace. The artwork is too old to make out much detail.
- [[Tilly]] is able to communicate telepathically with the robed figure since the middle of the room is only 40 feet away. For the first time, she feels *3* mental presences when the connection occurs. She hears the commanding voice of a woman (the party got *VERY* rowdy upon hearing this) and is forced to make a Wisdom save.
- From [[Tilly]]'s mouth, the party hears "I am the guardian of the citadel. Leave at once or perish." She is still able to speak normally afterwards.
- [[The Collector]] knows that The Celestial Citadel is something his father has mentioned offhand after showing up in [[Kota-Ken]]. After "dealing with the natives" they were screaming and looking up into the air, speaking in their native tongue. He asks father what they were saying, and he explained they were crying out for The Celestial Citadel.
- [[NG]] uses this opportunity to lie to the robed figure, saying that the party is the next generation of guardians for the citadel and they are awaiting further instructions.
- [[Tilly]] responds as the figure again, and it states that "The guardian is only replaced on death. You are either here to kill me, or die."
	- She gets a vision of a necklace with a black orb, that's comically large relative to the chain, attached to a severed head. Blackened char with drips of blood on the side of it, like the head was unnaturally removed from its body. To the side is a portal with machines coming out of it (geometrically shaped, like triangles and squares, golden in colour, and almost angelic in a sort of goofy way), and more mummies standing near it. The entire room goes black, feeling her body disintegrate, and she reawakens back in the room with the party.
	- [[Zrandovorski]] feels like he's heard or read about these machinery creatures before, but is unable to pinpoint exactly what or where he's seen it.
	- (The players know these are *Modrons*, but none of the characters know that)
- [[Tilly]] asks the figure why the portal she saw in her vision needs to remain open. The voice thing happens again; "The peons of The Citadel protect this place and its people from those who would seek to do it harm." [[Tilly]] asks for clarificatioin on whether the mummies getting out and hurting people in [[Kota-Ken]]. The figure replies "They hurt people who seek to do harm to others."
	- Perhaps the real monster is capitalism after all. And the [[Kota-Ken]]s doing some, uh, [[Dispose of Natives for the Gnomish Industrial Complex|native removal]] is probably not helping.
- The party feels satisfied with their discussion, and combat begins as they run into the room!

Unfortunately the figure rolls a Nat 20 on the Dex save, taking ~~20~~ 10 lightning damage. She enters the room and takes cover behind the nearest pillar to the left of the entrance. She feels herself washed with some form of holy energy.
- [[Son]] enters the room to follow [[Tilly]] and the *MOMENT* he passes the threshold of the entrance he glows brilliantly and explodes into viscera after taking 37 radiant damage.
- [[The Collector]] fires *Eldritch Blast* without entering the room. He misses with a 12 and a 9 against the robed figure. He pathetically ends his turn, and nervously reaches out his hand to cross the threshold before chickening out and pulling it back.
- [[Kolvar]] timidly steps into the room and discovers that nothing bad happens. He moves 30 feet into the room and *Misty Steps* into melee range (gaining 10 temp HP in the process). He attacks with his sword and hits with a 21 to deal ~~7~~ 0 necrotic damage! Looks like she's immune to necrotic. The next attack misses with a 15, and the turn is over.
- [[NG]] casts *Faerie Fire* on the figure, and it just barely passes with a 14 on the Dex save. He moves 20 feet to the left behind another pillar.
- [[Zrandovorski]] debates which spell to use, and ultimately decides on casting *Sleep*. The figure either doesn't need to sleep or is immune to the Exhausted condition, as it instantly succeeds on the saving throw. He backs up 20 feet towards the exit and ends his sad turn.
- The figure speaks through [[Tilly]]'s mouth and says "LEAVE THIS PLACE" and raises her arms. A swirling mass of sparkling light appears and 6 single-eyed machins descend from it. Their eyes are glazed over, as if they aren't really in control of themselves. [[NG]] signals that it's about time to leave.
- [[Garrison]] runs up on the back of [[Furret]] and uses his lance to hit the pedestal she hovers above. He hits with an 18 to deal 9 damage. Since [[Kolvar]] is standing right next to pedestal, and sees that the stone cracks a bit ot reveal a black orb hidden underneath the stone. 	- The orb is visible but the cracks are not large enough to pull it out yet.
- The figure uses a Legendary Action to open 2 of the side sets of doors, and 2 mummies walk out.

- The priestess uses a Legendary Action to reach out her hand towards [[Kolvar]] with the same move that she used against [[Tilly]] earlier. [[Kolvar]] rolls a 5 and fails the Strength save. Same as before, they are pulled towards the priestess taking 13 bludgeoning and 10 necrotic, becoming both grappled and cursed.

	- [[Zrandovorski]] fails the Con save with an 11, and the *Fireball* goes through now that his own *Counterspell* is cancelled. [[NG]] passes with 16, [[The Collector]] passes with 18, and [[Zrandovorski]] passes with an 18. Everyone takes ~~29~~ 14 fire damage.
