## Notes

### "Good Enough"

- The Reconstellators stand in the cave of the [Catoblepas](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Catoblepas), still reeling from their encounter.
- [[NG]] comes running into the cave asking what happened, and upon seeing [[Blake]]'s dead body, asks for an update
- The party shares [[Session 14#Let's Kill the Fuckers|their plan and agreement with Toran]], but [[NG]] is unconvinced as to why they should be involved at all. They argue the advantages of having [[Toran the Red|Toran]] as an ally in [[The Frozen March]], but [[NG]] argues that they're better off leaving [[The Frozen March]] and bringing allies back.
- The party presses the limited timeline on this mission as [[The Iron Fang]] are preparing their attack NOW, and a final plea from [[Tilly]] arguing that more people like them will die when entering [[The Frozen March]] if [[The Iron Fang]] succeed convinces [[NG]] that the plan is worth it.
- [[The Collector]] investigates the cave for any secrets while Son attempts to eat the monster corpse, but not before [[Tilly]] remembers her card from [[The Lady of the White]] and places it on the monster corpse.
- The card animates, saying "good enough" before unfolding and transforming into a large door, and [[The Lady of the White|the hag]] walks through. She throws down a single platinum piece with a star carved into it, then uses her long claws to stab into the corpse and begin dragging it back through the door, but not before [[NG]] and [[The Collector]] interrupt her, asking if she would sell them a diamond. [[The Lady of the White]] does have one for her own spellcasting but may be willing to part with it if they have the funds available. [[The Collector]] asks if she would return if called, and she says she will, provided they have another body, then leaves with the monster corpse, offending Son.

### ++ Kolvar

- With [[Garrison]] carrying [[Blake]]'s body, The Reconstellators start making their way back to [[The Hearth of Hope|The Hearth]], trudging along through what feels like a blizzard with the snow and the wind.
- Through the snow and wind, a figure appears, an Elven man with white, shoulder-length hair, humming as he walks and unbothered by the cold despite only wearing a trench coat. 
- He asks what the group of them are doing out here; [[The Collector]] tells him they're going back to town for a drink, while [[NG]] says they're a funeral procession. Despite the terrible vibes in the club, the traveler, named [[Kolvar]], decides to accompany them back to [[The Hearth of Hope|The Hearth]], as he also has business there.
- Upon their return, The Reconstellators notice it's more lively today than yesterday; there's a few more people just walking around, and even a small gathering outside of [[The Mana Drop]]. Initially they plan to find somewhere to keep [[Blake]]'s body safe, but decide to head straight to [[Toran the Red|Toran]], hoping maybe pity will get them a bigger reward (it doesn't).
- The re-enter the central cabin, approaching [[Toran the Red|Toran]] and informing her of [[Session 14#That New Gorillaz Album, Or Something|their success in killing the monster, and the loss of their party member]]. She apologizes for their loss, but before they can get their reward her attention turns to [[Kolvar]], asking if he accomplished their mission.
- He says he did, but requires a larger payment because;
	- The route was more dangerous than expected and had more monsters
	- THOSE monsters brought reinforcements, which they also had to kill
	- Now all his equipment is worn and he needs additional funds for repairs and upkeep
- All of this is enough to convince [[Toran the Red|Toran]] to increase his payment, giving the adventurer 250 GP for their work.
- After their exchange, [[The Collector]] asks [[Toran the Red|Toran]] if they could borrow [[Kolvar]], but [[Toran the Red|Toran]] says he's not under her command, just another adventurer and they can ask him directly; [[Kolvar]] says he finds the party interesting so he'll accompany them for now.
- They ask [[Toran the Red|Toran]] if she can watch over [[Blake]]'s body until they can get stronger and (possibly) bring him back. She's hesitant, but tell them that there are rumours of clerics at [[The Observatory]]. She doesn't know their loyalties, but they would certainly be capable of bringing someone back to life.
- [[NG]] asks about their transportation methods; after learning of [[Session 14#Let's Kill the Fuckers|the plan]], he wants to know how everyone gets around [[The Frozen March]] considering it's a frozen hellscape. She doesn't know about the other factions, but those at [[The Hearth of Hope|The Hearth]] mainly use her portals for single-day expeditions; anyone who doesn't make it back before her portal moves simply walk back.
- [[NG]] proposes the idea of using a boat, since [[Utopia]] uses them in [[Fair Helm City]], but [[Toran the Red|Toran]] says it's tantamount to suicide; most boats crash and sink, and she suspects the Utopians have a very specific planned route they travel to make it through the constant storms and threat of crashing on rock faces and icebergs.
- They ignore the potential death of it all and ask if there IS anyone around who would pilot the boat anyway, and [[Toran the Red|Toran]] tells them of a man named [[Grain Verigor]] who had gone missing some months ago. He seemed crazed, speaking of the stars and how they could map his way out, but there are some rumours that he went west.
- [[The Collector]] asks if he had a place in town they could investigate, and he does, with [[Toran the Red|Toran]] providing them a [[Hearth of Hope Skeleton Key]].
- The Reconstellators discuss what they should do about [[Blake]]; they're now in a bit of a time crunch to stop [[The Iron Fang]], and whether they head to [[The Observatory]] to find the clerics or attempt to find a way to ressurect him on their own, they're unsure if they have the time to accomplish everything. With assurances from [[Blake]]'s ghost, they decide it best to put [[Blake]] to rest.
- [[Toran the Red|Toran]] offers the crypt and graveyard here in [[The Hearth of Hope|The Hearth]], but warns them that digging a grave will be more diffiicult due to the weather. They still decide to bury [[Blake]] and return him to nature and head to the graveyard, with [[Kolvar]] accompanying them for moral support.

	- [[Garrison]] is not sure where his glowing strike comes from but is pretty sure anyone can do it if they try hard enough, so [[NG]] decides to test this theory. [[Garrison]] holds up Son so [[NG]] can attempt a divine smite, but he completely wifs it, punching [[Garrison]] in the chest and shattering his hand, which [[The Collector]] heals before going in for round 2.

- It turns out he is also looking for [[Grain Verigor|Grain]], and [[Kolvar]] offers his assistance if he has more information, but the mercenary doesn't know more than they do, AND he's already ransacked the house they were hoping to! The only thing of note he found was a rambling note from [[Grain Verigor|Grain]] saying he needs to find others who are connected to the stars
- The Reconstellators believe that he could be connected to [[The Cult of the Stars]], but the mercenary doesn't know anything about them, and the party informs them of their activity at [[The Observatory]] (leaving out the "everyone who goes there dies" part). He tries to leave right then, but [[NG]] says they'll accompany him in the morning to assist his journey since they're looking for the same person, which he complies to.
- After passing on what [[NG]], [[Garrison]] and [[Kolvar]] learned from the mercenary, [[The Collector]] is disappointed that now they won't have a change to use the [[Hearth of Hope Skeleton Key]], but [[NG]] proposes they could just go now for a late night drunken investigation, which everyone is ecstatic about and they immediately head off.

### Nothing Good Happens After 2am

- The Reconstellators drunkenly head over to [[Grain Verigor]]'s home, inventing a thrilling new dance along the way *(see, it was the most straight forward gif)*.
- The home is already an absolute disaster; windows left open with snow billowing in, completely ransacked, couch cushions torn open, and paintings ripped apart. Upon further investigation, they also notice that someone clearly tried to clean up a murder scene here. 
- When [[NG]] casts mending on various objects in the room, [[Kolvar]] notices a knife returns itself to being embedded in the table, and some sort of cleaning on the rug was clearly done to remove blood, but there is a bloodstain in the room. 
- [[The Collector]] heads into the one other room to the back of the house, a bedroom; the mattress has been torn open like the other cushions, floorboards smashed and caved in, more painting torn off the walls and a chair with missing legs. 
- [[The Collector]] mends the painting, revealing it is painted by someone named "Isabelle"; it shows a halfling family with wooden swords, as if preparing for some LARPing or Medieval-Times-esque event. He also notes the other painting in the living room is by the same person, and also features a pair of halflings holding each other side by side with their thumbs up, and a huge expanse of stars behind them.
	- The stars in the painting are not an accurate star map; it's a collage of notable constellations, including: The Void, The Architect, The Father, The Chalice, The Dragon and The Archer
	- Even after mending the stars painting, there are still some notable stars crossed out; the stars of the void
	- [[Tilly]] notes that the stars painting is noticeably newer than the the other painting
- [[NG]] continues mending around the house, but right before mending the mattress he notices there's a hidden compartment built into it. It would be large enough for a medium bag or urn, but there's nothing in there right now
- [[Tilly]] asks BD to try and follow the trail of blood, to which he leads them right to the bloodstain on the couch. What a good boy.
- The Reconstellators theorize that [[Grain Verigor]] must be the halfling man from the pair, and either him or his sister got involved with [[The Cult of the Stars]], and there was a violent altercation resulting in a death and the other fleeing. Since a body isn't here, they decide to investigate the local crypt, since [[Grain Verigor|Grain]] was a notable enough figure to have a family crypt in [[The Hearth of Hope|The Hearth]]. 
- [[The Collector]] FINALLY gets to use the [[Hearth of Hope Skeleton Key]] to open the crypt, and they find the Verigor family crypt, where there is one coffin noticeably out of place (artstyle must've been different so we know it's interactable). 
- They open it and find the body of a woman, wearing a large black crystal around her neck. Someone recognizes it as a black emerald, a rare magic component needed for dark magic.
- The party debates fervently about whether they should take the crystal or if that would be desecrating the dead, and fail to notice [[Tilly]] and Son making a plan of their own, which nothing could possibly go wrong because of. Son stealthily grabs the emerald from the corpse right as [[Tilly]] puts the card from [[The Lady of the White]] on the body.
- [[The Lady of the White|The hag]] appears as corpses begin rising out of the other coffins in the crypt, and she quickly grabs the corpse and throws down some platinum pieces, telling them good luck as The Reconstellators are surrounded by undead.

The party gains 320 XP for a total of 3,850!

## Recap

We return to The Reconstellators still reeling from [[Blake]]'s passing, but manage to catch up [[NG]] on their current plans to assist [[Toran the Red|Toran]] with [[Session 14#Let's Kill the Fuckers|the assassination plot against the Iron Fang]]. Before they return to [[The Hearth of Hope]], [[Tilly]] tries one more time to place [[The Lady of the White]]'s card on the [Catoblepas](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Catoblepas) corpse, FINALLY summoning [[The Lady of the White|the Hag]], who drops a single platinum piece with a star carved into it in exchange.

The party journeys back to [[The Hearth of Hope|The Hearth]], and meet another adventurer along the way; an Elven man with shoulder-length white hair humming a tune and unbothered by the cold named [[Kolvar]], who decides to accompany them back to the town.

They head straight to [[Toran the Red|Toran]] to report on their defeat of the Catoblepas and collect their reward, but not before [[Kolvar]] receives a reward for a mission of his own, and even manages to talk it up to 250GP. [[The Collector]] asks if they can borrow him since they're down a party member, but [[Toran the Red|Toran]] reveals he's a free agent, and [[Kolvar]] agrees to accompany them since they seem amusing.

[[NG]] gets some clarification about how people get around [[The Frozen March]] (apparently only walking or weird winter magic), and he suggests instead using a boat to get from here to [[Draskhrr]] more quickly, since [[Utopia]] uses them in [[Fair Helm City]]. [[Toran the Red|Toran]] says anyone who tries is suicidal, but there is one man who would be willing to try that she knows of; [[Grain Verigor]], a halfling man who went missing a few months ago, but is rumoured to be somewhere in the West.

They ask [[Toran the Red|Toran]] for a place to keep [[Blake]]'s dead body until they can find someone or something to resurrect him. [[Toran the Red|Toran]] mentions some rumours about clerics in [[The Observatory]] who may be able to ressurect [[Blake]]. After some inter-party discussion and assurances from [[Blake]]'s ghost, they decide it best to leave him to rest, and [[Toran the Red|Toran]] gives them permission to bury him in their graveyard. 

The Reconstellators have the most emotional character funeral ever had. Shoutout to [[NG]] for finally getting to craft shovels, and [[Garrison]] for digging the grave. Solo. In the land of perpetual winter.

The Reconstellators spend the next few hours getting absolutely plastered and talk about... uhhhhh way too many random different things to remember, but are eventually interrupted by the presence of a mercenary entering [[The Mana Drop]]. He is also looking for [[Grain Verigor|Grain]], and [[NG]] seemingly send him to his death at [[The Observatory]] with incomplete information, but very smoothly convinces the man to travel with them the next day to look for [[Grain Verigor]]. He also reveals he's already investigated [[Grain Verigor|Grain]]'s home but only found a rambling note saying to "find others connected to the stars."

Despite any real person's better judgement, The Reconstellators decide to go investigate [[Grain Verigor|Grain]]'s home right now, absolutely plastered (what could possibly go wrong?). They find his house absolutely trashed; ripped cushions, destroyed floorboards, and clear signs of some sort of struggle. As [[NG]] and [[The Collector]] starts mending the room, [[Kolvar]] notices clear signs of a murder cover-up, with a large bloodstain on the couch and a knife embedded in the counter. 

Using the paintings in his home, they identify that [[Grain Verigor|Grain]] must have a sister, and one of them got involved with [[The Cult of the Stars]] and one didn't, leading to some level of violence where one of them died and one of them fled. Since there's no body found here, they decide to investigate [[The Hearth of Hope]]'s crypt, since the Verigors have a family crypt there.

With nothing but liquid confidence, The Reconstellators use [[a Hearth of Hope Skeleton Key]] to enter the crypt with no issues, quickly finding the Verigor section and finding a coffin ajar. Inside is the body of a woman, likely [[Grain Verigor|Grain]]'s sister, with a black emerald necklace, a rare magic component needed for dark magic. While everyone debates over whether to take the necklace, [[Tilly]] and Son come up with their best plan yet. Right as Son extracts the necklace from the corpse, [[Tilly]] places the card of [[The Lady of the White]] on the corpse, immediately causing undead to rise in the room. [[The Lady of the White]] quickly extracts the corpse, throwing down some platinum and wishing them luck as the party finds themselves surrounded by dead Verigors.

The party gains 320 XP, bringing them up to 3,850 XP total.
