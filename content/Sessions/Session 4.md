## Notes

- [[The Collector]] investigates the sarcophagi in the 2nd layer of [[The Tomb of Booyahg]] now that we have the Amulet that lets us avoid the effects of Wild Magic. The sarcophagi are from the 80th year in the Exelcium era
	- The current year 133 in the Excelcium era
- This level has repeated sarcophagi and chests along the entire hallway. [[The Collector]] gets [[Garrison]] to open one, claiming it's impossible to be a Mimic so we don't need to be careful about fighting anything.

### The Party Fights a Mimic

- It's a Mimic.
- [[Son]] tries to scare the Mimic as it begins to try to eat [[Garrison]]. He fails, since he isn't very scary.
- A second Mimic appears as one of the chests and begins to make its way over to us, as the first one attacks [[The Collector]] and sticks its tongue to his face. He's able to launch a Hellish Rebuke at it in punishment. He is pulled in to the Wild Magic fog and receives 120 feet of Darkvision.
- [[NG]] launches a Fire Bolt onto the chest Mimic and casts Sanctuary to make it more difficult to hit us.
- [[Garrison]] takes a devastating critical hit and is reduced to 5 HP.
- This fight goes extremely poorly. Both [[The Collector]] and [[Garrison]] end up going down after some nasty turns from the two Mimics.
- [[Tilly]] uses Healing Word (the word was "Reboot") to bring [[Garrison]] back up, and [[Zrandovorski]] is able to Magic Missile down the Mimic attacking [[The Collector]] and kill it for good.
- [[Garrison]] is able to hit the Mimic attacking him and demolish it with a Divine Smite, ending the fight.
- The party is not looking good, and decides to immediately return to the top floor and rest.

### The Rest

- [[Zrandovorski]] and [[NG]] set up Alarms around the 2 entrances to this level to warn of any incoming danger.
- During the first watch, [[Zrandovorski]] asks [[NG]] why he's named that. He responds that it's short for "Engineer". [[NG]] asks [[Zrandovorski]] if he was born in [[The Frozen March]], and he responds that he was. This surprises [[NG]] as he remarks [[Zrandovorski]] is exceptionally well informed and storied for a Hobgoblin, who are not typically known for their vast knowledge of the world and care for little other than battle. [[Zrandovorski]] says his family is well educated, and his parents taught him to read from a young age. Most Hobgoblins care little for academics, but his family is an exception. [[NG]] doesn't press him for any more details.
- Second watch is [[NG]] and [[The Collector]]. [[NG]] asks him if he has a real museum or if it's all hypothetical, and if the current party is his first group. The museum is hypothetical, and he evades the question on how many groups he's been part of who have died. Spooky.
- During the third watch, [[NG]]'s Alarm goes off to warn that something is coming from the front entrance. [[Zrandovorski]] recognizes them as members of [[The Iron Fang]]; a Bugbear and 4 Goblins. [[Garrison]] and [[NG]] try to shut the door to prevent them from getting in. This doesn't work, and one of the Goblins fires an arrow at [[Zrandovorski]] and misses. Combat begins!

They say that they were ordered to investigate, but the Bugbear says it's no longer worth it and should head back to [[Draskhrr]].

- [[Garrison]] is able to know that what he saw was The Far Realms; the plane where Aberrations come from. His description of the hellscape makes [[The Collector]] remember his Father for some reason.
- The party discusses that the typical dungeon crawling approach isn't going to work because we would have to stay within 5 feet of [[NG]] the entire time. We decide to continually reroll stepping into and out of the Wild Magic fog and then into the Amulet's anti-Wild Magic zone until we get effects that don't suck.
	- [[Tilly]] gets poisoned, then asleep, then Fear, and settles on darkvision.
	- [[Zrandovorski]] rolls into deafness and settles on that.
	- [[The Collector]] got darkvision and settles.
	- [[Son]] becomes invisible and settles.
	- [[Garrison]] also gets darkvision and settles. He grabs [[Zrandovorski]]'s hand since he can't hear the plan of when to continue and what to do in the dungeon.
- [[NG]] uses his Manifold Tools to unlock a chest on the 2nd level. It's trapped and it explodes immediately. [[The Collector]] investigates another chest and it's also trapped. The party is starting to notice that there are burn marks and chunks of stone missing all over this room, and there are still 3 chests left. Is *every* check here trapped?
- [[The Collector]] shoots a Fire Bolt at one of the remaining chests (after the party backs up) and the top of it pops off. Inside is a red-coloured shield that looks like a compass rose. [[Zrandovorski]] Identifies it as a **[[Shield of Pursuit]]**.
	- +1 bonus to AC (instead of the normal +2 of a shield). When an ally within 5 ft of you is hit by a ranged attack and you can see the attacker, you can use your reaction to teleport up to 60 ft to appear 5 ft next to the attacker. If the attacker is beyond 60 ft you cannot use this.
- [[NG]] sees that the Wild Magic fog gets much thicker into the 3rd level of the tomb. He attempts to use a trapped chest to throw it down the staircase to see if the blast can disperse the fog. He rolls a 4 on Strength and barely chucks it down the stairs at all. It detonates all of the fog and the party takes a bunch of fire damage, with [[NG]] falling unconscious from the damage. [[Tilly]] is able to "Reboot" him.

### The Third Floor

A big chunk of the floor has fallen out as Wild Magic fog pours out of the room, filling it with more fog. It's likely there's only a few minutes left until it refills with fog.

He remembers, "back at the factory", stories of large magical presence leaving behind a residue after their immediate influence is gone. He pockets a vial of it for later research. He has a theory of its connection to [[The Underdark]].

[[Tilly]] attempts to coax the spiders into being docile so that the gem can be safely retrieved. ## Recap

After securing an amulet that protects its wearer and any ally within 5 ft against the effects of Wild Magic, the party huddles up (literally) and squeezes through the stairwell into the 2nd level of [[The Tomb of Booyahg]]. [[The Collector]], not 10 seconds after confidently claiming that there can't be any mimics on this level due to the Wild Magic, touches something that turns out to be a mimic. And then a second mimic wakes up after the first one. Both [[Garrison]] and [[The Collector]] are knocked unconscious by this very deadly fight, and the party quickly discovers that being forced to stay within 5 feet of each other is quite inconvenient and dangerous. They barely manage to escape to the tomb's lobby after defeating their foes.

Their only options to either rest in the tomb or risk the march back to [[Draskhrr]], the party decides to camp out on the top level of the tomb and set up Alarm spells next to both entrances. While taking watches, the party can't help but talk the hours away. [[NG]] asks [[Zrandovorski]] about his life in [[The Frozen March]] and his family, and [[The Collector]] regales [[NG]] about his plans for his museum. Their conversations are cut short as one of the Alarms alerts the party of encroaching danger outside. Turning out to be a band of Goblins led by a Bugbear bearing the symbol of [[The Iron Fang]], a fight breaks out as they desperately try to shut the door. [[Garrison]] is able to cause a fuss before finally slamming it on one of the Goblin's fingers, and they were able to eavesdrop on the encroaching Goblinoids bickering about whether they should try to storm the tomb or give up. Thankfully, they chose the latter option and left without any more complications, allowing the party to finish their much-needed rest.

Still needing to get to level 3 and rescue [[Volo]], the party devises a plan to use the anti-Wild Magic amulet to allow them to continually re-roll their effects by jumping into the fog and then retreating to the amulet's protective aura if it's too undesirable. They're able to mostly end up getting 120 feet of Darkvision and are now able to move freely within the tomb since the Wild Magic can only affect a creature who isn't already affected by a Wild Magic complication. They're able to loot a chest ~~easily~~ with great difficulty as it turns out they're basically all trapped and for whatever reason they were really dead-set on triggering them instead of disarming the traps or leaving them alone. They find a shield capable of teleporting its wielder as a reward.

[[NG]] chucks a trapped chest into the 3rd level after seeing the fog gets much thicker, blowing up all of the fog and himself in the process, only looking a little bit incredibly foolish. Now free from the Wild Magic's harmful effects, the party easily finds and rescues [[Volo]] and is able to take him back to his furry companion on the top floor. In exchange for their help, [[Volo]] provides them with information on [[the Toppelgangers]], how [[the observatory]] is likely a point of interest for a cult worshipping abberations, and that the fuzzy tentacle creature that [[Garrison]] and [[Tilly]] saw [[Session 2#The Creature|the other night]] might be a creature known as a "Mage Ripper"; which the players fully recognize from Campaign 2 but their characters have never heard of it before. Finally, [[Volo]] adds that if the party can visit his laboratory in The Southern Isles, he will grant them a "mountainous reward". He adds in an edgy dagger perfect for [[Blake]] to sweeten the exchange even more, but mainly to shut up [[The Collector]] who has been pestering him for a more *tangible* show of thanks during the entire conversation.

The session ends by handing in a bunch of teefs to Berkley Longnose as posted by [[Find Teefs|his quest listing]]. He ends up being the greatest character ever devised in a campaign of Dungeons and Dragons.
