## Notes

### How Long Can We Delay Going to the Factory?

- The Reconstellators are reconvened on [[Polaris]], well-rested and ready to rumble... right back to the Myconid's domain so that they can ask about the copper rot that plagues [[The Factory]]! Notably, [[Garrison]] is made of metal so the metal-eating mushrooms pose a great threat to him remaining alive.
- [[NG]] asks the sovereign if there's anything that can be done about the mushrooms. [[Zrandovorski]] gives him a 20 using his [[Zrandovorski#Foretelling Rolls|Foretelling Rolls]], and the sovereign tells the party everything they know.
	- They know of the plague that was brought on by someone of the Material Plane from the Fey Wild.
	- It's a mushroom cultivated by "the queen of air and darkness" of the unseelie fae. Therefore it will require some extra-planar solution; whether by the fae's domain or the infernal.
- [[Garrison]] asks if there are any preventative measures he could undertake in order to give himself a better chance against the fungus that eats metal, considering he is almost entirely made of the stuff.
	- Short of [[Garrison]] becoming organic or making a pact with the one who originally cultivated the mushroom, the sovereign doesn't know of any way to avoid the mushroom or its spores.
- The party leaves and heads towards to [[The Factory]], not any more prepared against the copper rot and perhaps doubly scared. This is somehow an improvement, I think.

### I Preferred the Squeakquel

- The [[Polaris]] sails another 32 hexes (10 and 2/3 days) to get to the hex near [[The Factory]].
- Due to the long journey, the party has a lot of time to talk. [[NG]] asks [[Zrandovorski]] and [[The Collector]] what they remember from "before the time reset", and [[Zrandovorski]] has no idea what he's talking about. Turns out both of the old farts forgot that [[Zrandovorski]] was born post-Exelcium; they both thought he was much older. Instead, [[NG]] reminisces on how poorly managed [[The Factory]] was for many years.
- Suddenly, [[NG]] and [[The Collector]] spy a dragon high above which quickly dives into the ocean next to the [[Polaris]]. They peer over the railing and see an orange-ish metallic dragon absolutely demolishing a large fish. [[NG]] initially suspects its a Copper dragon, but thinks on it more.
	- Bronze dragons are often mistaken for Copper dragons. Copper dragons are big into art and generally good-natured. Bronze dragons are a bit more playfully mischevious (but not in a hurtful way). They choose some kind of creature, place, or thing to protect and then like to play tricks on them for fun.
	- [[NG]] doesn't feel like the party is in danger, so he crafts a few baskets and asks [[Garrison]] to write "Do something epic" on the underside in Draconic. He places the baskets in the water to communicate with the dragon.
	- *"Hello! I'm Theodore! What are your names?"*, the dragon stands at 9 feet tall and explains that this is its coast, and has been for around 80 years. The natives call it "The Bronze Coast", and he suspects it didn't have a name before that.
	- Having few opportunities to talk to an aged dragon that wouldn't love to tear their head-from-neck, [[NG]] asks Theodore if he knows anything about [[The Great Reset]]. Apparently, [[The Great Reset]] is a creature that wakes up pretty often to determine if it needs to destroy the universe, and has thus far pretty quickly determined that now's not the time. Theodore has heard a rumour of a large bird man running around in [[The Underdark]] trying to search for it.
		- *The players suspect this could be DB; the previous character of Dani (of [[Tilly]] fame) from Campaign 2*
	- [[NG]] asks if the party can do anything for the dragon. Theodore's not convinced that they can really do anything for him. [[Zrandovorski]] asks if he has any itchy spots on his back that he can't reach, and [[NG]] ends up giving him a backscratcher (a 10-foot pole with a grappling hook tied to the end). He is ecstatic at how effective it is. He welcomes the party to make a return with more backscratchers whenever they please.
		- As thanks, Theodore mentions that there's a shipwreck (technically it's a semi-futuristic airship) about a mile underwater that was previously used by some form of "Fae poachers", whatever that means. The party makes a plan to get down there.

### The Briny Depths

- [[Zrandovorski]] ritual casts *Water Breathing* on the party (plus [[Furret]]) and calculated that it takes 35 minutes to swim 1 mile at the 15 feet per 6 second speed.
- The wreckage is MASSIVE! Bits and pieces of what would be a truly enormous airship are scattered among the ocean floor. A couple sharks are seen nearby, presumably hiding from Theodore who was preying on them less than an hour ago. The party members with darkvision can see a few bloated corpses surrounded by some shiny things deeper within the wreckage.
- One of the sharks is also abnormally huge; larger than Theodore. The party needs a plan to get around that. [[The Collector]] has the idea to use [[Son]] as a decoy by having him transform into a toad (which has a pretty fast swim speed) and cast *Light* on a coin in his mouth to distract the shark while he swims away.
	- Unfortunately, [[Son]] rolls a 5 for pseudo-initiative so the shark spots him instantly and swims over quickly to bite [[Son]]. It hits with a 22 and deals 26 damage to instantly kill him.
- [[NG]] sees that the shark is further away from the shipwreck and with his Stealth roll of 17 is able to make it over to the thrusters on the ship. He manages to break it open and find the inner mechanisms of the machinery. He thinks he can turn it on despite it being waterlogged.
	- He's able to turn it on, giving a thumbs-up to the party by sticking his hand out of the thruster. **This kicks off initiative?!?!?**

#### Round 1

- **Engine Phase 1:** The ancient engine begins to *spin* as multiple discs on the thruster begin to whir up and crack free of the sand. A beam of fire blasts out of the back as the nearby water boils. A couple spots on the engine light up with lots of coloured panels as it begins to activate.
- [[Garrison]] mounts [[Furret]] (UwU), swims over to the shark, casts *Sacred Weapon* on his [[Ol' Reliable]], and hits the shark with a 14. He puts a *Divine Smite* into the hit, totalling 10 Slashing damage and 9 Radiant damage. His second attack is also a 14 to hit, dealing another 15 Slashing damage and applying the *Sap* weapon mastery condition. The shark is *NOT* pleased, and it lurches back in pain as the two quick cuts hurt it significantly.
	- Because the shark killed [[Son]] by eating him, a beam of golden light escapes its mouth when it recoils in pain.
- [[Zrandovorski]] casts *Shatter*, and the shark succeeds with a natural 20 on the Con save to take ~~15~~ 7 Thunder damage. He doggie-paddles 15 feet toward the shipwreck.
- [[The Collector]] casts *Hex* on the shark and hits it with *Eldritch Blast* to deal 11 Force damage and 5 Necrotic damage. He doesn't move on his turn.
- The shark allows [[Garrison]] to make an Insight check on it. He watches as the shark's eyes are dead-set, not on the actual thruster, but to the entrance of the wreckage where [[NG]] swam into. [[Garrison]] is able to opportunity attack it, dealing another 12 Slashing damage as it speeds away to [[NG]]. It attacks one of the spinning rings on the engine near the ship entrance.
- [[NG]] notices that the shark is behaving strangely, and its enormous size is also a bit suspicious. Perhaps this shark was created or summoned to guard the shipwreck? Regardless, he investigates more of the engine to see if he can do anything more with it. He's kicked off the "standard startup procedure", but beyond that it's hard to say. Who's to say what this airship engine will do after being stuck under the water for months?
	- [[NG]] decides he's had enough of a scare for one evening, so he casts *Rope Trick* and escapes into the pocket before ending his turn.

#### Round 2

- **Engine Phase 2:** Where the beam of fire and energy being blasted out of the thruster was once a cone, it is now a cylinder as the power output skyrockets. The spinning discs sync up and more lights turn on as the thruster begins to shudder.
- [[Garrison]] has [[Furret]] chase after the shark and reminds it what it *should* be focusing on, swinging [[Ol' Reliable]] again dealing 19 more damage with its first hit and another 9 with his second.
- [[Zrandovorski]] attempts to hit the shark with *Chromatic Orb* as spell attacks are not affected by the underwater penalty, but unfortunately he's not used to swimming underwater as doing this in his homeland of [[The Frozen March]] will cause anyone to freeze to death instantly. He rolls a nat 1 on the attack roll and misses.
- [[The Collector]] fires *Eldritch Blast* again, dealing 9 Force damage on the first bolt and 10 Force damage on the next one. **This kills the shark!**
	- He makes the motions for *Eldritch Blast* as one bolt splits into two, skewering the shark on a prong of force energy.
	- He swims 15 ft towards the wreckage and combines it with his free usage of *Misty Step* to teleport an additional 30 feet. He's able to end his movement inside the wrecked thruster.
	- He rolls 18 for Investigation to try and find some treasure in the area, noticing Electrum pieces scattered along the floor, with his remaining movement he can pick up 30 EP. He also notices a metallic door with some sort of text written above it in a script that he can't read.
- [[NG]] can see that the "shark problem" has been dealt with. He surveys the trajectory of the engine, rolling a 19 on an Intelligence check, and determines that the thruster is likely to barrel directly into a high-speed collision with the main body of the ship in the distance. That's, uh, not good.
	- He leaves the *Rope Trick* pocket and suspects that he could redirect the thruster in under 12 seconds. This means he would still be inside the thruster at the start of the next phase.

#### Round 3

- **Engine Phase 3:** All of the rings are now spinning opposite eachother. [[NG]] can feel the rotation of the entire chassis. The flames at the back begin to crack the fins that control the direction of the output. The inside room is heating up.
- [[Garrison]] sends [[Furret]] over to [[NG]] and begins swimming away from the thruster.
- [[Zrandovorski]] turns around and swims away from the thruster plus a *Misty Step* to get another 30 feet away.
- [[The Collector]] is still happily collecting Electrum without a care in the world, oblivious to the immense danger he's in. He swims over to one of the other rooms in this area, picking up an additional 37 EP, and a small lockbox that has a magical lock on it. He spies a massive metal lever that kind of reminds him of an E-brake. He rolls a 4 on a Strength check so it doesn't budge.
- [[NG]] feels as though he's able to raise the nose of the thruster enough that it will narrowly miss the top of the other wreckages in the distance. He swims back into his *Rope Trick* pocket.
- [[Furret]] swims over to [[NG]]'s *Rope Trick* and goes in the pocket.

#### Round 4

- **Engine Phase 4:** The engine crackles and lurches as its immense thrust overcomes the friction and pressure keeping it in place and it *SNAPS* across the ocean floor at immense speed and careens into the distance. Everyone nearby must make a Dex save, and all of them ([[The Collector]], [[Garrison]], [[Zrandovorski]]) fail catastrophically. They all take 27 Force damage.

That is, technically, the end of the encounter!

### The Last Bit of Travel

- **Bastion Actions:** The Library finishes its research on *The Great Reset*, and the Storehouse sells 500 GP of stockpile for 550 GP, which The Reconstellators adds into party gold.
	- [[The Great Reset]] is taller than buildings, fires a beam of light from its mouth, and has wings on its back. Legends say, if it consumes an artifact, it's destroyed *forever*.
- Because of the 10-day total journey and the fact that the Bastion Actions started before they left, they can actually complete another 7-day action before they arrive at [[The Factory]]. Another Library action lets the party learn about the [[Unseelie Queen]].
	- [[Unseelie Queen]] has a pseudonym of *"The Queen of Air and Darkness"*. Claimed to be evil by many mortals and fae alike, however research shows that fae in general have an alien perception of morality. What they consider to be right and wrong is much different than mortals. To a lot of fae, especially ones in the Unseelie court, evil is actually the act of going against your word and breaking promises. Fulfilling a promise, even if it hurts people, is considered always a morally good thing regardless of circumstance.
		- She's consistently creating, cultivating, and building things at their request. Although she has a reputation to meddle, in reality she never fulfills deeds without explicitly being asked to do so first.
- This also allows them to re-fill the Storehouse with another 500 GP of items, and get 3 days of progress into selling it again.

### Factory-Oh

A large clearing, evidently man-made, houses the actual factory building itself along with other small buildings even further off in the distance. A small area in the middle of the clearing is vibrant and healthy grass, a stark contrast to the surrounding environment. A wrought-iron staircase on the outside of the factory leads directly up to the second floor. A large stone the size of a medium boulder is blocking the entrance to the main factory building; a double-door of solid iron at ground-level.
- One of the buildings is [[NG]] and [[Garrison]]'s abode, where they've lived together for over a century. The interior is mainly two open-concept connected rooms, one half belonging
	- [[NG]]'s side has an elven desk; a precisely shaved log of cypress finely shaved over decades to perfectly fit the space. It contains tons of neatly labelled drawers and cabinets all about the walls. A dozen scrapped automatons lay about the room. There are two visible photographs; one of two tall elves (one male, one female) and a smaller elf holding their hands in-between them, and a second one that appears much later of [[NG]] and [[Garrison]] having just fixed a ballerina-shaped robot which they're sending off back into the world.
		- [[NG]] confirms that the picture of three elves is him and his parents. He muses that the picture makes him seem like an only child; in reality he has many siblings. They just weren't depicted here.
	- [[Garrison]]'s room is more bare. Lots of scrap metal, presumably the fasteners and pieces that he would have affixed onto himself depending on the day during his routine pathwork-repairs . A well-used training dummy is in the corner. A map of the world on the wall looks to have some pins and markers on it; evidently the places that he and [[NG]] have visited together. [[Garrison]] was clearly more of the planner and navigator for their many voyages. Organized onto a series of shelves are piles of well-used survival and wilderness equipment. A few holy symbols, some books on animals and history and religion, but other than that there's a lack of personal keepsakes.
	- The main area in between them houses a small kitchen, a fireplace, and a small seating area.

### It's the Pictures That Make You Loredump, Not the Factory

- [[The Collector]] is feeling *very* nosy having just entered a private space where a great personal tragedy has befallen two beloved party members, so he goes to look at the third picture hidden underneath the other two on [[NG]]'s shelf. It's a picture of a raging bonfire in the middle of the forest with not much else visible in the frame.
	- When [[NG]] got back from a year away, the people here were gone and they had been for some time. Their bodies were infested with rot. The fire is what [[NG]] did with what remained. His past, present, and future... whether you see him as a kindly elf, a friend of robots, or anything else, you can't take that bonfire out of him. He had a cleric *Hallow* the ground; the only perfect patch of nature left in this place.
- [[NG]] explains that [[The Factory]] used to create toys for children to play with. For species of all shapes and sizes. The elven council decided to pivot the focus of the factory to make "helper robots". Clearly the real reason was robots for the purpose of war. [[NG]] suspects they would be sold to [[Utopia]].
- [[Zrandovorski]] bluntly asks [[NG]] if he's the one who brought the copper rot here. [[NG]] thinks about it for a moment before confirming that indeed it was him. He realized that they were building military robots and selling them to [[Utopia]], and he didn't want that to happen. So he tried to turn off the production, and ran away. When he came back to check up on the situation, everyone he knew had died from his actions.
	- [[Zrandovorski]] muses about how it could have done so much damage to the *people* who lived here if it only attacks inorganic matter. [[Garrison]] explains that the effects of the mushroom can make automatons go haywire. They probably attacked their creators and then succumbed to the rot, but isn't sure.
- [[The Collector]] asks the big question; we're here now where it all started for [[NG]]. What's next? [[NG]] thinks he has to start the factory back up. He almost doesn't regret a thing, he doesn't feel that he needs to apologize. He stands by what he did.
	- What he *doesn't* know is why it caused this much damage. It was supposed to just shut down the factory and force everyone to leave. It shouldn't have *massacred* everyone.
- [[Garrison]] asks why [[NG]] didn't tell him sooner. He explains that, from his perspective, an automaton of his kind showing up when he did was unlikely to be on friendly pretenses. So perhaps this friendship was built on a lie, in a way. And *that* is what he regrets.
- [[The Collector]] asks about the boulder blocking the entrance. [[NG]] would've liked to cover the whole thing in a dome, neon green and screaming and if you got within 60 feet it would cast *Fireball* on you repeatedly until you ran away. With that infeasible, a big ass rock was the most he could manage.
- [[NG]] asks what the party would have done (individually).
	- [[Garrison]] would have done the same destructive eventuality as [[NG]].
	- [[The Collector]] would have tried to kill those who changed the focus of [[The Factory]].
	- [[Zrandovorski]] would have "not done anything better".
- [[The Collector]] asks about what Gods [[Garrison]] follows, harking back to the religious texts in his room. [[Garrison]] doesn't follow any particular God (*"I'm not under any one banner"*), and is more about the spirit of protection and honesty. Perhaps mostly aligned with the teachings of Helm, but it's not like he worships Him in any special capacity.
	- Religion was just one of his fascinations. Animals were another; he was in awe of seeing horses for the first time, for example.
- [[The Collector]] asks about the bodies infected with copper rot, and if those were the only bodies. [[NG]] clarifies that some of the bodies were chopped to bits, and that while copper rot prefers inorganic material they're still mushrooms and will infect decaying organic bodies.
	- [[NG]] also suspects that, whatever killed the bodies physically, had little to do with the actual copper rot itself. The cuts aren't clean like from swords and daggers; it could've even been animals.
- [[NG]] has a final point of order. As of tomorrow, he will have lived *exactly* 150 years since the incident. One year for every body he could recover. He promised himself that if he lived 1 year for every one of them, he would finally allow himself to let go. And as Fate would have it; on the very day he can finally let go, he's right back in the thick of it. The main difference, perhaps, is that he is no longer facing it alone. ## Recap

Having just arrived back at [[Polaris]] after leaving the colony of myconids and their leader known as "sovereign", The Reconstellators decide to do a 180 and immediately walk all the way right back to their newfound fungal friendos. The goal was to see if the sovereign, being a leader of mushrooms, had any info on how they could make [[Garrison]] more resistant to the copper rot. This was an important detour considering copper rot mushrooms eat metal, and [[Garrison]] is sort of 95% made of the stuff. What is he, some kind of... some kind of... Metal Boy? We can workshop that. Unfortunately, the sovereign has no idea if there's anything that allows metal to resist copper rot besides somehow transfiguring [[Garrison]] into a being made of organic matter. With no hope in sight of becoming a real boy, and no available strings to pull on that thread, The Reconstellators lumber back to [[Polaris]] for who ~~nose~~ knows how much more walking on this spur-of-the-moment detour. Although I can't lie, I do in fact know exactly how long it was (8 hours of walking).

As they continue their sea-faring journey towards [[The Factory]], something *big* crashes down from the sky only a few dozen feet off the port side of [[Polaris]] and makes a huge splash into the ocean. [[NG]] and [[The Collector]] managed to spy it as a Bronze dragon, which they know to be largely harmless and generally good-natured. [[NG]] crafts some baskets to float on the water's surface and [[Garrison]] paints the message *"Do something epic!"* on their undersides in the hopes that the dragon can read it. The dragon obliges, as Bronze dragons are known to love a good opportunity to show off a bit, and it does a triple axel backwards flip onto the deck. This is Theodore, guardian of *The Bronze Coast* that [[Polaris]] is cruising the coastline of. The party is able to get some info on [[The Great Reset]], gift the gilded reptile a backscratcher, and it parts with the knowledge that there's a pretty interesting shipwreck about a mile blow the party's current location. However, ever eager to make it to [[The Factory]] as fast as possible and see what became of [[NG]]'s home, the party **obviously** cannot afford a detour as dangerous and time-consuming as this one.

After swimming for 35 minutes straight down thanks to [[Zrandovorski]]'s *Water Breathing* spell, the party arrives at a truly *enormous* wreckage of what appears to be a high-tech airship that's been blown to shreds on the ocean floor. It appears guarded by sharks, one of which is unnaturally large for such a creature; larger than even the dragon that informed them of this place, so [[The Collector]] turns [[Son]] into a frog and puts a copper piece with *Light* cast on it in his mouth. The hope is to use this as a distraction so the party can investigate the wreckage, and this works flawlessly for about 4 seconds until the shark eats [[Son]] in a single *chomp!* This does give enough distance for [[NG]] to stealth his way to the nearest structure, the broken hull piece containing a part of the engine and a couple thrusters. He skillfully turns on the engine to kickstart its launch sequence, dooming the party to a horrendous and painful death in about T-minus-18 seconds unless they can do something to switch it off.

What ensues is a sweaty-palm fiesta of everyone doing everything they can to accomplish one of several important survival goals:

1. Steer the engine away from another piece of the wreckage nearby to prevent it from exploding so close by.
2. Get [[NG]] out of the thruster so that he doesn't instantly turn into a pile of ash on the ocean floor when the ignition kicks on.
3. Fend off the shark that decided [[Son]]'s morsel of a meal wasn't enough and is now hunting the rest of the party in its territory.
4. Pick up the little shiny coins on the floor nearby! :D (mostly [[The Collector]])

[[NG]] is able to use *Rope Trick* to create a safe zone for himself before doing everything he can to steer the engine's trajectory just a couple degrees upwards so that it will narrowly miss careening into the nearby wreckage and killing them all. [[Garrison]] and [[Furret]] wreak havoc on the shark thanks to some good rolls and a devastating *Divine Smite*. [[Zrandovorski]] may have also been here for this encounter, if memory serves. Thankfully, [[Garrison]] and [[Furret]] are able to take down the shark right after [[NG]] manages to steer the engine just barely out of devastation range and hide in his *Rope Trick*, saving the party from certain death as it shoots off with a ***BOOM!*** and explodes terrifically against an undersea mountain a ways off. The party gets 67 EP for their troubles, which [[The Collector]] scooped up while helping [[Garrison]] battle the shark, for their troubles.

At long last, the party makes it to [[The Factory]]. It's a marshy area full of dead grass, muddy fields, uneven ground, and many colourful varieties of mushrooms. A large clearing, evidently man-made, houses the actual factory building itself along with other small buildings even further off in the distance. A small area in the middle of the clearing is vibrant and healthy grass, a stark contrast to the surrounding environment. A wrought-iron staircase on the outside of the factory leads directly up to the second floor. A large stone the size of a medium boulder is blocking the entrance to the main factory building; a double-door of solid iron at ground-level. [[NG]] and [[Garrison]] lead the party to their home; a small but lovely hut with an open-concept floorplan. [[The Collector]] wastes no time snooping all of their belongings, asking [[NG]] about a series of pictures he has set up about his workshop. One is a picture of him as a wee elf with his parents, another depicts him and [[Garrison]] having just repaired a ballerina robot to entertain children, and a third more hidden photograph simply depicts a raging bonfire taller than the trees.

[[NG]] explains that the bonfire was previously a pile of 150 bodies; his former coworkers at [[The Factory]] who were infested with the rot. He had a cleric *Hallow* the ground afterward, which explains the one small area of vibrant and healthy grass in the courtyard. [[The Factory]] used to make toys to make children laugh and smile, but management suddenly had other plans one day. They began making automatons of battle to sell to the [[Utopian Empire]] and aid in their conquest of the midlands of [[Teradin]]; modern-day [[Utopia]] and formerly the ancestral homelands of the old goblin society pre-[[Clerical War]]. [[NG]] was horrified of what [[The Factory]] had become. [[Zrandovorski]] bluntly asks if he's the one who had sown the copper rot on [[The Factory]]'s lands after bringing it from the Faewild, and he admits to this. He only sought to render it inoperable, that's all! He didn't know that the copper rot would wreak so much unforeseen havoc on the surrounding area and the people working there. It was just supposed to eat the metal they used to make their Warforged to halt production.

And yet, he doesn't regret what he did and he doesn't feel the need to apologize. He is not a tool to manufacture weapons of war, and putting a stop to it, no matter how bloody the means, has saved an uncountable number of lives in the long run. What he does regret is how he lied to [[Garrison]] about it upon their initial meeting, and kept up the lie this entire time. Before heading off to bed, [[NG]] has a final point of order. As of tomorrow, *The 17th of Growth's Dawn, Excelcium Era 134*, he will have lived *exactly* 150 years since the incident. One year for every body he could recover. He promised himself that if he lived 1 year for every one of them, he would finally allow himself to let go. And as Fate would have it; on the very day he can finally let go, he's right back in the thick of it. The main difference, perhaps, is that he is no longer facing it alone. He faces it with The Reconstellators at his side.

Destiny is a funny thing, isn't it?
