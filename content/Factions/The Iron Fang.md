#  [[The Iron Fang]]

- **Leader:** [[Rykor Steelhand]]
- **Located In:** [[The Frozen March]] ([[Draskhrr]])
- **Primary Motivation or Domain:** Consolidation of military power

A powerful band of Hobgoblin mercenaries. The closest thing to a policing force that exists in [[The Frozen March]], and not exactly concerned with maintaining any sort of peace. Rumour has it that their ultimate goal is to break out of [[The Frozen March]] through a bloody assault on its only gate.
