#  [[The Cult of the Stars]]

- **Leader:** [[The Soul Sage]]
- **Located In:** [[The Frozen March]] ([[Caelia]])
- **Primary Motivation or Domain:** Receiving wisdom from Gods in [[The Far Realm]]??

According to [[Volo]] [[Session 4#Dungeon Delved|they reside in The Observatory]], and have also [[Investigate Underdraskhrr|taken an artifact from a town in the Underdark]] relating to some sort of ritual.

Their largest operation appears to be running out of [[Caelia]], where they are [[Session 22#The Fault in Our Stars|attempting to complete a ritual]] to reach a god from [[The Far Realm]] ([[Session 23#The God of Good? This Guy?|turns out it's The Abberant]]). Whatever ritual was meant to happen was [[Session 24|interrupted by the Reconstellators]], leading to [[Caelia]] being destroyed.
