#  [[The Cult of the Stars]]

- **Leader:** [[The Soul Sage]]
- **Located In:** [[The Frozen March]] ([[Caelia]]) ([[The Observatory]], according to [[Volo]])
- **Primary Motivation or Domain:** Receiving wisdom from Gods in [[The Far Realm]]??

[[Session 4#Dungeon Delved|According to Volo]], they are abberation worshippers who reside in [[The Observatory]]. He also suspects that they have [[Investigate Underdraskhrr|taken an artifact from a town in the Underdark]] relating to some sort of ritual.

Their largest operation appears to be running out of [[Caelia]], where they are [[Session 22#The Fault in Our Stars|attempting to complete a ritual]] to reach a god from [[The Far Realm]] ([[Session 22#Rule 34 Is Being Written Before Our Very Eyes|turns out it's The Abberant]]). The civilians of [[Caelia]] do not regard themselves as a cult; they simply want to attain knowledge beyond mortal means from a god. The populous and even their leader do not seem aware that [[Session 23#The God of Good? This Guy?|the ritual is actually meant to bring the Abberant back to life]].

Whatever ritual was meant to happen was [[Session 24|interrupted by the Reconstellators]], leading to [[Caelia]] being destroyed.

## Additional Notes
- [[Session 13#The Obsidian Dragon|The Obsidian Dragon leader]] of [[The Toppelgangers]] has some people who have infiltrated [[The Cult of the Stars]], but would not elaborate on their purpose or location.
- The party think that [[Grain Verigor]] or his sister could have gotten involved with [[The Cult of the Stars]], and this lead to his disappearance.
